Our Story

Rooted in nature, crafted with purpose.

The Beginning

In 2018, our founder Sarah Chen noticed the overwhelming complexity of modern skincare routines. After years of working in dermatology research, she envisioned a simpler approach—products with fewer, more potent ingredients that work harmoniously with the skin's natural processes.

Sourcing Philosophy

We partner directly with sustainable farms and ethical suppliers worldwide. Each ingredient undergoes rigorous testing for purity and efficacy. Our green tea comes from mountain estates in Japan, our shea butter from women's cooperatives in Ghana, and our essential oils from certified organic farms in France.

Craftsmanship

Our formulations are developed in small batches in our facility nestled in the Pacific Northwest. We use cold-processing techniques to preserve the integrity of botanical compounds. Each product is hand-inspected before leaving our lab.

Sustainability

Packaging is crafted from recycled glass and biodegradable materials. Our shipping materials are plastic-free and compostable. We're committed to carbon-neutral shipping and donate 2% of revenue to rainforest conservation efforts.

Key Ingredients

Green Tea Extract

Rich in polyphenols and antioxidants, helps protect against environmental stressors while soothing inflammation.

Shea Butter

Naturally rich in vitamins A and E, deeply nourishes and restores the skin's protective barrier.

Hyaluronic Acid

Retains up to 1,000 times its weight in water, providing intense hydration without heaviness.

Rosehip Oil

Packed with vitamin C and essential fatty acids, promotes regeneration and evens skin tone.
