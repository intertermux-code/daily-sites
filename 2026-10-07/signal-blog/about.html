About Signal

Independent journalism at the intersection of technology and society since 2018.

Our Mission

Signal was founded in 2018 with a simple mission: to provide in-depth, independent coverage of the technologies shaping our future. We believe that understanding these developments is crucial for navigating the challenges and opportunities ahead.

In an era of rapid technological change, we serve as a trusted guide, cutting through the noise to deliver thoughtful analysis and reporting. Our commitment is to accuracy, transparency, and editorial independence.

"Technology is not neutral—it reflects the values and priorities of those who create it. Our job is to examine both the promise and the peril."

We focus on stories that matter: the societal implications of artificial intelligence, the democratization of computing resources, the intersection of technology and policy, and the human stories behind innovation.

Our Values

Independence

We maintain editorial independence from technology companies, investors, and political interests. Our reporting is funded solely by subscriptions and reader support.

Rigor

We fact-check every story extensively and provide context that helps readers understand complex technical concepts and their broader implications.

Accessibility

We believe that important technological developments should be accessible to everyone, not just experts. We translate complex ideas into meaningful insights.

Impact

We focus on stories that have lasting significance, examining not just what technology can do, but what it should do and how it affects society.
