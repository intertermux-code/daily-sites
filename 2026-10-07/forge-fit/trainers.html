COACHING METHODOLOGY

Every member undergoes a comprehensive assessment to establish baseline metrics and identify specific needs. Our coaches analyze movement patterns, strength imbalances, and performance limitations.

  • Functional Movement Screen
  • Strength Benchmarking
  • Mobility Assessment
  • Goal Setting Session

Based on your assessment, our coaches design a personalized program using evidence-based protocols. Each program follows systematic progression models tailored to your goals.

  • Periodized Training Blocks
  • Exercise Selection
  • Progressive Overload Strategy
  • Recovery Protocols

Regular check-ins and adjustments ensure continued progress. Our coaches provide hands-on guidance, form correction, and motivation throughout your journey.

  • Weekly Progress Reviews
  • Form Correction Sessions
  • Nutrition Guidance
  • Motivational Support
