PROGRAM SELECTION PROTOCOL

Our system evaluates your current fitness level, goals, and physical limitations to assign you to the optimal program combination. New members begin with a comprehensive assessment.

Initial evaluation includes strength benchmarks, mobility assessments, and cardiovascular baseline measurements. We use this data to create your personalized training protocol.

  • Strength Testing: 1RM Bench, Squat, Deadlift
  • Mobility Screen: Hip, Shoulder, Ankle Range
  • Cardio Baseline: VO2 Max Estimation
  • Body Composition: DEXA Scan

We monitor your progress through regular testing and adjust your program accordingly. Metrics tracked include strength gains, body composition changes, and performance markers.

  • Weekly Strength Tests
  • Bi-weekly Mobility Screens
  • Monthly Body Composition
  • Quarterly Performance Evaluations

Programs are adjusted every 4 weeks based on your progress and feedback. Modifications ensure continued adaptation and prevent plateaus.

  • Load Adjustments
  • Exercise Selection Changes
  • Volume Modifications
  • Recovery Periods
