Our Story

Building the future of analytics, one insight at a time.

The Pulseboard Journey

Founded in 2020, Pulseboard emerged from a simple frustration: existing analytics tools were either too complex for everyday business users or too simplistic to provide meaningful insights. Our founders, Sarah Chen and Michael Rodriguez, both former data scientists at major tech companies, recognized the need for a platform that democratizes data analysis without sacrificing depth.

Starting from a small office in San Francisco, our team of engineers, designers, and data scientists set out to create an analytics solution that empowers teams of all sizes. We believe that every organization, regardless of size, deserves access to powerful, intuitive analytics that drive real business outcomes.

Today, Pulseboard serves over 500 companies worldwide, from fast-growing startups to established enterprises. Our mission remains unchanged: to transform raw data into actionable intelligence that enables better decision-making.
