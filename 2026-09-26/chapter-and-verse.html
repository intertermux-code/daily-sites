More Than Just Books

Founded in 1984, Chapter & Verse has been a sanctuary for story lovers in the heart of the city. We believe that a bookstore should be a living, breathing part of the community.

Our shelves are stocked not just with bestsellers, but with hidden gems chosen by staff who genuinely love to read. Whether you’re looking for a first edition or a cozy mystery, we’re here to help you find it.
