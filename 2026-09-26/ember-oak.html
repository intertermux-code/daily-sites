Rooted in Tradition, Refined by Flame

At Ember & Oak, we believe cooking over an open fire is more than a technique—it’s a ritual. Our kitchen centers around a custom-built oak wood hearth that imparts a distinct, smoky complexity to every dish.

We partner with local farmers and foragers to source ingredients at their peak, allowing the natural flavors to shine through the alchemy of heat and smoke. Whether you’re joining us for an intimate dinner or a celebratory feast, expect warmth, authenticity, and culinary excellence.

“Fire is the oldest ingredient.”
