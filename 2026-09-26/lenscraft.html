The Artist

Hello, I’m Elena.

I believe that the most beautiful images are the ones that feel true. My approach is rooted in documentary storytelling mixed with editorial elegance. I don’t just capture how your day looked; I strive to preserve how it felt.

Based in San Francisco but available worldwide, I specialize in intimate elopements and grand celebrations alike. When I’m not behind the lens, I’m likely hunting for vintage vinyl or perfecting my sourdough recipe.
