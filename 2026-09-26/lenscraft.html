The Artist

Hello, I’m Elena.

I believe that the best photographs aren’t posed—they’re felt. With over eight years of experience documenting love stories across the globe, my approach blends fine-art aesthetics with documentary candor.

My work has been featured in Vogue Weddings, Harper’s Bazaar, and Overjoyed. When I’m not behind the lens, I’m likely hunting for vintage ceramics or drinking too much espresso.
