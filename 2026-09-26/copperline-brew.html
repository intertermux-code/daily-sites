01 — Our Story

A Copper Shop Turned Brew House

In 1923 this building rang with hammers — a copper smithy shipping stills and roofing seams across the Northwest. When we signed the lease in 2011, we kept the forge, the crane rail, and the name etched over the door: Copperline.

Our brewhouse runs on a copper-clad kettle we rebuilt from a retired Idaho sugar refinery. Copper scrubs sulfur out of the boil and rounds every beer we pour. It is finicky, it is gorgeous, and it is the reason our lagers taste this clean.

“We didn’t set out to build a big brewery. We set out to build a honest one — one batch, one barrel, one neighbor at a time.”Marisol Vega — Co-Founder & Head Brewer

Fifteen years on, we still mill grain every morning, still hand-pitch every fermenter, and still ring the shop bell when a new beer taps for the first time. Come hear it.
