Frequently Asked Questions

Is Mintwise really free?

Yes! Our Starter plan is completely free forever and includes basic budgeting tools and one bank connection. You can upgrade to Pro anytime for advanced features.

How secure is my financial data?

We use 256-bit AES encryption and read-only access via Plaid. We never store your banking credentials on our servers, and we cannot move money out of your accounts.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Absolutely. There are no contracts or hidden fees. You can cancel your Pro subscription directly from your dashboard, and you'll retain access until the end of your billing cycle.

Does it work with international banks?

Currently, we support over 10,000 financial institutions across the US, Canada, UK, and EU. We are constantly adding more banks based on user requests.
