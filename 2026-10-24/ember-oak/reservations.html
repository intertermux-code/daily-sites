Directions & hours
02

How the House Works

  1. 01 Fifteen minutes, then the table turns. Late arrivals past a quarter hour are seated at the bar if we can hold them; after that we release the chairs to the next name.
  2. 02 Two hours at the table. A full service is roughly two hours. If you are celebrating something, say so in the notes and we will build the timing around you.
  3. 03 Parties of nine or more. The Coal Cellar only, and only by arrangement. Submit the form above with “9 or more” and a host calls you back the same day.
  4. 04 Children are welcome. Before 7:00 PM in the long room, happily. The counter is an adult table — it is hot, low, and close to the flame.
  5. 05 Everything is cooked over wood. We cannot grill an item without smoke, and we would rather say no than send out something from a pan on a hot plate.
