Everything Has Met Smoke

The menu is written twice a week, after the market closes and before the fire is loaded. Prices move with what is good, not with what is easy.

Printed nightly · 4:30 PM Coal beds: searing · roasting · holding Kitchen closes thirty minutes before the room

The Carte

Four sections. Everything below is cooked to order over live coals, so a whole fish may take twenty-five minutes and we would rather tell you now.

An optional flight of six glasses, drawn from the low-intervention list, 105 per guest. The feast runs at 5:30, 7:00 and 8:40 only, and the whole table must take part. Dietary changes are handled honestly — tell us when you book, not when you sit.
