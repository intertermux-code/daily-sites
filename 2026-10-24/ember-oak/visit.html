Getting Here

Three ways most of our guests arrive, ranked by how likely they are to make you late.

  • Rail Foundry Station is a six-minute walk — exit east, follow the tram tracks two blocks, turn at the iron doors. Trains run every nine minutes until 11:40 PM.
  • Bus Routes 14 and 62 stop at Kiln & Foundry, directly outside. The 14 connects to the riverfront in twelve minutes.
  • Car The Barrelworks lot on Kiln Street takes reservations through our front desk; it is 12 with validation. Street parking on Foundry is free after 6:00 PM.
  • Bike Eight rack spaces under the awning, lit and watched from the host stand. Bring the light; the lane is dark past the bridge.

Private Nights & Access

What we can change for you, and what the building will not let us change.

Hire 01

The Coal Cellar

Twenty-two covers below the floor, its own grill and a dedicated cook for the night. Set menu written with you two weeks ahead.

1,400 minimum spend
Hire 02

Buyout Tuesdays

The whole room, fifty-four seats, on any Tuesday we are not already dark. Bands are fine; amplified ones are not.

54 covers · from 4,800
Hire 03

Cook at Your Table

One grill, one cook, twelve to thirty guests, delivered with our own coals and a plancha. We have done it forty feet above the river.

From 110 per guest
  1. Access Step-free throughout. The Kiln Street entrance, the long room, and one accessible washroom on the ground floor. The cellar is reached by sixteen stairs only — we run a service bell down if you need something.
  2. Sound The room is loud by design. Brick, iron and one fire carry sound. We keep no music after ten and we can seat you at the quiet end, which is still not quiet.
  3. Light Low, warm and non-negotiable. Candles and the hearth. If you need to read, ask for a table lamp and we will bring one out from the office.
  4. Diet Handled before you arrive. Note it on the booking form; the kitchen plans the fire around it. We cannot cook anything without smoke, and we will tell you plainly if a dish cannot be adapted.

Gift a night at the fire

Vouchers for the counter, the long room or the cellar are issued at the host stand and by email, never expire, and can be topped up. Ask for one on your way out and we will hand you a card printed on the same stock as the menu.
