Powerlifting block

Twelve weeks to a bigger total

Wave-loaded work on the competition big three, with accessory slots chosen from your own weak-point test. Coaches set your opener in week eleven.

  • Weekly 1RM estimate instead of arbitrary percentages

Engine and HIIT block

Conditioning that respects your joints

Thirty-minute sessions built on bike, ski erg, sled and kettlebell work. No burpee punishment, no unloads on a fatigued spine.

  • Zone 2 base on Monday, short intervals Thursday
  • Heart-rate bands shown on the floor clock, not hidden
  • Scaled to any starting point — the interval stays, the load drops

Mobility block

Get the range, keep the lift

Forty-five minute guided sessions for shoulders, hips and ankles — the three joints that decide how deep you can squat and how long you can train.

  • Screen first, then load the position you earned
  • Bring your lift numbers — we test against them
  • Mat room heated to 24 °C, twelve mats, never crowded

On-Ramp beginners block

Your first eight weeks, fully coached

Start from zero or from twenty years of training — everyone runs On-Ramp at Forge. Technique slots are capped at six people so a coach is always watching your bar.

  • Baseline screen, then five hinge, squat, press, carry, get-up patterns
  • Load goes up only when the position holds under fatigue
  • Graduate into Comp Lifts, Engine Room or Range Lab at week eight

Chalk policy

Block chalk on all platforms and the sled lane. Liquid only in the mat room.

No-mirror zone

The first three platforms face the wall. You learn to feel the position instead of checking it.

Onboarding protocol

How a first month runs

Four steps, no sales pressure, and a coach with a clipboard on every one of them.

  1. Book the free trial

    Pick a Tuesday 18:00 intro slot or any coached class that fits your week. Arrive fifteen minutes early and we will show you where everything lives.

  2. Baseline screen, twenty minutes

    Ankle, hip and shoulder range, plus a bodyweight hinge, squat and press. You get the findings in writing, with the loads we think you should start at.

  3. Eight coached technique weeks

    On-Ramp runs three times a week in groups of six or fewer. Loads progress only when your position holds under fatigue, which is why the groups stay small.

  4. Choose a block, keep logging

    At week eight you pick Comp Lifts, Engine Room or Range Lab. Your logbook carries forward, so nothing resets and no coach has to guess your history.
