§ 01

Three Tracks. One Floor. Zero Fluff.

Every member picks a lane: heavy bar work, engine building, or joint durability. Swap tracks any month — your coach rebalances the programming so nothing stacks on top of something already working.

Track 01

Powerlifting

Squat, bench, deadlift on a 12-week block. Weekly max-effort platform, filmed reps, and a coach who tracks your bar velocity, not your vibes.

Track 02

HIIT / MetCon

45-minute conditioning sessions built around rower, sled, kettlebell, and bodyweight intervals. Heart-rate targets printed on the wall each week.

The floor

14 Competition Platforms

Eleven-inch platforms, calibrated plates, monolifts, and deadlift bars that never leave the floor. You will not queue for a rack at Forge.

The coaching

1 Coach per 8 Athletes

Every class is capped at eight. Your coach films your working sets, writes the notes, and texts you before the next block starts.

The engine room

60 Meters of Sled Track

A dedicated turf lane for prowler pushes, drags, and farmer carries — the part of conditioning most gyms quietly skip.
