Programs · Issue No. 07

Pick a Lane.
Then Load the Bar.

Three coached tracks, written in 12-week blocks and revised every January. You can run one, stack two, or swap monthly — the programming bends to your calendar, not the other way around.

02

Track 01

Powerlifting

Squat, bench, deadlift — periodised across three 4-week phases: volume, intensity, and a peak week where you test on camera.

Sessions run 90 minutes on competition equipment with a coach rotating across no more than eight lifters. Every working set is logged in your Forge sheet; bar-speed bands are optional but encouraged. Blocks end with a mock meet on the last Friday so numbers mean something.

  • 3 sessions/week
  • 90 min
  • Beginner-friendly

Inside a block

Day A
Heavy squat + bench accessories
Day B
Deadlift variation + pressing volume
Day C
Comp-style volume + weak-point work

Track 02

HIIT / MetCon 45

Forty-five minutes, three zones, zero wasted rest. Intervals on rower, assault bike, sled, and kettlebell with targets printed on the wall before you walk in.

Each week alternates two long-engine days and two short-burst days, so you build capacity without grinding your joints into the floor. Coaches cap class size at twelve and scale every movement — the workout is honest, not humiliating.

  • 4 sessions/week
  • 45 min
  • Scalable

A typical Tuesday

Warm-up
8 min row + hip/ankle prep
Main
6 × 90 s sled / kettlebell complex
Finisher
3 min max-calorie row, pairs

Track 03

Mobility Lab

Loaded stretching, end-range strength, and breathing work for people who sit all day and still want to move like athletes.

Classes run in a dedicated bay with mats, bands, lacrosse balls, and a projector cueing tempo. Every session targets one regional chain — hips and ankles on Mondays, thoracic and shoulders on Thursdays — so range of motion actually transfers into your lifts within a block.

  • 2–3 sessions/week
  • 50 min
  • Low impact

What changes

Weeks 1–4
Ankle and hip dorsiflexion → squat depth
Weeks 5–8
Thoracic extension → bench arch and bar path
Weeks 9–12
End-range isometrics → deadlift setup stability
