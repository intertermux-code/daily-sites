§ 03

Questions Before You Commit

Contract

Can I cancel mid-month?

Yes. Give seven days' notice by email or at the desk and we stop billing on your anniversary date. No fee, no retention speech, and your Friday platform reservation moves to the next member.

Injury

What if I can't lift for a while?

Every tier includes a six-week annual pause for injury, travel, or surgery. Elena will write you a mobility-only plan to run during it, and the pause does not reset your member pricing.

Beginners

I have never touched a barbell.

Good — the on-ramp course is built for exactly that. Six evening sessions, empty bar first, load added only when your setup holds. Roughly 40% of Forge members started with zero lifting history.
