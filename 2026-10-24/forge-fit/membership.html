Small print, written plainly

Joining terms

01

Rolling monthly, thirty days notice

Cancel by email or at the desk, no exit interview, no fee. Your access runs to the end of the paid month and your logbook stays yours — we export it for you.

02

Freeze it for holidays or injury

Two weeks on Floor Pass, three on Forge Pro, six on Private Coach, up to twelve months at a time. Injuries are frozen on a coach’s note with no allowance deducted.

03

Direct debit on the first

Billed through BACS on the first working day. Miss one and we text you before we charge a £5 return fee — it is never a surprise.

04

Bring a friend, six times a year

Guest passes cover any coached class except Saturday’s Comp Lifts Total. Your friend trains free; you keep the platform they were using.

05

Data, kept small

Name, contact details, emergency contact and your training log. No sale of data, no third-party trackers on this site, and deletion on request within thirty days.

06

Floor rules, four of them

Re-rack your plates, strip the bar for the next person, fifteen-minute platform cap when busy, and a coach always has the final word on a lift.

Try it before you price it

The free session is the same coached class a £79 member takes. If it is not for you, you have lost an hour and gained a screening result.
