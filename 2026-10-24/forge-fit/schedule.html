Booking protocol

How to hold a slot

01 — Window

Slots open fourteen days ahead

The diary opens at 06:00 each morning for the fortnight. On-Ramp and Comp Lifts fill within a day; Engine Room and Range Lab usually have room until the evening before.

02 — Late

Two no-shows, one conversation

Miss a booked class twice in a month and a coach will ask whether the time is wrong, not whether you are committed. We move you rather than fine you.

03 — Cancel

Cancel two hours ahead

That is enough time for the waitlist to fill your place. Cancel inside two hours and the class still counts against your weekly allowance.

04 — Waitlist

Join it instead of giving up

Full class? Tap waitlist. Roughly one in four slots comes back, and you get a push notification the moment one does.
