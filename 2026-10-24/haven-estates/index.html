What We Actually Do

Four Services, No Branch Office

We are eleven people. There is no queue of junior negotiators learning on your sale, and no committee deciding what your house is worth. One director takes the instruction and stays with it until the keys move.

  1. Valuation by research, not by average

    Every figure we give you is footnoted with three comparable sales, the actual achieved price, and the length of time each took. We tell you when the market is not on your side.

  2. Photography and the written particulars

    Two days on site with an architectural photographer, a measured plan by an RICS surveyor, and copy written by the director who walked the house. Nothing is templated.

  3. Off-market introduction

    Ninety-four percent of our transactions never appear on a portal. We hold a register of four hundred and ten registered buyers who have told us, in writing, precisely what they want.

  4. Conduct to completion

    We chase solicitors, hold the surveyor’s diary, and stay on the line when a chain wobbles. Our withdrawal rate is three percent against a London average nearer eleven.
