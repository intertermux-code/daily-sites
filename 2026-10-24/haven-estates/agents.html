From Instruction to Keys

How an Instruction Runs

Six weeks of real work, most of it before a single buyer walks in. We tell you the date each of these steps will happen, and we tell you if it slips.

  1. 01

    Ninety Minutes on Site

    The director walks every room, opens the boiler cupboard, and photographs the view from the back steps. You get the notes the same evening.

  2. 02

    The Research File

    Priya assembles three true comparables with achieved prices and time on market. If the market has moved against you, this is where we say it.

  3. 03

    Two Days of Photography

    An architectural photographer, a measured RICS plan, and the history researched from the local archive. We do not shoot a house on a phone.

  4. 04

    Quiet Introduction First

    Four hundred and ten registered buyers hear about it before the portals do. Ninety-four per cent of our sales end here.

  5. 05

    Negotiation With One Voice

    The director takes every call, in writing, so you know exactly where each party stands — and who is bluffing.

  6. 06

    Conduct to Completion

    We chase both solicitors weekly, hold the surveyor’s diary, and stay on the line when a chain wobbles. Three per cent of ours fall through.
