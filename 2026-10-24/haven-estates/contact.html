Asked Constantly

Buyer & Seller Questions

Will my house appear on Rightmove or Zoopla?

Only if you ask us to, and only after the quiet phase. Ninety-four per cent of our sales complete before the particulars ever reach a portal. We find that the people who care about their neighbours not knowing are the ones who ask, and we honour it every time.

What do you charge?

1.35 per cent of the achieved price plus VAT on a sole agency instruction, 1.75 per cent if you want us to share with another agent. No upfront marketing fee — the photography, the measured plan, the research file and the particulars are ours to pay for.

How soon can you value my house?

Usually within three working days of your call, and we have been known to do the same evening if the seller is moving abroad. You will get the written figure and the comparables within forty-eight hours of the visit.

Do you work with buyers who need a mortgage?

Yes. Use the calculator above for a planning figure, then ask us for the broker we use — she is independent, works on prime London lending, and will not charge you for a decision in principle. Bring the agreement to your first viewing and negotiations start from a stronger position.

Can I view a house without an agent hovering?

On request, for second and third viewings. We unlock the house, leave you the keys and the plan, and stay on the telephone if you need us. Most buyers take us up on it, and most come back with better questions.

What happens if the chain breaks?

The director who took your instruction calls both solicitors weekly and holds the surveyor’s diary. Three per cent of our transactions withdraw, against a London average nearer eleven, and we tell you the number before you instruct us — not afterwards.
