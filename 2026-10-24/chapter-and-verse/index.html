What we keep, and what we won't

We stock what we can defend at the counter. That means a shelf of translated fiction three deep, a poetry bay that takes up more floor than any high street chain would allow, and no table of titles that only sell because someone shouted about them on television. If a book is worth your evening, it is worth our opinion.

Everything is shelved by hand, by genre and then alphabetically, and every bay has a letter-card on it written by whoever opened that morning.

The poetry bay was added in 2013 after a customer asked, four days running, for a book none of us had read. We now sell more poetry per square foot than fiction — which is either a miracle or a local accident, and we have stopped trying to explain it.
