Secondhand, Priced Honestly

We buy books on Wednesdays between noon and four. Bring the whole box, not the good ones: the box tells us who you are. We pay in shop credit at forty per cent, or in cash at twenty-five, and we say plainly when something is common.

Hinges, foxing and a previous owner’s name on the flyleaf are not faults to us. A sun-faded spine is a record of a window, and we will price it accordingly — usually a little lower, always with a reason.
