§ 02

What Actually Arrives

Nothing digital, nothing branded with a logo, and never a book we could not defend at the counter.

Item 01

The chosen title

Picked by one of three booksellers, rotating monthly. We buy it from the same distributor you do — no publisher free copies, no deals, no advance-reading obligation.

Item 02

A letterpress note

One folded card, letterpressed in town, saying why that book and what the picker expected to dislike about it. Two hundred and forty words, always.

Item 03

The margin-sheet

A loose A5 sheet of passages worth arguing over, with page numbers, so you can dog-ear the book without guilt and still find them at the meeting.

Item 04

An event ticket

Any night in the long room that month, including the ones marked fully booked. Two names on it, so you can bring somebody who has not read a page.

Item 05

The standing discount

15% off everything else on the shelves for the month your parcel lands, and two special orders at member price with the import fee on us.

Item 06

The ledger line

Your name in the green ledger at the counter, which is how we remember to keep your next pick off the rail until it reaches you.

§ 05

Questions We Are Asked at the Counter

Answered by whoever is on the till, which is why the tone varies.

What if I do not like the book?

Bring it back. We refund the book's cost against your next month, or swap it for anything else on the shelves at member price. Nine parcels have come back in thirteen years, and two of those people are still members, which we count as a good ratio.

Do you send the same book to everyone?

Paperback and Two-Household members share a monthly choice. Clothbound members get one of two books, split by the shelf you told us you browse first — which is why one parcel can be Le Guin and the next Bulgakov in the same week.

Can I pause it?

Yes, up to three months a year, and we will note what you paused so the picker does not double up when you return. Say so by the 25th and the next month simply does not arrive.

I already own the chosen title.

Then you get the notes, the margin-sheet and the ticket, plus a different book of equal price from the rail. Tell us the day it lands and we will hold the swap at the counter for a fortnight.

Do you post overseas?

We do, ninety-one members outside the country. Postage is charged once a month at whatever the post office asks; we take no margin on it and we pack it in the same brown paper, which is why parcels look the way they do.

Is there an app, or an online forum?

No. There is the long room on Thursdays, a page in the green ledger by the till that members write in, and the argument you can have at the counter while someone waits for a special order. That is the whole service and it is deliberate.
