来店の作法

How to Come to an Event

Arrive when you like; we open the doors fifteen minutes early and put the kettle on at six. Readings are free and we never take a collection at the door — if you enjoyed it, buy the book or bring somebody on Thursday.

Workshops are capped at twelve and the seats go in the order the emails arrive. If a night is full, reply to the confirmation and we will add you to the list; roughly half of our waitlists turn into chairs.

The front room is step-free from Linden Row, the Quiet Room is up four stairs with a handrail, and we keep two folding stools behind the counter for anyone who would rather sit than stand.
