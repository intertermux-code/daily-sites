Folio 03 · The Venue

The Long Room, Precisely

It is thirty-one feet long and eleven feet wide, at the back of the shop past bay P. We built it out of the old stockroom in 2012 and have changed almost nothing since, including the door that needs lifting.

Long room specifications
Capacity34 seated, 40 standing at the door
AccessStep-free from Ferndale Row; 2 wheelchair spaces at bench height
HearingLoop installed 2018; we print the reading list large by request
LightFour dimmable lamps; candle nights run at the lowest setting
SoundOne microphone, never used unless the room is full
RefreshmentCoffee and shortbread; wine on Friday and Saturday nights

Nights 01–20 · Readings

Open-mic, candle-lit

Thirty-four chairs in a horseshoe and one rule: ten minutes, no notes, no apologies for being new to it.

Nights 21–48 · Talks

Authors at the counter

Twenty minutes of argument, then the room asks the questions. Signed where the author has a pen, which is rarely.

Nights 49–74 · Bench & floor

Workshops and shelving

Twelve at the re-binding benches, or an hour of your evening against the Monday delivery. Aprons and wine both provided.

§ 04

How Booking Works

Short version: members get two days' head start, everyone else gets the door.

Free nightsNo ticket, no list. Arrive within ten minutes of the start and your chair is yours. A · doors 19:00

Reserved seatsName at the counter, or the form on the membership page. $5 no-show fee goes to the re-binding fund. B · counter

Paid workshopsMaterials listed on the event. Full refunds up to 48 hours before; swap a friend any time. C · $28

Waiting listWe call in order and never more than two deep. Roughly a third of “full” nights open up. D · by phone

RecordingsAuthor nights only, with permission, posted to members. Readings are never recorded — that is the point. E · members

Bring us an evening

The room is free for any group that buys its books here: three self-organised reading circles and a translation practice already meet on weeks we are not using it. Write to the counter with who you are and what you want to read, and we will hold the lamps for you.

Propose a night

The room has hosted a wedding rehearsal, a funeral wake for a cat, and one very heated argument about shelving crime by colour. All of them are in the green ledger; only the last one is redacted.
