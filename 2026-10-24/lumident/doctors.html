The Hands
Behind the Smiles.

Every LumiDent dentist is a specialist first and a generalist second — and every one of them trained at least one anxiety-protocol year before joining the practice. Here is who you will actually meet, in plain language.

01 The Clinicians

Founder · Implant & Surgical Director

Dr. Mireya Okafor, DDS, MS

Mireya opened LumiDent in 2004 after a decade of hospital oral surgery, convinced that dentistry could be planned like engineering rather than sold like insurance. She has placed more than 2,600 guided implants and teaches a residency month each year at OHSU. If your case involves bone grafting or full-arch work, she is the one drawing the plan.

Cosmetic & Restorative Lead

Dr. Elias Halvorsen, DMD

Elias treats anterior aesthetics like a portrait photographer: shade, proportion and lip line before any drilling. He hand-layer-checks every veneer case at the lab himself, which is why our cosmetic rework rate is under 2%. Patients who say “I just want my teeth to look natural, not fake” get sent to him.

Orthodontic Director

Dr. June Park, DDS, MS

June completed a two-year orthodontic residency and now runs our aligner and braces programme — over 700 finished cases. She is famous in the waiting room for the phrase “let's see the render first,” and for calling adult patients who skipped care for years “my favourite beginners.”

Paediatric & Anxiety Specialist

Dr. Sofia Lindqvist, DDS

Sofia runs our Thursday paediatric clinic and every sedation consult in the practice. She trained in behavioural dentistry in Gothenburg and built the stop-raised-hand protocol we use clinic-wide. Parents hand her the hardest first-visits in Portland; kids leave holding a sticker and asking when to come back.

02 The Rest of the Room

Hygiene Team

Six Registered Hygienists

Each hygienist keeps the same recall patients for years, so your gum chart is read by someone who remembers last spring. Every one is certified in low-pressure water scaling.

Front Desk

Two Scheduling Coordinators

Nadia and Chris build your treatment calendar around your work hours, insurance pre-authorization, and — for sedation cases — the driver you brought.

Lab & Imaging

In-House Milling, On-Site CBCT

Our technician mill crowns next door and our imaging room lives on this floor, which is why same-week definitive teeth are routine rather than a boast.

Pick Your Dentist at Booking

Every appointment request lets you name a clinician in the notes — first available, or first choice. Specialists consult together weekly, so your plan never lives with just one person.
