04 Before You Call

Questions We Answer Daily

Do you take my insurance?

We are in-network with Delta Dental PPO, MetLife, Cigna Dental, Guardian and Aetna. For other PPO plans we still file claims on your behalf — email your member services number and we will confirm your benefits before your first visit, free.

How much does a first visit cost without insurance?

$149 flat: comprehensive exam, full digital X-ray set, intraoral photos and a written treatment plan with prices. You keep copies of everything even if you never book a second appointment.

I haven't been to a dentist in years — will I get lectured?

No. Roughly a third of our new patients arrive after a long break. Tell us on the booking form; we will pair you with a clinician trained in anxiety protocols and start with the exam only, treatment later at your pace.

Can I bring my kids, and at what age?

From age three. Dr. Lindqvist runs paediatric clinics Thursday afternoons; the first visit is a five-minute “chair ride and count the teeth” introduction with no treatment unless everyone agrees.

What if I need to cancel?

Reschedule free up to 24 hours before your slot, by phone or by replying to your confirmation email. Inside 24 hours we charge a $50 no-show fee once, then simply talk to you about why the timing isn't working.

Do you do payment plans?

Yes — 0% financing over 12 months on any plan over $500, and longer terms through our third-party partner Cherry. Nothing begins until the monthly number is one you approved in writing.
