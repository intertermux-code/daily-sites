01 Treatment Departments

Every Fee on
the Table.

We run five departments under one roof and publish every price before you sit in the chair. Below is how each one works, who does it, and what it realistically costs — including what the same procedure runs elsewhere in Portland.

/ 01 Preventive & General

Cleanings That Teach, Not Scold

Our hygiene visits run a full 60 minutes. You get low-pressure water scaling, digital X-rays only when your chart calls for them, and a two-minute coach session on the one area that actually needs work — not a lecture on flossing.

  • 60-minute hygiene visit, 30-minute recall
  • Oral cancer screen, gum-depth map, photos
  • Twice yearly, or tailored 3/4/6-month plans
  • $148 — adult cleaning & exam
Book a cleaning →

/ 02 Dental Implants

Guided Surgery, Same-Week Teeth

Dr. Okafor plans every implant from a CBCT 3D scan and prints a surgical guide, so the fixture lands within half a millimetre of the plan. You leave the placement visit with a temporary tooth — a gap is never part of the process.

  • CBCT scan + printed surgical guide
  • Straumann & Zimmer, 15-year warranty
  • Placement day one, final crown week six
  • $3,450 — single implant, all-in
Book an implant consult →

/ 03 Orthodontics

Straighten Up Without Living in Brackets

We start with an iTero scan and show you your finished smile before you agree to anything. Most simple adult courses finish in 9–14 months; complex cases run 18–24. Photo check-ins mean you visit us once every six to eight weeks, not every month.

  • Clear aligners, ceramic braces, lingual
  • Remote photo review between visits
  • Retainer included in course price
  • $3,900 — light aligner course
Book an ortho scan →

/ 04 Cosmetic Dentistry

Designed Against Your Face, Not a Catalog

Dr. Halvorsen shades veneers and bonding to your skin tone, age and lip line. You approve a digital mock-up — or a physical trial smile in your own mouth — before a single tooth is prepared. Whitening is done to a shade target you pick, not “as white as possible”.

  • Hand-layered porcelain, 10-yr lab warranty
  • In-chair one visit, or custom trays
  • Digital DSD render included free
  • $1,180 — porcelain veneer, per tooth
See real results →

/ 05 Anxiety & Sedation

For People Who Haven't Been in Years

Roughly a third of our new patients arrive with dental anxiety. Every LumiDent clinician is trained in tell-show-do pacing, and you carry the stop signal: raise a hand, everything stops, no questions asked. Sedation is offered, never pushed.

  • Nitrous · oral sedation · IV deep sedation
  • Hand-raise stop rule, always honored
  • Optional meet-and-greet, zero treatment
  • $95 — nitrous, per visit
