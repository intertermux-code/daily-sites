How Does Your Skin Feel Thirty Seconds After Rinsing?

Towel-dry, nothing applied, count to thirty. This single answer tells us more about your barrier than any questionnaire about “skin type”.

Where Do You Wear It?

Humidity changes how much occlusion you need; hard water changes how a cleanser rinses. Choose the closest description of your nine-month average.

What Is Already On Your Shelf?

We never layer an active on top of something that is already doing the job. Be honest about the last four weeks, not your ambition.

“Thin to thick, damp to dry, one new thing every fourteen days. Every protocol we publish obeys those three lines.”

Rui PintoSenior Technician, Verdant Lab

Order Beats Quantity

A watery serum under an occlusive cream does the work; the same serum over the cream does nothing but sit there. Four products in the wrong order lose to two in the right one.

Judge At Four Weeks

The corneocyte cycle is roughly twenty-eight days. Anything you assess at day four is a story about hydration, not change — so your protocol states the review date.

Irritation Wins

If stinging appears, actives stop and barrier work resumes, whatever the goal was. We would rather slow your progress than ship you a routine that breaks the wall.

12 — Honest Limits

Cosmetics are not treatment. Four answers will not diagnose you, and we would rather send you elsewhere than pretend.

See A Dermatologist If…

Not a sales objection

Persistent rosacea papules, eczema that weeps, or any lesion changing shape or colour. Cosmetic products are the wrong tool and delay the right one.

Tell Us If You Are Pregnant

Ask Before Layering

Bakuchiol is generally preferred to retinoids here, but the decision belongs with your clinician. Write to us and we will build a two-product protocol instead.

Prescription Actives Come First

Tretinoin, Adapalene

If you are on a prescribed retinoid, keep it. We recommend the cleanser and serum as support and leave the evening active entirely in your doctor’s hands.
