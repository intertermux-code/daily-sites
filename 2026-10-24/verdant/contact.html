14 — Frequently Asked

Where Is My Order?

Dispatch confirmation carries a tracking number within twenty-four hours of the batch release. EU deliveries arrive in two to four working days, US and UK in four to seven with duties already paid. If a parcel stalls past seven, write with your order number and we reship rather than wait on the carrier.

Can I Mix Bakuchiol With My Retinoid?

Not on the same night. Bakuchiol is our alternative for people who cannot tolerate a retinoid, not a booster to layer on top of tretinoin. On prescription actives, keep the cleanser and the serum as support and leave the evening treatment to your prescriber.

Why Does My Serum Feel Different This Batch?

Shea, squalane and ceramide pre-mixes vary slightly by harvest and refinery run. We hold tolerance to 0.2 pH and a narrow viscosity band; inside that, texture can read lighter or richer. If it falls outside, the batch never left the lab — send us the code and we will check the retained record.

Do You Test On Animals?

No, and EU law prohibits it for cosmetics. Our panels are human volunteers in Porto who patch-test for twenty-eight days with signed consent and paid attendance. Ingredient suppliers provide animal-free declarations with each certificate of analysis.

Is Verdant Safe In Pregnancy?

Cleanser and serum are fragrance-free and contain no retinoid, which is why most clinicians are comfortable with them. Our recommendation is always the same: confirm with your midwife or dermatologist first, then write to us and we will build you a two-product protocol.

Do You Ship To My Country?

We ship to 38 countries, listed at checkout with duties. Where a market requires local registration we do not ship rather than send informally — no unlabelled parcels, no grey-market resellers, even when customers ask.
