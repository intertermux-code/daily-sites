01

Barrier Serum 3.4

Ceramide NP, ceramide AP and phytosphingosine at a 3:1:1 ratio, carried in squalane with 2% glycerin. It is a leave-on lipid replacement for skin that stings after cleansing, flaking under makeup or reacting to actives you started too quickly.

Fourteen drops is a face and neck. Apply to damp skin within twenty seconds of rinsing, then seal with cream while the serum is still tacky.

02

Gel Cleanse pH 5.5

Sodium cocoyl glutamate and coco-glucoside at 7% total surfactant, buffered to pH 5.5 with citrate so the rinse water in hard-water cities does not leave a film. It is a single cleanser for both steps: thirty seconds for the morning wash, two passes on SPF days.

Dispense a almond-sized amount into dry hands, emulsify with water, then rinse lukewarm. No cloth, no brush, no second cleanser required.

03

Night Bakuchiol 1

Bakuchiol at 1% with 0.4% bisabolol in a light ester base. It works the same receptor pathway as retinaldehyde without the retinoid inflammatory window, so sensitive and rosacea-prone skin can use it four nights a week through winter.

Evening only. Two drops, on dry skin, followed by serum after five minutes. Introduce on nights one, three and five, then build upward as tolerance shows.

06 — Refill Program

Keep The Jar, Send The Pod

Every Verdant vessel is designed to outlast three formula cycles. When you run out, order a pod at twenty per cent less, drop the empty into the prepaid pouch that came with your first order, and post it back.

  • Pods ship 40% lighter than full jars — lower freight, lower emissions
  • Returned aluminium is re-sterilised in-house at Bonfim
  • Three returned pods earns a complimentary full formula of your choice
