Toolkit

Host a Food Drive People Actually Finish

The best drives in the Valley collect 120 lbs of protein and pasta, not 400 lbs of mystery soup. Here is the list we send every host.

Always short on our shelves

  • Canned tuna, salmon and chicken — the single most-requested item
  • Dry and canned beans, lentils
  • Peanut and sunflower butter
  • Whole-grain pasta and rice
  • Low-sodium tomato products and broth
  • Oats, cereal without added sugar, shelf-stable milk
  • Baby food, formula (sealed, in date) and diapers
  • Coffee, tea, cooking oil, foil and trash bags

Please do not send

  • Home-canned goods or anything with a broken seal
  • Glass jars we cannot insure on a pallet
  • Anything past its date, or with a date we cannot read
  • Opened boxes, mixes with missing instructions, expired infant formula

What the toolkit includes

  • Printable posters and bin labels in English and Spanish
  • A four-week timeline with the two dates that matter most
  • Goal tracker template for classrooms and break rooms
  • Free bin delivery and pickup anywhere in six counties
  • A thank-you photo and pound count for your bulletin

Request it through the form above (topic: Hosting a food drive) or call (541) 555-0151 and ask for Dev.

For organisations

Become a Partner Distribution Site

Thirty-eight agencies already hold a Bright Harvest shelf. You need four open hours a week, a dry or cold storage corner, and one trained food-handler on duty. We supply stock, signage, restocking twice weekly, insurance coverage for distributed food and the county reporting paperwork.

  • Application review takes about three weeks, including a site visit
  • No fees, no minimum purchase, no requirement to serve anyone’s faith
  • Partner requirement: never ask for ID, income proof or immigration status
  • New partners start with a 400-lb weekly allocation, adjusted by actual counts

For neighbours

Share Your Story on Your Terms

Use the message form above with the topic “Sharing my story,” or call Marisol directly at (541) 555-0144. She will ask what you want people to know, then send you the draft before anything is published — and you can pull it any time after.

  • Choose your name

    Full name, first name, first name and initial, or a pseudonym. Neighbours who used pseudonyms still got recognised by their own block.

  • Choose your picture

    We photograph hands, bags, garden rows and doorsteps on request. No face, no address, no school visible.

  • Keep the veto

    Every story carries a standing withdrawal. One neighbour pulled hers in 2024 when her workplace found the site; we removed it the same afternoon.

Accountability

We follow the national charity standards on donor privacy and program transparency. If we get something wrong — a short shelf, a rude intake, a number that does not add up — tell us at access@brightharvest.org. Complaints are read by the executive director, answered within three days and reported to the board quarterly.
