Teams & groups

Bring Eight People and a Thursday

Corporate and group days run 9 a.m.–1 p.m. and 2–6 p.m., twelve people maximum, booked two weeks out. We supply aprons, a safety brief, a shift lead and a photo of what you moved. You get 60 kits packed; your team gets a warehouse tour and lunch from the kitchen.

  • Minimum eight people, $180 suggested group pledge or 200 lbs of shelf-stable food
  • Wheelchair-accessible packing station with a seated line always available
  • Youth groups age 12+ with one adult per four students
  • Book through groups@brightharvest.org or the contact form
Do I need a background check?

Only for School Plate backpack routes and any shift working directly with children. Warehouse, market and kitchen shifts do not require one.

Can teenagers volunteer?

Yes — ages 14 and up on the Dawn Sort with an adult, ages 12 and up on the Sunday kit line with a group sponsor. Community-service hours are signed off in the book you bring or emailed the same day.

What should I wear and bring?

Closed-toe shoes, no loose sleeves near the conveyor, hair tied back, and a water bottle. The warehouse runs at 45°F, so bring a layer. Aprons and gloves are ours.

Is there a minimum commitment?

None whatsoever. Roughly a third of our roster is one-shift-only, and those shifts still move 340 tons a year. If you come back, we will remember you.

Do you serve meals for food-insecure neighbours?

Yes — volunteers eat from the same pots as the Tuesday supper service. There is no separate “staff lunch.”
