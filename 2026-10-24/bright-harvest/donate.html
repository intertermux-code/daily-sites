Beyond the card

Other Ways to Fill the Truck

Employer matching

Forty-one Midland Valley employers double employee gifts through Benevity, YourCause or a paper form. Search “Bright Harvest Food Bank, EIN 47-0912836” in your benefits portal before you give — it takes ninety seconds and roughly $58,000 goes unclaimed here every year.

Donor-advised funds and stock

We are registered with Fidelity Charitable, Vanguard, Schwab and the Community Foundation of Midland Valley. Appreciated stock held over a year is the smartest gift you can send us: you skip the capital gains and we get the full value.

Gifts in honour or memory

Dedicate any gift and we mail a hand-stamped card to the person you name. The Harvest Table Collective keeps a standing bench for memorial gifts; three families this year funded a Mobile Market stop in someone’s name.

Legacy and planned gifts

Nine of our cold trucks exist because someone named Bright Harvest in a will. Talk to Marlene, our development director, at planned@brightharvest.org — no pressure, no solicitor, just numbers.

Food, equipment and time

  • End-of-season garden harvests, picked up free at your gate
  • Unopened, in-date shelf-stable groceries on Tuesdays only
  • Pallet jacks, shelving, coolers and unused pallets
  • Catering surplus, rescued same-day by the 6 p.m. van

Before you click

Donor Questions, Answered Plainly

Is my gift tax-deductible?

Yes. Bright Harvest is a 501(c)(3) under EIN 47-0912836, and every gift of $2 or more gets an itemised receipt by email within one minute. January brings a bundled annual letter.

Can I change or cancel a monthly gift?

Any time, yourself, no phone call. Every monthly receipt has a manage-gift link; it opens a page where you can change the amount, swap the card, pause a month or cancel. Roughly one in five givers pauses once — usually in December or June — and most come back.

Do you take a cut of my donation?

Payment processing costs us 2.2 per cent, which we absorb rather than ask you to top up. Ninety-one cents of every dollar reaches food; six cents funds the asking and three cents keeps the lights, insurance and audit honest.

Can I designate my gift to one pantry or one town?

For a gift of $500 or more, yes — name the site and we will send a photo of the shelf it stocked. Below that, designating to a single pantry would leave other sites short, so we point your gift at the thinnest shelf that week and show you where it landed.

What happens to my information?

We keep your name, email and giving history to send receipts and comply with tax record-keeping. We do not sell, rent or trade donor lists, and we do not share addresses with partners. Ask us to be removed from mailings and we do it the same day.

My employer gives through a workplace campaign — does that count?

It counts, and it arrives with lower fees than a card. Give through your campaign and send us the confirmation so we can credit the total correctly; matching funds usually land in our account the following spring.

Money Buys the Food. Hours Move It.

Two hours on a Wednesday dawn sort keeps 4,000 meals from spoiling. If you gave above, thank you — now come stand next to the results.
