The journey of a donation

How Twenty Dollars Becomes Dinner

Follow one $20 gift through the Bright Harvest line. Six hands, four counties, fifty-two hours — and a family that eats together on Saturday.

  1. 01
    Hour 0 · Your gift

    You Give Twenty Dollars

    Achorage, pay-at-the-counter, monthly preset — it does not matter. Ninety-one cents land in the food fund the moment the gift clears.

    $0 See the giving options
  2. 02
    Hour 6 · Rescue

    Our Truck Hits the Farms

    Refrigerated routes leave the Cedar Street warehouse at dawn. Sunflower-swept carrots, misshapen tomatoes and bakery day-ends that would otherwise be tilled under.

    0
  3. 03
    Hour 14 · Warehouse

    Volunteers Sort at Dawn Shift

    Twelve people, two hours, one conveyor. Everything is weighed, graded and photographed so the county ledger stays honest and the allergen labels stay correct.

  4. 04
    Hour 30 · Network

    Forty-One Pantries Restock

    Church basements, school libraries, a barbershop in Fordell and a laundromat in Eastgate. Neighbours pick up food where they already go.

    0
  5. 05
    Hour 44 · Table

    Danielle Cooks Saturday Dinner

    A kit with potatoes, greens, a roast, milk and bread. Enough that she is not deciding between the electric bill and the grocery aisle.

Three Ways to Put Food on a Table This Week

  • 01

    Give Money

    Our buying power beats any grocery run: $20 buys sixty meals because we purchase wholesale and rescue at cost.

    • $20 — sixty meals
    • $60 — one month of Mobile Market produce for a family of four
    • $250 — a full Family Kit shelf for one pantry
    Donate now
  • 02

    Give Hours

    Two-hour shifts, no experience, no minimum commitment. Sixty per cent of our volunteers started exactly once.

    • Dawn sort · 6–8 a.m. Wed & Sat
    • Market set-up · Thu afternoons
    • Route driver · weekend slots
    Sign up to help
  • 03

    Give Food

    We accept unbranded, in-date groceries only on Tuesdays — but fresh produce and protein are what actually changes a meal.

    • Drop bins at 14 Cedar & Sage stores
    • End-of-season garden harvest pickups
    • Bakery day-old from Golden Flake
    Food drive toolkit

Need Food This Week? Start Here.

  1. 1

    Call or text (541) 555-0148. A real person answers 7 a.m. to 7 p.m., seven days.

  2. 2

    Pick a site nearest you from 41 pantry locations, or wait for the Mobile Market bus.

  3. 3

    Bring a bag. No ID, no income test, no paperwork. Take what your household eats.
