Instrument panel · rooms

Four Rooms, Four Different Physics

Room A — online

Pulse Mainstage

Capacity 14,000 · 128 kW L-Acoustics array · 42-metre LED canopy hung on the old hangar door rail

The headline room. Curved soffit throws every set back into the salt apron, so the field sounds like the front rail.

Room B — online

Chrome Dome

Capacity 5,200 · Geodesic shell · 360° projection · 12 sub-hangs at floor level

Inside the mirrored dome there is no front. Laser maps trace the struts and the crowd rotates with them until late.

Room C — limited capacity

Analog Vault

Capacity 1,100 · All-analogue rigs · No laptops · No phone photography

A concrete fuel bunker. Warm, slow, and deliberately under-lit. Door policy is first-come and it fills by 22:30.

Room D — online

Nebula Garden

Capacity 3,400 · Open air · Sun-facing terraces · Harp-lit eucalyptus ring

Daytime listening and the two sunset slots. Deepest hour is the last light of Sunday, when the whole garden goes quiet on the drop.

Site report

A Hangar, an Apron, and a Bay Full of Fog

Terminal 9 sat empty for thirty years before we wired it. The mainstage leans against Hangar 3’s original door track, the Chrome Dome is bolted to the fuel-apron pad, and Nebula Garden grows out of the old flight-test lawn. Everything is flat, everything is walkable, and the last shuttle leaves when the last stage stops.
