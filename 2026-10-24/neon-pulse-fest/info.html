Module 02 — arrival

Getting There and Back

There is no parking at Terminal 9. We bought the lots next door for production. Plan on the ferry or the shuttle — both are included or nearly free, and both run past curfew.

Ferry · included with every pass

Pier 9, San Francisco

Every 30 min, 13:00–04:30 · 22 min crossing · dock walk is 150 m to Gate A

Shuttle · $35 add-on

Embarcadero, Oakland City Center, San Jose Diridon

Every 30 min from 12:00 to 05:00 · last pick-up at the Gate A curb

BART + bike

Alameda Station, then 2.4 km

Free bike valet at Gate B until 03:30 · lighted route along the waterfront

Accessibility transport

Paratransfer drop-off

Kiss-and-ride at Gate A with staff escort · book through the access desk by 10 December

Leave on time

Sunday is the tight one: the last ferry leaves the dock at 04:30, ten minutes after the site finishes clearing. If you are driving anyone, send them on the 04:00 boat.

Module 03 — access

Access Is Not an Add-On

Every stage deck at Terminal 9 is step-free, and we built the routes before we built the stages. The access desk is staffed from gate opening to site clear, and companion tickets are free with any paid pass — no documentation to upload, no argument at the door.

Decks

Raised, level, covered viewing platforms at Mainstage, Dome, and Nebula

Seating

Reserved seats with sight-line guarantee; book per night, bring one companion

Quiet

Sensory room in Hangar 3, open 15:00–04:00, no bass below 60 Hz, staff trained in de-escalation

Hearing

Loop system at all four stage desks and both information tents; hi-fi plugs free to access holders

Mobility

Free wheelchair and scooter charging at Gate B; golf-cart runs on request between rooms

Service animals

Always welcome, with a relief area beside the Vault concourse and water bowls provided

Module 04 — gate policy

What Comes In and What Stays Out

Allowed

  • Yes

    One small clear bag per person, plus a empty reusable bottle under 1 L

  • Yes

    Layered warm clothing, blankets, and a change of socks for the apron

  • Yes

    Prescription medication in its original label, with a copy of the script

  • Yes

    Camera with interchangeable lenses, selfie sticks excluded

  • Yes

    Unsealed ear defenders for children and sound-sensitive attendees

Not allowed

  • No

    Outside food or drink of any kind — the 19 vendors are the festival

  • No

    Drones, fireworks, laser pointers, or anything flared

  • No

    Folding chairs, gazebos, or tent structures on the field

  • No

    Professional video rigs or any tripod over 1.2 m

  • No

    Re-entry after 01:30 — the gate count is a licensing condition
