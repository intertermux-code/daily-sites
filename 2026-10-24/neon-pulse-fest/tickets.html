Guarantee

We charge no service fee and no convenience fee. If Neon Pulse is cancelled and cannot be rescheduled, every pass is refunded in full automatically — no form, no waiting.

What the wristband opens

One Pass, Every Door on the Apron

There is no second ticket for the Dome and no paid fast lane into the Vault. Your pass scans once at the gate you choose and then works on all four rooms, all three nights, including the 01:00 to 03:00 stretch when most festivals have already stopped the music.

  • Scan

    Live QR in the app, refreshed every 30 seconds so screenshots and resales fail at the gate

  • Re-entry

    Unlimited until 01:30 on Friday and Saturday, then in only — the licence caps night-time exits

  • ID

    Name matched at Gate A and B only for access-seating and Chrome VIP holders
