Common Questions

Is my financial data secure?

Absolutely. We use 256-bit AES encryption and read-only access tokens. We never store your banking credentials, and we never sell your data to third parties. Our infrastructure is audited annually for SOC 2 compliance.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no contracts or cancellation fees. If you cancel Premium, your account reverts to Essential at the end of your billing cycle, and you retain access to all historical data.

Do you support international banks?

Currently, Mintwise supports over 12,000 institutions across the US, Canada, UK, and EU. We are expanding coverage quarterly. Check our supported institutions list in the app for real-time availability.
