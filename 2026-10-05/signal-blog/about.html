SIGNAL.
01 — Mission

Technology is not neutral.
Neither is our coverage.

Signal was founded in 2021 on a simple premise: tech journalism should serve readers, not advertisers. We are fully reader-funded, carry no display ads, and accept no sponsored content. Every story we publish answers to one question: does this help people understand how power actually works in the digital age?

03 — Ethics

Our Commitments

No Advertising

We carry zero display ads, native ads, or sponsored content. Our revenue comes exclusively from subscriptions and donations.

Source Protection

We use SecureDrop and maintain strict source confidentiality protocols. Whistleblowers can reach us safely.

Corrections Policy

Errors are corrected transparently with timestamps. Significant corrections trigger editorial review.

Financial Transparency

We publish annual financial reports detailing revenue sources, expenses, and compensation ranges.
