Our Story

Built on Ash & Intention

01

Why Wood Fire?

In an era of precision induction and combi-ovens, we chose the hardest path. Live fire is unpredictable. It demands attention, respect, and years of intuition. No two logs burn identically. No two embers radiate the same heat. This imperfection is our signature.

We source oak, cherry, and applewood from sustainable orchards within 100 miles. Each wood type imparts distinct character: oak for structure, cherry for sweetness, apple for delicate smoke. Our chefs read the fire like musicians read silence—knowing when to push, when to rest, when to let the ingredient speak.

“Fire doesn’t lie. You can’t hide behind sauce or technique. The flame reveals everything.”
— Chef Elena Vasquez, Founder

The Hands Behind the Hearth

Elena Vasquez

Executive Chef & Founder

Formerly of Noma and Asador Etxebarri. Elena spent three years apprenticing with Basque pit masters before opening Ember & Oak. She believes vegetables deserve the same reverence as protein.

Marcus Chen

Sous Chef

A pastry chef turned fire cook, Marcus brings precision to primal methods. His ember-roasted desserts have become legendary. He manages our fermentation program and house-made charcuterie.

Amara Osei

Beverage Director

Amara curates our natural wine list and craft cocktail program with the same ethos as our kitchen: minimal intervention, maximum expression. Her smoked old fashioned uses house-charred maple syrup.
