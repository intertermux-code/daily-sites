The Hearth Is Our Heartbeat

We don’t use gas. We don’t use electric ovens. Every dish at Ember & Oak is kissed by live flame, cooked over sustainably sourced oak and fruitwoods. The result isn’t just flavor—it’s texture, aroma, and a connection to culinary traditions that predate modern kitchens by millennia.

Our menu shifts with the seasons because the fire demands honesty. If the ingredient can’t stand up to the heat, it doesn’t make the plate.

Signature Plates

Hand-cut from prime beef, aged in-house for 45 days to develop deep umami. Seared directly on white-hot coals, finished with smoked bone marrow butter and flaky sea salt. Served with charred shallot ash.

Sustainably caught Mediterranean sea bass, stuffed with preserved lemon, fennel fronds, and garlic confit. Slow-roasted over dying embers for 25 minutes until skin crackles and flesh flakes. Finished with green olive tapenade.

Heirloom beets buried in hot ashes for two hours, then diced and folded with capers, cornichons, mustard seed, and horseradish crème fraîche. Topped with rye crisps and dill oil. A vegetarian centerpiece that eats like meat.
