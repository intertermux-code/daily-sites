Reservations

We hold tables for 15 minutes past reservation time. For parties of 8 or more, private events, or special requests, please call us directly at (503) 555-0198.

Before You Book

  • Dinner Service: Tue–Sun, 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM
  • Bar Seating: First-come, first-served. Full menu available.
  • Cancellation Policy: Please cancel by noon on the day of your reservation to avoid a $25/person fee.
  • Allergies: Note dietary restrictions in the form below. We accommodate most needs but cannot guarantee nut-free environments.

Peak Hours

Fri & Sat evenings book 2–3 weeks in advance. Midweek availability is typically open 3–5 days out.
