Guardians of the Physical Page

In an age of infinite scroll, we chose to remain finite. Chapter & Verse was founded on the belief that a bookstore is not merely a retail space, but a civic institution—a place where ideas have weight, texture, and smell.

Our shelves are curated not by algorithms, but by hands that have turned thousands of pages. We favor the strange, the beautiful, and the enduring.

Our Principles

Curation Over Volume

We’d rather have 100 perfect books than 10,000 forgettable ones. Every title earns its shelf space.

Slow Commerce

No rush. No upsells. Stay as long as you like. The tea is always hot and the chair is always yours.

Community First

Profits support local literacy programs and emerging writers. When you buy here, you build this neighborhood.
