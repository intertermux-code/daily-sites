Kyoto Field Notes

LAT 35.0116° N • LONG 135.7681° E

Seasonal Calendar

January: The Quiet Season

AVG TEMP: 4°C • CROWD LEVEL: LOW • COST: BASELINE

New Year’s shrine visits (Hatsumode) offer profound cultural insight. Snow dusts golden pavilions. Onsen culture peaks. Best for solitude seekers and photographers avoiding masses.

April: Sakura Peak

AVG TEMP: 14°C • CROWD LEVEL: EXTREME • COST: +40%

Cherry blossoms transform the city into pink clouds. Philosopher’s Path becomes impassable. Book 6 months ahead. Beautiful but demanding. Consider neighboring Nara for slightly less density.

November: Autumn Foliage

AVG TEMP: 12°C • CROWD LEVEL: HIGH • COST: +25%

Maple leaves ignite temple gardens in crimson and gold. Evening illuminations at Eikando and Kiyomizu-dera are magical. Crisp air perfect for walking. Second peak season.

03.A

District Index

HIGASHIYAMA

Preserved historic streets. Yasaka Pagoda. Traditional shops. Highest tourist density. Visit at dawn or dusk.

ARASHIYAMA

Bamboo grove (overrated). Tenryu-ji garden (essential). Monkey park. Scenic train. Better on weekdays.

KAMIGYO

Imperial Palace. Nishijin textile district. Authentic neighborhood feel. Excellent kissaten coffee shops.

OHARA

Sanzen-in moss gardens. Rural escape. 1hr bus ride. Fewer crowds. Perfect day trip for contemplation.

GION

Geisha district. Hanamikoji street. Exclusive restaurants. Photography restrictions enforced. Respect boundaries.

KIBUNE

Riverside dining (kawadoko) in summer. Mountain shrine. Romantic atmosphere. Combine with Kurama hike.
