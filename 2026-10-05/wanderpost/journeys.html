The Registry

Four curated geographies. Each vetted for seasonal alignment, cultural access, and logistical flow.

JP-01

Kyoto Hinterlands

14 DAYS • NOV–FEB

The Proposition

Bypass the Gion crowds. This itinerary focuses on the northern Kibune valley and the remote temples of Ohara during their most atmospheric season. Private tea ceremonies with third-generation masters, after-hours zazen meditation, and stays in century-old machiya townhouses.

Logistics

  • Transport: Shinkansen + Private Driver
  • Pace: 3 Nights Base / 2 Days Transit
  • Guide: Local Historian (English Fluent)
  • Est. Investment: CHF 12,500 pp
Request Dossier
AR-04

Patagonian Estancia

12 DAYS • OCT–MAR

The Proposition

Ranch life at the edge of the world. Split time between a working sheep estancia and a luxury eco-lodge near Torres del Paine. Horseback traversals across steppe, glacier trekking with glaciologists, and asado feasts prepared by resident gauchos.

Logistics

  • Transport: Domestic Flight + 4x4 Transfer
  • Pace: Single Base Deep Dive
  • Guide: Naturalist & Gaucho Host
  • Est. Investment: CHF 9,800 pp
Request Dossier
MA-02

Atlas & Medina

10 DAYS • MAR–MAY

The Proposition

From the sensory overload of the souks to the silence of the High Atlas. A study in contrasts. Private artisan workshops in Fes, mule-trekking to Berber villages, and sunset camel rides in Agafay Desert. Sleep in restored kasbahs with zero light pollution.

Logistics

  • Transport: Vintage Land Rover Convoy
  • Pace: Linear North-to-South Traverse
  • Guide: Cultural Anthropologist
  • Est. Investment: CHF 7,200 pp
Request Dossier
NO-07

Lofoten Light

9 DAYS • JUN–AUG

The Proposition

Midnight sun photography and fishing village immersion. Stay in converted rorbuer (fishermen’s cabins) on stilts. Sea kayaking through fjords, midnight hikes to jagged summits, and dining on fresh-caught cod in intimate harbor restaurants.

Logistics

  • Transport: Electric Ferry + Rental EV
  • Pace: Island-Hopping Circuit
  • Guide: Landscape Photographer
  • Est. Investment: CHF 8,900 pp
