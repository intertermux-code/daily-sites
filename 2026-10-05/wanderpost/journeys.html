01

Kyoto: The Floating World

Japan • 12 Days • Cultural Immersion

+

Bypass the tourist traps of Gion at peak hour. This journey focuses on the quieter northern hills of Kibune and Kurama, artisan workshops in Nishijin, and private tea ceremonies with third-generation masters. Includes a 3-night stay in a restored Machiya where silence is part of the architecture.

  • Private Zen Garden Meditation
  • Kaiseki Dinner with Chef Tanaka
  • Indigo Dyeing Workshop
  • Nara Deer Park at Dawn

02

Patagonia: Southern Silence

Chile/Argentina • 18 Days • Wilderness

+

A traverse of the Carretera Austral that respects the scale of the landscape. Long stays at remote estancias, navigation of fjords by private boat, and guided trekking in Torres del Paine away from the circuit's congestion. Weather dictates the rhythm; we provide the flexibility.

  • Estancia Helsingfors Private Access
  • Glacier Grey Kayaking
  • Gaucho Asado Experience
  • Puma Tracking Expedition
From CHF 12,200 / person Request Dates
03

Marrakech: Atlas & Medina

Morocco • 10 Days • Sensory

+

Balance the sensory overload of the souks with the profound quiet of the High Atlas. Stay in a palace riad within the medina walls, then retreat to a berber lodge in the Ourika Valley. Focus on craft: leather tanning, pottery, and culinary history with local historians.

  • Souk Tour with Artisan Guide
  • Hammam Ritual at Heritage Spa
  • Atlas Valley Village Homestay
  • Cooking Class in Organic Garden
From CHF 5,900 / person Request Dates
04

Lofoten: Arctic Light

Norway • 14 Days • Maritime

+

Experience the archipelago beyond the postcard. Base yourself in converted fishermen’s cabins (rorbuer), join working fishing vessels for cod season, and hike ridges where the ocean meets the sky. In winter, chase aurora; in summer, bask in midnight sun. Always at sea level.

  • Rorbuer Cabin Exclusive Use
  • Traditional Cod Fishing Trip
  • Viking History Museum Visit
  • Arctic Surfing Introduction
From CHF 9,800 / person Request Dates
