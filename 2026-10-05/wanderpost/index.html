001

Manifesto

DURATION

We reject the weekend hop. Minimum 10-day immersions allow circadian rhythms to sync with local time.

TRANSPORT

Ground-first logistics. Trains, ferries, and private drivers replace internal flights wherever geographically viable.

ACCOMMODATION

Family-run ryokans, heritage estancias, and restored riads. No international chains. Direct economic injection.

PACING

One anchor activity per day. The remaining hours are unstructured canvas for serendipity and rest.
