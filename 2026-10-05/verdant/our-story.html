01 — Origin

Rooted in Science,
Refined by Nature

Verdant began with a simple contradiction: why must effective skincare feel clinical, and natural skincare feel ineffective? We refused to choose.

Founded in 2024 by a team of cosmetic chemists and botanists, Verdant bridges the gap between laboratory precision and ecological responsibility. Every formula starts with peer-reviewed research on active compounds, then sources those compounds from sustainable, regenerative agriculture.

The Verdant Standard

We maintain an exclusion list of over 1,400 ingredients—not because they’re trendy to avoid, but because our stability testing proves they compromise long-term skin barrier health. What remains is potent, stable, and genuinely necessary.

Ingredient Integrity

Our rosehip oil is cold-pressed within 4 hours of harvest to prevent oxidation. Our niacinamide is pharmaceutical-grade, verified at 99.8% purity. We publish batch-specific COAs (Certificates of Analysis) because transparency isn’t marketing—it’s accountability.
