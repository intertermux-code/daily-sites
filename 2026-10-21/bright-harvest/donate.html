    Other ways to give

    Money is the most useful. It is not the only thing.

    • Donate shelf-stable groceries

      Drop off at the Orchard Lane depot any weekday, 7am–6pm. We need shelf-stable milk, pasta sauce, cooking oil, and baby food — never expired, never glass that has been re-sealed at home.

    • Match it at work

      Thirty-one local employers double charitable gifts. Send your HR confirmation to match@brightharvest.org and we will file the paperwork for you.

    • Host a harvest drive

      Offices, congregations, and PTA groups collect an average of 340 pounds each October. We deliver the totes, the signage, and a volunteer to load the van.

    • Give a legacy or a vehicle

      Six neighbours have named Bright Harvest in their will, and one left us a 2009 refrigerated box truck that still runs the Hollis route twice a week. Talk to Renée first: (555) 014-8807.

    Before you give

    Questions we get at the front desk.

    Will I get a tax receipt?

    Yes, within two minutes, by email. Bright Harvest Community Food Bank is a registered 501(c)(3) under EIN 47-0912884, and gifts over $250 include the fair-market description the IRS asks for.

    How do I cancel or change a monthly gift?

    Reply to any receipt with the word “change” and we will adjust or stop it the same day. No retention call, no hoops — about two percent of monthly donors cancel each year and we would rather keep their trust than their twelve dollars.

    Does my money buy cheap processed food?

    We buy at retail only when a donation does not exist: onions and potatoes in February, infant formula year-round, and shelf-stable milk. Fifty-eight percent of what leaves the depot is produce, dairy, or bakery, and none of it is purchased.

    Can I direct a gift to one program?

    Yes. Write the program name in the memo field — “Gleaning,” “Mobile Market,” “Senior Boxes,” or “Baby Groceries.” Gifts of $1,000 or more to the Mobile Market are named on the bus itself, if you want them to be.

    Is this donation page taking my real card details?

    No. This is a demonstration of the giving flow, so nothing is charged and nothing is stored. On the live site the widget posts to a hosted payment processor and card data never reaches our own servers.

    Prefer to give your Saturday?

    A four-hour warehouse shift packs 620 meals.

    No experience, no lifting above fifty pounds unless you want to, and coffee at 7:15 from Orchard Lane Bakery. That is the least demanding thing you will do all month and the most useful.
