Security Architecture

Built Like a Vault, Because You’re Trusting Us With Everything

Mintwise is read-only by design — we can see your balances, but we can never move a dollar. Credentials are tokenized and encrypted before they hit our network, and every session is watched by automated anomaly detection.

  • AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit
  • Independent SOC 2 Type II audit, renewed every year
  • Biometric lock, session alerts, and a public bug-bounty program
