How Your Rate Stacks Up

The 50/30/20 Floor

A classic for a reason: roughly 50% of take-home to needs, 30% to wants, 20% to saving and debt payoff. Below 10% consistently is where emergencies start becoming credit-card problems.

The 20–35% Growth Zone

Members who cross 20% usually get there by fixing three things: one subscription bundle, one food-delivery habit, and one savings sweep they stopped thinking about. Mintwise finds all three for you.

35%+ — The Optimizer’s Range

At this level the game changes from budgeting to allocation: retirement catch-ups, taxable brokerage, house down payments. Keep the rate honest by including taxes on investment income.
