Terms

How Payment and Dates Work

A date is held for fourteen days from my reply. After that it takes a signed agreement and a deposit, and the ledger closes on that Saturday.

Deposit
30 % of the package rate, due on signature. This is what takes your date out of the ledger.
Balance
Payable in two instalments: 40 % at eight weeks before, 30 % on the wedding day.
Methods
Bank transfer (DK, SE, EU), or card through the hosted invoice with a 1.8 % fee.
Travel
Included within Denmark. Outside Denmark I quote flights and one night of accommodation per shooting day, at cost.
Rescheduling
Once, free of charge, if the new date falls within twelve months and I am free.
Cancellation
The deposit is non-refundable. If I cannot shoot your date through illness, I will find a photographer you approve and pay the difference myself.
Usage
You may print, share, and post the entire gallery. I ask to be credited online and to keep two frames for the studio archive; say no and I will.
Invoicing
Issued in DKK with VAT. EU businesses: reverse charge applies with a valid VAT number.
