What Happens After You Press File

Five steps, no call centre, and no contract until you have asked every question you came with.

I check the ledger, look at your venue in the light of that month, and answer with availability, a realistic price including travel, and two or three honest observations about the day as you have described it.

Not a highlights reel — the whole record of a day, in order, from a venue like yours and a wedding like the one you are planning. You will have forty minutes of looking to do, which is the point.

Thirty minutes, video or in person on a Tuesday. Bring the schedule, the seating plan argument, and the photographs you are afraid of not getting. I bring the negative boxes so you can hold a finished wedding in your hands.

Two pages, written in Danish and English, with the hours, the money, and the delivery dates on it. The thirty per cent deposit is what moves your date from “on hold” to “booked” in the ledger.

We walk the venue at the same hour as your ceremony so I know where the light is and you know where I will be. You send the family list that day, and the only thing left to do is get married.
