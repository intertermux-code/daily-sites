Before you ask

Five questions we get weekly

Five per cent of completion, all of it contingent on the sale going through. Preparation costs — photography, drainage surveys, permit close-out — we pay first and reclaim at completion. If a house does not sell, you owe us nothing beyond the third-party reports you approved.

Not on this register. Every Haven viewing is accompanied, partly because the houses are insured that way and partly because the best questions get answered on the spot. Lockboxes on our properties are the exception, and only after you are in contract.
