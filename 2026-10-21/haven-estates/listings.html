Reading the numbers

What each card actually tells you

01

Interior area

Rule One

Square feet are measured above grade, finished, and heated

Cellars, porches, garages and the barn are counted separately and named in the dossier. If an agent elsewhere quotes 11,600 sq ft for Harbor Light, ask them what they included.

Rule Two

A half bath is listed as a half bath

Our bathroom figures count full fixtures: tub or shower, basin, toilet. Powder rooms appear in the detail page as a separate line, because pretending otherwise changes the price per room by six figures.

Rule Three

Asking price is an opening position, not a valuation

Every card links to the three comparables we actually used. On Marlowe Bay, homes sell for 97 per cent of list on average; estates with docks have gone over for the last four years running.
