The Haven Standard

We do not tour houses.
We introduce people to them.

01

First, the light

Chapter One

Every viewing starts at the hour the house looks best

We schedule around the sun, not the calendar. A west-facing sitting room at 4 p.m. tells you more than a floor plan ever will. If a home fails at golden hour, it fails, and we say so before you fall for it in a photograph.

Chapter Two

A deed binder before the first handshake

Survey, easements, flood certificate, septic records, every permit since the foundation was poured — assembled by our own office and handed to you in a linen binder. Sellers who have hidden something from us find out quickly; buyers never inherit a surprise.

Chapter Three

The neighbours get told first

Roughly six in ten Haven sales never appear on a screen. We call the families on the lane, the boatyard, the person who has watched the cove for thirty years. A home finds its owner on Marlowe Bay long before the market hears about it.

Chapter Four

The relationship outlasts the closing

Our concierge book holds the roofer who fixes slate properly, the arborist who will not top a pine, the caretaker who opens the house in March. You keep it whether you bought through us or simply found us.
