One night, sideways

How an Evening Runs

Scroll the four stops the way you would walk them on the night itself.

Arrows, drag, or the left and right keys.

1 / 4

  • 6:15

    Chairs Before Doors

    席

    Twenty-four folding chairs, name cards in burgundy ink, the kettle on. Whoever arrives first sits in the middle, because the middle is where the reading lands.

  • 7:00

    Twenty Minutes, Hard Stop

    読

    The reader goes first without introduction — the book is the introduction. Then one question from the room, chosen by whoever asks it most quietly.

  • 7:35

    Tea and Arguing

    茶

    Hojicha, two biscuits, and the part people actually come for. The Long Table seats eight at a stretch; nobody is asked to leave until the chairs go away.

  • 9:40

    Folding Away

    閉

    Books restocked, name cards kept for next time, and one shelf moved slightly so that whatever was read tonight is at eye level tomorrow morning.

For readers who want the floor

Read to Twenty-Four People

We book the calendar four months ahead and answer every request, including the ones we cannot fit.

  • Twenty minutes. Bring one book, one author, or one page of your own; we will time you from the counter.
  • No cover charge for debuts. If it is your first public reading anywhere, the door money is yours.
  • Access matters. Step-free entrance, an induction loop at the Long Table, and large-print copies of anything you read on request.
