Hold a Chair

Tell us how you read and we will place you in the room that fits. If your first choice is full, tick the box and we will offer you the next opening instead of a number on a list.

Asked at the counter

Before You Sign

What if I have not finished the book?

Come anyway and listen. Fourteen people out of every hundred do exactly that, and the room does not keep score. If you miss two nights running, though, we pass your chair on — and you can take it back any month the seat is free.

Do I have to buy the book from you?

No. Borrow it from the library, read it on your phone, or bring the water-damaged copy you have had since college. Members pay our wholesale price — usually four or five dollars under the shop rate — on the current title if you do want a new one.

Is there an age limit?

The Young Stacks is for 14 to 18. Every other club is open to anyone sixteen and up; two of our Slow Room regulars are eighty-four and have never once let anybody speak during the third hour.

How noisy is the room?

Clubs run at conversational volume with the door shut; the shop is closed to browsing after 7:00 pm on club nights. The Slow Room is genuinely silent except for the bell at eleven.

Can I join two clubs?

Three people do, and they look tired. If you want variety, take a poetry night and a philosophy night rather than two long-novel rooms — the reading loads are designed not to overlap.

What happens after I send this form?

Imogen replies within two working days with your chair number, the book you are starting on, and the date. If the club is full and you ticked the box, she offers you the next opening instead. You will get one email per month after that, and you can ask to stop them at any time.
