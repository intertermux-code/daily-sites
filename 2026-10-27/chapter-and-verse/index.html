Come in slowly. Leave with a chapter.

A small shop of twelve thousand books, kept quiet on purpose. Put your phone in your coat, take a cup of hojicha from the counter, and read for as long as you like. Nobody here will rush you toward the door.

Six clubs, one shared rule

Nobody Reads Alone Here

Every club meets in the shop, holds twelve chairs, and asks one thing of you: turn up having read the pages. Silence counts as participation.

  • Twelve chairs, always. No waiting lists on the night — the twelfth sign-up closes the room.
  • Books at cost. Club members pay our wholesale price on the current title.
  • Skip a month, keep your seat. Two absences in a row pass the chair to the next name.
