§ 03

From sign-up to first shift

  1. Send the form

    Tell us your name, how to reach you, and which crew fits. Takes ninety seconds and no résumé.

  2. Twelve-minute orientation

    A short video at the desk, the hygiene rules, where the knives and the exits are. Every volunteer does it once, ever.

  3. You get a text

    The coordinator confirms your date and names the person meeting you at the dock. Cancel once a month, guilt-free.

  4. Work, eat, go home

    Hours land in your record automatically. School and court-ordered community service paperwork is signed on the spot.

Who works here

Retired teachers, two probation officers on community-service duty, a high-school biology class that keeps asking for Tuesdays, and 41 neighbours who simply like the 5:15am quiet.

§ 05

Before you ask

Do I have to be fit or strong?

No. The sorting floor has seated stations, the front desk is a chair and a phone, and the garden beds are raised to waist height. Tell us on the form and we’ll place you somewhere you can work comfortably for the whole shift.

Can my teenager volunteer?

Fourteen and up, with an adult on site for under-16s. Under-14s are welcome at the Saturday garden and the backpack table. Service-learning and CSU hours are signed the same day.

I need court-ordered or school community service.

We sign for both. Bring your paperwork to your first shift and see the desk coordinator; keep your own copy too. We do not accept unpaid “workfare” substitutions for paid staff roles.

Can a company or church group come?

Eight to forty people, once a quarter is plenty — we cap group shifts because the floor is small and the produce is perishable. Use the form’s group field and we’ll call you.

What if I need to cancel?

Text the number on your reminder. A shift goes in the morning paper at 6am, so a day’s notice genuinely helps — and you can rebook from the same message.

Do volunteers receive food?

Yes. After your shift, take a bag from the unsellable pile — bruised fruit, ends of bread, anything we can’t ship to a partner shelf. It is yours and there is no form.
