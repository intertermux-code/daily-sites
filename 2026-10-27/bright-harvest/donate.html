§ 03

Other ways to give

3.1

Give food, not money

Unopened, unexpired pantry goods land at the Riverside dock, Monday to Saturday, 7am–4pm. Best needs: shelf-stable milk, tortillas, rice, pasta sauce, tinned fish, baby food.

Call (951) 555-0148 for a pallet pickup.

3.2

Match it through work

More than 340 employers double a charitable gift. Tick the box on the desk above and the ledger team emails you a one-page form; United Credit Union alone has added $218,000 this way.

Double your $34 without spending a cent more.

3.3

Donor-advised fund or securities

Grants and appreciated stock clear our account without a taxable event. Use fund number 47-0918823 and tell us the so we can send the acknowledgement the same week.

Ask for a giving statement any time.

3.4

Adopt a pantry shelf

$65 a month keeps one partner shelf stocked with fresh produce and the signage in the language its neighbours read. You pick the site; we send a photograph every quarter.

Fourteen shelves left unadopted.

3.5

Run a harvest drive

Churches, teams and classrooms collect a season’s worth in two weeks. We drop off branded bins, send a floor volunteer, and mail you the weigh-in total framed for your board.

22 drives ran last year.

3.6

Leave the harvest behind

A bequest keeps a truck on the road in 2050. Tell us you’re writing us in and we’ll send the sample language our attorney uses — nothing else, ever.

Legacy circle meets each October.

§ 04

The 1,940 who show up monthly

Food doesn’t arrive on a schedule. A heatwave strands a lettuce crop in Holtville on a Sunday, and suddenly we need $4,000 of refrigeration by Tuesday. Monthly gifts are the reserve that lets us say yes to the Sunday.

Sustainers are the only donors we call by name — not to ask for more, but to tell them what the month looked like. Cancel in one email, no guilt trip, no retention script.

Questions people ask before giving

Is my gift tax-deductible?

Yes. Bright Harvest is a registered 501(c)(3); our EIN is 47-0918823 and the receipt that lands in your inbox carries the date, amount and the statement that no goods or services were exchanged.

What if I can only give $5?

Give it. At $0.42 a meal that is twelve meals, and small gifts are the most reliable money we hold — they double when the county gets hit by a bad harvest year.

Do you sell donated food?

Never. Our Mobile Market charges a $1 token per item because neighbours asked to pay something; the tokens fund produce we then give away. Everything else leaves the building free.

Where can I read your books?

Audited statements, Form 990 and the quarterly weigh-in report sit on a clipboard at the warehouse desk and go out with any letter request. Ask for Marisol.

Will you share my address with other charities?

No. We rent a mailing list once a year for the fall appeal and that is the only time a donor record leaves our system.
