03. Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

We offer free standard shipping on orders over $75. Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days, while express shipping is available for 1-2 business day delivery.

Are your products cruelty-free?

Yes, all our products are cruelty-free and vegan. We never test on animals and ensure our suppliers follow the same ethical practices.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy with your purchase, you can return it for a full refund. Products must be unopened and in original packaging.

Do you have physical stores?

Currently, we operate exclusively online. However, we're planning to open select boutique locations in major cities starting next year.
