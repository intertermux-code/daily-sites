Our Story

Rooted in nature, crafted with purpose.

01. Origins

Verdant was born from a simple belief: that skincare should be as pure as the ingredients that nourish it. Our journey began in the lush gardens of Japan, where ancient traditions of botanical wisdom meet modern science.

Founder Yuki Tanaka spent years studying traditional Japanese skincare practices, learning how generations of women have used carefully selected plant extracts to achieve healthy, glowing skin. Inspired by this heritage, she set out to create a line of products that honors both tradition and innovation.

02. Philosophy

Our philosophy centers on the Japanese concept of wabi-sabi – finding beauty in imperfection and simplicity. We believe in the power of fewer, better ingredients that work harmoniously with your skin's natural processes.

Each formula is meticulously crafted with ethically sourced botanicals, free from unnecessary additives. We prioritize transparency in our ingredient sourcing and maintain rigorous quality standards throughout our production process.

03. Process

Our products are formulated using cold-press extraction methods and low-temperature processing to preserve the integrity of each botanical component. Each ingredient is carefully tested for purity and efficacy before inclusion in our formulations.

We partner with local farmers who practice sustainable cultivation methods, ensuring that our ingredients are harvested at peak potency while supporting regenerative agriculture practices.

04. Key Ingredients

Camellia Oil

Rich in omega fatty acids, deeply nourishes without clogging pores.

Green Tea Extract

Potent antioxidant that helps protect against environmental stressors.

Rice Bran

Gently exfoliates while providing vitamins and amino acids.

Sake Lees

Natural source of kojic acid for brightening and evening skin tone.
