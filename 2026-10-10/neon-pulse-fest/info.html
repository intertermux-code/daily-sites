01. THE VENUE

The Nexus - Cyberpunk Playground

THE NEXUS

Formerly an industrial warehouse in the heart of the city's tech district, The Nexus has been transformed into a multi-level electronic music venue featuring:

  • Three distinct stages with 360° LED walls
  • VR experience pods
  • Holographic art installations
  • Rooftop terrace with city views
  • Luxury VIP lounges
  • Indoor and outdoor dance floors

Address

123 Digital Avenue
Tech District
Cyber City, CC 10001

Hours

Friday: 6:00 PM - 11:00 PM
Saturday: 4:00 PM - 1:00 AM
Sunday: 3:00 PM - 12:00 AM

02. TRANSPORTATION

Getting to and from the Festival

🚗 BY CAR

Ample parking available at The Nexus. $20/day or $45/weekend. Valet parking available for $35/day.

🚇 PUBLIC TRANSIT

The Nexus is accessible via Metro Lines 1 and 3. Get off at Cyber Station and walk 3 blocks north.

🚲 BIKE

Secure bike parking available free of charge. Bike valet service available for $10/day.

🚕 RIDE SHARE

Designated pickup/dropoff zone at the main entrance. Surge pricing may apply during peak hours.
