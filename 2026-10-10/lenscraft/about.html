About Lenscraft

Behind the lens telling your love story

Sarah Chen, Photographer

With over a decade of experience capturing life’s most meaningful moments, I founded Lenscraft with a simple philosophy: every photograph should tell a story that transcends time. My journey began in Tokyo, where I studied traditional Japanese aesthetics that deeply influence my approach to composition and light.

Each wedding is a unique narrative, and I specialize in documenting authentic emotions and fleeting glances that speak louder than words. My style blends documentary photography with fine art sensibilities, creating heirloom images that families treasure for generations.

I believe in the power of negative space and the beauty of imperfection—wabi-sabi principles that guide my compositions. These influences help me capture not just what happened, but how it felt.

“Photography is the art of freezing time while preserving the soul of the moment.”

My Approach

Unobtrusive Presence

I become part of your celebration without disrupting the natural flow of events. My goal is to capture genuine moments as they unfold.

Artistic Vision

Inspired by Japanese design principles, I focus on composition, light, and negative space to create images with lasting emotional impact.

Heirloom Quality

Every image is carefully curated and retouched to meet museum-quality standards, ensuring your memories remain vivid for generations.
