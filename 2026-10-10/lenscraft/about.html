REN TAKAHASHI

Founder & Principal Artist

// THE ARTIST & CRAFT

Capturing spirit, not just form.

Trained in architectural design at Kyoto University and editorial photojournalism in Paris, Ren approaches weddings as living architectural compositions of emotion and light.

// BACKGROUND

The Journey

Before founding Lenscraft in 2018, Ren spent seven years documenting historical timber structures in Nara. This instilled a deep reverence for organic decay, grain, and the fleeting nature of time.

“When I transition into a wedding environment, I bring that same patience. A wedding is not a stage show—it is a sacred gathering. My camera is an instrument of quiet observation, translating human connection into tactile heirlooms.”

// MEDIUM & PROCESS

Analog & Modern Craft

01

Medium Format Film

We shoot selected moments on Leica M rangefinders and Hasselblad 120 film for rich color depth and unmatched highlights.

02

Custom Color Grading

Every single frame is hand-calibrated using bespoke film emulation plates derived from classic Kodak and Fuji archival stock.

03

Handmade Washi Prints

Our flagship wedding albums are bound in Kyoto with mulberry papers and Japanese silk ribbon binding.
