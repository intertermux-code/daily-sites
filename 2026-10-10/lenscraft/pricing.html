Investment Questions

Do you charge travel fees for non-Japan weddings? +

For our Global Monograph collection, all travel and lodging are completely bundled into the flat fee. For other packages outside Japan, we calculate a transparent flat travel fee with zero hidden surprises.

How far in advance should we secure our date? +

Because we strictly limit our commissions to 18 couples per year, most clients reserve 8 to 14 months in advance, especially for autumn maple and spring cherry blossom seasons.

What is your turnaround time for the full gallery? +

A "Sneak Peek" collection of 30 curated images is delivered within 72 hours. Your complete handcrafted digital plate archive and film scans are delivered within 6 to 8 weeks.
