Our Taproom Experience

Step into our welcoming taproom where the aroma of fresh hops mingles with the warmth of copper and wood. Our space features original exposed brick walls, reclaimed timber tables, and a view directly into our brewing operations.

With 16 taps featuring our year-round favorites, seasonals, and limited releases, there's always something new to discover. Pair your beer with our rotating selection of locally-sourced snacks prepared fresh daily.

Getting Here

By Car

Free parking available in our lot behind the building. Street parking also available on Brewery Lane and surrounding streets.

Public Transit

Take MAX Blue Line to Brewery District Station (0.3 miles walk). Bus lines 15 and 44 stop at nearby intersections.

Rideshare/Bike

Bike racks available outside. Rideshare drop-off at main entrance. Please plan ahead for safe transportation after enjoying our beverages.
