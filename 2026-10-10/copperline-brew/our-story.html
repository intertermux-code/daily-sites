Our Story

The journey of passion, craftsmanship, and community

From Hobby to Heritage

In 2010, brothers Jake and Marcus Copperfield turned their shared passion for homebrewing into something greater. What started as weekend experiments in Jake's garage grew into Copperline Brewing Co., a testament to their dedication to the craft.

With backgrounds in engineering and chemistry respectively, the brothers approached brewing with scientific precision while maintaining respect for traditional techniques. Their first batch, a robust American Amber Ale, was served at a neighborhood gathering and sparked the realization that they had something special.

Building on Tradition

Starting with a modest 7-barrel system, we've grown to a 15-barrel brewhouse while maintaining our commitment to quality over quantity. Our flagship Copperline Amber became the cornerstone of our lineup, followed by innovative seasonals that showcase our creativity.

We source our ingredients locally whenever possible, partnering with Pacific Northwest farms for the freshest hops and malts. Each batch is tasted and approved by our founding brewers, ensuring consistency and quality that reflects our standards.

Community First

Beyond brewing exceptional beer, Copperline Brewing Co. aims to foster community. Our taproom has become a gathering place for locals, beer enthusiasts, and visitors alike. We host weekly trivia nights, live music sessions, and seasonal festivals that celebrate both our beer and our community.

As we look to the future, we remain committed to our core values: craftsmanship, authenticity, and community. Every beer we brew carries forward the legacy of those early experiments in Jake's garage, refined through years of experience and unwavering attention to detail.
