Our Programs

Bright Harvest operates several programs designed to address food insecurity at its roots. From emergency food assistance to long-term nutrition education, we offer comprehensive support to our community.

Support Our Programs

How We Serve Our Community

Emergency Food Assistance

Our cornerstone program provides immediate relief to families facing food insecurity. Through our main distribution center and mobile pantries, we offer bags of groceries to households in need, no questions asked.

Available Tuesday through Saturday, 9am-4pm.

Mobile Pantry Program

Bringing fresh food directly to underserved neighborhoods, our mobile pantry visits 12 locations weekly. This program addresses transportation barriers and ensures food access in areas with limited grocery options.

Visit our schedule for locations and times.

Community Gardens

We maintain four community gardens throughout the city, providing plots for families to grow their own food. These spaces also serve as outdoor classrooms for our nutrition education programs.

Garden plots available for $25/year.

Nutrition Education

Our culinary workshops teach families how to prepare healthy, affordable meals using ingredients from our pantry. Classes cover meal planning, food safety, and budget-friendly cooking techniques.

Classes offered in English and Spanish.

Senior Nutrition Program

Specialized support for adults 60 and older, including home delivery of nutritious meals and social engagement opportunities. Our senior program also includes health screenings and wellness education.

Free for qualifying seniors.

Backpack Program

During the school year, we provide weekend food packages for children whose families struggle with food insecurity. Each backpack contains easy-to-prepare items for Saturday and Sunday meals.

Serves 450 children weekly.

Eligibility & Access

Our programs are designed to be accessible to anyone experiencing food insecurity. We operate on a trust-based model - no documentation or proof of income is required to access our services.

How to Access Services

  1. Visit our main distribution center or attend a mobile pantry event
  2. Complete a brief intake form with household size information
  3. Receive a food package based on your household size
  4. Join optional nutrition education workshops

For special programs like the Senior Nutrition Program or Backpack Program, specific eligibility requirements apply. Contact us for more information.
