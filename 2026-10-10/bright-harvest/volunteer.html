Ways to Serve

Food Sorting & Packing

Help sort donated items, pack food boxes, and prepare mobile pantry distributions. Tasks are suitable for all ages and abilities.

Shifts available: Mon-Sat, 8am-4pm

Distribution Support

Assist families during food distribution events, help with registration, and provide customer service. Great for people who enjoy interacting with the community.

Shifts available: Tues-Sat, 9am-4pm

Garden Maintenance

Help maintain our community gardens, plant seeds, harvest produce, and assist with educational workshops. Perfect for gardening enthusiasts.

Shifts available: Wed-Sun, 8am-1pm

Education Assistant

Support our nutrition education classes by helping with preparation, assisting participants, and translating materials. Bilingual volunteers especially needed.

Flexible scheduling based on class calendar

Volunteer FAQs

What is the minimum age to volunteer?

Individuals aged 16 and older may volunteer independently. Those aged 14-15 may volunteer with a parent or guardian present. Children under 14 may participate in family volunteer activities.

Do I need to commit to a regular schedule?

We appreciate regular volunteers, but we also welcome occasional help. You can sign up for specific shifts that work with your schedule, or establish a recurring time that fits your routine.

Is training required?

All new volunteers complete a brief orientation covering our mission, safety protocols, and procedures. Additional training is provided for specialized roles.

Can groups volunteer together?

Absolutely! We welcome corporate groups, clubs, and families. Group activities are scheduled separately to ensure adequate space and supervision.
