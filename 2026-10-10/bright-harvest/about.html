Our Story

Bright Harvest was founded in 2010 with a simple mission: to eliminate hunger in our community. What started as a small group of volunteers distributing surplus food has grown into a comprehensive network serving thousands of families.

Support Our Mission

From Humble Beginnings to Community Impact

2010: The Beginning

Founded by local community members concerned about food insecurity, Bright Harvest began operating out of a church basement with just a few volunteers and limited resources.

2013: First Facility

Opened our first dedicated facility allowing for proper storage and distribution of food items to families in need.

2016: Program Expansion

Launched our mobile pantry program, bringing food directly to underserved neighborhoods throughout the region.

2019: Community Kitchen

Introduced cooking classes and nutrition education programs to help families prepare healthy meals on a budget.

2022: Current Impact

Serving over 15,000 families annually and distributing more than 250,000 meals each year.

Our Core Values

Compassion

We approach every family with empathy and understanding, recognizing their dignity and worth.

Integrity

We operate with transparency and accountability, ensuring resources reach those who need them most.

Community

We believe in the power of collective action and partnership to address systemic challenges.

Empowerment

We provide resources and education that enable families to achieve long-term food security.
