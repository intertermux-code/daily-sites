Other Ways to Support Us

Food Donations

Donate non-perishable items directly to our food bank. We especially need protein sources, whole grains, and shelf-stable produce. Drop off items at our main facility during business hours.

View our current needs list for specific items.

Volunteer Time

Contribute your time and skills to support our operations. Volunteers help with food sorting, distribution, administrative tasks, and special events. Even a few hours monthly makes a significant impact.

Learn more about volunteer opportunities.

Corporate Partnerships

Engage your business in our mission through workplace giving campaigns, employee volunteer programs, or cause marketing initiatives. We offer recognition for corporate sponsors.

Contact us to discuss partnership opportunities.

Impact of Your Gift

  • $25 provides 50 meals for families in need
  • $50 supplies a family of four with groceries for one week
  • $100 funds nutrition education classes for 20 participants
  • $250 supports our mobile pantry operation for one day

Giving Tuesday and Special Campaigns

Join us for Giving Tuesday, our largest annual fundraising campaign. On the Tuesday following Thanksgiving, millions of people worldwide donate to causes they care about.

This year, we're aiming to raise $100,000 to expand our mobile pantry program and serve 2,000 additional families. Your donation, whether large or small, contributes to this important goal.

Consider making your gift a monthly recurring donation to provide ongoing support for our programs throughout the year.
