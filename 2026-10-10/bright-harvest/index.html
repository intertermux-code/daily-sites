Our Mission

Bright Harvest believes that no one in our community should go hungry. We work with local farms, grocery stores, and volunteers to rescue surplus food and distribute it to those who need it most.

Through our network of partner organizations, we provide emergency food assistance, nutrition education, and support for long-term food security solutions.
