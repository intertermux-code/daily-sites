About Chapter & Verse

Established in 1984, Chapter & Verse has been a sanctuary for bibliophiles in the heart of the city. We believe that a bookstore is more than just a retail space—it’s a community hub where ideas are exchanged and imaginations are sparked.

Our shelves are stocked not just with bestsellers, but with stories that challenge, comfort, and inspire. Whether you’re looking for a rare first edition or the latest Booker Prize winner, our knowledgeable staff is here to guide you to your next great read.
