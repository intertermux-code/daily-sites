The Foundry of Flavor

Copperline began in a rusted-out machine shop on the edge of the industrial district. We didn't want a shiny new brewhouse; we wanted character. We kept the exposed brick, the steel beams, and the grit.

Our philosophy is simple: respect the grain, honor the water, and never rush the process. Every batch is a tribute to the blue-collar roots of our city, brewed by hand for those who appreciate authenticity over perfection.

Whether you're here for a pint after work or a tour on Saturday, you're part of the crew.
