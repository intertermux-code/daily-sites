TRAINING SPECIFICATIONS

Every program at FORGE is engineered around exercise mechanics, precise progressive overload, and energy systems development. Select a discipline below to examine the methodology.

// SPEC-01

POWERLIFTING & BARBELL STRENGTH

FOCUS: Absolute Force Production INTENSITY: 75% - 95% 1RM LOAD TYPE: Calibrated Steel & Competition Bars

Engineered for lifters looking to maximize their Squat, Bench Press, and Deadlift. Designed around block periodization (Hypertrophy, Strength, Realization).

METHODOLOGY & STRUCTURE

  • Primary Lifts: RPE-guided main work with video velocity measurement options.
  • Accessory Work: Targeted weakness corrections (pin presses, block pulls, tempo squats).
  • Equipment Utilized: Rogue Combo Racks, ER Equipment, Eleiko IPF Bars, Texas Power Bars.

// SPEC-02

METABOLIC CONDITIONING (HIIT)

FOCUS: Aerobic & Anaerobic Capacity WORK-REST RATIO: 1:1 to 3:1 Variable TOOLING: Ergometers, Sleds, Sandbags

High-intensity functional conditioning created to build a relentless cardiovascular engine without compromising muscle mass.

METHODOLOGY & STRUCTURE

  • Zone 4/5 Workouts: Interval efforts designed to push VO2 Max boundaries.
  • Sled & Turf Protocols: Heavy pushing, dragging, and farmer carries for conditioning.
  • Heart-Rate Monitored: Live display on main turf screens to ensure correct zone targeting.
Find HIIT Sessions
// SPEC-03

MOBILITY & ATHLETIC DURABILITY

FOCUS: Joint Health & Active Range REGIMEN: FRC (Functional Range Conditioning) RECOVERY: Fascial Release & End-Range Isometrics

Strength requires mobility. This session expands usable ranges of motion, improves capsular space, and prepares joint structures for heavy load exposure.

METHODOLOGY & STRUCTURE

  • CARs (Controlled Articular Rotations): Daily joint maintenance work.
  • PAILs/RAILs: Isometric contractions at end-range to lock in new mobility.
  • Spinal & Hip Decompression: Inversion and tissue hydration techniques.
