Good to Know Before You Order

  • Whole-grain loaves can be reserved at the till or by phone until 10 a.m. the day before.
  • All allergen sheets live behind the counter — ask and we will bring the folder, not a guess.
  • Sourdough crusts and yesterday’s buns go to the neighborhood soup kitchen in Graça at closing.
  • Cards, MB WAY, and exact coins all welcome; the tip jar is a jam jar.
