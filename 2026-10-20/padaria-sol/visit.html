The Address

Rua da Bica do Duarte Belo 42, 1200-401 Lisboa, Portugal. Look for the blue awning and the chalkboard that reads “Forno aceso desde as cinco.”

Getting Here

  • Tram 28EGreiário stop, 90 m downhill — the bell is our alarm clock.
  • Funicular da BicaTop stop, 3 minutes down the steps on your left.
  • MetroBaixa-Chiado (green line), 11-minute uphill walk. Bring patience.
  • CarDo not. Park at P1 Chile and enjoy the climb.
