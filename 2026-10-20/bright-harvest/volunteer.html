Five Jobs Nobody Gets Paid For Here

Every role has a lead who shows you the first two shifts. Nothing requires lifting over 40 pounds — say so on the form and we will keep you on a table job.

3 hrs · indoor

Dock & Sort

Everything arrives at Dock 2 and leaves as someone’s dinner. You grade, weigh, label and shelve. Loud music, cold air, fast hands — the easiest place to start.

Lifts up to 40 lbs · closed-toe shoes

5 hrs · on road

Van Route Crew

Ride along to two or three stops: unload the shelves, stock the aisles, help people carry bags to their cars. You need a valid driver’s license only if you want the wheel.

License preferred · ages 21+

3 hrs · counter

Choice Pantry Greeter

Meet people at the door, walk them in, answer the question about the formula, and make sure nobody is handed a bag of food they did not choose. Spanish and ASL greeters especially welcome.

Second role · after two dock shifts

4 hrs · kitchen

Bright Kitchen Line

Wash, chop, portion, chill, label. Our two cooks run the heat; you run the trays. Food handler cards cost nothing and we schedule the class on site.

Hairnet + apron provided

3 hrs · field

Garden & Gleaning

Keep the 48 plots, or spend a Friday morning at Cane Hill filling flats with apples nobody will sell. Boots, gloves and coffee on us; sun hat on you.

Outdoors · April through November

Not Sure Which?

Come to the open orientation on the first Wednesday of the month at 6 p.m. in the Mill Race mezzanine. Twenty minutes, one coffee, and you can leave having decided to try the dock once. No commitment, no clipboard.

Can not make a date? Call (555) 014-2200 and ask for the volunteer desk.

    What Your First Shift Looks Like

    1. Arrive At 8:20

      Ring the bell at Dock 2, not the glass front door. Sign the board sheet, get a neon vest and a locker.

    2. Twenty Minutes Of Training

      Your lead shows you the grading cards, the allergen bin and the sink. Then you start; nobody quizzes you.

    3. Work, Break, Work

      A fifteen-minute break at ten with coffee from Grain & Gable and whatever bread did not sell last night.

    4. Count What You Moved

      At the end of the shift the scale gets read out loud. That number goes on the board on our homepage.
