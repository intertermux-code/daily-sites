Six Other Ways To Fill The Warehouse

Workplace Giving

Nineteen local employers already match employee donations dollar-for-dollar. North River Credit Union, Grain & Gable and Pine Top Logistics all match — check your payroll portal before you give twice.

Match code: BRHT-1198

Host a Food Drive

We drop off the crates, send you the shelf-stable wish list, and collect. Last year 41 church groups, ball teams and office floors moved 63 tons of shelf-stable food.

Minimum six weeks’ notice

Donor-Advised Funds & Stock

Our DAF and brokerage team can settle a gift of appreciated stock in about four days, and you avoid the capital gains tax while the warehouse buys a month of produce up front.

Ask for the gift memo at hello@brightharvest.org
