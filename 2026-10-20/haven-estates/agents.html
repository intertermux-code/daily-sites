How We Work

Small on purpose

A principal handles no more than four live transactions at once. That cap is written into our partnership agreement, and we have turned down listings to keep it. It is the only structural reason our advice stays honest.

Buyers receive the same dossier discipline we give our sellers: comparables you can audit, maintenance histories we chased down, and a frank answer when you ask "should we walk away?"
