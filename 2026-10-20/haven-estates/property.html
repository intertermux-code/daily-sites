The House

Architecture that defers to the canyon

Elaine Voss set the house low and long — a single-storey reading, though there is a garden level — so the ridge line stays yours. Boards of white oak, lime-plastered walls, and 64 linear feet of sliding glass that disappear into the structure.

The current owners commissioned the wine room, the 2,100-bottle cellar beneath it, and the rooftop terrace with its unobstructed descent to the pool. Every element was specified once, correctly, and has been maintained on Voss's own schedule since.
