Connect the whole picture in under four minutes

Station 02

Classify every dollar without a spreadsheet

Mintwise labels 98.6% of transactions correctly on the first pass and learns from the corrections you make. Subscriptions, transfers, reimbursements, and split charges are handled as their own objects, so your numbers stop lying to you.

One tap on any line opens the full lineage: merchant, rule that fired, and the three previous matches, so you can see exactly why a charge landed where it did.

Station 03

Automate the transfer you keep meaning to make

Set a rule once — “every payday, move 12% to Emergency, then round the rest into Japan” — and Mintwise queues it for you to approve at your bank. Bill Radar watches the next 30 days and warns you two days early when an envelope will not cover what is coming.

Station 04

Launch goals with a date, not a wish

Every goal shows the monthly thrust required to land on time, and the console recalculates the moment your spending shifts. Miss two months and Mintwise tells you plainly whether to extend the date or trim a category — with the three cuts that would close the gap.

Instrument, not sermon

Guilt Does Not Fit in a Console

Mintwise never lectures. It reports. A red line on coffee is not a character flaw — it is a reading, and readings are the only thing you can steer with.

What changes in month three

  • You stop opening four banking apps and start reading one panel.
  • You know the exact number that lands you in a safe month versus a tight one.
  • You approve purchases before they happen instead of discovering them on the statement.
  • Your partner and you finally argue from the same data.

What we deliberately left out

No stock tips, no crypto price alerts, no “investor ideas” inbox, no credit-score bait. Mintwise is a measuring instrument; the decisions stay yours.
