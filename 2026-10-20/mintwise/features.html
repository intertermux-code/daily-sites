Auto-Categorization

Labels every transaction on arrival and shows the rule that fired.

Station 01 / 06

Mintwise systems in detail

A model trained on 1.4 billion labelled transactions sorts new charges into the 84-category household taxonomy. It gets 98.6% right before you touch anything, and the remaining 1.4% surface as one review queue you can clear in about ninety seconds a week.

  • Lineage view. Tap any transaction to see the merchant record, the rule that fired, and its three closest historical matches.
  • Split lines. One hardware-store run can be “Home Repair” and “Tools” in the same charge, without a duplicate row.
  • Transfer detection. Movements between your own accounts never count as spending, even when the bank descriptions are deliberately vague.
  • Rule inheritance. Correct a category once and every matching merchant follows, including future charges at that chain.
  • First-pass accuracy98.6%
  • Median weekly review time1 m 30 s
  • Merchant rules per household17

What it refuses to do: Mintwise never guesses a category to hit a target, and never auto-merges two charges you have labelled differently.

Bill Radar builds the next thirty days from what your recurring charges actually did over the last year — including the ones that drift. It then checks each day’s total against the balance your forecast says you will have, and warns you two days before an envelope goes short.

  • Drift tracking. A streaming bundle that moved from $9.99 to $15.49 is flagged the day the increase posts, with the prior 12 charges beside it.
  • Renewal calendar. Annual and semi-annual items appear on the month they hit, so the $240 software renewal is not a surprise in a thin month.
  • Cancellation packet. One tap produces the merchant’s cancellation path, your last four charges, and the exact effective date.
  • Shared household view. Both partners see the same runway, with each person’s autopays attributed.
  • Median recurring items found22
  • Forgotten subscriptions cancelled per household4.1 / year
  • Warning lead time2 days

What it refuses to do: we never cancel on your behalf or negotiate bills for a cut of the savings.

A goal is a date and a number, nothing else. Mintwise converts it into the monthly thrust required, then reports whether your current savings rate clears it. When two months slip, the console shows the trade rather than a nag.

  • Thrust math. $8,400 by October needs $700 a month. The panel shows that number before you commit, not after.
  • Priority order. Emergency fund first, then debt with the highest rate, then dated goals — or your own order, which the app respects exactly.
  • Impact preview. Move one autopay and every goal recalculates instantly, so you see the consequence of a decision before making it.
  • Milestone stamps. 25%, 50%, 75% each log a dated entry you can export with the account statement.
  • Goals per household (median)4
  • Goals completed on schedule71%
  • Median emergency fund after 12 months$5,940

What it refuses to do: no goal is ever funded by moving money you do not have, and no credit product is ever suggested to close a gap.

Most tools divide a year by twelve and call it a budget. Mintwise keeps the calendar: payday lands on the 15th and 30th, rent leaves on the 1st, and the utility bill drifts. The forecast walks sixty days forward through those actual dates and shows the lowest balance you are likely to touch.

  • Trough marker. The single lowest projected balance, with its date, is the number that decides whether a purchase is safe.
  • Irregular income. Freelancers get a range built from their own deposit history rather than a fake average.
  • Scenario strip. Test “skip the car payment” or “delay the trip” in a scratch forecast that never touches your real plan.
  • Confidence band. Every projection carries a wide and narrow estimate, and the model tells you when it has too little history to be useful.
  • Forecast horizon60 days
  • Median absolute error at day 143.9%
  • Minimum history required2 months

What it refuses to do: the forecast never smooths a bad week to make a plan look achievable.

Your bank credentials are held by our aggregation partner in an encrypted vault, never in Mintwise storage. Access is read-only, every session is pinned to a device, and every export is logged in a ledger you can read.

  • Two-factor that resists phishing. Passkeys and hardware security keys are first-class; SMS is available but never required.
  • Session pinning. A new device requires approval from a signed-in one, and the approval request names the device and city.
  • Field-level encryption. Balances and merchant data are encrypted per household with keys that rotate every 90 days.
  • Audit ledger. Every read of your data by our own support staff is recorded with a reason code and shown to you.
  • One-button revocation. Disconnect an institution instantly, or delete the account and export everything first.
  • SOC 2 Type IICurrent
  • Bug bounty paid since 2023$318,000
  • Data sold to third partiesNone

Found a problem? Write to security@mintwise.app — we answer within one business day. Full disclosure policy.

Reports are built from the same numbers the console shows, so a PDF never contradicts a screen. Generate a month, a quarter, or a year and send it as a file you actually own.

  • Monthly packet. Savings rate, category burn, goal progress, and the three largest changes, on two pages.
  • Tax helper. Deductible-looking categories isolated with the underlying transactions listed, ready for your preparer to accept or reject.
  • Proof of funds. A dated, shareable statement of balances for a landlord, lender, or lease office.
  • Open formats. CSV, JSON, and PDF exports with no watermarks and no re-import penalty.
  • Report templates9
  • Generation timeUnder 6 s
  • Scheduled deliveriesMonthly, quarterly

What it refuses to do: no insight is ever a product recommendation, and no report contains a sponsored placement.

Command bar

Fly the Panel Without the Mouse

The Mintwise web console is built for people who keep a spreadsheet open anyway. Every command is reachable from the keyboard, and the command bar accepts plain language — “show health spending since March” returns a chart, not a settings page.

Press ⌘ K on this site right now to try the same pattern: it jumps between the five pages of this build.

  • Open command bar⌘ K
  • Jump to consoleG C
  • New transfer ruleN T
  • Review unlabelledR
  • Toggle forecast bandF
  • Export current view⌘ E
  • Lock console⌘ L
  • Close any overlayEsc

Shortcuts are listed in the in-app help panel and can be remapped per device. Screen-reader users get the same actions from the navigation landmark list.
