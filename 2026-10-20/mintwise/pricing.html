Which Plan Fits Your Situation

Take Grounded. Two accounts is your whole picture, and the free plan already gives you the number that matters. Upgrade the day you open a brokerage account or a second card and want them in the same readout.

Navigator pays for itself in one month. The 60-day forecast exists precisely for sawtooth income: it shows the trough balance before you commit to a lease, a trip, or a repair. Bill Radar catches the annual renewals that land in slow quarters.

Flight Deck. Two signed-in adults on one plan, with autopays attributed to a person so nobody carries the mental load alone. Unlimited goals means the house deposit, the school fund, and the holiday all sit on the same panel with their own dates.

Start free and stay free. Grounded tracks every card you link and the monthly packet shows payoff progress. When you want the forecast to prove a payment plan is survivable before you commit to it, Navigator is a $9 experiment you can end in one tap.

Billing, Downgrades, and Refunds

Nothing is charged, because we never ask for a card up front. On day 31 the console switches to Grounded and your paid features go read-only with a banner explaining exactly what is locked. Your data stays where it was.

No. History, categories, rules, and exports are kept on every plan. Downgrading limits how many accounts stay connected and which systems run — you choose which two remain linked, and the others go dormant rather than deleted.

Reply to any receipt with the word “refund” within 60 days and we process it, no questions and no retention call. Median time from request to money moving is four minutes, and annual plans are refunded pro rata for unused months.

Never, in either direction. Subscription revenue is the entire business model, which is why there is no sponsored card offer anywhere in the product. Aggregated, anonymised benchmarks like the ones on the calculator page are the only thing that ever leaves your account, and you can opt out without losing any feature.

Navigator is free for full-time students and educators while your enrolment holds. Registered non-profits and public-housing financial counsellors get Flight Deck at no charge through our partner programme — write to the support desk with your EIN or institution name.
