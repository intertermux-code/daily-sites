Where the desk sits

A Small Company, Deliberately

Mintwise is eleven people in Denver who answer their own support queue and publish the schedule they intend to keep. That is the entire pitch: fewer features, honest numbers, and a person you can email.

What that size means for you

  • Feature requests are read by the engineers who would build them, usually within a day.
  • We publish a real launch schedule and write a reason in the changelog when something slips.
  • There is no growth budget for advertising, which is why the subscription is the whole business model.
  • Some things take longer than they would at a giant. Sync outages get a status note, not a press release.
