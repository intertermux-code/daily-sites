The Shop

A bookshop that behaves like a living room.

We took over a former ribbon warehouse in 2009 with four shelves, one dodgy radiator, and a rule we still keep: nothing gets stocked because a distributor pushed a pallet of it. Every title on the floor was read, argued about, or defended out loud by someone on the counter.

The result is a shop that is smaller than it feels. Fiction and poetry share a wall. The bindery smells of wheat paste. Upstairs, a long oak table sits unreserved for exactly one reason — so that strangers can finish a sentence together.

We are closed on Tuesdays, which is when the reading happens. If you find us open on a Tuesday, something unusual is afoot — check the events calendar.
