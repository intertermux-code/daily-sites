Plan the weekend

Venue & Info

Nine acres of working waterfront, four stages, and a set of rules that keeps 6,000 people happy until sunrise. Here’s all of it, in plain language.

The site

Meridian Yards, Pier 42

Oakland, California — 94607. The yard is a decommissioned shipping terminal: three hangars, a loading-dock tunnel, and a south-facing grass bank above the bay. Everything is on one level, and the longest walk between stages is four minutes.

Getting there

Leave the Car at Home

There are 400 parking spaces and roughly 2,000 people who want them. The shuttle and BART combination takes 22 minutes from Downtown and drops you at Gate A.

BART + Free Shuttle

Ride to Lake Bay Terminal, follow the magenta lane markings to the shuttle bay. Shuttles run every six minutes from 7:00 p.m. and every four minutes after midnight, all the way back until 9:30 a.m. Add the $12 round trip to your pass at checkout.

Ferry to the Jack London Square Pier

Last ferry from San Francisco leaves at 9:15 p.m.; the return leaves the yard at 4:40 a.m. on Saturday. Twenty-minute walk from the pier to Gate A along the waterfront, lit the whole way.

Driving and Parking

Pre-book only — Lot K on the north side, $28 per night, in and out once. Drop-off and pick-up is Gate C on Middle Harbor Road; waiting vehicles are ticketed by the port authority, so don’t idle.

Bike and Scooter

Free attended bike corral inside Gate A, open from doors to close, staffed by the same crew all weekend. Docked scooters must be left at the public racks 60 metres from the fence — the yard itself is scooter-free.

Access for everyone

Accessibility at the Yard

The whole site is step-free: compacted asphalt, matting over the grass bank, and ramps at every stage deck. If something on this page doesn’t cover your situation, email us and a human will answer within two working days.

  • Companion passes are free. Book your accessible seat, then request a companion wristband — no proof of need required.
  • Viewing platforms at Hangar 9 and Solaris, with lift access and a barrier-free front row.
  • Quiet room in the Port Authority building by Gate B: low light, soft seating, no music, open all night, staffed by two responders.
  • Assistive listening headsets loan free at every stage gate; induction loops at all four bar and info points.
  • Accessible toilets and showers at Gates A, B and the camp field, with hoists and carers’ space.
  • Service animals welcome everywhere, including the mosh pit. Water and a rest corner at the info desk.
  • Medical warnings — tell us at booking about conditions we should plan for and we’ll flag your gate check-in.

Before you pack

Site Rules, Bag Policy, Payment

Bag Policy

Bags smaller than 30 × 20 × 15 cm — roughly a daypack — get in. Nothing else: no hard cases, no professional camera gear, no drones, no outside food or drink apart from one sealed 500 ml water bottle and any medication you need. Searchable bag storage is $8 at Gate A if you misjudge it.

Cashless Yard

No physical cash on site. Load your pass app before you travel, or use one of the two top-up terminals by Gate B. Card and phone payments work at every bar and food window; the terminals take chips, not just taps, and they work when the mobile network doesn’t.

Behaviour

Zero tolerance for harassment, photography without consent, or pushing someone over a barrier. Say something to anyone in a magenta vest and they’ll come get you out of it. There’s a first-aid post at each stage and a welfare tent between The Void and Bassment.

Camping and the Late Field

Camp spots are Friday and Saturday only, inside the fence on the north apron. Quiet hours are 5:00–9:00 a.m. — generators and speakers are confiscated, tents are not. Showers, lockers, drinking fountains and a hot-breakfast truck at 7:00 a.m. are included with every pitch.

Ask the obvious

Frequently Asked Questions

Under 18s are welcome until 10:00 p.m. at every stage with a parent or guardian, and until 10:00 p.m. on Sunday for the whole Afterglow day. After 10:00 p.m. the yard is 21+ and photo ID is checked at the gate — no exceptions, including for the camp field.

December on the bay sits between 7 and 13 °C, with wind off the water that makes the grass bank feel colder than the number says. Hangar 9 and The Void are heated and stay comfortable. Bring layers you don’t mind spilling on, and closed shoes — the concrete has drainage grates.

Full refund until 20 November, half until 6 December, and after that we transfer your pass to the waitlist on your behalf and credit you the face value less the $6 processing fee. Payment plans stop billing the moment you cancel.

The festival runs rain or shine. Three of the four stages are indoors; Solaris has a covered deck and a marquee over the front third of the bank. Only lightning within five miles or a port-authority wind closure stops a set, and if that happens we extend the indoor stages and post updates in the app.

Free festival Wi-Fi across the yard — register once at the login page and it holds all weekend. Cell signal is unreliable once the crowd is above 4,000, so agree a meeting point: the yellow crane base outside Gate B is the official one, and it’s lit all night.

Two charging bars — Gate A and the Solaris deck — with lockable slots at $6 for 90 minutes, and free standing charge posts beside the info desk. Camp pitches include one power tap per spot.

Eleven Bay Area kitchens, four of them vegan and two halal, plus a late-night noodle bar in Bassment that opens at 1:00 a.m. Expect $9–$16 for a plate. Free still-water fountains sit next to every bar, and there’s a proper sit-down canteen on the north apron for camp-holders.

Wristbands are tamper-evident and non-replaceable once cut, so put it on before you drink the first beer. If it fails or breaks through no fault of your own, the info desk re-issues it against photo ID and the pass purchase record. Lost through carelessness means buying a new pass at the gate price.
