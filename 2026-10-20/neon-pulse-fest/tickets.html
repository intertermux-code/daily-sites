How this works

No Bots, No Rescalping, No Surprise Fees

  1. Passes are name-locked. Transfer is free in the app until 15 December, then frozen for the weekend.
  2. One household may buy up to eight passes per phase. We check, and we do cancel the ones that don’t add up.
  3. Split any pass over three payments at checkout — first today, last on 1 December. No interest, no credit check.
  4. Refunds are full until 20 November, 50 percent until 6 December, and after that we hand your pass to the waitlist for you.
  5. Resale above face value is voided automatically. If you bought from a scalper, email us and we’ll sort it.
