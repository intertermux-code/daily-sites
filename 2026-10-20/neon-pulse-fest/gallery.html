The Archive

Three marquee rows from last winter: the mainstage at full tilt, the void at 3 a.m., and the sunrise nobody wanted to leave. Hover any row to pause it.

Row one

Mainstage, Full House

Hangar 9 holds six thousand and looks like one on a Saturday. These are the frames the crew shot from the truss tower between 11 p.m. and 1 a.m.

Row two

The Booth

Every act on this row played a room with no daylight in it. Shot on the deck, one metre from the monitors, with permission and a very short lens.

Row three

Last People Standing

Four in the morning, the fog coming in off the bay, and about two hundred people who decided sunrise was worth the walk back to the shuttle.

Selected frames

Six Pictures That Explain the Weekend

If you only look at one page of the archive, make it this one. Captions credit the shooter — all six are available as prints at the merch window this year.

Crowd vote

Shot of the Year Goes to “Two Forty”

Dev Anand took it on a borrowed camera at 2:41 a.m. on Saturday, three minutes after the conf cannons fired on the mainstage. It won with 38 percent of 6,200 votes.
