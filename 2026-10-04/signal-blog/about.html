About Signal

Independent tech journalism committed to depth, accuracy, and integrity.

Our Mission

Founded in 2026, Signal emerged from a conviction that technology journalism needed a new approach. In an era of clickbait headlines and superficial coverage, we committed ourselves to deep, analytical reporting that explores the implications of technological change.

We believe that understanding technology's impact on society requires more than breaking news—we need context, analysis, and the time to explore complex subjects thoroughly. Our articles average 1,500 words, featuring original research, expert interviews, and comprehensive analysis.

Signal operates as an independent publication, funded primarily through subscriptions and supported by grants from foundations committed to quality journalism. This independence allows us to pursue stories that matter rather than those that generate clicks.

Our History

Signal began as a newsletter in early 2026, founded by a group of veteran technology journalists concerned about the decline in depth and accuracy in tech coverage. What started as a weekly digest of carefully researched insights quickly grew into a comprehensive publication.

By mid-2026, Signal had published over 50 in-depth articles, developed a loyal readership of technology professionals, researchers, and executives, and secured funding to expand operations. Today, our team of eight full-time journalists produces original content six days a week.

Our commitment to fact-checking and accuracy has earned recognition from industry peers. In 2026, Signal received the Excellence in Technology Journalism award from the Digital Press Foundation.
