Our Story

At Ember & Oak, we believe in the transformative power of fire. Our kitchen revolves around a traditional wood-burning hearth, where centuries-old cooking methods meet contemporary culinary artistry. Chef Marcus Williams brings his heritage of Italian and Mediterranean influences to create dishes that celebrate both technique and terroir.
