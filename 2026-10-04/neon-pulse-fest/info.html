Everything you need to know for an amazing experience at Neon Pulse

VENUE DETAILS

Cyber Arena

123 Digital Boulevard

New York, NY 10001

Date: December 18-20, 2026

Time: Gates open at 4:00 PM daily

Parking: Available on-site for $25/day or purchase our Parking Pass in advance

WHAT TO BRING

  • Ticket confirmation (printed or on mobile)
  • Valid ID for age verification
  • Small bag/backpack (maximum size: 12" x 12" x 6")
  • Portable phone charger
  • Comfortable shoes for dancing

Prohibited Items: Large bags, outside food/drinks, glass containers, professional cameras with detachable lenses
