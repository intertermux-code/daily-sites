OUR STORY

From humble beginnings to craft excellence

The Beginning

In 2009, two friends with a passion for craft beer decided to turn their hobby into something more. What started as a small garage operation quickly grew into something special.

The First Brew

Our first official batch was a simple pale ale, brewed with locally sourced hops and premium malts. The response was overwhelming, and we knew we had something worth pursuing.

Growing Up

By 2012, demand had outgrown our original space. We moved to a larger facility, installed proper brewing equipment, and hired our first employees. The Copperline legacy was taking shape.

Today & Tomorrow

Now, over a decade later, we continue to innovate while honoring our roots. Our commitment to quality, community, and craft remains unwavering as we look toward the future.
