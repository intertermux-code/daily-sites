PWR LIFT

Our Powerlifting program is designed for those who want to maximize their strength potential. Using proven periodization methods, we focus on the three competition lifts: squat, bench press, and deadlift. Our program emphasizes progressive overload and technique refinement to help you achieve new personal records consistently.

PROGRAM SPECIFICATIONS

FOCUS SQUAT, BENCH, DEADLIFT
INTENSITY 85-95% 1RM
FREQUENCY 4 SESSIONS/WEEK
CYCLE LENGTH 12 WEEKS

What You'll Achieve

Participants in our Powerlifting program typically see a 25-40% increase in total lifting capacity over a 12-week cycle. We track your progress with detailed metrics including lift ratios, recovery indicators, and technique assessments.

HIIT PROTOCOL

High-Intensity Interval Training is the most efficient way to improve cardiovascular fitness and burn fat simultaneously. Our HIIT Protocol combines short bursts of maximum effort with strategic rest periods to push your metabolic capacity to new limits. Each session is carefully structured to maximize EPOC (Excess Post-Exercise Oxygen Consumption) for continued calorie burn post-workout.

PROGRAM SPECIFICATIONS

DURATION 30 MINUTES
WORK/REST RATIO 2:1 TO 3:1
CALORIE BURN 500-700 CAL
FREQUENCY 3-4 SESSIONS/WEEK

Performance Metrics

Track your improvements with our advanced monitoring system. We measure VO2 max, heart rate variability, and lactate threshold to optimize your training zones. Participants typically see a 15-25% improvement in cardiovascular efficiency within 8 weeks.

MOBILITY

Mobility is the foundation of all movement quality. Our Mobility program combines dynamic stretching, fascial release, and joint-specific exercises to enhance your range of motion and prevent injury. This program is essential for athletes in any discipline and becomes increasingly important as we age.

PROGRAM SPECIFICATIONS

SESSION LENGTH 45 MINUTES
FOCUS AREAS HIPS, SHOULDER, ANKLE
IMPROVEMENT +30-50° RANGE
FREQUENCY DAILY RECOMMENDED

Benefits of Enhanced Mobility

Better mobility translates directly to improved performance in all other training modalities. Increased range of motion allows for better exercise technique, reduces injury risk, and contributes to longevity in training. Our clients report significant improvements in both athletic performance and daily functional movement.
