Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

I recommend booking 8-12 months in advance for peak season weddings (May through September). Popular dates fill quickly, so early booking ensures availability for your special day.

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Absolutely! I love traveling for destination weddings. Travel fees apply for locations outside the Portland metro area, and international weddings are welcome. Let's discuss details during your consultation.

How many photos will we receive?

For an 8-hour wedding, you can expect 600-800 carefully edited images. I focus on quality over quantity, delivering only the best moments from your celebration. All images are high-resolution and ready for printing.

When will we receive our photos?

Your complete gallery will be delivered within 4-6 weeks after your wedding day. I prioritize quality editing to ensure each image meets my artistic standards. Rush delivery is available as an add-on service.
