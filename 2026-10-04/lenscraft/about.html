ABOUT LENSRAFT

Behind the lens with our founder

MY JOURNEY

My name is Elena Rodriguez, and I am the founder and lead photographer at Lenscraft. With over a decade of experience capturing life's most precious moments, I specialize in wedding photography that tells the unique story of each couple.

I began my journey in photography during my university years, initially focusing on landscape and street photography. However, it was during my first wedding assignment that I discovered my true passion – capturing the raw emotions and intimate connections that unfold on one of life's most important days.

My approach combines artistic vision with documentary storytelling. I believe that the best wedding photographs are those that capture genuine emotions and fleeting moments that might otherwise go unnoticed. My style is characterized by a blend of candid shots and carefully composed portraits that reflect both the grandeur and intimacy of your special day.

When I'm not behind the camera, you'll find me exploring new destinations, reading classic literature, or spending quality time with my family. These experiences enrich my perspective and inform my artistic vision, allowing me to bring a deeper understanding of human connection to my work.

I am committed to providing an exceptional experience from our initial consultation through the delivery of your final gallery. My goal is to ensure that your wedding photographs become treasured heirlooms that you and future generations will cherish forever.

PHILOSOPHY

Authenticity

I believe in capturing real emotions and genuine moments as they unfold naturally. My approach allows couples to enjoy their day while I document the authentic connections and spontaneous expressions that make their love story unique.

Artistry

Each photograph is carefully composed with attention to light, composition, and timing. I strive to create images that are not just documentation, but works of art that will stand the test of time.

Connection

Building a rapport with my clients is essential to capturing their true personalities. I take time to understand your story and vision, ensuring that the photographs reflect who you are as a couple.
