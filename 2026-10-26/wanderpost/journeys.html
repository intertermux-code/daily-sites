Four journeys, each tied to one weather.

We are not a catalogue. These are the only routes we plan all year, because they are the only four places we know well enough to leave holes in. Open a journey for the day-by-day, the stays, and the honest note about crowds.

Four routes, four seasons.

Japan · Kyoto and Kōyasan

Deep Autumn

Eleven nights chasing the maple line north to south: three days in the city with a tea teacher, two in Ōhara under the cedars, then the mountain temples for the morning bells. We arrive the week before the colour peaks and structure the busy gardens around a 07.00 opening, so your afternoons stay your own.

11 nightsMid-Oct – mid-NovGentle pacefrom £6,400 pp

Chile · Torres del Paine

The Long Wind

Fourteen nights in one valley instead of a lap of the park. Two estancias, a horse day up to the frozen lake, and the towers watched from three different weathers rather than one photograph. The schedule is written around the wind forecast, which is the only honest itinerary in Patagonia.

14 nightsNov – MarStill pace, one basefrom £7,250 pp

Morocco · Marrakech and the High Atlas

Slow Medina

Nine nights split between a courtyard riad inside the walls and a foothold at 1,600 m in the Aït Bouguemez valley. Souks at eight in the morning, gardens at four when the coaches have gone, and two days where the only plan is a roof, a book and mint tea brought up on a tray.

9 nightsMar – AprGentle pacefrom £4,180 pp

Norway · Lofoten Islands

Blue Hour

Ten nights in a rebuilt rorbu on the water at Reine, with the light as the destination: a nine-hour day that never quite becomes noon, a wood-fired kitchen, cod landed twenty metres from the door, and two aurora evenings driven out by whoever is awake.

10 nightsJan – FebStill pace, one basefrom £5,900 pp

What is inside the price

Nine things, every journey, no upsells.

  1. iThe guide who drew the route

    The person who planned your journey leads it. Nobody hands you to a stranger at the airport.

  2. iiEvery stay, two nights minimum

    Small family-run places we have slept in ourselves, chosen for a room with a view of something living.

  3. iiiAll ground transport in the region

    Trains, ferries, private drivers, the airport transfers, and the bus that only runs on Tuesdays.

  • Breakfast daily, plus eight of the included dinners on a typical journey.
  • Entry fees and the reservations that need them — including the temple that will not take a booking from you.
  • Two hands-on sessions: in Kyoto, tea and paper; in Marrakech, bread and argument.
  • A printed field guide for your route, with the blank hours marked in pencil.
  • £2 million ABTA-bonded financial protection and Allianz comprehensive cover as standard.
  • Two rounds of edits on your draft itinerary, and a planning call that has no time limit.
