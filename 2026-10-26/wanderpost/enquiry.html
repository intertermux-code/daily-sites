Before you write

Four questions people ask at this stage.

Do I need to know where I want to go?

No, and half of our best journeys started with “somewhere quiet in October”. Tell us the season and the pace and we will bring you back two or three places, one of which we will argue against.

Can I change the route you send?

That is the point of it. Two rounds of edits are included and most people use one: drop a night, add a bath, swap a workshop for a long lunch. If a change breaks the season, we say so rather than quietly agreeing.

What if my dates are fixed and awkward?

Say so in the message box. Fixed dates are a real constraint and we plan to them — usually by moving you a month later or a valley over rather than into the middle of the crowd.

Are you members of anything?

ABTA (Y2026/1842) and AITO, with £2 million financial protection on every booking. Our flights are ATOL-bonded through the airlines and airport transfers we book, and we will show you the certificate before you pay anything.
