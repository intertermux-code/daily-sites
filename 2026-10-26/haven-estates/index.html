How Haven works

Four chapters, one file, your name at the top.

We take a client the way a bookshop takes a special order: slowly, in writing, and with someone responsible for the whole thing. This is the sequence, unchanged since 2004.

1:1Advisor to client
I

The Conversation

Ninety minutes at the Sausalito office, or on your porch if that suits better. No forms, no listing presentations. We ask about mornings — where coffee gets made, who drives whom to the gate — because those details decide a house long before a square-foot target does.

  • Budget stress-tested against real tax and insurance figures
  • A written brief you keep, not a CRM note we keep
II

The Private Register

Six of every ten homes we place never reach a portal. Advisors hold them in a register circulated to sixty households at a time. If a property matches your brief, you see it with a hand-annotated sheet — the good, the fixable, and the honest cost of each.

III

The Walk-Through

You visit with your advisor and a contractor you choose — never one we broker. We open the crawl space, read the permit history out loud, and write down what the seller's disclosure does not say. Two hours, one page of notes, no follow-up calls from anyone else in the firm.

  • Flood, fire and easement maps printed before you arrive
  • Neighbour interviews at your request, on foot, not by phone
IV

The Offer & After

One advisor drafts it, one advisor signs off on it, and you meet both. After closing we stay on for the first year: contractors, chimney sweeps, the harbourmaster who answers at six in the morning. That relationship is the reason our clients call us about their second house.

Preparation, priced properly

Most of our listings spend four to eight weeks in the yard before a photograph is taken. We budget $18,000 to $60,000 of owner money and we can show you, sale by sale, what it returned. Last year the median lift over our own first estimate was 7.4%.

  • Permit and boundary reconciliation before market
  • One staging set, one photographer, one drone window
  • No lockbox handed to outside agents — ever

Offer night, in the open

We set a single review date and give every registered buyer the same information at the same minute. Sellers get a comparison sheet on the kitchen counter with all offers side by side, net figures included. Nineteen of twenty-one sellers last year chose to look at their own bidding war rather than have it explained to them.

Twelve months after closing

Your advisor stays reachable for a year at no charge — the contractor who fixes the ridge vent, the assessor who misfiled the parcel split, the neighbour who asks whether you know anyone looking. It is the least measurable part of what we do and the reason most of our business arrives from it.
