The house, plainly described.

Nine acres of private point once belonged to this parcel. The house takes a tenth of it and leaves the rest as windward cypress and a lawn that runs to the water's edge.

Alder & Wren rebuilt the 1974 original in 2019 without enlarging the footprint, which is why the Tiburon association and the coastal commission both signed off without a hearing. The structure is shingle, brass and Douglas fir, detailed to weather rather than to photograph: the bronze will patina, the shingle will silver, and the dock piles were driven to bedrock at thirty-eight feet.

Inside, the plan is unusually compact for its acreage. A single spine corridor runs from the entry courtyard to the boathouse, with every principal room opening on both sides so the house cross-ventilates on the afternoon breeze. You will not hear mechanical cooling unless you stand under a diffuser.

Rooms worth knowing

  • Entry courtyard — an enclosed gravel court with a 90-year-old olive, separating the owner's wing from the main house so neither hears the other.
  • Long room — 46 feet of fir-lined living space with two wood-burning hearths and doors onto the windward terrace.
  • Kitchen & scullery — a 14-foot marble island with a working scullery behind it, fully fitted, so a dinner for twenty leaves no trace in the room beyond.
  • Guest house — one bedroom, one bath and a kitchenette above the boathouse, with its own stair from the dock path.
  • Boathouse — insulated, plumbed and currently holding a 26-foot launch on a hydraulic lift; storage for two tenders above.
  • Studio — a north-lit 420 sqft room over the garage, wired separately, used by the owners as a painting studio for six years.

History of the point

One hundred and six years, four owners.

The file on this parcel is unusually complete. We keep it as a bound chronology and you may read it in the office, or take the copy that comes with the particulars.

1920First deed
  1. 1920 The Whitmore cottage

    A single-storey summer cottage of 1,400 square feet, built by a San Francisco shipping agent who wanted to be able to see his boat from breakfast.

  2. 1974 Rebuild at the current footprint

    The cottage came down and the shingle house that stands today went up in the same envelope, with the dock piles re-driven to bedrock.

  3. 2011 Coastal resiliency works

    Permitted revetment and a 900-foot bluff stabilisation. Engineering report and as-built drawings are in the file; the insurance premium is notably lower for it.

  4. 2019 Alder & Wren renovation

    Gut to frame, seismic retrofit, new glazing, and the owner's wing moved behind the courtyard. Full permits, certificate of occupancy 14 October 2019.

  5. 2026 Into the Haven register

    Accepted 3 August 2026 following a fourteen-month sale elsewhere that did not close. Never previously offered on this coast.

The neighbourhood

Cypress Point, and the eleven minutes around it.

The point is a nine-house street with a private gate and a shared dredging covenant. Tiburon's village is a six-minute drive or a twenty-two-minute walk along the shoreline path, and the ferry lands you at the Ferry Building in twenty-eight.

Things we would tell you anyway

  • The 6:15 ferry horn carries to the upper terrace; noise study dated March 2025 is in the file.
  • Two of nine neighbours hold easements across the driveway apron — recorded, narrow, and never exercised.
  • The parcel sits outside the mapped tsunami inundation zone but inside the coastal flood fringe; elevation certificate on file.
  • Winter water is cold and the dock is shaded after 3pm — matters if you keep anything that wants sun.
