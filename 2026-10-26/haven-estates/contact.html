Jumbo reality on this coast

Nine of the eleven homes on the register sit above the conforming loan limit, which means two deposits, a reserve requirement, and a rate that moves independently of the headline index. Ask an advisor for this week's jumbo spread before you set a ceiling.

What sellers actually check

On a competitive file, proof of funds matters more than pre-approval. Two of the last three offers we drafted for waterfront buyers were all-cash with a 21-day inspection, and one of those lost to a financed bid that closed faster.

Insurance is the moving number

Wildfire and coastal policies have repriced twice since 2023. We keep a running table of what our last fifteen clients actually paid, by parcel type, and share it on request — it changes the affordability maths more than a quarter point on rate.

Book the visit

Valuation walk-throughs run Tuesday to Saturday, ninety minutes on site. Bring the permit folder if it exists, the roof age if you know it, and any letter from a neighbour about a boundary. We measure, photograph and take notes by hand.

  • No obligation, and no listing agreement on the spot
  • You keep the report whether or not you instruct us

What the report contains

Three closed comparables with adjustments, one active competitor, the measured area against the tax record, a condition and cost schedule for the preparation window, and a single recommended guide price with the reasoning for it. Where we would be wrong to list, the report says that instead.

You will get a named lead date, a preparation budget, and one advisor responsible for every one of the two hundred decisions between that meeting and closing. Our average seller signs to completion in 27 days from first photograph.
