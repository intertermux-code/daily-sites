Reading the register

What these cards do not tell you.

A square footage is a number someone drew a line around. What matters is which rooms hold light at 4pm, and whether the easement behind the fence belongs to anyone.

24hAdvisor reply

Square footage, measured twice

Every home on this page has been re-measured by our own firm, not by the assessor's card. Where our figure and the tax record disagree, the card shows ours and the particulars list both. Two of the eleven currently differ by more than 300 square feet — we say so out loud.

The price is the opening line

We do not round down to straddle a search filter. Where a home has been reduced, the badge says so and the file records the original ask, the number of showings in between, and the seller's stated reason. You get all three before you form a view.

Private means private

Five residences are held off-market at the owner's instruction. They appear in the register we circulate by hand, at the moment we decide they should. Ask, and we will tell you honestly whether your brief matches anything we are holding this week.

  • No portal syndication on private holdings
  • No lockbox access for outside agents
  • Proof of funds reviewed before a walkthrough
