03What the session looks like

No pitch deck, no discovery theatre.

Session stages

The session opens on your question, not on our product. Two people from your side is ideal: whoever owns the number and whoever disputes it. We take notes on which definitions are in tension and which teams are downstream of them.

Using sample data shaped like yours, we write the metric as a versioned definition, attach the exclusion that finance disagrees with, and put both answers on one board with their as-of times visible. If a filter is the real problem, you will see it in the diff rather than in a description of one.

  • No warehouse credentials required in this window
  • You watch, we type; you can take the keyboard at any point
  • The board we build is emailed to you the same day

We name the plan, the migration effort in engineering days, and what would need to be true for this to fail. If Pulseboard is the wrong tool for your situation, we will tell you and suggest something cheaper or closer to your stack. Trials start when you say so, never automatically.

Names, company, and your question are stored in our own workspace and deleted ninety days after a trial ends unless you ask to keep it. We do not sell enquiries, we do not run ad retargeting on this list, and one person on the team is accountable for the export if you ask for deletion.

  • Request deletion at any time: privacy@pulseboard.example
  • Sub-processor list available before signature
  • EU or US storage choice on Team and Scale
