03Behind the number

Why we do not price per event.

Volumetric pricing transfers our costs onto your busiest weeks. It also quietly taxes good product decisions: launch a campaign, ship a fix, grow, and the analytics bill rises on top of the growth. We decided against it in 2022 and have not revisited it, because the two companies that asked us to move to event pricing were the two whose invoices we could never have explained.

So the plan you see is priced on the things you actually choose: how many people build, how much history stays warm, how many systems connect, and which controls your security team requires. If your event volume doubles next quarter, nothing on this page changes. If it grows beyond twenty billion events a day, we start a conversation about capacity, and put the commitment in writing rather than in an invoice.

Warehouse spend is the one cost that is genuinely yours, which is why we report it per team and per board every week and let you cap it. The average workspace sees its warehouse bill fall in the first month, because the same question stops being re-answered from raw tables.
