Why cash, when everyone has a pantry?

Because the things neighbors need most are the things nobody donates. Milk does not arrive in a shoebox. Neither does formula.

  • $1.85 buys, chills and delivers one fresh meal.
  • $50 stocks a school-lot pantry for a full weekend.
  • $250 runs one refrigerated rescue route for a week.
  • $1,200 monthly circle keeps the Food Pharmacy stocked for a year.

The giving ladder

What each rung actually buys

These are 2025 averages from our purchase orders and route logs, not marketing math.

  2. $15
    Rung one

    Eight fresh meals and a bus tank

    Eight dollars of produce, five dollars of dairy and protein, two dollars of refrigerated transport. Enough for a family of four to skip the canned aisle for a week.

  3. $50
    Rung two

    A weekend on one pantry shelf

    Fills the three gaps our rescue vans almost never see: low-fat milk, eggs, and stage 1 formula. Twenty-seven meals, and the reason a Thursday pickup happens at all.

  4. $100
    Rung three

    One Mobile Market stop, start to finish

    Fuel, cold-chain, two staff hours and the printed recipe cards. Fifty-four meals chosen by the neighbor who wants them, not by a distributor’s pallet.

  5. $250
    Rung four

    A rescue van on the road for a week

    Driver pay, diesel and temperature logs for one of twelve daily routes. That single van keeps about 1,900 lbs of good food out of a landfill each month.

  6. $1,200
    Rung five · monthly circle

    A year of Food Pharmacy for one neighbor

    Prescribed produce, a dietitian check-in every six weeks, and the lab work that shows it is working. Sustained giving lets us promise clinics a supply instead of a maybe.

Beyond the button

Five other ways to fill the shelves

Start a food drive

Office, team chat, birthday page. We send the bins, a printable list of what we actually need, and a pickup before the drive ends.

Email drives@brightharvest.org

Match it at work

Three of our largest grocery partners double employee gifts, and most employers will match a monthly one without any paperwork from you.

Search “Bright Harvest” in your portal

Give appreciated stock

Skip the capital-gains hit and let the full value buy food. Our broker takes public stock, mutual funds and crypto with a two-day turnaround.

gifts@brightharvest.org

Join the Thursday Circle

Monthly donors of $30 or more. One dinner at the cannery each spring, a standing tour of the dock, and first word on which gaps we are chasing.

Switch to monthly above to join free

Leave the pantry a legacy

A bequest, IRA beneficiary name or donor-advised grant keeps vans running in 2050. Tell us once and we handle the rest with your advisor.

legacy@brightharvest.org

Buy a shelf, not a can

$2,500 outfits one partner-site pantry with refrigerated shelving and a temperature logger. Twelve sites, four funded, eight to go.

Straight answers

Questions donors ask us

  • Any time, in one email to circle@brightharvest.org — no phone call, no retention script. Monthly gifts are the single most useful thing you can do, because they let us buy milk in advance instead of apologizing after.

  • We do, at the cannery dock, Tuesday through Saturday, 7 am to 3 pm. Unopened, in-date, no glass, no homemade anything. Grocery surplus we take for free — which is why $1.85 of purchased food goes further than a store-bought jar.
