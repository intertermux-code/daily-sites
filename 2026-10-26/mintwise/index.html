01 The premise

Six accounts, one truth

Most people do their finances in five different apps and one anxious feeling. Mintwise merges checking, credit, savings, investments, and bills into a single ledger you can read in ten seconds.

Connect Plaid-secured accounts in under two minutes. Transactions auto-categorise, subscriptions get flagged, and the forecast tells you next month’s ending balance today — not the morning it overdrafts.
