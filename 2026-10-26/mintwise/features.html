The feature edition — departments 01 through 04

Everything Mintwise does, plainly stated.

No mystery boxes, no “AI-powered magic.” Four departments, each doing one job well — wired together so the number you see is the number you can spend.

§ 01 — Clarity

Every account on one page

Connect checking, credit cards, savings, brokerage, and loans through read-only, Plaid-secured links. Mintwise merges them into a single running ledger, refreshed hourly, so “how much do I actually have?” takes one glance instead of five apps.

  • 3,800+ supported institutions, including most regional credit unions
  • Auto-categorisation that learns your habits in about two weeks
  • Shared household view with per-person privacy controls
  • CSV export whenever you want the raw ledger

§ 02 — Foresight

A 90-day forecast you can argue with

The forecast blends your recurring bills, true spending rhythm, and known one-offs into a daily balance projection. When a slow month looms, you’ll see it in August — not on the morning an overdraft fee lands in September.

  • “What if?” slider for one-time purchases like flights or repairs
  • Bill-date collision warnings when two drafts share a day
  • Variable-income mode for freelancers and tip-based work
  • Weekly email digest — three lines, no charts for charity
The forecast is the feature. Everything else is plumbing that finally stopped leaking.

§ 03 — Discipline

Saving that runs while you sleep

Envelopes, round-ups, and paycheck splits move money toward goals before it reaches your checking temptations. Pause any rule with one tap — Mintwise keeps score, not grudges.

  • Goal envelopes: emergency fund, holidays, a deposit, “someday”
  • Round-up spare change into whichever goal needs a win
  • Auto-transfer scheduled for the day after payday, not before
  • Found-money prompts: “Invest this $62 refund?” — one tap, yes or no

§ 04 — Assurance

Security you never have to think about

Your credentials are encrypted, tokenised, and stored in hardware-backed enclaves. We hold no money and take no deposits — Mintwise reads balances and writes budgets, nothing else.

  • 256-bit AES encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit
  • SOC 2 Type II audited annually; report available on request
  • Biometric unlock and mandatory two-factor authentication
  • We never sell, rent, or advertise against your data — full stop

05 Bonus department

The subscription radar

Recurring charges are found automatically, named clearly, and priced against how often you actually use them.

The radar flags three things: trials about to convert, renewals that hiked their price since last year, and services you haven’t touched in sixty days. Cancelling is a one-tap email draft you approve — never an auto-cancelled surprise.
