How we assign a story to a desk

A File Belongs to the Question It Answers

A story about a data centre's power bill appears under Energy and Infrastructure, never only under one. We index by the question a reader would type, not by the industry that would prefer the framing. Every topic page carries its editor's name and the method note for that beat.

Tips Shape the Index

Nine of the fourteen desks on this page exist because readers kept asking the same question. If a beat here feels thin, that is an invitation: write to desk@signal.press with the document, the outage, or the docket number. We reply to everything, and we tell you when we cannot run a story.
