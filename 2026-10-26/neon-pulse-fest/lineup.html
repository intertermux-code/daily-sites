How the bill was built

“Two thirds of a good lineup is knowing which 03:00 room you want to be standing in.” — Rae Marlowe, Pulse resident

Booking ran for fourteen months across three rooms in Leeds and one in Rotterdam. We started with the closing slot and worked backwards, which is why the Sunday sunrise set is the earliest confirmed name on the page and the last Friday opener was added in September.

Eleven of the sixty-two acts are from within sixty miles of the site, and nine are fronting a label or a collective rather than an agency roster. There is no age ceiling and no genre quota on the bill; the only rule we applied was that every act had to want the length of slot they were given.

Set times are final and published in the on-site app the Thursday before gates. Last-minute changes — illness, visa, weather on the Rooftop — go out by SMS to ticket holders within fifteen minutes of confirmation.

Open call 2027

Send us the mix you actually want to play

The Rooftop slot for the 2027 edition is decided by open submission — 340 entries last cycle, four of them from people who had never played above 200 capacity. We listen to all of them, and we reply with a real sentence, not a form letter.
