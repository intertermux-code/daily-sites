How entry works

One scan, one name, one wristband

Passes are personal and printed with the name on the booking. Wristbands post out between 25 November and 5 December; if yours has not arrived by the 8th, email the box office and we re-issue before Friday.

Lost or broken on site? Go to the box office desk inside Gate B with your booking reference and photo ID. Re-issues cost nothing the first time, and the old band is killed in the system at the counter so nobody else can use it.

Transfer a pass to a friend before 30 November from the same desk — name change is free, unlimited, and does not need a refund.

Payment plans

Split any tier into three, interest free

We would rather you paid us slowly than paid a scalper quickly. Instalments run on the card you first use; nothing is charged again until the 1st of the following two months.

  • Deposit33% at booking, taken immediately
  • Second1 Nov 2026 — £99.34 on a camping tier
  • Final1 Dec 2026 — £99.32, then the wristband posts out
  • MethodsVisa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay, bank transfer

No card surcharge

The 9% booking fee is the same on every method, including bank transfer, because that is what the payment processor and the ticketing platform charge us. There is no line item for “delivery” — wristbands go out Royal Mail tracked at our cost.

How an instalment order runs

  1. Build the order

    Choose tiers above, then select “Pay in three” at checkout instead of paying in full. No credit check, no third-party finance company.

  2. Deposit takes the allocation

    The 33% deposit reserves your passes against the live meter. Availability stops moving for your order the second it is taken.

  3. Two automatic collections

    1 November and 1 December. You get an email seven days before each one, and you can clear the balance early from the same link.

  4. Wristbands post out

    Between 25 November and 5 December, tracked, one envelope per order. Lost in the post? We re-issue at the box office with your booking reference.

Refunds & resale

Change your mind by 30 November and it is easy

Passes are transferable and refundable up to 23:59 on 30 November 2026, minus the booking fee. After that date the only route off a pass is our internal exchange, which is where resale prices stay at face value.

The exchange opens on 1 December and closes at 18:00 on Friday 18 December. You list a pass at exactly what you paid, we take a £4 handling fee, and the buyer is matched from the waiting queue in the order they joined. Nothing on the exchange is ever priced above face value — that is the whole point.

Tickets bought from Viagogo, StubHub or any other third party are void at the gate; we know the resale market on this site well enough to spot the patterns, and we have never been able to make an exception that did not punish the person who queued legitimately.
