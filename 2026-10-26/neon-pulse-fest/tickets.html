Buying straight?

We do not use bots, queues or lottery codes. Every tier sells in the open and the last 10% of each allocation is capped at two passes per payment card, so a group of friends can all get in.

Ticket Questions, Answered

Yes, free and unlimited until 10 December. Open your pass in the account portal, type their email, and the wristband QR re-issues to them. After December 10 transfers still work but need a name-match at the gate, which means both of you should arrive with photo ID.

If we cancel outright, every pass is refunded in full automatically within ten business days — you do not need to ask. If a single night is lost to weather, we credit 40% of a pro-rated night pass or 15% of a 3-Day Circuit toward next year, at your choice. The Basement and Pulse Warehouse are covered spaces, so roof-only closures never stop the whole site.
