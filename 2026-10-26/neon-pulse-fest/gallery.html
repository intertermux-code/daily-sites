Print sale

A2 pigment prints, £24

Twelve frames from the 2025 edition are available as signed A2 prints from the Gate B shop all weekend and online from 21 December. Two hundred copies of each, and half the margin goes to the welfare crew’s chosen charity, Leeds Nightline.

Accreditation 2026

Apply by 20 November

We accredit twenty photographers and four moving-image crews per edition. Send three links to work you have shot in the dark, plus the kit you plan to bring, to press@neonpulse.fm. Flash is banned on all five stages, and pit access is at the stage manager’s call.

Shot something from last year?

Crowd photographs land in one shared drive every morning at 10:00. Send yours with the day and the stage and we will add them to the archive with your name on the caption — 1,411 of the frames below were taken by people in the queue, not by us.
