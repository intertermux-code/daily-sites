04 Photographic PLATES

One thousand two hundred and four frames came out of the last two editions. Sixty-one were printed for the archive; the strip below is running twelve of them, in order, past you, forever.

Hover or focus a plate to stop the strip · shot on the harbor by our accredited photographers · no staged frames

04.1 Plate Strip A — The Apron

Pulse Main, Friday, 01:40. Cranes lit, nine thousand people, and the bass arriving through the steel grating before the light reaches you.

04.2 Plate Strip B — Rooms

The Vault stair, the Bay 7 ceiling, the pier rail. Three rooms that never look like each other on a poster and never sound like each other either.

04.3 Still Index — Sunrise And Out

The last ninety minutes are the reason people book the camper field. These six are from Sunday morning, 2025, on the dock.

print sale

Plates 014, 122 and 204 are printed at 50×70 cm in an edition of sixty, on the same paper stock as the site map. Ten per cent of every sale funds the San Pedro youth music programme that supplies Harbor Choir. Orders post from the harbor itself, in January, once we have slept.

04.4 The Attendee Archive

Alongside the accredited plates we keep everything you send. Two thousand nine hundred frames, unedited, tagged by night and room.

The archive is not a competition. Nobody selects favourites, nothing is retouched, and the caption is whatever you wrote. It exists because the official photographs are made by people with passes and lenses, and the best frame of 2025 was taken on a phone at 04:12 by someone who was already leaving.

Send frames to archive@neonpulse.fest with the night, the room and the approximate time. We publish within a fortnight, credit the handle you give us or nothing at all if you would rather stay unnamed, and we never license an attendee image to a sponsor. That last rule has been on the site since 2019.
