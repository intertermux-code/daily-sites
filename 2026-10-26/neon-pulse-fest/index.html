02 Four Rooms, Four Temperatures

Each stage is tuned by a different engineer. You will hear the difference before you see it.

  1. 02.1

    Pulse Main

    Container apron · 9,000 · 180° arc · 96 kW line array

    The old ship-to-shore crane now carries the lighting rig. Sub floor is laid on steel grating, so the low end arrives through your feet first. Headliners close here at 01:00–03:00.

    $ sound: function/loop, 40 Hz ±3 dB

  2. 02.2

    Neon Vault

    Cold-storage basement · 3,400 · concrete · 22 m reverb tail

    A walk-in freezer from 1968, still minus-twelve in winter before we heat it. Dead air, hard reflections, no sightlines to the outside. Where the 4 a.m. records get tested.

    $ sound: dry, close, deliberately unkind

  3. 02.3

    Cyan Warehouse

    Bay 7 · 5,200 · 340 overhead fixtures · 12 min B2B sets

    Sixteen acts trade the deck in back-to-back pairs, nobody leaves the booth mid-set. Ceiling rig is a grid of cyan tubes you can see the seams in on purpose.

    $ lighting: continuous, no strobe before 23:00

  4. 02.4

    Afterglow Pier

    Open dock · 1,800 · ocean front · sunset → sunrise

    Two hundred metres of timber pier with the runway lights behind you. Ambient, dub, and vocal sets until the first ferry. Free hot drinks from 04:30, no queue policy.

    $ rule: no phones past the third piling

03 Run of Show

How the three nights are shaped, hour by hour. Individual set times publish to your wristband app on December 10.

Fri 18 Ignition

  • 18:00Gates open. Pier market and food crates live from the first hour.
  • 20:30Sunset ceremony on Afterglow Pier — Lira Fathom plays to the water.
  • 22:00All four stages run simultaneously. Venue becomes 18+.
  • 01:30Kessler Void closes Pulse Main with a 90-minute live modular set.
  • 04:00Vault takeover: unknown headliner, announced on the door.
  • 06:00Last call, out via the apron. Shuttle every ten minutes.

Sat 19 Deep

  • 17:00Weekend campers wake to the low-volume pier session (no PA, monitors only).
  • 19:45Cyan Warehouse opens the 12-minute B2B chain — sixteen acts, no gaps.
  • 00:30Halcyon Drift, full surround, warehouse blackout at 01:55.
  • 02:00Naomi Static on Pulse Main with the harbor crane rig at full tilt.
  • 05:20Sunrise ambient on the dock, 40 minutes, phones away.

Sun 20 Return

  • 16:00Community afternoon: local openers, all ages, free with any weekend pass.
  • 21:00Closing ceremony begins — every stage plays the same 60 seconds.
  • 01:00Vex Meridian, 120 minutes, no support act.
  • 03:00Afterglow final: Lira Fathom and the harbor choirstill on the timber.
  • 06:40Lights up, cranes down, last train leaves Terminal Nine.
