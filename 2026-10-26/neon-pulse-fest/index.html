The programme

Three Nights, Walked Left to Right

Each night has its own key, its own weather, its own crowd. Slide through the diorama the way you would walk the yard — one exhibit at a time.

Chapter 01 / 03

Friday 18 Dec Mainframe opening

Ignition

The yard doors swing at 6 p.m. and Mainframe starts slow: dub techno under sodium light, a 90-minute ambient set on the gantry, then KASSEN takes the headline slot at 1 a.m. with a full laser rig.

Saturday 19 Dec Peak night

Overload

Both warehouse rooms run at capacity and the Pulse Warehouse roof retracts for an open-air peak-time block. VELVET STATIC closes at 4 a.m. with a live modular build and a 40-second blackout drop.

The ground

Ironworks Yard, Pier 7

A 14-acre decommissioned ship-repair yard on the west bank, the last industrial block in Portland that still looks the way the music sounds. Concrete, gantry cranes, salt fog off the Willamette, and enough power draw to run four sound systems at once.

Address400 Halcyon Quay, Portland, OR 97210
GatesFri 6 p.m. · Sat 6 p.m. · Sun 4 p.m.
