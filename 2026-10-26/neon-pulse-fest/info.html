03 Practical MANUAL

Terminal Nine is a working freight terminal that we borrow for four days a year. That is the reason it is extraordinary and the reason it needs explaining before you come.

Gates 18:00 Fri 18 Dec · last shuttle 06:40 Sun 21 Dec · lost property line open 24/7 from 15 Dec

03.1 The Terminal

Two hundred and fourteen acres of apron, warehouse, cold storage and timber dock, fenced on three sides and facing the water on the fourth.

◈ main gate, Harbor Ave◈ Pulse Main, apron north◈ Neon Vault, cold store B◈ Cyan Warehouse, Bay 7◈ Afterglow Pier, dock 4

Address for navigation: Terminal Nine, 2100 Harbor Avenue, San Pedro, CA 90731. Gate 3 on Harbor Avenue is the only pedestrian entrance; Gate 1 takes shuttles and campervans only. Ride-share drop-off is on Dana Street — the app will try to put you on the freight road, ignore it.

Inside, distances matter. It is 380 metres from Pulse Main to the Vault stair, 620 metres from the Warehouse bar to the pier rail, and the walk back to the camper field at 05:00 is a kilometre. The dock path is lit the whole way and staffed until the last shuttle leaves.

Water refill is free at eleven stations. There are no branded bottles for sale; if you arrive empty-handed, a flask costs $6 at any bar and we top it up all weekend.

$ note: phone signal drops to one bar after midnight when 22,000 people are on the apron. Agree a meeting point at Gate 3, sign 12.

$ note: wind funnel off Pier 4 is the coldest spot on site. Afterglow has heaters and blankets at the bar.

03.3 Access

Written for the person who is coming, not for the lawyer who is reading. Ask us anything at access@neonpulse.fest and a disabled member of the team will answer.

Step-free
Every stage, bar, toilet block and food crate is step-free. The Vault is reached by lift from Bay 6; the pier has a ramp to the rail.
Platform seating
Raised viewing platforms at all four stages, with companion spaces. No booking, no queue — show your wristband to the cyan armband.
Companion tickets
Free and uncapped. We do not ask for evidence, a letter or a diagnosis.
BSL & captions
Two interpreted sets per night on Pulse Main and every headline show captioned live on the side screens.
Quiet room
Bay 4, open 18:00–07:00, low lighting, ear defenders to borrow, one staff member who does not talk.
Hearing protection
Free foam plugs at every bar; moulded plugs fitted on site for $22 at the Access tent.
Caching & meds
Fridge storage for medication at the medical point, 24-hour access, no forms.
Assistance dogs
Always welcome. Relief area behind the Access tent, water bowls hourly.

The access team runs a phone and SMS line from 15 December: +1 310 555 0148. If you arrive and something does not work — a lift is blocked, a route is muddy, a queue has gone wrong — call that number from anywhere on site and we will fix it while you wait.

03.4 What To Bring, What To Leave

Bag checks are one line per gate and we do not confiscate out of spite. When in doubt, email us — the box office answers the same day.

  • Warm shell and a hat — 8°C at 03:00
  • Earplugs, or buy them at the bar
  • Power bank, under 20,000 mAh

03.5 Safer Spaces Policy

Thirty-two trained crew wear cyan armbands and can be asked for anything at any hour: a walk to your camp, a word to someone who will not leave you alone, a taxi, an earplug, a blanket. They are not security and they will not take your ticket away from you.

Harassment of any kind ends the weekend for the person doing it. Report at the Access tent, any bar, or by SMS to +1 310 555 0148. We do not require a statement, we do not require a witness, and we ban on the word of the person reporting unless the facts say otherwise.

Consent is a festival policy, not a personal preference. The 2026 edition runs a named-point protocol: if you need help with someone who is too intoxicated to consent, say “pier rail” at any bar and two crew will come without asking you to explain in front of your friends.

Substance checking is available free and anonymous at Bay 4 from 20:00, run by an independent harm-reduction charity, with no police presence and no records kept. There is a medical team on the pier every night until sunrise.

Photography in the bathroom lines, the camper field and the quiet room is prohibited and reported immediately. Our crew are briefed to intervene on sight.

03.6 Frequently Asked Questions

Twelve questions our box office actually receives, in the order it receives them. Click any entry to expand it.

No general parking — not limited, none. The apron is a live freight surface for the other 361 days of the year and the insurer will not let us put 22,000 people on it with cars. Take the shuttle from Downtown (every ten minutes, $12 return), the 60 bus to Pacific Avenue, or a ride-share to the Dana Street pen. If you have a blue badge, seven accessible bays sit at Gate 1, first come.

Gates open at 18:00 on Friday 18 December and the last shuttle leaves at 06:40 on Monday 21 December. Each night the stages run to roughly 06:00, with the pier the last room standing. Weekend pass holders get in on Thursday afternoon for the pre-party and the camper field.

Yes, unlimited re-entry on every tier except Single Night after 02:00, when the Vault lane closes for a changeover. Use Gate 3 on Harbor Avenue for walking; Gate 1 is shuttles and campervans only. Your wristband is the ticket — keep it dry, keep it on.

Card and phone payment at every bar and all but two of the pier food crates. There is a free-to-use ATM at Gate 3. No top-up wristband, no account to fund, no minimum spend, and bar prices are fixed for all three nights.

Bring an empty refillable flask — full containers do not pass the gate. Eleven free refill stations run the length of the site, staffed until 07:00. A soft-sided drink pouch of up to half a litre is fine; glass is not, in any form, including perfume.

December nights at this altitude on this water sit between 7°C and 11°C, and the wind off the pier makes it feel four degrees lower. The apron is warmer than the dock because the cranes break the wind. Bring a shell with a hood; blankets are free at the Afterglow bar from midnight.

No detachable lenses, no tripods, no gimbals. Compact cameras and phones are welcome everywhere except the bathroom lines, the camper field and the quiet room. Working press should apply to press@neonpulse.fest before 2 December with a portfolio link and the outlet you are shooting for.

Sixteen and over with a responsible adult until 21:30 on Friday and Saturday; eighteen and over after that and all day Sunday night into Monday. Challenge 25 is enforced at the gates and at every bar — bring photo ID even if you are fairly sure you will not be asked.

Free and unlimited until 1 December through the transfer tool in your order email. From 1 December the pass locks to the original name and resale is void, which is deliberate: it is why our ticket prices have not moved much in three years. Refunds are full until 1 November and 50% until 1 December.

Free charge lockers in Bay 3 ($22 for the weekend, holds a phone flat for four days), plus open charging benches at Gate 3, the Warehouse bar and the pier rail. Benches are cable-free with a USB-C and a USB-A socket each. Do not bring a large power tool or a space heater.

The show goes on. Three of the four stages are fully covered and the apron rig is rated for rain, so only the pier rail section has a plan B — it moves into Bay 7 with twenty minutes’ notice on the wristband app. We do not refund for weather and we do not cancel for it; December here is usually dry and cold rather than wet.

Lost property desk sits inside Gate 3 and is staffed 24 hours from 15 December. Descriptions go to lost@neonpulse.fest with the item, the night and the approximate time, and we answer with a photograph rather than a guess. Anything unclaimed ships at our cost after 24 December; phones, wallets and ID go straight to the San Pedro police counter the same night.

03.7 Terms, Privacy & The Small Print

Booking terms

A ticket is a licence to attend, not a right to a particular performance. If an artist cancels we program a replacement and we do not refund the difference — the weekend is the product, not one name on a poster. If the event is cancelled outright, every ticket is refunded in full inside thirty days without you asking.

Refunds: full until 1 November 2026, 50% until 1 December, none after. Transfers are free until 1 December. Payment plans that lapse release the pass back to the pool without penalty beyond losing it.

Privacy

We collect the name and email you give the box office, the wristband serial, and — if you book a shuttle or a pitch — the vehicle registration. That is all. We do not sell it, we do not share it with promoters, and we do not fingerprint anyone. Wristband serials are deleted 90 days after the last shuttle leaves. CCTV operates at the gates for insurer reasons and is held 31 days.

Cookies: this site sets none. There is no analytics script and no third-party tracker — the only external request the pages make is for the two web fonts. You can read a ticket purchase and never be measured.

Conditions of attendance

Bag checks are a condition of entry. Crew may refuse admission to anyone carrying glass, sprayable items or large luggage, and may remove anyone who harasses another attendee. Decisions at the gate are final for the night and reviewed by email within a week. Full text of the 2026 conditions is attached to every order confirmation.
