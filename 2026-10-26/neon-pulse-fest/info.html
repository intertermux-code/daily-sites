The site

Four Stages on Fourteen Acres of Concrete

Everything is under 400 metres from the gate, and every room is signed in the same cyan and magenta so you can find your way back from the bar with your eyes half-shut.

Mainframe

The open-air headline stage, built against the old gantry. 30-metre truss, a functionally full laser rig, and a sub array tuned for an outdoor concrete bowl. Covered from the waist down — rain hits the deck, not the crowd.

Capacity 9,000 · Standing · Viewing platform step-free

Pulse Warehouse

The 1940s fitting shed. Brick, steel roof on a retractable run, and the loudest room on site. Peak-time techno from 9 p.m. and the last set of the night at 4 a.m. Saturdays.

Capacity 4,200 · Standing · Hearing protection issued free at both doors

Night Garden

A landscaped corner of the yard with string lights, a fire bowl, and a low-volume melodic programme. Quietest room, warmest drinks, and the 5 a.m. sunrise set on Saturday.

Capacity 2,600 · Grass and gravel · Seated areas on timber decking

The Basement

The former pump house, one flight down, four hours of hard groove every night. No phone photography at all — the room is dark, sticky and completely offline.

Capacity 1,400 · Standing · Two step-free entrances from the north yard

Getting there

Arrive Tired, Leave Warmer

There is no general parking at the yard — Pier 7 has 140 spaces, all of them reserved. Plan on transit or the shuttle; both run past last set.

MAX Light Rail

Yellow Line to Halcyon Quay, then an 8-minute signed walk along the quay boardwalk. Trains run every 10 minutes until 12:40 a.m. and every 30 minutes after — the last northbound departs the yard end at 2:15 a.m.

From Portland Union Station: 14 minutes

Festival Shuttle

Union Station to the north gate every 20 minutes, 5 p.m. to 3:30 a.m. across all three nights. $39 for the weekend on the tickets page, or $9 for a single run bought on board.

Board at the Taylor Street curb, bay 4

Rideshare & Taxi

Drop-off on Halcyon Quay at the mile marker 2 pylon; pick-up is the dedicated north ramp, level 1. Expect a 12-minute wait between 4 and 6 a.m. — the free coffee kiosk at the ramp covers it.

Surge zone ends at the ramp gate, not the yard

Bike & Scooter

Two supervised bike lots with 900 racks, free, at the west and north gates. Dockless scooters must be left outside the cordon on Quay Street — the barrier crews tow anything inside.

Repair stand and pump at the west lot

Address for navigators

Ironworks Yard, 400 Halcyon Quay, Portland, OR 97210. Set your destination to “Halcyon Quay & SE Pier Street” — some navigator apps route to a fenced service road on the south side that adds a 20-minute walk.

Access Companion Scheme

One companion goes in free on any pass, at any tier, no proof required. Add it in your account portal, or claim it at the north gate with a “companion” note that auto-attaches once you tell us.

Getting Around the Yard

The ground is concrete and steel plate, level throughout, with 1-in-12 ramps. It gets wet and slick from midnight — grip soles genuinely matter more than a warm jacket. Golf-style buggies run a loop between the four stages on request, free, and take six minutes at most.

Communication Support

Text and video relay to the box office runs all night on the number below. Two staff on every shift are trained in British and American Sign Language, contactable through the access desk radio.

Bag policy

What Comes In, What Stays Home

Search takes under 40 seconds if you pack to this list. Anything in the right column can be left in a $4 day locker at the north gate — lockers are cashless and re-entry is open until 2 a.m.

Allowed

  • Bag smaller than 21 × 15 cm (a bum bag or jacket pocket)
  • Empty reusable bottle under 1 litre — four free refill stations
  • Phone, keys, wallet, earplugs, personal medication in original packaging
  • Small torch or headlamp — genuinely useful on the quay walk home
  • Bug wipes, lip balm, tampons, non-glass deodorant
  • One unframed folding chair for Night Garden only

Not Allowed

  • Large bags, suitcases, hard cases of any kind
  • Outside food or drink of any kind, including sealed bottles
  • Glass, cans, sprays, lighters in bulk, aerosols over 100 ml
  • Professional cameras, drones, GoPros and selfie sticks
  • Laser pointers, flare, pyrotechnics, smoke devices
  • Illegal substances — searched, confiscated, and reported

Payment On Site

Cashless everywhere. Tap, phone, or a pre-loaded festival wristband top-up; the last ATM in town with a working machine is the one in Union Station concourse.

Welfare & Drugs

The quiet room is staffed all night by medics and peer volunteers. Nobody gets thrown out for asking for help — an anonymous amnesty tent at the east gate takes disposal of anything, no questions, no scan.

Lost & Found

Report an item through the account portal or at the north gate desk. Everything found is photographed and posted to the community board each morning at 11 a.m. Pacific — including the twelve leather jackets a year.

FAQ

The Twelve Questions We Get Weekly

Use arrow keys to move between questions. Anything we have not covered goes to the box office below.

Gates open at 6 p.m. on Friday and Saturday, 4 p.m. on Sunday. The queue is shortest between 6:15 and 7:30 p.m.; it peaks hard in the 45 minutes before each headline. If you are chasing a specific slot, arrive an hour before and be inside the room rather than inside the fence line.

Expect 1–4°C with a river wind that makes it feel 4 degrees lower, and a real chance of rain on Saturday. Layers, a waterproof shell, and grippy closed shoes beat a costume every time. You will be standing still for 20 minutes between sets more often than you expect, and that is when the cold gets you.

Six free 4-port lockers at the north gate and Night Garden, 90 minutes per cycle. Beyond that, the $45 Locker & Recharge add-on gives you a bank for the whole weekend, collected at the desk. There are no wall sockets on the stages — a real limitation, not a policy.

3-Day Circuit, GA+, VIP and Backstage re-enter unlimited. Single Night passes re-enter until 2 a.m. only, which stops the people who leave, sleep, and try to come back for the sunrise set. Your wristband scan is the record; keep it dry.

Eighteen hatch windows around the yard; eleven of them stay open past 4 a.m. Fourteen of the eighteen offer vegetarian, and nine are fully plant-based. Two halal and two kosher-certified stalls sit between the Warehouse and the Basement. Expect $9–16 a plate and a peak wait of eight minutes at midnight.

Phone photography is welcome on Mainframe and in Night Garden. The Basement and the Warehouse’s lower deck are no-camera rooms — crews put a cyan dot on the lens sticker of anyone who forgets, and the room stays dark for everyone else. Accredited photographers apply through the site form by 1 December.

No tent camping — the yard floods. There are 48 heated caravan pods in the north corner, sold as an add-on with GA+ and above; the 2026 allocation sold out in 40 minutes and the waitlist converted about one in six last year. We publish a shortlist of walked-distance hotels that keep late check-in.

Full refund within 14 days of purchase, no reason needed, no form to argue with. After that, passes are transferable free of charge until 10 December. If the festival is cancelled we refund every pass automatically within ten business days — you never have to ask. Details in the refund section below.

Shuttles run from the north gate to Union Station every 20 minutes until 3:30 a.m., then two dedicated 6:10 and 6:30 a.m. departures after Sunday’s close. MAX resumes at 4:32 a.m. on the Yellow Line. Rideshare pick-up is the north ramp, level 1, and the 6 a.m. wait is genuinely shorter than the 4 a.m. one.

Personal-use quantities of anything are searched, confiscated and discarded at the gate; dealing gets you removed permanently and reported. The amnesty tent at the east gate exists so nobody carries something in for fear of being scanned. We do not run drug testing and we do not share welfare attendance with police.

Any crew member in a magenta vest can radio the safety team immediately — you do not need to explain, and you do not need to be the person affected. Text the same team on 503-555-0199, which is monitored all night. We can relocate you, escort you out, or remove the person; the choice is yours and it is never escalated to a police report unless you ask.

Headline slots are contractually locked and have never changed. Support slots do move — usually a rescheduled tour date, occasionally a cancelled flight. If an artist is replaced we announce within six hours, and if you bought specifically for that act you can refund rather than accept the substitute.

Refunds & Transfers

Full refund within 14 days of purchase with no reason required. After that window, transfers are free and unlimited until 10 December; from December 10 the name on the pass must match photo ID at the gate. Cancellations of the festival refund automatically. Payment-plan balances stop when a pass is returned to allocation.

Code of conduct: Neon Pulse runs a zero-tolerance policy on harassment, discrimination and dealing. Crew in magenta vests can radio the safety team from anywhere on site. Full policy text and the independent report route are published in the account portal.
