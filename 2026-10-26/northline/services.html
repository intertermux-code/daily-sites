Engagement Models

Three Ways To Contract

Fixed-Scope Sprint

Best when the problem is clear. One fee, one date, one named team. Changes go through a written scope note, priced before they start, so nobody discovers a surprise in month three.

6–16 weeks · €24k–€240k

Staged Programme

Best for multi-release platform work. We contract one stage at a time — usually a quarter — with a go/no-go review at each boundary. You keep the option; we keep the momentum.

2–8 quarters · €90k+ per stage

Embedded Retainer

Best when your team already ships and needs senior design or front-end capacity. Monthly hours, a standing critique slot, and a shared backlog you prioritise.

Rolling · €9k–€26k per month

Questions Before The Call

Answered In Advance

How soon can you start?

Usually four to six weeks after a signed agreement — we hold one project per discipline at a time. If you need to move faster, tell us: we keep one short-response slot open each quarter for urgent audits.

Do you work with our existing agency?

Yes, when the roles are written down. We are commonly the product-design or engineering partner inside a brand agency’s account, and we are equally comfortable being the ones who leave after the system ships.

Can you sign our paperwork?

Standard DPA, NDA and insurance certificates are on file and go out the same day. Security questionnaires take us two working days; we have answered 40-plus of them, so the process is quick.

What do you need from us?

One decision-maker, access to customers, and the analytics logins you already have. Missing those is the single biggest cause of overrun — we will name it in week one if it looks likely.

Do you do fixed hourly rates?

For retainers yes: €145–€210 per hour depending on discipline, invoiced monthly against actuals with a two-week forecast. For defined work we prefer fixed scope, because it prices the outcome rather than the calendar.
