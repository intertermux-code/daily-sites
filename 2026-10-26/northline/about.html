Process

How A Project Runs, Week By Week

Five stages on one spine. Durations are honest averages, not best cases. You get a written note at every boundary, so nothing depends on being in the room.

  1. Stage 01 — Interrogate

    Two weeks with your data, your customers and your support queue. We come back with the argument your product has to win and the metrics that will settle whether it did.

    • Research
    • Analytics
    • Interviews

    Weeks 1–2

    You decide whether the brief was even the right brief. About one in six projects stops here, with a written plan and no further invoice.

  2. Stage 02 — Draw The Line

    Structure before surface: flows, information architecture, content model, the grid. We draw in black and get agreement on shape before any colour enters the room.

    • IA
    • Wireframes
    • Prototypes

    Weeks 3–6

    Weekly demos, every Wednesday. Your engineers sit in from this stage, not after handoff, because that is where the cost of being wrong is cheapest.

  3. Stage 03 — Make It Real

    Identity, UI, motion and the component library, built as one system and tested against your hardest three applications — usually a print piece, an interface and a sign.

    • Brand
    • Design system
    • Motion

    Weeks 6–11

    Tokens, states, empty screens and error copy are deliverables, not leftovers. They arrive documented.

  4. Stage 04 — Build & Prove

    Front-of-page engineering with performance budgets and automated accessibility checks in CI. We pair with your team on pull requests so the knowledge moves, not just the files.

    • Engineering
    • CMS
    • QA

    Weeks 10–14

    Launch happens behind a switch you control, with a rollback plan written before the first deployment.

  5. Stage 05 — Hand Over

    Two workshops, a decision log, a measurement dashboard your team reads alone, and thirty days of support included in the fee. Then we leave on purpose.

    • Docs
    • Training
    • Support

    Week 14+

    Success check at 90 days: we compare the numbers we agreed to against what actually moved, in writing, whether or not it flatters us.

Principles

Four Rules We Actually Follow

Proof Beats Polish

A screen that looks finished and has no evidence behind it is a draft. Every recommendation carries a source: a session, a transcript, a funnel number or a published standard.

One Team, No Relay

Strategists, designers and engineers share the same weekly call and the same document. Handoff is a handover of understanding, not a folder of assets.

Delete Before Add

The cheapest performance and clarity win is usually a screen, a field or a template nobody uses. We audit for removal at every stage boundary.

Accessible By Default

Keyboard paths, contrast, focus order and reduced-motion behaviour are specified in the design, verified in the build, and documented at handover. AA is the floor, not the goal.

Publish The Price

Ranges on the website, line items in the agreement, forecasts on the retainer. If you cannot budget for us without a discovery call, our information is not doing its job.

Leave A Paper Trail

Decisions written down, in plain language, with the rejected options named. Six months later your new colleague can reconstruct why things look like this.
