After You Send

What Happens Next, In Order

No nurture sequence, no gatekeeping. Four steps, each with a named person responsible.

  1. A Partner Answers Within One Working Day

    You get a first opinion on scope, the name of who would lead it, and one blunt question: what would make this a failure? That question shapes the proposal.

    Day 1

    Answered by Anders, Marta or Tobias — never routed through an inbox triage queue.

  2. Forty-Five Minutes, No Deck

    We bring your own screens and your numbers, not case-study theatre. You bring the data you can share. By the end we either agree on a shape of work or say honestly that we are not the right studio.

    Day 2–5

    Video or the Copenhagen studio. Bring the engineer who will build it — that call is better in threes.

  3. A Written Proposal With Line Items

    Scope, stages, named team, price band, dependencies on you, and the measurement plan. Three pages, not thirty, and every number traceable to a discipline.

    Day 6–10

    Valid 30 days. We hold the slot for that window and release it after, publicly, to whoever asks next.

  4. Kickoff Within Six Weeks

    Contract, access, and the interview calendar. Stage one starts with your customers, not with a moodboard, and the first demo lands the following Wednesday.

    Week 1

    You get the shared channel, the file access and the weekly note template before day one.

Logistics

Practical Things People Ask First

Can we call you right now?

Between 09:00 and 17:00 CET, Monday to Thursday, on +45 32 12 00 88. Fridays are for internal work and proposals — the line goes to voicemail and gets answered the same evening.

Do you work outside Europe and the US?

Yes. We currently run engagements with teams in Berlin, Amsterdam, Toronto and Singapore. We keep overlap hours with your core timezone rather than pretending to be everywhere at once.

What about NDAs and security review?

Send yours; we sign most standard forms within a day. Our DPA, insurance certificates and SOC 2-aligned policies are on file. Full vendor questionnaires take two working days.

Is there a minimum?

€24,000 for defined work, or 60 hours per month on retainer. Below that we would rather point you to a trusted independent than give your project the attention it cannot afford.

How do you handle data in this form?

Enquiry data goes to one encrypted mailbox with two partners on access. It is deleted or anonymised after 12 months, and you can ask us to forget it at any time by writing to the studio address.
