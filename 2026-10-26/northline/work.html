Case Anatomy

Inside One Engagement: Halvard Bank

Fourteen weeks, four designers, two engineers, one shipped console. Read the spine from top to bottom and you will see where the time actually went.

  1. Weeks 1–2 · Evidence

    We sat with eleven small-business customers and nine bank staff, pulled two years of support transcripts, and mapped every task a customer attempts in their first 30 days.

    01

    Output: a 62-screen inventory, a ranked task model, and the one sentence the product had to win.

  2. Weeks 3–6 · Structure

    Eleven views replaced sixty-two screens. We wrote the navigation language before we drew it, so the IA and the copy arrived as one decision.

    02

    Output: flows, wireframes, a clickable prototype tested with 14 customers in five days.

  3. Weeks 7–9 · System

    Type scale, spacing tokens, data table patterns and number formatting went in as a library, reviewed jointly with Halvard’s engineering leads.

    03

    Output: 78 tokens, 41 components, Figma and code kept in one source of truth.

  4. Weeks 10–13 · Build

    Our engineers paired with theirs: pull requests reviewed in the studio, accessibility checks in CI, performance budgets enforced per route.

    04

    Output: 100% keyboard coverage, zero critical axe violations, 1.4s median interaction latency.

  5. Week 14 · Handover

    Two workshops, a written decision log, and a measurement plan Halvard owns. Thirty days of support included, no upsell.

    05

    Output: activation +31%, navigation-related tickets −40% within one quarter.
