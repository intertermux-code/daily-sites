Section D · The Circles

Twelve chairs, one book, ten weeks

Our circles are not book clubs in the wine-and-cheese sense. Somebody is wrong about the ending every fortnight, the tea goes cold, and nobody is asked to have read the introduction. Enrol below for the Michaelmas term; four of the five circles still have places.

Entry D.2 · Form

Enrol for the Michaelmas term

Term fees are paid at the desk, in cash or card, on your first night. Nothing is taken online.

Optional. We text if a session moves or the Cellar floods again.

Which circle shall we write you into?

One choice. Guests are welcome at a second circle for £5 a night once you have attended twice.

This is how Ruth decides where to seat you. It is not an application; nobody is refused.

Nothing is charged and no card details are taken. The desk posts your confirmation, by hand if you live in Ashborne.

What happens after you send this

Ruth or Marisol reads every form at the Thursday desk and writes back within a working day with your night, your seat number and the page you should be at by the first session. If the circle you chose is full, we offer you the next one rather than adding your name to a queue that never moves.

The only two rules

Come, and if you cannot come, tell somebody. Reading the book is expected but not enforced: three members of the 2023 Middlemarch run never read past Book IV and are still on the list, and still right about Dorothea.

Entry D.3

Fees, and what they buy

A term is ten weeks unless the circle says otherwise, and a term fee covers the room, the candles, the tea and the person who boils the kettle. Concession places are half price and we do not ask what your concession is — say the word at the desk and the book shows a lower number.

Members of circles get a tenth off any title the circle is reading, all term, and we will hold copies behind the counter so nobody has to hunt. If you buy elsewhere that is fine too; the shop survives on the forty people who walk in for something else entirely.
