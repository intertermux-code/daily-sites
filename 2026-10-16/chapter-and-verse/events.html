Register · Conditions

Tickets, seats and the eleven steps

Nothing here is sold through a machine. Pay at the desk, telephone and we hold your name, or ask us to post a paper ticket with your initials on it. If a session sells out we add names to the list by the stair and write to you when a chair frees — we do not send reminders three days beforehand, because you are busy.

The Cellar Room is reached by eleven steep stone steps and has no lift; the Long Room and the front shop are level with the street and a folding chair is kept by the door. Both rooms have hearing loops; ask for the panel code at the desk. Children are welcome at anything unless the entry says otherwise, and the marathon is the one exception.

“I came for the Whitman because a friend dragged me, and I have not missed a Thursday since. Nobody explains the poem to you here; they just argue about line nineteen.”
