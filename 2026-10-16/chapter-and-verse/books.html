Section B · Notes

How to buy from this shelf

Reserve by shelfmark

Quote the shelfmark — Castle 3·G, Cellar 2·P — at the desk, by telephone, or in a note. We hold reserved books for fourteen days and will not sell them from the counter by mistake, because that is how shops lose customers.

Condition is stated, not implied

Fine means unread and square. Very good means it has been read with care. Good means it will be read again, and we have reinforced it if it needed us to. Where a volume has a name in it, we say so, and where a name in it makes the book better, we say that too.

Buy-back on the same terms all year

£4 for a cloth classic in honest condition, £1 for a paperback, and £12 upward for anything the bench dates before 1950. We pay half in cash, half in shelf credit, and we will always tell you when a dealer would pay more.
