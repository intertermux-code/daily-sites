Our Core Initiatives

Six targeted programs designed to address specific gaps in the local food security network.

01— Emergency Distribution

Rapid response teams deliver essential non-perishable staples to families within 24 hours of referral from social workers or shelters.

12k Boxes/Mo 24hr Response

02— Farm Rescue

We partner directly with regional growers to collect "ugly" produce that doesn't meet retail cosmetic standards but is perfectly nutritious.

45 Partner Farms Zero Waste Goal

03— Kids Backpacks

Weekend meal kits sent home with students who rely on school lunches, ensuring no child goes hungry over Saturday and Sunday.

3,200 Students Weekly Delivery

04— Senior Delivery

Door-to-door service for elderly individuals with mobility issues, providing fresh produce and dairy products twice monthly.

800 Seniors Monthly Route

05— Nutrition Education

Free workshops teaching budget-friendly cooking techniques using rescued ingredients, empowering recipients with culinary skills.

12 Workshops Monthly

06— Disaster Relief

Pre-positioned stockpiles activated during natural disasters, providing immediate sustenance to affected communities.

500 Family Kits Ready State
