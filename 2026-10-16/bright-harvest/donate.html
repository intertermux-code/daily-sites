Beyond the card

Six more ways to fill the warehouse

Prefer to give something other than money? Most of our food arrives that way already.

Grow it or bake it

Surplus from a garden, orchard, or bakery case is welcome at the loading dock any weekday morning. We weigh it, thank you, and get it out within a day.

Dock open 8–11 a.m.

Sponsor a shift

Businesses cover the cost of one sorting shift — about $380 — and their team spends the morning on the floor instead of writing a cheque.

12 crews sponsored this quarter

Match it at work

Nine local employers match giving dollar for dollar, including Meridian Credit Union and Sunbelt Logistics. Send us your portal link and we will file the paperwork.

$61,400 matched in 2026

Stock and donor-advised funds

Appreciated shares and DAF grants clear capital-gains tax and arrive at full market value. Our finance desk handles the transfer — call (555) 519–0142.

The pantry wishlist

Short on: shelf-stable milk, infant formula size 1, retort pouches, low-sodium beans, and reusable bags. Long on: canned soup. Full list updates monthly at the dock office.

Give in someone’s name

Memorial and birthday gifts fund a named box each year, and the family gets a photograph of the packing line with a note about where the food landed.

Before you press give

Questions we get at the front desk

Is my monthly gift easy to stop?

Yes. Reply to any receipt, call the office, or use the cancel link in your donor portal and it stops the same day — no retention phone call, no minimum term.

About one in five monthly givers pauses for a season when their own circumstances change. We would rather you paused than resent us.

Do you take food donations instead of money?

We do, with two rules: nothing past a use-by date, and nothing that needs refrigeration on a Saturday. Unopened shelf-stable items, produce, and bakery goods are always usable.

Cash is still the most efficient gift — $3 buys a meal, whereas a donated jar of sauce costs us a label, a shelf, and a hand to sort it.

How do you know who needs the food?

We do not ask. Our partner agencies confirm that a household lives or works in Midland County and wants food; that is the whole eligibility test. No income documents, no immigration questions, no limits on visits.

Self-nomination is welcome: walk into any Mobile Market stop or community kitchen and eat.

What does the 92¢ figure include?

Food purchase, rescue transport, cold storage, packaging, and the distribution network that moves it to 24 sites. The remaining 8¢ covers staff administration and fundraising.

The full reconciliation is published every quarter, and our Form 990 is available on request within one business day.

Will you sell my information?

Never. We do not rent, swap, or sell donor lists, and we do not use third-party telemarketing firms. Processing happens through a PCI-DSS Level 1 payment provider; Bright Harvest never sees or stores a full card number.
