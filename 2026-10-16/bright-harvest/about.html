The Story Behind The Harvest

Founded in 2018 by three local farmers frustrated by seeing tons of perfectly good produce rotting while families went hungry.

01— The Problem

In our region alone, an estimated 15% of all produced food never makes it to a plate due to cosmetic standards, logistical errors, or market fluctuations. Simultaneously, one in six households faces food insecurity. This disconnect is what drives us.

02— Our Approach

We operate on a "Just-In-Time" logistics model. Instead of warehousing goods for weeks, we coordinate pickup immediately after harvest or store closing, ensuring maximum freshness and nutritional value when delivered to community centers.
