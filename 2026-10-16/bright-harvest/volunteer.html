Your first morning

What actually happens when you arrive

Nine out of ten first-timers book a second shift. The other one is usually stuck on a delivery route.

  1. Sign the board, take a lanyard.Ring the bell at the pedestrian gate; Renée or Marcus meets you with a green lanyard and a pair of gloves.
  2. Six minutes of training.The safety card, the four crate colours, and what gets composted instead of boxed. That is the whole curriculum.
  3. Work a table, not a quota.Four to six people per table with one staff member. Nobody is timed, and the line stops when the shift ends.
  4. Take home a box and breakfast.Every volunteer leaves with a share of what came in that day, plus coffee on the loading step if the weather holds.

The short version

House rules, in five lines

The full policy pack is emailed with your confirmation. It fits on one page on purpose.

Dignity rule. No photographs, filming, or social posts of anyone collecting food — including your own family at a site. Staff photographs people who have consented in writing, and nobody else.

Food safety. Gloves on, hair tied back if it is over a collar, and anything doubtful goes to the compost crate rather than a box.

No proselytising. Sites serve everyone. No selling, recruiting, or preaching on the floor or at the markets.

Show up, or tell us. A text to the rota line costs nothing; an empty table at 6 a.m. costs a shift lead an hour of scrambling.

Be kind about the food. It is imperfect, short-dated, and completely good. That is the whole arrangement with the farms.

Do I need a background check?

Only for Backpack Program stuffing at schools and for solo delivery routes that enter someone’s home. Both are free, arranged by us, and take about two weeks. Warehouse, market, and kitchen shifts need nothing.

Can my company, team, or family come as a group?

Yes — groups of up to twelve take the Thursday pack line, and we will set aside a shift lead for you. Sponsor a shift for $380 and your team’s gift covers the food cost of the boxes you pack together.

Is there work I can do from home?

Grant writing, translation (Spanish, Nepali, Somali, and Vietnamese are live needs), route planning on Sunday evenings, and the phone rota between 9 a.m. and noon. Tick “dock admin” on the form and say what you are good at.

I use a wheelchair. Is the floor workable?

It is. The pack line has two seated stations at 90 cm with knee clearance, the dock ramp is permanent, and the accessible washroom is beside the loading bay. Say the word in the notes and we will confirm the station is free before you commit.
