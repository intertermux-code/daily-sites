The LumiDent difference

Modern dentistry, minus the dread

Four things shape every appointment here. They are the reason 92% of our patients bring their whole family, and why most of us stop dreading the drive over.

Unhurried appointments

We book 60 minutes for a routine check and twice that for anything surgical. Nobody is ever asked to “hold still while we squeeze in the next patient”.

A studio, not a clinic

Daylight in every operatory, noise-cancelling headphones, weighted blankets and a scent-free policy. The room is designed to lower your heart rate.

Prices on the table

Every plan arrives with an itemised, written quote and an insurance estimate the same day. If a fee changes, you approve it before we touch a tooth.

Technology in service of calm

Low-dose 3D scanning, same-day milled crowns and a needle that numbs faster than you expect. Fewer visits, less noise, better margins.

Your first year with us

From “I should really go” to fully cared for

Every patient follows the same five-stage path, whether they arrive with a chipped front tooth or a full set of crowns. Nothing moves to the next stage until you say so.

  1. 01

    Day 0 · Two minutes online

    You book, we hold the slot

    Choose a clinician and a time on our booking page. You will get a text confirmation within a minute, plus a short form to fill in on your phone so nothing gets asked twice at reception.

    • No deposit, no card details required
    • Free to move or cancel up to 24 hours ahead
  2. 02

    Visit 1 · 60 minutes

    A full examination, gently

    Dr. Osei or Dr. Bell starts with your history and your concerns — not your teeth. Then a gum chart, low-dose scans, intraoral photographs and an oral cancer check. You watch every image on the screen with us.

    • New-patient exam and scans: $149, or fully covered by most plans
    • Nothing is treated on this visit unless it is an emergency
  3. 03

    Within 48 hours

    A written plan with real numbers

    You receive a plan split into “must do”, “should do” and “for later”, each with a fixed price, an insurance estimate and two payment options. Read it at the kitchen table; nobody phones to chase you.

    • Zero-interest spreads from $89 a month via CareCredit and Sunbit
    • A second clinician reviews any plan over $2,000 at no charge
  4. 04

    Visits 2 to 4

    Treatment in blocks that suit you

    Long work is split into morning or evening blocks so you can keep your job and your gym session. Nitrous, oral sedation and numbing gel are offered without you having to ask, and headphones stay in the drawer if you prefer quiet.

    • Same-day crowns milled in our studio, no temporary to lose
    • A named coordinator answers your messages within one working day
  5. 05

    Month 6 and beyond

    Aftercare that never lapses

    We re-scan, re-polish and re-photograph so you can see the difference the year made. Hygiene reminders arrive when you want them — by text, email, or not at all.

    • Membership from $32 a month covers two checks, two cleans and 15% off treatment
    • 10-year written guarantee on every implant we place
