Why the handle moves slowly here

Whitening and bonding change a photograph in one visit. Alignment changes how a jaw functions, so the honest answer is months rather than minutes — and the result is only permanent if the retention is worn.

  • Typical duration6–18 months, reviewed every 8 weeks
  • Appointments8–20, most under 25 minutes
  • Price band$2,400 Lite · $4,800 Full · $5,600 ceramic braces
  • RetentionBonded wire plus a night retainer, from $480
  • We photograph and scan at every stage, so you can watch the movement yourself instead of trusting a promise.
  • Crowding that has come back after earlier braces is common and still treatable — bring your old retainer to the assessment.
  • If veneers are being discussed as a shortcut for crooked teeth, we will say so, and offer the slower option first.
Orthodontics, priced in full

Before anything is cut

You try the smile on first

Nobody commits to cosmetic work on the strength of a photograph. This is the sequence we run in studio.

  1. 01

    Visit one · 60 minutes

    Assessment and scan

    We check the teeth are healthy enough for the work you want, photograph the current smile, and take a digital scan. Shade is chosen in daylight, not under an operatory lamp.

  2. 02

    Two weeks

    A designed mock-up on screen

    Dr. Bell or Dr. Osei shapes the proposal with the lab and sends you a rendering. Bring it to a friend. Change your mind now, when it costs nothing.

  3. 03

    Visit two · 45 minutes

    Temporary smile, worn for a week

    We bond a trial version over your own teeth. You eat, talk and photograph it for seven days. $260 for the mock-up, credited against the treatment if you go ahead.

  4. 04

    Visit three

    Final fit, then a review at twelve months

    Ceramics are cemented, the bite is checked twice, and you are booked back at twelve months for a polish and a photographic review — included in the price.

Keeping the result

How long each outcome lasts, and what it needs

Veneers and crowns

Twelve to twenty years with a night guard if you clench. Coffee, red wine and curry stain the composite around them long before they stain the ceramic, so re-polish at each hygiene visit.

Guarantee: five years on ceramic, replaced by us if it debonds or chips.

Whitening

Six to fourteen months, depending on coffee, wine and tobacco. Two top-up trays of gel, fifteen minutes each, keeps it going far cheaper than repeating the full visit.

Top-up trays are $65 the first time and included in our $49 membership.

Alignment

Permanent — if the retainer is worn. Teeth drift because the gums around them take a year to remould, which is why relapse is nearly always a retention problem, not a treatment one.

We review retention free for three years after any orthodontic course.
