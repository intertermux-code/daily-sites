      From request to chair

      Four steps, and you always know which one you are in

      No call center, no “we’ll come back to you”. Here is exactly what happens after you press send.

      1. 01

        Within seconds

        Your slot is held on the screen

        The time you pick is ring-fenced for two business hours while a coordinator checks the diary against your treatment length. Nothing is charged and no card is stored.

      2. 02

        Same working day

        A human confirms by text and email

        Will Fraser or Kai Brennan reads the request — never an automated inbox. They confirm the clinician, how long the chair is booked for, and the exact price band from our published list.

      3. 03

        48 hours and 2 hours before

        Two reminders, one tap to change

        Reply “move” to either text and we phone you within the hour to rebook. If you are running late, reply there and we will hold the slot for fifteen minutes rather than reroute you.

      4. 04

        On the day

        Ten minutes early, then straight in

        Check in on the tablet in the quiet nook, meet your hygienist, and the dentist comes to you. Every new-patient exam includes a full intraoral scan and a written plan before you leave.

      Fair rules, written plainly

      Changing, cancelling and paying

      Changes and cancellations

      Move or cancel free of charge with 24 hours notice — one tap in the reminder text, or call the studio. Inside 24 hours we charge $45 to cover the chair we held for you; we waive it first time, and we waive it for any hospital, bereavement or childcare clash. No exceptions for illness: just tell us.

      What it costs, when

      Check-ups and hygiene are paid at the desk after treatment. Anything over $500 — crowns, implants, orthodontics — needs a 20% deposit to order materials, with the rest spread interest-free over six to twenty-four months through CareCredit or Sunbit, from $89 a month. Insurance is claimed for you, and we tell you the out-of-pocket number before you agree.

      Access and support

      Step-free entry, an accessible WC, a hearing loop in reception and room 4, and treatment chairs that lower to 40cm. Spanish, Portuguese, Swedish and American Sign Language interpreters are bookable at no charge — mention the language in the notes and we arrange it before your visit.

      Before you press send

      Booking questions, answered

      Do I need to be an existing patient to book?

      No. We open roughly a third of each day’s slots to new patients, and the form above treats everyone identically. The $149 new-patient exam is the same price for a first visit and a tenth, and takes sixty minutes rather than thirty.

      What if my preferred time shows as unavailable?

      Greyed-out chips are either already taken or, if you are booking for today, too soon for us to prepare a chair. Pick the nearest time you can manage and add a note — coordinators can often shuffle a hygienist slot, and we release cancelled appointments into the diary each morning at 7:15.

      Can I book two family members into the same afternoon?

      Yes, and it is popular on Saturday mornings. Send one request per person and put the intended time in the notes, for example “with parent’s 9:00 appointment”. We keep adjacent rooms free so you can stay in the building rather than shuttle between them.

      How soon can I be seen for an emergency?

      Call (503) 555-0188 rather than using the form. We assess emergencies within four working hours, and weekday slots are held back until 3pm specifically for acute pain, swelling, and knocked-out or broken teeth. Trauma to the jaw or facial bones, or swelling that affects breathing or swallowing, belongs in an emergency department — go now.

      Is my appointment detail kept private?

      Yes. Requests go to two coordinators on a password-managed system inside the practice, we never use your email for marketing without a separate opt-in, and you can ask us to delete a draft request at any time. Full details, including how long clinical records are held, are on the privacy & records page.
