Program one

Dental Implants

An implant is a titanium root placed into jawbone, left to bond for three to five months, then restored with a crown that we mill here rather than send away. We place guided surgery using a printed sleeve, which means the flap stays small, the numbness fades faster and we know exactly where the screw lands before we start.

  • Guided placement from CBCT scan, so the surgeon works from a map, not a guess
  • A same-day temporary crown in most front-tooth cases — you never leave toothless
  • Bone grafting and sinus lifts handled in-house, quoted separately and up front
  • Ten-year written guarantee on the fixture and abutment, transferable if you move

The implant pathway

What six months of implant treatment actually looks like

Placed in the right order, an implant is the most predictable thing we do. Here is the sequence, stage by stage.

  1. 01

    Week 0

    Assessment and clearance

    We check bone volume, gum health, smoking status and any medication that affects healing. If you are not a candidate yet, we say so and treat the reason first.

  2. 02

    Week 1

    3D scan and printed guide

    A CBCT scan becomes a surgical sleeve printed in our studio. Dr. Ibarra plans depth, angle and diameter on screen, then signs the plan that your surgeon follows.

  3. 03

    Week 2 to 4

    Extractions or grafting, if needed

    Decayed teeth come out and the socket is packed with graft material where the bone has thinned. Two to three months of quiet healing follows before anything is placed.

  4. 04

    Month 3

    Placement day

    Forty minutes in the chair, guided, with nitrous or sedation if you have asked for it. Most patients drive themselves home and go back to work the next day with paracetamol.

  5. 05

    Month 4 to 5

    Osseointegration and the healing cap

    The fixture bonds to bone. We review it with a scan at week twelve, then uncover it and take a digital impression for your crown — no putty trays, no gagging.

  6. 06

    Month 6

    Crown fitted, then annual review

    Your zirconia crown is milled, tried, adjusted and cemented. From then on it is checked once a year with the rest of your mouth, and the guarantee is registered in your name.

Program three

Cosmetic Dentistry that stays undetectable

Cosmetic work fails when it is done tooth by tooth instead of face by face. We start with a photograph, a scan and a digital mock-up you wear in your own mouth for a week — so the shape and shade are agreed before a single tooth is prepared.

  • Porcelain veneers from 0.3 mm, minimally prepped and bonded in one or two visits
  • Take-home whitening with custom trays, or in-chair whitening in a single 90-minute slot
  • Composite bonding to close black triangles and reshape one stubborn tooth without cutting it

Program four

Children who are not frightened of the dentist

That is the whole brief. We see babies from their first tooth, keep the first visit to ten happy minutes, and never do treatment on a child who is saying no with their body.

First visit, age one to four

A lap visit: count teeth, show you the brush, answer feeding questions. No instruments, no cost, and no invoice if you decide not to register.

School-age check-ups

Sealants, fluoride varnish and a decay-risk conversation with you rather than with the child. $95, or free under our family membership.

Trauma and knocked-out teeth

Ring us immediately on the emergency line. A tooth avulsed before 6pm is re-implanted the same evening roughly nine times out of ten.

Before you ask

Questions we answer most days

Do you really hold the price once treatment starts?

Yes. Your written plan is fixed for ninety days. If we discover something unexpected mid-treatment, we stop, photograph it, show you, and quote the addition separately. We never proceed on a fee you have not agreed to.

What if I need work your insurance will not cover?

We will tell you the clinical reason it is not covered, then offer a cheaper route if one honestly exists — a filling instead of a crown, an Invisalign Lite instead of Full, monitoring instead of replacing an old bridge. If there is no cheaper safe option, we say so plainly and spread the cost.

How painful is implant surgery, really?

Placement itself is numb, so it is comparable to a straightforward extraction. Most patients rate the first two days at three out of ten and go back to work on day two with over-the-counter pain relief. Guided surgery means less swelling because we do not raise a large flap.

Can I bring my children, and is there somewhere to wait?

Absolutely. Our Riverbend studio has a bookshelf and screen in the corner of the waiting area, a family operatory with two chairs so a parent can sit beside a child, and we treat under-fours free of charge. Children can start with a ten-minute happy visit with no instruments at all.

Do you treat nervous patients, and how do I ask for it?

Tick the anxiety box on the booking form, or say it on the phone — either way we will book a longer slot, agree a stop signal before you sit down, and never ask you to open your mouth without telling you what happens next. Nitrous, oral sedation and IV sedation are all available in-studio.

How quickly can you see an emergency?

We hold weekday slots until 3pm for same-day emergencies, and our emergency line is answered 24 hours. Knocked-out teeth and uncontrolled bleeding are seen immediately; pain that can be managed with medication is usually booked for the next working morning.
