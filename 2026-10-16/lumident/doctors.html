Our story

Seventeen years, told down the middle

From two chairs above a bookshop to a nine-operatory studio on the river.

  1. ’09

    2009

    Two chairs above a bookshop

    Amara Osei opens LumiDent on Riverbend Walk with one hygienist, a second-hand microscope and a rule she had written down beforehand: no treatment begins before the patient has seen the price.

  2. ’13

    2013

    The surgical suite and the first implant

    Rafael Ibarra joins and we build a dedicated surgical room with its own sterilisation line. The studio’s 1,000th implant is placed in November by a patient who had been told elsewhere it was impossible.

  3. ’17

    2017

    We publish the whole price list

    After a year of patients asking the same question on the phone, we put every fee on the website. Two insurers complained; three hundred new patients called. We have updated it every January since.

  4. ’21

    2021

    Digital dentistry, in-house milling

    Putty trays go out of the building. A CEREC mill and a 3D printer move into the old stock room, which turns two-week crowns into one-appointment crowns and cuts remakes by more than half.

  5. ’24

    2024

    The family floor and the outreach van

    Priya Raman’s paediatric clinic moves to its own level with a family operatory. We also start funding a school outreach van that sees about 400 children a year who have never been to a dentist.

  6. ’26

    2026

    Nine operatories, same rule

    The studio now employs fourteen people across three floors, with a sedation suite, a laser room and a rooftop planting bed. The rule on the wall by reception has not changed since 2009.

Safety and governance

  • Sterilisation logged per cycle and audited quarterly by an external assessor
  • Sedation delivered only by clinicians on the state sedation register, with a dedicated recovery room
  • Emergency equipment checked every Monday, with drills run twice a term

If something goes wrong

  • Tell Kai Brennan and you will hear from him within one working day, in writing
  • Clinical complaints are reviewed by a dentist from outside the practice, at our cost
  • Guarantee work is done free, including the appointment time, for the stated period
