    Getting here, and getting in

    • By carTwelve free bays behind the building, entered from Willow Lane. Two are wider, marked for wheelchair transfer, and there is a drop-off point directly beside the step-free door.
    • On foot or bikeFour minutes from the Willamette river path; covered bike racks beside the entrance. The building corner has a ramp with a 1:15 gradient and a handrail on both sides.
    • By transitBus 15 and 77 stop at Elm Street and Riverbend Walk, 180 metres away. The Max Green Line at Riverbend station is a seven-minute level walk.
    • InsideReception desk at 90cm with a lowered writing section, a quiet nook for waiting with reduced light and sound, accessible WC, and treatment chairs that lower to 40cm.
    • Tell us you are coming and we will hold the drop-off bay; a coordinator meets you at the door rather than leaving you to find the desk.
    • Assistance dogs are always welcome, with water bowls kept in reception. A relief area is signposted beside the parking bays.
    • Signs in the studio use large, plain lettering, and every appointment card is available in large print or read aloud on request.

    Access and adjustments

    What we have done, and what we will change for you

    This page itself is built to the same standard we ask of our own materials: keyboard-operable, readable at 200 percent zoom, no information carried by color alone, and motion that stops when your system asks it to.

    Physical access

    Step-free entry, a 1:15 ramp with double handrails, wide doorways throughout, an accessible WC with a 150cm turning circle, and four of our six treatment rooms sized for a wheelchair transfer. Hoist access is available in room 4 by arrangement.

    Hearing, sight and speech

    A hearing loop in reception and room 4, a portable loop that comes to any chair, ASL interpreters booked free, written or text-based appointment summaries, and large-print or read-aloud versions of every consent form and invoice.

    Appointments that work

    Shorter, more frequent visits for anyone who finds a long chair time difficult; the first fifteen minutes of an appointment held for travel or anxiety; a quiet waiting nook; home visits within three miles for house-bound patients, twice monthly.

    Something on this site that does not work for you

    Write to access@lumident.studio and name the page. We aim to fix barrier-level problems — a form that cannot be completed by keyboard, text that fails contrast — within ten working days, and we will always offer you the information another way in the meantime. This site is checked with a keyboard, a screen reader, 200 percent zoom, and the reduced-motion setting each time it changes.

    Your information

    Privacy, records and complaints

    What we hold and why

    LumiDent holds your contact details, appointment history, clinical notes, radiographs and intraoral scans, and insurance correspondence. We hold them because treating you safely requires them, and because Oregon requires clinical records to be retained. We do not sell your data, we do not send marketing without a separate opt-in, and the booking form on this site stores nothing until a coordinator opens it.

    How long we keep it

    Adult clinical records are kept for ten years from the last entry, or until age twenty-six for patients treated as children, whichever is longer. Financial records are kept for seven years. Newsletter subscriptions are deleted the moment you unsubscribe — the link is in every issue, and no confirmation step is required.

    Your rights, in practice

    • Ask for a copy of your notes or X-rays and we send them within forty days at no charge — email records@lumident.studio.
    • Ask us to correct something inaccurate and a clinician reviews it with you before the record changes.
    • Ask how your data was shared with an insurer or lab and we will show you the exact correspondence.
    • Withdraw consent for any non-essential contact at any time, without affecting your treatment.

    If something goes wrong

    Tell whoever treated you first — most problems are solved in the chair. Beyond that, complaints go to complaints@lumident.studio and are acknowledged within two working days by a named partner who was not involved in your care. We reply in writing within ten working days with findings, reasons and what we intend to change. If you are still unhappy, you may escalate to the Oregon Board of Dentistry, and we will send you their details with our reply rather than waiting to be asked.

    Who to contact

    Data and records questions: Kai Brennan, practice manager — records@lumident.studio, or write to 418 Riverbend Walk, Portland, OR 97209, marked private and confidential. Oregon license CDS-4417.

    Answered most often

    Practical questions

    How quickly will someone reply to my message?

    Within one working day for anything sent before 4pm, and two at most. Messages sent at the weekend are answered Sunday afternoon for emergencies — call, though, do not rely on email for pain — and Monday morning for everything else.

    Can you send my records to another dentist?

    Yes, the same working day if they email our records address with your signed authorisation. Radiographs and intraoral scans go as full-resolution files, not photographs of a screen, so the receiving clinician is not asked to work from a worse copy than we used.

    Do you take walk-ins?

    We reserve a chair each weekday for urgent problems and will usually see you the same working day if you call before 3pm. For a routine check-up, booking online is faster because you can see the live diary rather than wait for a callback.

    Where exactly is the parking?

    Twelve free bays behind the building, entered from Willow Lane — the second drive past Elm Market. Two wide bays beside the step-free door are marked for wheelchair transfer. If you need the drop-off point held, ring reception on arrival and a coordinator will meet you there.

    Can I bring someone with me?

    Always, and we ask you to. A partner, friend, advocate or family member can sit in on any consultation and any treatment. Our family operatory has two chairs so a parent can sit beside a child, and we will explain everything twice if that is what it takes.
