Before you write in

These four questions are half of everything we receive, and each has a fix you can do without us.

  • Sync stalled? Settings › Accounts › Reconnect takes about twenty seconds.
  • Wrong category? Long-press the transaction, choose Correct, and every future match follows.
  • Missing charge? Pending authorisations appear within an hour of the card tap.
  • Charged twice? Refunds from our side land in three to five business days.

Accessibility and language

Support runs in English and Spanish by text, and we arrange interpretation for phone calls with a day’s notice. Screen-reader users can request replies as plain text — say so in your message and we will switch format.
