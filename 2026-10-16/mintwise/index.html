The Four-Card Method

Four Cards, One Calm Routine

Most money apps hand you a dashboard and wish you luck. Mintwise gives you a deck you work through — connect, sort, plan, protect — until checking your finances takes ninety seconds a week.

Card One

Connect in Ninety Seconds

Link checking, savings, credit, brokerage, and 401(k) accounts through one encrypted handshake. Mintwise reads; it never moves your money.

Card Two

Sort Itself, Correctly

Transactions land in your categories within seconds. Teach it once — “that’s a work lunch, not dining out” — and it remembers forever.

Card Three

Plan the Real Week

Mintwise subtracts upcoming bills from what’s left and prints one number: safe to spend. Adjust it on Friday, not in the new year.

Card Four

Protect the Future

Automate transfers on payday, watch your emergency fund fill, and get a heads-up before a balance runs dry — three days before, not the night of.

Why It Works

Clarity Beats Willpower Every Time

You do not need another budget to feel guilty about. You need one honest number, shown before you swipe. Mintwise builds it from your real balances and your real bills, then refreshes it the moment money moves.

  • Upcoming bills are reserved first. Rent, insurance, and that annual domain renewal are set aside before the number is printed.
  • Irregular costs get smoothed. Holiday spending, car repairs, and dentist visits spread across the year instead of ambushing December.
  • Two incomes, one truth. Partner accounts merge into a shared view while each person keeps a private, judgment-free lane.

Security Is the Product

Built the Way a Bank Should Be

Your accounts connect through read-only credentials held by our aggregation partner — Mintwise never stores your bank password, and we cannot send money anywhere. Here is exactly what protects it.

Encrypted End to End

256-bit AES at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, with keys rotated every 90 days and held in a managed HSM.

Audited by Outsiders

SOC 2 Type II every year, plus quarterly third-party penetration tests. Summaries go to any member who asks.

No Data Brokerage

We charge members, not advertisers. Your transactions are never packaged, scored, or sold to third parties.

Leave With Everything

Export twelve months of transactions as CSV or OFX in two taps, then delete your account. No retention maze.
