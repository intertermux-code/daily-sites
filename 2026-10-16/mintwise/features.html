All 9 features

Live Account Feed

Checking, savings, credit, and brokerage refresh every four hours, or on demand in one tap. Pending charges appear the moment your bank posts them.

Categorisation That Learns

Nine out of ten transactions sort themselves correctly on day one. Retrain a merchant with two taps and Mintwise applies the rule to every future charge.

Safe-to-Spend Number

One figure, recalculated all day: what is genuinely yours to spend before the next payday, after every scheduled bill is set aside.

Bill Calendar

Mintwise finds recurring charges — including the annual ones you forgot — and reserves the money across the weeks leading up to each due date.

Payday Auto-Transfer

Set it once: on the day payroll lands, Mintwise moves your savings target to a separate vault before the money becomes spendable.

Subscription Audit

A single list of every recurring charge, its price, its last increase, and what it costs you each year. Cancel the dead weight in about four minutes.

Overdraft Warning

Three days of runway, not three minutes. Mintwise texts you when a balance is heading under your chosen floor, with the exact gap named.

Impulse Nudge

Add a purchase to a 48-hour waitlist from the lock screen. Most weeks, half of them quietly disappear from the list on their own.

Partner View

Shared goals and a joint calendar, with private lanes each person keeps to themselves. Two phones, one honest picture.

A Tuesday, Start to Finish

What a Day With Mintwise Feels Like

Three moments, roughly forty seconds of attention in total. Everything else happens while you are not looking.

08:12

The Runway Text

“Electric bill $96 leaves Friday. You will sit $34 under your floor unless Monday’s transfer moves.” One tap reschedules it.

13:40

The Checkout Pause

A $240 jacket goes on the waitlist from the lock screen. On Thursday it comes back to you — with the number it would take out of your weekend.

17:05

The Sunday Review

Ninety seconds: last week’s real spend, this week’s committed bills, and the one category that drifted. Adjust, close the app, done.

01 / Tracking

Where It Actually Went

See Ninety Days of Spending Without One Manual Entry

Mintwise pulls your history the moment you connect, splits it into categories you recognise, and shows the total before you have decided how you feel about it.

  • Ninety days of history imported free, two years on Plus.
  • Merchant logos and readable names, not “SQ *JVH 4471”.
  • Split a single charge across two categories when you need to.
  • Search by payee, amount, date, or note in under a second.

02 / Planning

Budgets That Bend

Plans That Survive a Bad Week

A budget that breaks on Thursday is a budget that gets abandoned. Mintwise rebalances the remaining days automatically, so one flat tyre does not cost you the month.

  • Weekly safe-to-spend recalculates after every posted charge.
  • Sinking funds for car repairs, gifts, and the dentist.
  • Variable income mode averages your last six months of pay.
  • Rollover: unspent money moves forward instead of vanishing.

03 / Saving

Automation Over Willpower

Money Moves Before You Can Argue With It

Choose a target and a rhythm. Mintwise handles the transfers, tracks the goal, and keeps the saved money out of your spending balance so it stays saved.

Emergency Fund Tracker

Set three, six, or nine months of essential spending as a target. Watch the bar fill and see the exact date you will get there at your current rate.

Debt Payoff Planner

Compare avalanche against snowball on your real balances, then follow the schedule Mintwise builds — including the month you finish.

04 / Insight

Numbers Worth Reading

Reports That End With a Decision, Not a Chart

Every Mintwise insight arrives with a suggested action and the dollars attached to it. If a report cannot change what you do this week, we do not ship it.

  • Month-over-month drift, per category, in plain sentences.
  • Rate comparisons on savings accounts and credit cards.
  • Fee radar: overdrafts, foreign transaction, and balance fees.
  • Weekly email digest, off by default, one click to enable.

Households

Two Incomes, One Honest Picture

Mintwise lets everyone in the household see the shared numbers without anyone surrendering privacy. Each person keeps a private lane; the joint calendar stays visible to both.

Shared Calendar

Every committed bill, in one column, with who is covering what.

Private Lanes

Personal accounts stay hidden from your partner. No receipts, no lectures.

Goal Splitting

Caregiver Access

Your Data, Still Yours

Read-Only In, Portable Out

Mintwise cannot move money, so a compromised login cannot drain an account. It cannot sell your transactions, so nothing about your spending leaves the building. And it cannot trap your history — export it whenever you like.

  • CSV and OFX export for up to seven years of transactions.
  • Read-only credentials held by our aggregation partner, never by us.
  • Account deletion removes derived profiles within 30 days.
  • Regional storage — pick US, EU, or UK data residency at signup.
