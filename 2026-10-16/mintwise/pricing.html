§ 04 — How We Set These Numbers

Priced From What It Costs Us, Not What You Will Pay

A Mintwise account costs us about $2.40 a month to serve — bank connections, encryption, storage, and support. The rest of the price funds the two things we refuse to do without: keep the reading engine free, and never sell your data.

  • Free is not a trial. It has no expiry and no card, and it keeps working if you cancel a paid plan.
  • Prices move with wages, not investors. We have raised Plus once since 2021, by $1, and announced it three months ahead.
  • Students pay $4 on Plus with a .edu address, and anyone in financial hardship can write to us for a waived year.
  • Your data leaves free. Export everything at any tier — we would rather you stay because the app is useful.

Four Promises We Put in Writing

60-Day Refund

Cancel a paid plan within 60 days of any charge and we return it in full, without asking why.

Price-Change Notice

Any increase lands in your inbox three months before it applies, and your current rate is locked for the rest of that billing term.
