Three Slow-Travel Secrets for Kyoto

1. Embrace Early Morning Zen

Kyoto wakes up softly. Visiting popular temples like Nanzen-ji or Tofuku-ji at 7:00 AM reveals silent gravel paths, chanting monks, and pristine morning light without a single crowd in sight.

2. Stay in a Restored Machiya

Skip high-rise western hotels. Our guests stay in traditional wooden townhouses (machiya) featuring private inner courtyard gardens (tsuboniwa), paper shoji screens, and heated tatami floors.

3. Escape to the Northern Cedar Valleys

Just 45 minutes north of Kyoto city center lie Ohara and Kurama—tranquil cedar forest valleys famous for moss-covered temple gardens and outdoor thermal hot springs (onsen).

Fast Kyoto Facts

  • Ideal Stay: 7–12 Days
  • Best Transport: Walking & E-bike
  • Signature Taste: Uji Matcha & Kaiseki
