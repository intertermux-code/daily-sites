Vol. II Feature index

Four layers, filed in the order you will meet them.

Pulseboard is not a chart library with a login screen. It is a query engine, a registry, a watch layer and a set of controls — each one built so that a number on a page can be defended a year later by someone who never wrote it.

The four layers of the platform

A-01 Query engine A-02 Metric registry A-03 Watch layer A-04 Governance

A-01 · Query engine

Ask a question in plain English and get the same answer your warehouse would give.

The planner compiles each natural-language request into SQL against your registered models, runs it in your warehouse, and returns a governed result — never a guess stitched from a cached screenshot.

  • Compiled SQL is shown beside every answer, so a skeptic can read the exact predicate.
  • Two-pass caching keeps a p95 of 412 ms on boards with 40+ tiles.
  • Unregistered questions are refused and routed to an owner instead of invented.
  • Comparisons, cohort pins and period shifts are all one click, no formula editing.
See it answer a real question

A-02 · Metric registry

One definition, one owner, one place to look it up.

The registry is the heart of the product. Every metric is a versioned entry with SQL, a business description, an accountable owner and a list of every board that depends on it. Change a definition and the dependency graph tells you who needs to hear about it before you press publish.

  • Column-level lineage from raw table to the tile on the exec board.
  • Review history with diffs — read what changed and who signed it off.
  • Near-duplicate detection warns you when “GRR” and “Gross rev” already exist.
  • Time-travel on any figure: read the number as it stood on any past date.
18,400 metrics governed Median 2.1 owners per metric Unlimited revert window

A-03 · Watch layer

Noise is a design failure, not an expected cost of monitoring.

The watch layer learns the shape of each metric — weekday dips, month-end spikes, seasonal troughs — and only interrupts a human when the movement is genuinely unusual. Every alert arrives with the largest contributors already ranked underneath it.

  • Seasonality-aware bands, tuned automatically from 90 days of history.
  • Contribution breakdown attached to the message: region, plan, channel, cohort.
  • Upstream staleness raises a data-quality page before a wrong figure is ever shown.
  • Escalation paths with severity ceilings per recipient and per timezone quiet hours.
91% alert precision 6 min median detection 9 fewer pages per analyst per week

A-04 · Governance

Compliance that is a by-product of building a board, not a separate chore.

Access is granted to roles, not to individual copies of a spreadsheet. Every read is logged, every definition change is attributable, and every board can state on request who saw it and when. That is the difference between a company that can answer an auditor and one that has to reconstruct an answer.

  • Row- and column-level policies inherited from your warehouse grants.
  • SSO, SCIM provisioning and per-region data residency controls.
  • Immutable read log exportable for SOC 2 and internal audit cycles.
  • PII classification prompts before a sensitive column can reach a public board.
