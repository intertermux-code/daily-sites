Our Story

We believe data should be beautiful, accessible, and actionable.

The Beginning

Pulseboard was founded in 2022 by two engineers frustrated with the complexity of legacy BI tools. They wanted a platform that felt less like a database query and more like a conversation with your business.

Today, we serve over 5,000 companies worldwide, helping them make faster decisions with clearer insights.
