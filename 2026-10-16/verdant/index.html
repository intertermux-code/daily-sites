03 The Short List

Seven actives.
Zero decoration.

We publish the percentage of every active in each formula, because a list of names without doses is not an ingredient list — it is a story.

7

Actives across the range

Niacinamide, azelaic acid, retinaldehyde, ceramides, panthenol, zinc PCA, squalane.

0

Fragrance, essential oils, dyes

Nothing here exists to smell nice. Scent is the most common cause of contact allergy.

Days to a visible verdict

One full epidermal turnover. If your skin has not changed by day 28, we want to know.

96%

Reported barrier recovery

Instrument-measured transepidermal water loss in a 64-person use study, twice daily.

04 Where It Comes From

Named farms,
numbered batches

Our matcha comes from one estate in Uji. Our ceramides are fermented in Lyon. Our oats are milled in Jutland from a farm we can drive to in an hour.

Traceability is not a story we tell to justify a price. It is how we hold a batch accountable when a formula behaves differently in February than it did in September — which it will, because plants are not chemical plants.
