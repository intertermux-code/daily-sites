03 Before You Order

Shipping, refills
and the guarantee

Three things people ask us before they buy, answered without asterisks.

01Delivery

Carbon-Neutral, Tracked

Dispatched same day from Copenhagen before 13:00 CET. Europe 1–2 days, UK 2–3, US and Canada 3–5. Free over $75, otherwise $6. Every parcel is paper-taped and offset through PostNord Green.

02Refills

Keep the Glass

Pouch refills cost 20% less and use 71% fewer materials. Order one, pour it into the bottle you already own, and post the empty pouch back in the same envelope — we pay the return.

03Guarantee

28 Days, Either Way

If your skin disagrees with a formula within the first month, write to us and we refund it — opened, half-used, no photograph of your cheek required. Two products a year is the honest average.
