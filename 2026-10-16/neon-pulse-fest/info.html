LOCATION & TRANSIT

NEO-TOKYO CYBER ARENA

Located in the heart of the central district, the Neo-Tokyo Cyber Arena is a state-of-the-art acoustic complex built for high-decibel electronic audio visual shows.

  • Subway Direct: Line 7 (Cyber Express) — Exit Station 09 directly into security check.
  • Shuttle Buses: Free shuttle loops run every 10 minutes from major hotel centers.
  • Rideshare Zones: Dedicated pickup/dropoff bays at Gate B.

FESTIVAL FAQ

Neon Pulse is strictly an 18+ event. You must present valid government-issued photo identification (Passport, Driver’s License, or National Identity Card) at wristband pickup.

Your RFID/NFC wristband acts as your ticket and wallet. You can link your credit/debit card prior to arrival via our web app, or top up with cash or card at top-up stations located throughout the venue.

Prohibited items include: Professional recording gear (detachable lenses), weapons or sharp objects, illegal substances, glass containers, and outside food/beverages (empty re-usable water bottles under 1L are allowed).
