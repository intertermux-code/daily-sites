02 / ORIGIN

Rooted in Nature

Verdant began in a small laboratory in Portland, driven by a simple question: Why does skincare have to be so complicated?

The Problem

The modern beauty industry is saturated with products promising miracles but delivering irritation. Consumers are overwhelmed by 10-step routines, confusing ingredient lists, and marketing hype that obscures truth.

The Solution

We stripped everything back. No fillers. No fragrance. No fluff. Just high-performance botanicals and clinically proven actives, formulated at effective concentrations. Each product serves a distinct purpose in a streamlined routine.

Key Ingredients

01

Niacinamide

Vitamin B3. Reduces inflammation, minimizes pores, and strengthens the skin barrier.

02

Hyaluronic Acid

A powerful humectant that holds up to 1000x its weight in water, providing deep hydration.

03

Centella Asiatica

Also known as Cica. Soothes redness and accelerates healing for sensitive skin types.

04

Bakuchiol

A plant-based alternative to retinol. Stimulates collagen production without irritation.

Sustainability Commitment

We use recycled glass bottles and aluminum tubes. Our formulas are biodegradable. We offset 100% of our carbon emissions through verified reforestation projects.
