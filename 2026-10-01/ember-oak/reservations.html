Reserve your table

We release tables thirty days in advance. For parties larger than eight, private dining, or dietary accommodations, please call us directly at (503) 555-0142.

The things to know

A handful of house notes to help your evening run smoothly.

Hours Tuesday through Sunday, 5pm to close. The kitchen stops seating an hour before.
Dress Smart casual. No dress code, please — just come as you'd like to be seen.
Party Up to eight online. For larger groups, call us for the private dining room.
Parking Valet available Friday and Saturday. Street parking on NW Everett, two-hour limit.
Dietary We accommodate most restrictions — note them when booking and we'll plan ahead.
Cancel Please cancel by noon the day of. A $25 per-person fee applies to no-shows.

You'll receive a confirmation email within minutes. No card required.
