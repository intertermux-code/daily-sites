The Hearth

Twelve years over one open flame

We built Ember & Oak around a single premise: that the most honest food is cooked over live fire. Our hearth — fed with cordwood from the Willamette Valley — burns at 750°F and never goes out, not even between services.

Every cut of beef is dry-aged in-house for twenty-one days. Every vegetable is sourced within a hundred miles. Every plate leaves the pass with a trace of smoke.
