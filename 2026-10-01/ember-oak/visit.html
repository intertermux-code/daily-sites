Parking & Transit

Valet Service — Available Friday and Saturday from 5:30 pm. $15 per vehicle, paid at the host stand.

Street Parking — Metered parking on NW Everett and 11th Avenue. Two-hour limit until 8 pm; free after.

Lots Nearby — The Pearl District Garage at NW 10th & Glisan is a two-minute walk. Flat rate $10 after 6 pm.

Streetcar — NW 10th & Glisan stop, 90-second walk. The NS Line runs every fifteen minutes.

Good to Know

Accessibility — Step-free entry, accessible restroom, table-height adjustments available on request.

Dogs — Not permitted indoors, but our sidewalk tables welcome well-behaved pups on warm evenings.

Private Events — The Fireside Room seats 28 for private dining. Contact events@emberandoak.com.

Gift Cards — Digital and physical gift cards available at the host stand or by phone.
