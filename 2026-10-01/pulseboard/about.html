/ ABOUT

Built by the on-call.
For the on-call.

Pulseboard started in 2021 in a cramped SRE bullpen where three engineers had twelve dashboards open and still couldn't answer the question their VP was asking. We decided to build the console we actually wanted to look at at 3 a.m.

/ ORIGIN

A console we could be proud to page ourselves with.

Three of us were on-call for a payments platform serving 11 million users. The tools we had were powerful, but they spoke different dialects. Logs over here, traces over there, metrics somewhere else. We spent more time toggling tabs than diagnosing systems.

So we started building. A side-project became a rewrite. A rewrite became a product. In 2023 we left our jobs to do it full time, raised a seed round from a handful of SREs who had been customers of our previous lives, and have shipped weekly ever since.

/ BELIEFS

Four things we hold.

01

Tools should feel like flight decks.

An engineer at 3 a.m. shouldn't need to read the manual. The console should surface the answer before they finish asking the question.

02

Alerts are literature, not spam.

Every alert that fires is a message from the system to a human being. It deserves to be written, edited, and revised like any other piece of writing.

03

SLOs are promises, not dashboards.

The metric that matters is the one your customers hold you to. Everything else is a means to that end.

04

Keyboard first, mouse as a fallback.

The fastest engineers we know rarely touch a mouse. We design for them — and everyone else learns to fly.

Flight log.

  2. 2022 · Q2
