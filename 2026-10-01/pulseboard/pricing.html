/ QUESTIONS

Frequently asked.

Do you charge by the seat?

No. Pricing is based on the volume of telemetry you ingest. Users, viewers, dashboards, and alerts are unlimited on every plan — we don't believe in punishing a team for getting curious.

Can I migrate from Datadog, New Relic, or Grafana Cloud?

Yes, and we'll do it for you. We ship a migration kit that translates your dashboards, monitors, and alerting rules into Pulseboard equivalents — usually within a single afternoon.

Where does my data live?

Scout and Mission default to our US region. Mission customers can opt into EU residency on request. Orbit supports US, EU, and APAC with full data residency and private-link connectivity.

What about compliance?

All Orbit contracts are SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and HIPAA-eligible. Scout and Mission inherit SOC 2 Type II controls. We publish our latest report on request under NDA.

Can I cancel any time?

Monthly plans are cancellable at any billing boundary. Annual contracts are paid upfront but include a 30-day money-back window for any reason.

Still deciding?

Bring a real incident to a 30-minute console tour. No pitch deck.
