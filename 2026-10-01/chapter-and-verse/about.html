About Chapter & Verse

Our story began in 1987 with a simple passion for books and community

Our Journey

In 1987, Margaret and Robert Chen opened Chapter & Verse with nothing more than a love for literature and a vision of bringing their community together through books. What started as a small shop in a converted Victorian house has grown into a beloved local institution that continues to serve book lovers of all ages.

Over the decades, we've weathered economic downturns, the rise of digital media, and changing reading habits. Through it all, our commitment to fostering a love of reading and supporting both established and emerging authors has remained constant.

Today, we're proud to offer over 12,000 titles spanning all genres, host more than 250 events annually, and maintain the warm, welcoming atmosphere that has made us a cornerstone of the literary community for nearly four decades.

Our Mission

We believe in the transformative power of books to connect people, expand minds, and create empathy. At Chapter & Verse, we're committed to:

  • Providing access to diverse voices and perspectives across all genres
  • Supporting local and emerging authors through our event series
  • Fostering a sense of community among readers of all ages
  • Maintaining the tradition of the independent bookstore as a cultural hub
