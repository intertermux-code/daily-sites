About Lenscraft

Capturing life’s most meaningful moments with intention and artistry

The Story Behind the Lens

My journey as a wedding photographer began in Kyoto, Japan, where I spent years studying traditional ink painting. The practice taught me the profound beauty of ma—the art of negative space—and how the most powerful expressions often emerge from what isn’t said.

This philosophy shapes every photograph I take. Rather than orchestrating moments, I observe and anticipate them. I wait for the pause between laughter, the gentle touch that follows a tear, the quiet glance exchanged when no one else is looking.

Over the past decade, I’ve had the privilege of documenting couples from diverse backgrounds, each bringing their own traditions and stories. My camera becomes a silent witness, preserving these authentic fragments of joy, vulnerability, and connection.

Our Approach

Quiet Observation

I blend into your celebration, allowing natural moments to unfold without interruption. My presence is felt only in the quality of the images captured.

Artistic Vision

Each frame is composed with attention to light, shadow, and composition, creating heirloom-quality images that transcend trends.

Emotional Depth

I seek out the genuine emotions that define your relationship—the micro-expressions and gestures that speak volumes about your bond.
