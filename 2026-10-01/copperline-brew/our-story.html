Our Story

From humble beginnings to a beloved Portland institution, discover the journey that shaped Copperline Brewing Co.

The Beginning

In 2010, childhood friends Jake Morrison and Sarah Chen turned their shared passion for craft brewing into reality. What started as weekend homebrew experiments in Jake’s garage evolved into something much bigger when they discovered a century-old warehouse space in Southeast Portland.

With a loan from Sarah’s grandmother and countless hours of elbow grease, they transformed the industrial space into a brewing facility. The centerpiece was a vintage German copper brewing system they acquired from a defunct brewery in Munich—giving birth to the name “Copperline” and the distinctive character of their beers.

Growth & Recognition

Word spread quickly about their commitment to traditional brewing methods and innovative recipes. By 2015, Copperline had won gold at the Great American Beer Festival for their Rustic IPA, followed by numerous accolades for their diverse lineup.

Today, we’ve expanded our taproom to include a full kitchen featuring locally-sourced pub fare that pairs perfectly with our brews. Our commitment to sustainability includes solar panels on the roof, water recycling systems, and partnerships with regional farms for spent grain composting.

Our Values

Quality

We use only the finest ingredients sourced from trusted suppliers, maintaining rigorous quality standards throughout our brewing process.

Community

We’re proud to support local artists, musicians, and community organizations through events and partnerships.

Tradition

While we embrace innovation, we honor time-tested brewing techniques that have defined great beer for centuries.

Sustainability

We operate with environmental responsibility, minimizing waste and supporting eco-friendly practices.
