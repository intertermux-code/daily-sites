What to Expect

Our taproom combines industrial charm with warm hospitality. Exposed brick walls, reclaimed wood tables, and our gleaming copper brewing equipment create an authentic brewery atmosphere. Whether you're stopping by for a quick pint after work or settling in for the evening, you'll find a welcoming space to enjoy our craft beers.

Take a tour of our brewing facility during weekend afternoons (2pm-4pm) to see how our beer comes to life. Tours are free and include a tasting of three seasonal selections.
