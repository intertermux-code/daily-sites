Our Beers

Discover our handcrafted selection of ales and lagers, brewed with traditional techniques and premium ingredients.

Rustic IPA

ABV: 6.8% IBU: 65 Style: American IPA

A bold and hoppy ale with citrus and pine notes, balanced by a firm malt backbone. Perfect for those who enjoy intense flavors.

Copper Lager

ABV: 4.9% IBU: 18 Style: Vienna Lager

A smooth and refreshing lager with a rich amber color and malty sweetness. Brewed with German hops for a clean finish.

Mountain Stout

ABV: 7.2% IBU: 45 Style: Irish Stout

A rich and creamy stout with notes of coffee, chocolate, and roasted barley. Smooth and full-bodied with a velvety texture.

Trail Pale

ABV: 5.4% IBU: 35 Style: American Pale Ale

A well-balanced pale ale with moderate hop bitterness and hints of caramel malt. Easy-drinking with a crisp finish.

Amber Ale

ABV: 5.8% IBU: 40 Style: American Amber Ale

A medium-bodied ale with a rich amber color and balanced malt sweetness. Features notes of toasted nuts and caramel.

Hazy Wheat

ABV: 4.7% IBU: 12 Style: Hefeweizen

A cloudy wheat beer with banana and clove notes from German yeast. Light and refreshing with a smooth mouthfeel.
