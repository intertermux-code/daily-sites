Questions

Frequently Asked

Everything we get asked before someone walks through the door for the first time.

Do I need experience?

No. Our onboarding program is designed for complete beginners. Kai, our junior coach, will walk you through every movement pattern before you touch a loaded barbell.

What should I bring?

Flat-soled shoes (Converse, Vans, or weightlifting shoes), a water bottle, and a towel. Everything else—from chalk to belts to straps—is provided.

How does billing work?

Month-to-month. We charge on the same date each month. Cancel before your next billing date and you owe nothing more. No annual fees, no initiation fees, no hidden charges.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes. You can freeze for up to 3 months per year for injury, travel, or any other reason. Freezing pauses your billing and holds your spot.
