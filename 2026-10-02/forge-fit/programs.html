Training Tracks

Three Programs. One Standard.

Every program at Forge is periodized, coached, and measured. Choose the track that matches your objective—or combine them. Most of our members do.

Track 01

Powerlifting

Structured barbell training built around the three competition lifts: squat, bench press, and deadlift. Our programming follows proven periodization models—accumulation, intensification, realization, deload—calibrated to your current strength level and meet timeline.

You will train with calibrated plates on IPF-spec bars. You will video your sets and review them with a coach. You will log every rep, every set, every RPE. Numbers do not lie—neither do we.

4× Sessions / Week
90min Session Length
12wk Block Cycle

Track 02

HIIT & Conditioning

High-intensity metabolic conditioning designed to build the engine that carries everything else in your life. Work-to-rest ratios are precise, equipment is rotation-based, and every session is coached—not just supervised.

You will push sleds, row on Concept2 ergometers, assault bike through intervals, and move through bodyweight circuits that leave you questioning every shortcut you ever took. This is not cardio. This is conditioning.

Track 03

Mobility & Recovery

You cannot build strength on a body that will not move through full range. Our mobility program combines functional movement screening, joint mobilization techniques, and active recovery protocols designed to restore what heavy training demands.

Sessions include banded distraction work, 90/90 hip drills, thoracic extensions on foam rollers, and loaded stretching progressions. The recovery suite—cold plunge at 4°C, infrared sauna, Normatec compression boots—completes the protocol.

60min Session Length
8wk Block Cycle
