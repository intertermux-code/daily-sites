Every protocol at Forge is a closed-loop system: input (you, today's readiness), process (the session), output (adaptation). Pick the discipline that matches your objective, or rotate through all three.

[P.01] HEAVY.IRON

POWERLIFTING

Raw strength, calibrated. Squat · Bench · Deadlift.

Our powerlifting program is built on a conjugate-meets-block-periodization model. Each 12-week cycle builds toward a 1RM test or a local USAPL-sanctioned meet. You'll lift heavy, you'll lift often, and you'll leave with data — not just fatigue.

Four sessions per week, each built around one primary movement with secondary and accessory work calibrated to your cycle phase.

  • MONSquat Focus + Lower Accessory
  • WEDBench Focus + Upper Accessory
  • FRIDeadlift Focus + Posterior Chain
  • SATTeam Session / Heavy Doubles

Sessions run 75 minutes including warm-up protocol (10 min), working sets (50 min), and cooldown/mobility primer (15 min).

We run a 12-week wave periodization. Weeks 1–4 accumulate volume at RPE 6–7. Weeks 5–8 intensify to RPE 8. Weeks 9–11 peak with heavy doubles and singles at RPE 9. Week 12 is deload + test.

Every set is logged in the Forge app. Your coach reviews weekly. If the bar speed drops, we autoregulate — no hero reps.

[P.02] HIGH.OUTPUT

HIIT ENGINE

Metabolic conditioning at 90%+ heart-rate reserve.

45 minutes. 12 stations. Heart-rate monitors on every wrist. HIIT Engine is built for athletes who want conditioning that matches their strength work — or for anyone who wants to leave the gym wrecked in under an hour.

Three rotating formats across the week:

  • HIIT.ATabata-style · 20s on / 10s off × 8 per station
  • HIIT.BEMOM · Every Minute On the Minute intervals
  • HIIT.CAMRAP · As Many Rounds As Possible, 15-min blocks

Equipment rotates between assault bikes, Concept2 rowers, ski ergs, sleds, and kettlebells. No two classes are identical.

Every session is scored. Calories burned, watts sustained, heart-rate zones hit. Your output is displayed on the floor's live telemetry board after class — anonymous, but visible to the room.

Top performers earn "Engine Tags" — small volt-yellow patches on your membership card. Collect three and you unlock the Saturday Metcon session.

  • Cross-training shoes (flat heel, lateral support — no running shoes)
  • Heart-rate monitor (Polar OH1 or Whoop recommended; chest strap optional)
  • Water bottle (we have a refill station)
  • Towel (we supply if you forget)

Heart-rate monitors can be rented at the front desk for $5/session if you don't have your own yet.

[P.03] RANGE.SYS

MOBILITY LAB

Joint-by-joint reconstruction. Unlock the limits holding your lifts back.

Mobility Lab is built on the joint-by-joint approach: each joint has a primary need (stability or mobility), and we work them in sequence. Ankle mobility unlocks knee stability unlocks hip mobility unlocks lumbar stability — you get the idea.

  • ANKLEDorsiflexion · Tibial IR/ER · Loaded stretch
  • HIP90/90 · CARs · PAILs/RAILs protocol
  • T-SPINEExtension · Rotation · Overhead position
  • SHOULDERScapular control · External rotation · Sleeper stretch

Each Monday-to-Friday rotation focuses on one joint with 60 minutes of dedicated work. Saturday is a full-body flow session that integrates everything.

We use Functional Range Conditioning (FRC) tools: Controlled Articular Rotations (CARs) daily, Progressive Angular Incremental Loading (PAILs) and Regressive Angular Incremental Loading (RAILs) twice per week per joint.

Your coach will baseline your ranges of motion in week one. Retest at week 4 and week 8. Expect 10–20° gains in hip and t-spine rotation.

Everyone. Literally everyone. If you lift heavy, you need this. If you sit at a desk, you need this. If you're over 35, you need this.

Mobility Lab is our most attended program because it's the one that fixes the thing that's been bugging you for three years. Come once. You'll be back.
