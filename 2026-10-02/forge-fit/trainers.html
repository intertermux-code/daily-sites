[COACHING.PHILOSOPHY]

THE FORGE METHOD

[01]

PLATFORM FIRST

We don't hire coaches who've never lifted competitively. Every coach on our staff has competed at the regional level or above. You learn from someone who's stood under the bar.

[02]

DATA, NOT OPINION

Bar speed. Heart rate zones. Range-of-motion baselines. We make decisions on measurements, not vibes. Your training log is our shared source of truth.

[03]

HONEST CUEING

We don't inflate. We don't soft-pedal. If your squat is ugly, you'll hear it. If it's a PR, you'll hear that too. The relationship works because the feedback is real.

[04]

LONG-TERM ATHLETES

We don't chase short-term PRs at the cost of long-term health. Autoregulation, deload weeks, and mobility work aren't optional — they're load-bearing parts of the program.
