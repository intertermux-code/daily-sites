READ BEFORE BOOKING

[01]

ARRIVE EARLY

Sessions start on the dot. Arrive 10 minutes early to warm up, sign in, and claim your station. Late arrivals forfeit their spot after 5 minutes.

[02]

CANCEL POLICY

Cancel at least 4 hours before class or you'll receive a late-cancel strike. Three strikes in 30 days = 7-day booking freeze. Life happens — just give us the heads-up.

[03]

CAPACITY

HIIT classes cap at 12. Powerlifting caps at 8 (two per rack). Mobility Lab caps at 16. When a class shows FULL, join the waitlist — 38% of no-shows are released 1 hour before start.

[04]

SUBSTITUTIONS

Coaches substitute for illness or emergency only. If your coach is out, the session still runs with a qualified substitute — no refunds, no reschedules.
