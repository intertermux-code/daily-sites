About the Index✦

Each dispatch is tagged by its author and reviewed by a desk editor. We use a fixed taxonomy of 23 topics, revised annually. A dispatch may carry up to three tags; the first tag is the primary category for indexing.

Every dispatch published since October 2014 is in the archive, except for 21 entries withdrawn at the authors’ request. We publish a full list of withdrawals, with reasons, in our annual corrections report.

We accept pitches year-round. Pitches should be 250 words or fewer, include at least two original sources, and specify the intended desk. We respond within five working days.
