About Signal

Independent journalism at the intersection of technology, society, and innovation

Our Mission

Signal was founded in 2020 with a simple premise: technology journalism needs independence. In an era of corporate consolidation and click-driven content, we provide thoughtful analysis of the technologies shaping our world. Our reporting goes beyond product announcements to examine the broader implications of technological change.

Editorial Ethics

Signal maintains strict editorial independence. We do not accept advertising, sponsorships, or paid content. Our funding comes solely from subscriptions and reader support, ensuring our reporting remains free from commercial influence. We adhere to rigorous fact-checking standards and provide transparent sourcing for all claims.

Our Principles

  • Independence from corporate interests
  • Rigorous fact-checking and verification
  • Transparency in methodology and sources
  • Balanced coverage of both benefits and risks
  • Accessibility and clarity in technical communication
