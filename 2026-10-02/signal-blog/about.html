About the Publication

The Masthead

Signal is a reader-supported, editor-run, engineer-informed publication. We publish quarterly in print and daily on the wire. We have no investors, no conferences, and no sponsored content.

What We Do

We practice slow, sourced, technically literate journalism about the machine age. Every dispatch is written by someone who can read the code it discusses, edited by someone who can read the reporting behind it, and published only when both are satisfied. We do not cover product launches. We do not take press trips. We do not publish under pseudonyms, except for one contributor whose identity is protected by legal agreement, and whose column appears on the last Tuesday of every month.

What We Don’t

We do not accept display advertising. We do not sell subscriptions to enterprise customers in bulk. We do not syndicate our dispatches to aggregators. We do not run content labeled as “opinion” — our editors and correspondents publish under their own names, and the publication’s view is expressed only in editorial notes, signed by the full board.

Our Principles✦

  1. Sources, not quotes.

    Every dispatch names at least two named sources. Anonymous sourcing requires Editor-in-Chief approval and is used only when the source would face retaliation for speaking on the record. We publish the reasoning in a sidebar.

  2. Code as evidence.

    Where a dispatch depends on a technical claim, the author must either demonstrate the claim in code or cite a peer-reviewed source. Unverifiable technical claims are not published, however well-sourced the surrounding reporting.

  3. Corrections on the same page.

    Corrections are appended to the original dispatch with a dated note. We do not silently edit. The corrections feed is available via RSS.

  4. No sponsored content.

    We do not publish sponsored posts, branded content, or native advertising. We do not allow any external party to review a dispatch before publication, except for legal review on specific liability questions.

  5. Financial disclosure.

    Every author discloses, at the foot of every dispatch, any financial interest in the subject that exceeds $5,000 or any advisory role within the last three years. Disclosures are reviewed by the Managing Editor.

Ownership✦

Signal is owned in its entirety by the Signal Editorial Trust, a 501(c)(3) not-for-profit incorporated in Delaware. The Trust is governed by a seven-member board, of which four are current or former editors. The Trust may not sell, merge, or take investment without a unanimous vote of the board and the approval of a simple majority of active contributors.

The most recent audited financials, for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2026, are published at signal.press/financials.
