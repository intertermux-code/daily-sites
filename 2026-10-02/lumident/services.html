Chapter 01

Implants & Restorative.

A single-tooth implant, from first scan to final crown, takes between 8 and 16 weeks depending on bone density. We use Straumann® BLX posts — the same ones placed in 40,000 Swiss clinics — and in-house milled zirconia crowns.

Every implant comes with a ten-year structural guarantee, documented in your patient file and mailed to you on paper.

It covers structural failure of the post and abutment — osseointegration loss, fracture, corrosion. It does not cover crown wear, gum recession from smoking, or damage from trauma. Replacement under guarantee is free, including the surgical visit.

You get a temporary crown the same day. The final crown is seated once osseointegration is confirmed — typically 8 to 12 weeks after surgery. Until then, you can chew on the opposite side normally.

Yes. We file directly with Helvetia, CSS, Sanitas, Swica, and Groupe Mutuel. You pay the out-of-pocket portion at the chair; the insurer pays us directly within 14 days.

Chapter 02

Orthodontics, invisible.

We print aligners in-house on a SprintRay Pro 3D printer. That means the scan, the treatment plan, and the first trays all happen before you leave the clinic. No waiting two weeks for a lab in another country.

Every case gets a digital mock-up of the final smile. If you don't like what you see, we adjust before anything touches your teeth.

For about 72 hours. The tongue adapts to the new surface quickly, and the trays are 0.75mm — thin enough that most patients report no noticeable lisp after the third day.

One replacement per case is included. Additional replacements are €45 each and printed the same day. The app keeps a full log of which tray you should be on.

Chapter 03

Cosmetic, on your terms.

Cosmetic dentistry is subjective. We treat it accordingly. Before any veneer, bonding, or whitening, we build a digital mock-up of your smile — and you approve it, in writing, before the first prep.

No upsells. No pressure. If the shade you want won't work with your natural tooth structure, we say so. Once.
