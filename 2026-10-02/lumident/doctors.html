Section 04 · The philosophy

No rotation.
No substitutes.

You see the same doctor, every visit. If that doctor is sick, we reschedule. We don't substitute.

Why it matters.

Continuity of care is the single strongest predictor of dental outcomes. A doctor who knows your bite, your anxieties, and your history makes better decisions — and catches problems earlier. Rotating doctors miss patterns. We don't rotate.

What you actually get.

  • A dedicated doctor, assigned at first booking.
  • Full case history, never re-told to a stranger.
  • Direct contact with your doctor between visits.
  • One signed plan, one signature, one responsible person.
