Getting here

By tram, by car,
by foot.

We're four minutes' walk from Cornavin station, on a side street off Rue de la Servette. If you're driving, the underground garage has reserved bays for patients.

  • Tram 14, 15, 18 — stop "Dent", 90 m walk.
  • Free underground parking, 90 min — code LUMI at the gate.
  • Bike racks at the entrance, covered.
  • Wheelchair accessible throughout; lift to all floors.
