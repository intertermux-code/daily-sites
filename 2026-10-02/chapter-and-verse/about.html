Our Story

Three decades of fostering literary connections

Founded in 1987

In the autumn of 1987, Margaret and James Thompson opened Chapter & Verse with a simple vision: to create a sanctuary for book lovers in the heart of our community. What began as a modest 800-square-foot shop with 3,000 carefully selected titles has grown into the region's premier independent bookstore.

Our original mission remains unchanged: to connect readers with stories that inspire, challenge, and transport them. We believe that books have the power to build bridges between diverse perspectives and create lasting bonds within communities.

Our Values

  • Community First: We host over 100 literary events annually, from author readings to children's story time, believing that books bring people together.
  • Curation Over Quantity: Every title on our shelves has been personally reviewed by our knowledgeable staff, ensuring quality recommendations for every reader.
  • Supporting Authors: We actively promote both emerging and established writers, providing a platform for voices that might otherwise go unheard.
  • Environmental Responsibility: Through our recycling program and partnerships with sustainable publishers, we're committed to protecting the planet for future generations of readers.
