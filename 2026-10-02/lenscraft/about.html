About Sarah Johnson

Your Story, My Passion

With over eight years of experience capturing life's most precious moments, I specialize in wedding photography that tells the authentic story of your special day. My approach combines artistic vision with documentary storytelling to create images that feel both timeless and deeply personal.

Based in Portland, Oregon, I've had the privilege of photographing weddings across the Pacific Northwest and beyond. Each celebration brings its own unique energy and narrative, and I'm committed to preserving those fleeting moments that you'll treasure for generations.

My philosophy centers on unobtrusive documentation – I blend into your day to capture genuine emotions and spontaneous interactions. From the nervous excitement before the ceremony to the joyous celebration under the stars, I ensure every meaningful detail is preserved.

When I'm not behind the camera at a wedding, you'll find me exploring the Oregon coast with my own camera, seeking inspiration in nature's ever-changing light and landscapes. This continuous exploration keeps my creative perspective fresh and helps me discover new ways to capture the beauty in everyday moments.

My Approach
