§03 · Venue Brief

THE RELAY

Logistics, coordinates, and the field manual for your three nights in the Mojave.

[03.A] Atlas-7 site. [†] 420 acres. [‡] Elevation 2,840 ft.
§03.A — The Site

Mojave Relay 7

Sited on the decommissioned Atlas-7 orbital relay station, 40 miles northeast of Los Angeles. The main stage — The Dish — is a repurposed 280-foot satellite antenna. Two smaller stages, Orbit and Nexus, occupy the old coolant towers.

Desert temperatures in late December average 64°F during the day and 38°F at night. Pack layers. The sun sets at 16:48 on Dec 18. Sunrise is 06:51.

[03.B] Four routes. [†] No on-site parking.
§03.B — Getting There

Four Routes to the Relay

01

Shuttle — LA Union Station

Direct shuttle departing 14:00 and 18:00 daily. 90-minute journey. Included with Standard and Chrome passes. Boarding requires pass + photo ID.

02

Rail — Palmdale Station

Amtrak Pacific Surfliner to Palmdale. A dedicated Pulse shuttle runs every 30 minutes from the Palmdale station to the gates, 16:00–02:00.

03

Rideshare Drop-Off

Dedicated drop-off lane on Relay Road. A half-mile walk to the main gate. Return rides must be booked in advance through the Pulse app.

04

On Foot — Campgrounds

Campers arriving on foot check in at the south gate. Campground access opens at noon on Dec 17. Showers and drinking water available 24/7.

[03.C] Field manual. [†] Pack layers.
§03.C — Field Manual

What to Bring. What to Leave.

Bring

  • Photo ID matching your pass name
  • Refillable water bottle (stations on site)
  • Layers — temperatures drop 26°F after sunset
  • Sealed sunscreen and lip balm
  • Ear protection (free foam plugs at every stage)
  • Cash — some vendors are card-only, not all

Leave

  • Outside alcohol and glass containers
  • Drones — grounded on sight
  • Professional cameras (no detachable lenses)
  • Weapons of any kind
  • Pets — no kennel facilities on site
  • Unreasonable expectations of cell service
[03.D] FAQ. [†] Updated weekly.
§03.D — Transmissions Asked Often

Field Questions

Passes are non-transferable and linked to your photo ID. Report lost passes immediately to signal@neonpulse.fm. A replacement can be issued at the main gate for a $40 reissue fee. Stolen passes cannot be reissued.

Coverage is unreliable. Three dedicated Wi-Fi hotspots operate at the main gate, medical tent, and Chrome lounge. Assume you will be offline for the duration of your stay — plan accordingly.

Neon Pulse is an 18+ event. Valid government-issued photo ID is required at the gate. Attendees under 18 will not be admitted under any circumstances, and tickets will not be refunded.

Thirty-two food vendors operate across the site, open from 12:00 until 02:00 nightly. Options include vegan, halal, gluten-free, and kosher. A Chrome lounge offers a seated, curated menu from 18:00–00:00. Outside food is permitted, outside alcohol is not.

The Mojave averages 0.3 inches of rain in December. All stages are rain-or-shine. In the event of severe weather, sets may be paused — announcements will be made from every stage and via the Pulse app. No refunds are issued for weather delays.
