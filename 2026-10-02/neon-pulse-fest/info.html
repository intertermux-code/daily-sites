/03.1 THE STATION

The Meridian Power Station was built in 1934 to feed the south London grid. Decommissioned in 1989, its turbine hall is 180 metres long, 42 metres wide, and 28 metres high — a natural acoustic chamber for sub-frequencies.

/03.2 GETTING IN

◈

BY TUBE

North Greenwich (Jubilee line) → 12-minute walk. Trains every 3 minutes from Central London. Night Tube runs all weekend.

◉

BY SHUTTLE

Our fleet of articulated buses loops London Bridge, Canary Wharf, and Stratford every 20 minutes. Schedule drops 10 days before.

◇

BY CAR

Limited on-site parking (pre-book only, £45/day). Off-site partner lots at Greenwich Peninsula with free shuttle every 10 minutes.

◎

BY BIKE

Secure, guarded cycle compound for 800 bikes. Free to use with any ticket. Bring your own lock for peace of mind.

/03.3 INVENTORY

✓ PACK

  • Valid photo ID (passport / driving licence)
  • Reusable water bottle (free refill stations)
  • Closed-toe shoes (concrete floors, 72 hours)
  • Ear plugs — high-fidelity recommended
  • Warm layers (site averages 8°C at night)
  • Portable battery pack (no power at stages)
  • Sealed pack of cigarettes (if you smoke — designated zones only)

✕ LEAVE BEHIND

  • Professional cameras (lenses over 50mm)
  • Glass of any kind
  • Selfie sticks, tripods, gimbals
  • Drones or any aerial device
  • Laser pointers or handheld strobes
  • Illegal substances (amnesty bins at entry)
  • Outside alcohol (confiscated)

/03.4 QUERY LOG

General Admission is single-entry per day — if you leave the perimeter, you cannot re-enter until the next gate window at 16:00. VIP and Platinum tiers have unrestricted re-entry via the dedicated Turbine gates.

Mainframe peaks at 118 dB(A). Subsonic and Voltage run around 108 dB(A). Glitch sits at 96 dB(A). Ear plugs are strongly recommended for every stage. High-fidelity pairs are available at the merch booth or can be added to your ticket at checkout.

Yes. All four stages have level access, dedicated viewing platforms, and accessible toilets within 50 metres. We run a personal assistant scheme — apply via our access form 30 days before the event. BSL-interpreted safety briefings run hourly.

The transmission continues. We maintain a standby roster of 12 substitute acts across all genres. If a headliner drops, a replacement of equivalent tier will fill the slot. No refunds are issued for individual artist changes — tickets are for the event, not specific performers.

Phone photography is welcome. Detachable lenses, tripods, selfie sticks, and any dedicated recording device are confiscated at the gate. Some artists enforce a "no recording" policy on their sets — respect the request. Subsonic is a strict no-cameras stage.

On-site camping is available as an add-on (see tickets). There are no sleep pods or hotels on the grounds. Partner hotels in Greenwich and Canary Wharf offer festival rates — link in the confirmation email.

Neon Pulse operates a zero-tolerance policy on harassment of any kind. Trained welfare staff wear magenta armbands and are stationed at every stage, plus the central welfare tent. If you feel unsafe, find one of them or approach any bar. Anonymous reporting is available via the app.
