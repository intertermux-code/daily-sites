§01.B — Stage Brief

Three Frequencies

Each stage tuned to a distinct harmonic of the transmission.

Orbit

Capacity: 8,000 · Ambient / House / Deep

Housed in the station's primary observatory dome. The sound system — a custom 180,000-watt Funktion-One — is tuned for low-frequency warmth and crystalline highs. Ideal for sunset sessions and sunrise codas.

Void

Capacity: 5,500 · Techno / Hard / Noise

Built in the decommissioned coolant tower. Dark, dense, and relentless. Home to the festival's most punishing four-to-the-floor. The walls sweat.

Nexus

Capacity: 6,500 · Live / Electro / Synth

The main stage, built around the Atlas-7 satellite dish itself. Lives projected directly onto the dish's 280-foot parabolic surface.
