/02.2 PROTOCOL

REFUNDS

Full refund available up to 30 days before transmission (Nov 18, 2026). 50% refund within 30–14 days. No refund within final 14 days. Transfers between tiers available for a £15 admin fee.

AGE RESTRICTION

Strictly 18+. A valid government-issued photo ID will be scanned at the gate. Anyone under 18 will be denied entry without refund.

PROHIBITED

Cameras with detachable lenses. Glass. Pyrotechnics. Drones. Laser pointers. Any recording device beyond a phone. Full list on arrival.

SECURE PAYMENT

All transactions run through Stripe. We never store card numbers on our servers. 3D Secure verified. Apple Pay and Google Pay accepted.
