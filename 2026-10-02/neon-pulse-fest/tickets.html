§02.C — Purchase Brief

Before You Confirm

01 — Quantity

Maximum four passes per household. Passes are non-transferable; the name on the pass must match the photo ID presented at the gate.

02 — Hold

Once added to cart, your pass is held for ten minutes. If you do not complete checkout, the pass returns to the pool.

03 — Refunds

Full refunds available until November 1, 2026. After that date, passes are final sale. A resale list opens December 1.

04 — Delivery

Passes are delivered digitally via the Neon Pulse app beginning December 10. Paper commemorative passes are available for Chrome VIP holders at gate check-in.
