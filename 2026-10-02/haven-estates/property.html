The Residence

Perched above Padaro Lane, Villa Serene is one of only eleven residences commissioned from Wallace Neff in his late-career period. The home has been in a single family since 1981 and underwent a meticulous two-year restoration completed in 2023 — led by architect Peter Becker of Montecito, with interiors by Los Angeles-based Atelier Noir.

The original terrazzo floors, rift-sawn oak paneling, and brass hardware have been preserved. New systems include a 22kW solar array, radiant heating throughout, and a whole-home Lutron system. The south-facing garden has been replanted with 140+ heritage specimens sourced from the original nursery records.

The primary suite occupies the east wing, with a private walled courtyard, a soaking room with freestanding concrete tub, and a dressing pavilion. A detached 900-square-foot guest house — also by Neff — sits at the rear of the property.
