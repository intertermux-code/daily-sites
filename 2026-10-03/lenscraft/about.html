The Photographer

Behind the Lens

I believe your wedding photos should feel like a memory, not a production.

Founded in 2018, Lenscraft began with a simple premise: luxury isn't about posing; it's about presence. My name is Sarah, and I approach every wedding with the eye of an editorial photographer and the heart of a documentarian.

I specialize in natural light, architectural composition, and the fleeting micro-moments that define your relationship. No forced smiles. No awkward staging. Just the authentic narrative of your day, elevated through intentional design.

My Approach

  • Intentional Observation I anticipate moments before they happen, ensuring nothing is missed without being intrusive.
  • Editorial Aesthetic Inspired by Vogue and Kinfolk, my editing style is clean, timeless, and emotive.
  • Quiet Confidence I guide gently when needed but mostly trust the organic flow of your celebration.
