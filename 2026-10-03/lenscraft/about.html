01 — The Photographer

Elena Vance

I believe that the most powerful images are the ones you didn't know were being taken. My approach is rooted in photojournalism, not fashion. I don't direct; I observe.

With a background in fine art and over 150 weddings documented across three continents, I've learned that perfection is boring. The messy hair, the tear-streaked cheek, the uncontrollable laughter—these are the artifacts of real love.

When I'm not behind the lens, I'm likely scouting brutalist architecture, developing film in my home darkroom, or searching for the perfect espresso in whatever city I'm currently passing through.
