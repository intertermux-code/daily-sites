§ 00 — THE GIFT
Every dollar becomes a meal.

◆ A monthly gift, a monthly promise

A dollar
is a plate.
A plate is
a Tuesday.

Ninety-two cents of every dollar you give goes directly to food. Not overhead, not campaigns, not executive titles — a bag of apples, a quart of milk, a hot dinner.

§ 02

Where your
gift goes.

i.

Ninety-two cents

becomes food — produce from Orchard Hill, bread from Holloway, milk from Northbank Dairy.

ii.

Six cents

keeps the refrigerator trucks cold and the pantry lights on.

iii.

Two cents

pays for the person who answers the phone when you call asking how to volunteer.
