Feeding hope,
one harvest
at a time.

Since 2014, Bright Harvest has moved 1.4 million pounds of fresh food from farm, grocer, and neighbor into the kitchens of families who needed it most. We are a pantry, a kitchen, a garden, and a gathering place — open to anyone, no questions asked.

§ 02

A pantry is a
place. We are
also a practice.

Hunger is rarely about a single missing meal. It is about rent, about shifts cut, about a child’s growth spurt, about a car that will not start. Bright Harvest meets that reality with dignity: no ID required, no means test, no waiting list longer than a week.

i.

Fresh, not filler.

Seventy percent of what we hand out is produce, dairy, or protein. We refuse to treat people as a market for food that cannot be sold.

ii.

Choice, not charity.

Our client-choice pantries let people shop. You pick the rice, the beans, the fruit your children will actually eat.

iii.

Roots, not rescue.

One-third of our food is grown on our own two-acre teaching garden in East Hollow. The other two-thirds come from partner farms within sixty miles.
