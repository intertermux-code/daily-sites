§ 00 — THE ASK
Two hours is enough.

◆ Volunteer with Bright Harvest

Two hours
a week.
A whole
life changes.

We don’t need your whole Saturday. We need your Tuesday morning, or your Thursday evening, or the hour after work. We’ll train you in fifteen minutes. You’ll leave having fed forty people.

§ 01

Pick your
shift.

Every role includes training, a Bright Harvest apron, and a meal at the end of your shift.

i.

Pantry Stocker

Unpack the morning truck, sort the produce, fill the shelves. A calm, method two hours.

When
Mon–Sat, 7–9am
Lift
Up to 30 lb
People
Mostly quiet solo work
ii.

Kitchen Line

Chop alongside the chef, plate the supper, serve at the window. The liveliest shift we have.

When
Mon–Fri, 3–7pm
Lift
Up to 20 lb
People
Fast, loud, fun
iii.

Garden Hand

Weed, harvest, water. Mostly outside, rain or shine. The quietest work we do.

When
Tue/Thu/Sat, 8–11am
Lift
Up to 25 lb
People
Solo or with one other
iv.

Truck Driver

Drive a refrigerated box truck on a Saturday route. CDL not required — we train.

When
Fri or Sat, 6am–1pm
License
Valid driver’s license, 3+ yrs
People
You + one navigator
v.

Welcome Desk

Say hello to every person who walks in. Point them where they need to go. Smile.

When
Any shift, 2 hr window
Lift
None
People
All of them
vi.

From Home

Write thank-you notes, translate flyers, manage our Instagram, answer the email inbox.

When
Flexible, 1–3 hr / week
Lift
None
People
Alone with your coffee
