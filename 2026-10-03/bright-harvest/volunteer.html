Your Hands Can Feed a Neighborhood

Whether you have two hours or two years, there’s a place for you at Bright Harvest. No experience needed—just a willing heart.

Current Opportunities

Warehouse Sorter

Tues & Thurs • 9am–12pm

Inspect, sort, and pack rescued produce into family boxes. Active role requiring standing and lifting up to 30 lbs.

Mobile Pantry Driver

Wed & Fri • 7am–11am

Deliver food boxes along established routes. Valid license and clean record required. Training provided.

Youth Garden Mentor

Mon & Wed • 3pm–5pm

Guide kids through planting, harvesting, and cooking activities. Background check required. Joy guaranteed.

Event Support

Flexible • As Needed

Help set up, run, and tear down community harvest festivals, fundraisers, and awareness events.
