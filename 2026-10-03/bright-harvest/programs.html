How We Serve

Programs Rooted in Dignity

We don’t just distribute calories. We deliver choice, freshness, and respect through specialized channels designed for real life.

Farm-to-Family Boxes

Weekly CSA-style boxes sourced directly from regional growers. Each box contains 12–15 pounds of seasonal produce, dairy, and protein. Families choose their pickup location and time, maintaining autonomy and reducing stigma.

  • Choice-Based: Swap items based on dietary needs or preferences.
  • Nutrition Education: Recipe cards and storage tips included.
  • Zero Waste: Imperfect produce celebrated, never discarded.

Mobile Pantry Network

Our fleet of refrigerated trucks brings the market to underserved neighborhoods, rural towns, and senior centers. No appointments, no paperwork barriers—just open access to fresh food in familiar community spaces.

12 Routes • 4 Days/Week • 3,000+ Stops/Mo

Youth Harvest Academy

After-school and summer programs teaching kids where food comes from. Through hands-on gardening, cooking classes, and farm visits, we cultivate lifelong healthy habits and food sovereignty skills.
