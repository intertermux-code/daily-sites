What We Do

Programs & Services

We go beyond emergency food distribution to address the root causes of hunger through specialized programs tailored to vulnerable populations.

Our fleet of three refrigerated trucks brings fresh produce, dairy, and proteins directly to food deserts and rural communities lacking grocery stores. Operating on a weekly schedule across 12 locations.

  • Schedule: Mon-Fri, rotating stops
  • Eligibility: Open to all, no ID required

A home-delivery service providing shelf-stable and fresh items specifically curated for seniors' dietary needs. Includes low-sodium options and easy-open packaging.

  • Delivery: First Tuesday of every month
  • Requirements: Age 60+, income verification

When school lets out, childhood hunger spikes. Our summer sites provide free, nutritious lunches to children under 18 in safe, supervised environments like parks and libraries.

  • Season: June – August
  • Format: On-site congregate dining
