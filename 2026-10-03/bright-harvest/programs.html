§ 00 — THE PROGRAMS
Four doors. All unlocked.

◆ What we actually do, every day

Four doors.
All unlocked.
All the time.

Bright Harvest is not one thing. It is a pantry, a kitchen, a garden, and a truck — each answering a different shape of need. You can walk into any of them without an appointment.

§ 01

A. The
Pantry.

Client-choice model, adapted from the Oregon Food Bank. First in Vermont to adopt it in 2018.

What happens

You walk in. A volunteer hands you a cart. You shop — picking the rice your children eat, the fruit that will actually be eaten, the brand of peanut butter your grandmother recognizes. You leave with five to seven days of groceries, including fresh produce, dairy, and protein.

Locations
River Ward · East Hollow · Northbank · Millbury
Hours
Mon–Sat, 9am–5pm · Closed Sunday
Requirements
None. No ID. No proof of income. No questions.
Last year
14,237 neighbors served
§ 02

B. The
Kitchen.

What happens

From 5 to 7, Monday through Friday, the Community Kitchen serves a hot, balanced supper to anyone who walks through the door. Last year we served 340 plates a night. A volunteer cooks alongside a paid chef. There are always seconds. There is always coffee.

Location
118 Main Street, East Hollow
Hours
Mon–Fri, 5–7pm
Meals served
88,400 last year
Menu
Rotates weekly — always vegetarian, always halal-friendly
§ 03

C. The
Garden.

What happens

On two acres behind the East Hollow pantry, we grow a third of our own produce. Three greenhouses keep us harvesting through November. Forty apprentices a year — teenagers, retirees, people in recovery — learn to grow food and take home their own plot’s yield.

Acres
2.0
Greenhouses
3
Apprentices
40 / year
Pounds grown
41,000 last season
§ 04

D. The
Truck.

What happens

Three refrigerated trucks leave the warehouse every Friday and Saturday morning, headed to rural towns that have no grocery store within twenty miles. We set up in church parking lots and school lots, and stay until every bag is gone.

3
Towns served
11
Stops per week
22
Miles driven
48,000 last year
