QUESTIONS

Frequently asked.

Can I switch plans later? +

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade anytime from your account settings. When upgrading, you'll only pay the prorated difference. When downgrading, the credit applies to future billing cycles.

What happens after my 30-day trial? +

You'll receive a reminder 3 days before your trial ends. If you don't choose a paid plan, your account automatically converts to the free Starter tier. No surprise charges, ever.

Is my financial data secure? +

Absolutely. We use bank-grade 256-bit encryption, are SOC 2 Type II certified, and never store your banking credentials. All connections are read-only—we can't move your money, only read it.

Do you offer refunds? +

Yes. If you're not satisfied within the first 60 days of any paid plan, contact support for a full refund. No questions asked.

Can I export my data? +

Yes. All paid plans include unlimited CSV and PDF exports. You own your data—we'll never hold it hostage. You can export transactions history, budgets, and reports anytime.

Which banks do you support? +

We connect to 12,000+ financial institutions worldwide through Plaid, MX, and Finicity. This includes all major US banks, credit unions, brokerages, and many international institutions. Check our integrations page for the full list.
