RESOURCES

Quick answers.

Find answers to common questions before reaching out.

How do I reset my password? +

Click "Forgot password" on the login screen. Enter your email, and we'll send a reset link valid for 24 hours. If you don't receive it, check your spam folder or contact support.

Why isn't my bank connecting? +

Bank connections can fail for several reasons: changed credentials, security blocks, or temporary outages. Try disconnecting and reconnecting the account. If it persists, your bank may require MFA—enable it in your bank's settings and try again.

How do I cancel my subscription? +

Go to Settings → Billing → Cancel subscription. Your access continues until the end of your current billing period. You can reactivate anytime. All your data remains available for 90 days after cancellation.

Can I export my transaction data? +

Yes. Go to Reports → Export. Choose your date range, categories, and format (CSV, PDF, or OFX for tax software). Pro and Family plans include unlimited exports; Starter plan includes 3 per month.

Is my data safe with Mintwise? +

Yes. We use bank-grade 256-bit encryption, are SOC 2 Type II certified, and never store your banking credentials. All connections are read-only. We cannot move your money—we can only read it to provide insights.

Do you have a mobile app? +

Yes, Mintwise is available on iOS (iPhone and iPad), Android, and Apple Watch. Download from the App Store or Google Play. All plans include mobile access.
