Mintwise isn't just another budgeting app. It's a mission control center for your money—24 interconnected modules working in concert to give you unprecedented clarity.

MODULE 01 // TRANSACTION ENGINE

Real-time transaction capture.

Connect 12,000+ financial institutions worldwide. Mintwise polls for new transactions every 4 hours (or instantly with push-enabled banks), categorizes them using ML trained on 2.4B historical transactions, and flags anomalies before they become problems.

  • Plaid, MX, and Finicity connectors
  • 98.7% auto-categorization accuracy
  • Duplicate detection across accounts
  • Split transactions for shared expenses
