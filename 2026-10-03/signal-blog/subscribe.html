Index · Questions

Before you subscribe.

Is the brief really free for four weeks?

Yes. We will send you four full briefs. On the fifth week, you will be asked to pay. If you do not, you will be quietly unsubscribed. No charge, no follow-up email, no guilt.

Can I pay more than $6/month?

Yes. We have a tier at $18/month and one at $60/month. The content is identical. The higher tiers fund the fellowship program and our freelance budget. 412 readers currently pay at $60.

Do you sell reader data?

No. We have never sold reader data, and our corporate charter forbids us from doing so. We use email only to deliver the brief and, rarely, to tell you about something we have made (a printed edition, an event).

Can I read the archive?

Every dispatch we have published since 2021 is free to read on the site. Subscribers get the archive plus the brief.
