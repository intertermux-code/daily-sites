Frequently Asked Queries

Can I switch plans mid-cycle?

Affirmative. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing. Downgrades apply at the next renewal cycle.

What happens if I exceed my event limit?

We never drop data. You'll receive a notification at 80% and 100% usage. Overage is billed at $0.00001 per event.

Do you offer on-premise deployment?

Yes, for Deep Space customers requiring air-gapped environments. Contact our sales crew for architecture review.

Is my data encrypted?

All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We are SOC2 Type II compliant.
