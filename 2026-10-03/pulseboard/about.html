Origin Story

Built by Engineers,
For Engineers.

We got tired of dashboards that looked pretty but lacked depth. So we built the tool we actually wanted to use.

The Signal in the Noise

Founded in 2024, Pulseboard began as an internal tool at a high-frequency trading firm. We needed microsecond visibility into system health, but existing tools were too slow and too vague.

We stripped away the decoration and focused on density, performance, and keyboard-driven workflows. When other teams started asking for access, we realized this wasn't just an internal utility—it was a new standard for observability.

Today, we're a distributed crew of systems architects, data scientists, and UI obsessives committed to keeping the world's infrastructure online.
