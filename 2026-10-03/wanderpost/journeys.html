Kyoto: The Moss & Timber Edit

Escape the neon rush. This journey focuses on the quiet hours of Kyoto—dawn temple visits, private tea ceremonies with third-generation masters, and stays in restored Machiya townhouses.

  • Stay: Heritage Machiya & Onsen Ryokan
  • Highlight: Private Zazen meditation at Daitoku-ji
