Kyoto Stillness

JAPAN / 24 DAYS / AUTUMN OR SPRING

Bypass the tourist trails of Gion and immerse yourself in the monastic rhythms of Kibune and Kurama. This journey focuses on Ma (negative space) — scheduling long, unstructured afternoons in private gardens, tea ceremonies with third-generation masters, and stays in heritage Machiya townhouses where silence is the primary amenity.

  • Private Zen meditation instruction
  • Kaiseki dining experiences
  • Artisan pottery workshop in Shigaraki

Patagonian Wind

CHILE & ARGENTINA / 19 DAYS / NOV-MAR

A traverse of the southern Andes that prioritizes isolation over landmarks. Stay in remote estancias, trek with gauchos through lenga forests, and witness the Perito Moreno glacier from private vantage points far removed from the public walkways. The pace is dictated by weather and wind.

  • Horseback traversals
  • Glaciology lectures
  • Estancia lamb asado traditions
Request Itinerary

Atlas & Medina

MOROCCO / 14 DAYS / OCT-APR

From the sensory overload of the Jemaa el-Fnaa to the absolute silence of the High Atlas. This journey balances the two extremes of Moroccan life. Sleep in restored kasbahs, hike Berber trails with local guides, and learn the mathematics of Zellige tilework from master craftsmen in Fez.

  • Berber village homestay
  • Sahara camel trekking (ethical)
  • Traditional Hammam rituals
Request Itinerary

Arctic Silence

NORWAY / 16 DAYS / JUN-AUG OR JAN-MAR

Experience the dramatic verticality of the Lofoten archipelago. Whether under the midnight sun or the northern lights, this journey connects you with the raw elemental forces of the Arctic. Stay in converted rorbuer fishing cabins, join sustainable cod fisheries, and kayak through fjords untouched by cruise ships.

  • Fjord kayaking expeditions
  • Viking history immersion
  • Nordic cuisine foraging
