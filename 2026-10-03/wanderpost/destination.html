01 — Field Guide

Kyoto:
The Old Capital

A living museum of Japanese tradition where every season paints a different masterpiece.

02 — Temporal
TEMP 13°C Avg
CROWDS Moderate

Plum Blossoms & Awakening

Winter's chill recedes as plum blossoms bloom in Kitano Tenmangu. The city prepares for spring; temples are quieter, and the light is crisp and clear. Ideal for contemplative walks before the cherry blossom rush.

TEMP 17°C Avg
CROWDS Peak

Sakura Season

The iconic cherry blossom period. Philosopher's Path becomes a tunnel of pink. While beautiful, it demands early mornings and strategic planning to find solitude. Night illuminations at Kodai-ji are magical.

TEMP 14°C Avg
CROWDS High

Momiji (Autumn Leaves)

Maple leaves turn fiery red and gold. Tofuku-ji and Eikando offer breathtaking vistas. The air is cool and dry, perfect for hiking the Fushimi Inari back trails without summer humidity.

03 — Protocol

Cultural Etiquette

01

Shoes Off

Always remove shoes when entering tatami rooms, ryokans, and many traditional restaurants. Look for the genkan (entryway) step.

02

Quiet Transit

Speaking loudly on trains or buses is considered rude. Set phones to silent mode and avoid calls entirely.

03

Tipping

Do not tip. Excellent service is standard and included. Leaving money can be seen as confusing or even offensive.
