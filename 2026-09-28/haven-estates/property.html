About This Estate

A masterclass in Craftsman restoration, The Hawthorn Estate seamlessly blends 1920s architectural integrity with modern luxury. Original hardwood floors, built-in cabinetry, and leaded glass windows have been meticulously preserved.

The chef’s kitchen features honed marble countertops, Wolf appliances, and a butler’s pantry. The primary suite offers a spa-like bath with heated floors and a walk-in closet system. Outside, the professionally landscaped grounds include a detached studio perfect for a home office.
