About This Home

Experience the pinnacle of coastal luxury in this architectural masterpiece. Perched on the prestigious Oceanview Drive, The Azure Cliffside Villa offers unobstructed, panoramic views stretching from the Pacific Ocean to the city skyline below.

Designed by renowned architect Julian Vane, the home features an open-concept living space where floor-to-ceiling glass walls blur the line between indoor and outdoor living. The gourmet chef’s kitchen boasts Italian marble countertops, Wolf appliances, and a hidden walk-in pantry.

The primary suite is a sanctuary unto itself, featuring a private terrace, dual walk-in closets, and a spa-inspired bath with a soaking tub positioned to capture sunset views. Additional amenities include a temperature-controlled wine cellar, smart home automation throughout, and a zero-edge infinity pool that creates a seamless visual connection to the horizon.
