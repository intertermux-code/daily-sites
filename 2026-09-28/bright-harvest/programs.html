Initiatives & Operations

How We Serve

Our programs are designed to create a sustainable loop of nourishment, education, and community resilience.

Core Service

Community Pantry Network

We operate 12 satellite pantries across the county, providing emergency food assistance with dignity. Each location offers fresh produce, dairy, and shelf-stable staples.

  • Locations: 12 Active Sites
  • Hours: Tue-Sat, 9am-4pm
  • Access: No ID Required
Logistics

Mobile Harvest Route

For homebound seniors and families without transportation, our fleet of electric vans delivers weekly harvest boxes directly to doorsteps.

  • Reach: 450 Homes Weekly
  • Fleet: 6 Electric Vans
  • Focus: Senior & Disabled
Education

Sprouts Nutrition Academy

Interactive workshops teaching families how to prepare healthy, budget-friendly meals using pantry ingredients. Includes kids' gardening clubs.

  • Classes: Weekly Workshops
  • Audience: Families & Youth
  • Output: Recipe Booklets
