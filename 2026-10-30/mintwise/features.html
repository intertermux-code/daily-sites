03 The ten

What each one does, and what it never touches

Ordered in the sequence a new household meets them: connect, understand, predict, control, grow.

01 Linking

Read-only aggregation across 12,400 institutions

Mintwise requests the narrowest scope each bank offers: transactions and balances, nothing else. Credentials are exchanged once for a rotating token that your bank can revoke from its own dashboard, and we store only the token.

  • Refresh cadence you control — hourly, daily, or manual pull only.
  • No transfer capability — the API scope cannot move money outbound, by design.
  • Manual fallback — CSV and OFX import for the credit union that never got an API.

Median setup: 1 min 32 s · 6 accounts

02 Classification

A merchant classifier that admits uncertainty

Rules first, then a model trained on your own history, then a fallback. When confidence drops below 80%, Mintwise marks the row amber and leaves it unfiled rather than guessing at 2 a.m. and quietly corrupting your budget.

  • Overrides are permanent — rename once and the merchant is pinned forever.
  • Split one charge many ways — a $240 warehouse run divided into household, resale, and gifts.
  • Nine custom levels deep, with tags that cross-cut categories for reporting.

03 Safe to spend

The one number, recalculated all day

Balance minus committed bills minus your own scheduled savings, then adjusted for the clearing lag your bank actually shows. It is deliberately smaller than your balance and it is the figure every other screen is built around.

  • Pending-aware — holds are subtracted the second the terminal posts them.
  • Per-account or pooled — read it across the household or per wallet.
  • Home-screen widget — one glance, no app open, no network call.

04 Projection

Thirty-two days forward, with the maths visible

The forecast walks day by day: scheduled items on their real dates, variable categories at your trailing 8-week median, income on its actual cadence. Tap any day to see exactly which lines produced the figure.

  • Dip warnings — a named date and amount, eleven days out on median.
  • Scenario pins — model a $3,000 repair or a bonus without editing real data.
  • Honest error bars — ±3.4% median accuracy, published per institution.

05 Leak detection

Price creep, duplicate charges, and trials that never ended

Mintwise keeps a price history per recurring merchant. When a renewal posts higher than its own March baseline, you get one line: what it was, what it is, and how long you have paid it. Nothing is auto-cancelled; nothing is “negotiated for you” by a third party.

  • Renewal calendar — every commitment on one timeline, with the cancel date beside it.
  • Duplicate sweep — same merchant, same amount, same day, flagged both times.
  • Orphan detector — charges with no matching subscription or invoice.

06 Vaults

Named goals that get funded the morning after payday

A vault is a rule, not a jar. You set the deadline and the target; Mintwise works backwards to a per-paycheck amount, then raises or lowers it as your real margin changes. Surplus rounding moves the leftover on the same run.

  • Priority order — rent, runway, taxes, then fun. Funded top-down.
  • Pause with a reason — skipping a month logs why and re-plans the deadline.
  • Real destination — vaults map to an external account or a partner savings product; your choice.

07 Shared wallets

Two people, one household, separate truths

Invite a partner to a shared wallet while your personal accounts stay private. Each side chooses, per linked account, whether the other person sees balances, transactions, or nothing. Nothing is silently merged.

  • Contribution fairness — split 50/50, by income percentage, or by agreed amounts.
  • No-argument ledger — who fronted the deposit, settled on the last Sunday of the month.
  • Clean exit — one member leaving exports their half of the history as CSV.

08 Taxes & self-employment

A set-aside that keeps pace with irregular income

Mark a transaction business in one swipe and it moves out of your budget and into a schedule. Mintwise estimates the quarterly set-aside from your filing status and rate, holds it in a dedicated vault, and produces a pack your accountant can open without a login.

  • Mileage capture — location-tagged trips logged with dates for a deduction record.
  • Quarterly estimate — a number and a deadline, revised as Q-to-date income changes.
  • Accountant export — Schedule-C-shaped CSV plus receipts where you attached them.

09 Reporting & export

Your data leaves as easily as it arrived

Twelve standard reports, one custom builder, and a full-fidelity export in CSV, JSON, OFX, or a printable one-pager. If you close your account, Mintwise hands over thirteen months of categorised history before it deletes anything.

  • Comparison mode — this month against the same month last year, category by category.
  • Scheduled delivery — the one-pager in your inbox on the 1st, or never.
  • Open API — a documented read endpoint for your own dashboards on Plus and up.

10 Calm by design

Notifications you opt into, one at a time

There is no streak, no badge count, and no red dot. Alerts ship off except for overdraft risk, and every rule is scoped to a category or merchant. Weekly review is a Sunday email with three questions and a two-minute answer sheet.

  • Quiet hours per rule — dining nudges can arrive Friday 8 p.m. and never again.
  • Accessibility first — dynamic type to 23pt, full VoiceOver labels, and a dyslexic-friendly type swap.
  • Colour never carries meaning alone — every state pairs a hue with a glyph and a word.

11 Security model

What we can see is deliberately very little

Mintwise is architected so that a worst day at our company still cannot expose your money or your identity to a third party.

0Staff with raw credential access

Tokenised, not stored

Bank credentials are exchanged for scoped tokens at the aggregator. We hold the token handle; the credential never touches our disks, and rotation happens on the aggregator’s schedule.

AES-256Every field, at rest and in transit

Keys you can revoke

Device keys live in the Secure Enclave or StrongBox. Revoking a session from the app kills it server-side inside fifteen seconds, and a lost phone cannot be used to authorise anything.

31 daysDeleted on request, verified by audit

Deletion is real

Close the account and the categorised history, exports, and backups go on a scheduled purge. You get the deletion certificate by email, and the ledger export beforehand if you want it.
