04 Answers

Eleven questions support gets daily

Ordered by ticket volume. Each expand shows the exact setting path so you can check before writing.

My bank says “connection refused” every time I link. What now?

Nine times in ten, the institution challenged your aggregator session and the retry loop stopped quietly. Open Account → Linked → Reauthorise and complete the challenge in the same window without switching tabs.

If it still refuses, some credit unions block automated sessions on weekdays but allow them overnight. Use “Schedule reauthorise for 03:00” on the same screen, and it will run against the aggregator’s idle path.

Why does my balance differ from what the bank shows by a dollar or two?

Mintwise reads posted items; some banks publish a “ledger balance” that includes interest accruals not yet posted. The app never invents the difference — it shows a small “Reconciles” row with the exact gap and its date.

Path: Account → tap the account → Reconciliation. If a gap older than three days appears, send us the screenshot and the aggregator ticket gets opened for you.

Can Mintwise move money out of my accounts?

No. The scopes we request are read-only, and the connection cannot authorise an outbound transfer even if someone had our tokens. Payments and vault transfers you see in the product are instructions you issue from your own bank, or scheduled through a partner you explicitly set up.

The classifier put something in the wrong category and it keeps happening.

Long-press the transaction, choose Recategorise, then tick “Always for this merchant.” The override writes a rule ahead of the model, so it survives future retraining. Overrides you create are never overwritten by the classifier.

For merchant name variants (“CALICO #12”, “Calico Grocer”), use Account → Rules → Merge merchant so one override covers both spellings.

How do I get my data out — and how fast is deletion?

Account → Data → Export gives you CSV, JSON, and OFX of every transaction, category, note, and attached receipt on every plan. The export is full-fidelity; nothing is watermarked or trimmed.

Account → Data → Close and delete starts a 31-day purge covering backups. You receive a signed deletion certificate by email, and Rosa replies personally to any GDPR or CCPA request.

Is there a web version, and does the app work without a connection?

Yes to both. The web app at app.mintwise.com covers budget editing, reports, and exports. Mobile caches the last 90 days locally, so safe-to-spend, categorisation fixes, and receipts all work on a plane and sync when you land.

My projection says I will dip but I know I will not. Can I trust it?

Tap the dip date, then “Show the maths.” The forecast lists each scheduled item with its expected posting day, and anything with low confidence is marked. The usual culprit is a bill that moved from the 1st to the 15th without the schedule following it.

Median accuracy is ±3.4% on the balance line. Where the projection is wrong repeatedly for one institution, we publish it on the accuracy page rather than hide it.

Can my partner see my personal accounts?

Only if you say so, per account. Each linked account has its own visibility selector: balances only, full transactions, or hidden. Hidden accounts still contribute to a shared wallet total when you choose to include them, without exposing individual rows.

I was refunded twice for a cancelled plan. Who do I contact?

Choose “Billing, refund, or plan change” in the form above and write “refund” in the message — that keyword short-circuits the review step. Refunds go back to the original card within five business days, and we do not ask for a reason.

Do you offer large-print, reduced-motion, or screen-reader support?

Yes, on every plan, including Free. Account → Display covers type scale up to 23pt, a dyslexic-friendly face, a reduced-motion switch that disables every animation, and high-contrast chart palettes that pair colour with shape and text. VoiceOver and TalkBack labels are complete on all primary flows.

If an assistive path is broken, that is a P1 bug for us. Report it through the form and we will usually ship a fix within two weeks.

Are you hiring, and can I send press enquiries here?

We hire roughly four people a year and post every opening to the support queue address with “careers” in the subject line, because the queue reaches the whole team. Press and partnership notes go to the same place — pick “Something else entirely,” and Ada forwards it correctly.
