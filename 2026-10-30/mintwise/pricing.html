06 Straight talk

When paying is worth it, and when it is not

We would rather you stay on Free and be honest with us than upgrade, resent the charge, and cancel in month three.

Stay on Solo if

One account, one income

Two linked accounts and a monthly glance is a legitimate relationship with a budgeting app. Solo does that forever and exports cleanly if you leave.

Move to Plus if

You pay for anything monthly

Eight or more subscriptions, a variable paycheck, or self-employment. Price-creep alerts alone recovered $61 a month for the median Plus household in 2026.

Take Household if

Money is a shared project

A partner, an ageing parent you support, or kids with their first card. The settlement ledger is the feature that ends the “who paid for that” conversation.

08 Billing questions

Seven answers about money taken

More detail, or a case that does not fit, goes to support — a human replies within one business day.

Can I cancel from inside the app, or do I have to email someone?

Two taps: Account → Plan → Cancel. No retention call, no “are you sure” maze, and no requirement to email. Your data stays exportable for 90 days after the plan lapses, then the paid features fall back to Solo rather than deleting anything.

What happens to my price if you raise it?

Your rate is locked for your continuous tenure. If Plus goes from $6.90 to $8.00 next year, paying subscribers keep $6.90 until they cancel. We publish any price change at least 30 days ahead on the changelog.

Is the annual plan really two months free?

Yes. Plus is $82.80 over twelve monthly payments and $69 charged once. Household is $154.80 monthly versus $129 annually. Switch from monthly to annual mid-cycle and we credit the unused portion against the new charge.

Do you charge per linked account or per person?

Neither, beyond the caps in the matrix. Household includes up to five members at the one price, and linking a thirteenth account on Plus costs nothing — it just sits in the manual-import queue until you upgrade.

How does the 30-day refund work?

Reply to any receipt with the word “refund”, or use Account → Billing → Request refund. We do not ask why and we do not require you to keep the plan. Refunds land on the original card within five business days.

Which taxes are included in the quoted price?

US prices are shown excluding sales tax, which is added at checkout by jurisdiction and by your tax-exempt status where applicable. UK and EU customers see VAT-inclusive prices automatically detected from the billing address.

Do you take a cut of anything I buy through the app?

No. There are no affiliate links, no sponsored recommendations, and no lead-broker relationships. Every recommendation Mintwise makes is derived from your own transaction history, and the algorithm that produces it is documented in the security model.
