The Chapter & Verse Book Club

One book. Three rooms. Forty minutes before anyone is polite.

Section One

How It Works

No audition, no reading list older than you are, and nobody assigned to explain the ending. Four steps, then you are in it.

  1. 01

    Take the month’s title at cost

    We buy twelve copies ahead at wholesale and hand them over for what they cost us — usually nine to fourteen dollars. Finished early? Pass it to the next person on your room’s list.

  2. 02

    Read at your own pace

    Every pick comes with a printed pace card: eight sections, one per week, with a page range and nothing else. We have never once asked a member whether they finished, and we are not starting.

  3. 03

    Pick a room, not a schedule

    The same book is discussed three times a month — Tuesday evening, Thursday late, Sunday afternoon. Attend any, all, or none, then swap rooms whenever your life changes. Bring a friend; they pay nothing for their first night.

  4. 04

    Argue for ninety minutes

    A staff member opens with ten minutes of context, then stops talking. Tea, one question card each, and thirty minutes of unmoderated disagreement. Cheese and bread at the end, because everything runs longer once there is food.

Section Five

Questions People Actually Ask

I have not read the book. Can I still come?

Yes, and roughly a third of every room has not finished it. Come and listen; half the members of this club started by hearing somebody else get excited about a paragraph. We never ask who read what.

What if I hate the pick?

Say so, out loud, in the room. That is the entire purpose of the night. Two members have run the February slot for years on the strength of a well-argued dislike, and the shop has sold more copies of the books they hated than of the ones they loved.

Do I have to buy the book from you?

No. Read it from the library, from your own shelf, or from a borrowed copy that has been through four hands. We sell it at cost because it is cheaper than the shops that can afford to be, and we keep four spares at the desk for anyone who turns up empty-handed.

Is it accessible?

The Gold Room is step-free from Warren Street with a hearing loop at the desk. The Thursday late room is on the first floor, reached by eight steps or by the lift in the passage — ask at the counter and we will have it waiting. Every pick is available in large print or audio if you tell us a fortnight ahead.

Can I bring my teenager?

Yes, from fourteen. Sunday afternoon has had the same two sixth-formers for three winters and they have out-argued the room twice. Under fourteen, come to Story Hour in the loft instead and join the grown-up club when the book suits them.

What happens if I miss three months?

Nothing. We do not remove names from the ledger, we do not send a guilt email, and your pace card stays on file for when you come back. Life is long and books are patient.

From the club ledger, 2019
