Section Three

How We Grade, and Why We Tell You

Every used book on this shelf carries a word we can defend out loud.

As new — nobody has opened it beyond a glance. Fine — square, tight, unmarked, possibly an old sticker. Very good — read once, well, and it shows only at the corners. Good — honest wear, complete text, sometimes a name. Poor — we sell it in the rebinding bin and say so on the sticker.

If a description on this page does not match the book you collect at the counter, bring it back. We will refund it or find you another, and we will write the difference into our shelf notes so it does not happen to the next reader.

A note from the buying desk

“We do not stock a book because a distributor told us to. We stock it because somebody in this building lost sleep over it.”

Theo Lindqvist runs the buying desk and the rarity room. If you want a title that is not on this page, ask him to look — he keeps a private list of forty books he expects to find, and he has found eleven.
