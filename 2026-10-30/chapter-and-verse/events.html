Events, Upstairs and Down

Everything we run between now and the New Year. Forty seats upstairs, twenty stools downstairs, one kettle that has outlived three of our coffee machines.

The Room, In Detail

What to expect before you walk up the back staircase

Upstairs seats forty on a good night and takes a load-bearing amount of opinion. There is no stage, which is deliberate: readers stand at the long table next to the window with the book in their hands, and the room can see their faces. The acoustics are terrible for music and excellent for one person talking.

Access is level at the front door on Marlowe Street; the back staircase has eleven steps and no lift, so for seated events we put the whole thing in the front room on request, no explanation needed. The hearing loop covers the front two rows. Assistance dogs are welcome and Verity has been trained, at great expense to her dignity, to keep her distance.

Downstairs, the counter area takes twenty stools around the long table. That is where Bad Reviews Society, blackouts and the wrapping table happen, and where you can hear the kettle.

Events start on time. Latecomers wait for a pause at the door, which lasts about four minutes in Dracula and about one minute in the Children’s Hour.

Hosting Something?

The upstairs room is free to local reading groups on Mondays, with the shelves moved back and the kettle filled. Write to the counter and we will work out whether your group is the right size for the chairs.
