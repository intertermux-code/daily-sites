Registry Nº 002 — Treatments & Fees

The whole register, printed.

Five departments, forty-six priced lines, and no “from” that quietly becomes something else at the checkout. Open a row to read how the treatment is done; scroll to the register for the numbers themselves.

Diagnosis before anything is drilled

New-patient examinations run sixty minutes and produce a chart, a full set of intraoral photographs, and a written inventory of every surface in your mouth — sound, filled, crowned, or failing. That inventory becomes the baseline all future visits are compared against.

Hygiene is performed to a guided biofilm protocol: the plaque is disclosed and stained so the clinician and you can both see exactly where the film sits. It is slower than a conventional clean and it is the reason our bleeding-on-probing rates fall within four months.

Guided biofilm therapy Air-polishing finish Oral cancer screen
  • New exam60 min
  • Recall3–12 months
  • RadiographsLow dose

We accept patients of any age and we do not charge for a consultation that ends with “nothing needs doing”.

One quoted number, fixture to crown

The implant, the abutment and the crown are priced as a single line. Bone grafting, sinus lifting and provisional teeth are itemised separately and only charged if they turn out to be necessary — and we tell you which are likely before surgery, from the scan.

Placement is flapless and guided wherever the bone allows, which means less swelling and a same-day temporary in the aesthetic zone when primary stability exceeds 35 N·cm.

CBCT guided Flapless where possible 10-year guarantee
  • Surgery45–90 min
  • Integration8–20 weeks
  • AnaestheticLocal or sedation
  • Survival98.6% at 10 yrs

If you have untreated periodontitis or smoke more than ten cigarettes a day, we defer implant surgery and say why. Placing an implant into an inflamed mouth is how the ten-year figure becomes a sixty-year figure.

A finished price, not a per-tray meter

Orthodontic fees are quoted for the complete case including all refinements, records, and twelve months of retainers. If your teeth need fourteen extra trays, that is our estimating error, not your invoice.

We review progress on a scan at eight-to-ten week intervals rather than taking impressions, and you can see the movement plotted against the plan we agreed on day one.

Fixed-price cases 360° scanning 0% finance
  • Aligner course6–18 months
  • Fixed braces12–24 months
  • Wear time20–22 hrs/day
  • Retainers2 yrs included

Children under nine are seen in our paediatric clinic, where the emphasis is on habit correction and space maintenance rather than full appliances.

Approve the shape before any enamel is removed

Veneers begin as a wax-up made from your scan, then a composite mock-up bonded over your own teeth that you wear for three to seven days. You eat with it, photograph it, and tell us what to change. Only then is anything prepared.

Our aesthetic work is milled in-house from lithium disilicate and layered by hand. We aim for tooth that looks like tooth: translucency at the incisal edge, slight irregularity, and a shade that still matches the two back teeth you never replaced.

  • Veneer$1,180 each

Whitening will not change the colour of a crown, veneer or filling. If you plan to whiten, we do that first and make the replacements afterwards.

The smallest restoration that will hold

We bond every posterior composite under a rubber dam with a bit of extra time spent on the matrix band, because overhanging margins are the single commonest cause of a filling that fails at year four.

Where a cusp is weak but the tooth is otherwise sound, an onlay preserves the healthy wall that a full crown would remove. It takes the same two visits and costs four hundred dollars less.

  • Crown2 visits, 10 days
  • Guarantee5 years

Amalgam removal is done with high-volume suction and a rubber dam so you are not inhaling mercury vapour, and we never replace a sound amalgam simply because it is grey.

Registry Nº 002b — Ways to Pay

Three routes. All of them interest-free.

No treatment is deferred at LumiDent for money reasons alone. If the plan is genuinely urgent, we will find a way to stage it.

Pay at the desk

Card, bank transfer or cash on the day of treatment. A receipt with every code is emailed before you leave, so you can claim it yourself if you prefer.

  • MinimumNone
  • SurchargeNone

0% over 24 months

Any treatment over $1,200 splits across two years with no interest and no arrangement fee. Approved in about eight minutes at the desk, on a tablet, while the chair is being prepared.

  • Cases over$1,200
  • Term6–24 months
  • APR0%

Staged plans

Large cases are broken into phases with their own dates and totals, so you can start the urgent work now and schedule the cosmetic phase in the spring without losing the fee you were quoted.

  • Price held18 months
  • Deposit15% per phase

Registry — Questions Before Commitment

Asked at the desk, answered here.

Yes, and it is a policy rather than a hope. Your plan carries a total, a date, and the name of the clinician who wrote it. If treatment runs long because we found something the scan could not show, we tell you before we do anything, and the plan is reissued — never the invoice quietly increased afterwards.

No clinician at LumiDent is paid on production, so there is nothing to gain. Early enamel lesions are monitored with photographs and recorded as “watch” rather than drilled. Roughly one in five of our examinations ends with no treatment recommended at all, and we still charge the examination fee — which is why it is $145 rather than free.

Fillings and onlays are guaranteed five years, crowns and veneers seven, implants ten. Inside that window the correction carries no professional fee — you pay only for a new shade if you have changed your mind about colour. Guarantee terms are printed on the recall card and the material batch is recorded against your file.
