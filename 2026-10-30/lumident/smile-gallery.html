A note on these images Each comparator is built live in the browser from one photograph supplied by the patient, with the untreated state rendered by a CSS filter rather than a second, less flattering exposure. Individual results differ: bone volume, gum biology and habits change what is achievable, and the fee you are quoted will be the one written on your own plan. Full unretouched records for every case are held at the practice and released to prospective patients on request.

Getting here

  • Streetcar: Line A to Pearl & 5th, then ninety metres east. The archway is immediately on your right.
  • Driving: Two free hours on the north side of Pearl between 5th and 6th. The disabled bay outside the archway is unmarked — telephone and it will be released.
  • Cycling: Two racks under the canopy and a shower on Level 2 for hygiene patients who cycle in.
  • Interpreter: Spanish, Russian, Vietnamese and ASL can be arranged with forty-eight hours’ notice at no charge.
