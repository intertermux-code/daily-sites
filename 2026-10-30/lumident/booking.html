Registry — Before You Arrive

Four small things that make the visit shorter.

None of these are mandatory and none of them will delay your appointment if they are missing.

  • Eat beforehand. Sedation appointments need a light meal three hours before, and a numb lip is a poor lunchtime companion.
  • Bring your medication bottles rather than a list from memory — the dosages matter more than the names.
  • Send old X-rays ahead if you have them elsewhere. Email records@lumidentclinic.com and we will load them into your file before you sit down.
  • Arrive five minutes early, not twenty. The sixty minutes you were booked is entirely chair time; there is no paperwork to fill in standing up.

Registry — After the Requisition

Four steps between this form and the chair.

Every patient gets the same sequence, and you will be told which line of it you are on at all times.

  1. Step 01

    Desk confirms in writing

    Within one working day, by email. You get the date, the duration, the clinician, and the fee band for what you asked about — so there are no surprises on the form.

  2. Step 02

    Registration, not paperwork

    Medical history is taken by the clinician, not a clipboard. Bring a medication list and your last two X-rays if you have them somewhere else.

  3. Step 03

    Sixty minutes to look

    Examination, photographs, chart to the millimetre, and a conversation about what the numbers mean. Nothing is begun on the first visit unless you ask for it to be.

  4. Step 04

    Your plan, on paper

    Options ranked by urgency with fees beside each line. Accept all of it, part of it, or none of it. Declining costs nothing and is not held against you.
