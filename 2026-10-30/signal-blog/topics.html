Subjects We Do Not Have a Desk For

And therefore do not pretend to cover

Consumer apps Unless an app is the reason a cable got laid or a grid got a new substation, we leave it to the trade press.

Funding rounds We report on a financing when it changes what gets built where. A priced round with no consequence is not news.

Rumours We will not publish what we cannot source twice, and we do not use the phrase “sources say” to mean “I hope.”
