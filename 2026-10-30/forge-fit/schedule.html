02

Reading the board

Load scale · booking · caps
  • Load 1/5Walking pace, breath work, no bar. Anyone can attend.
  • Load 2/5Technique and bodyweight. Sore-day friendly.
  • Load 3/5Working sets at RPE 6–7. You can hold a conversation after.
  • Load 4/5Heavy. Bracing required, chalk recommended.
  • Load 5/5Max-effort intervals or top singles. Coach checks in on you.

Full classes keep a three-name waiting list at the desk. Two of our last four seasons were won off a waiting list, so put your name down.

Booking rules

Reserve, drop in, or walk onto the floor

  • Members reserve up to fourteen days ahead; a spot holds for fifteen minutes past the start.
  • Drop-in is $22 and books forty-eight hours ahead, pay at the desk before the class.
  • Barbell classes cap at twelve, conditioning at sixteen, rebuild at fourteen.
  • Open floor never needs a booking — swipe in any hour the doors are open.
  • No-show twice in a month and your booking window shortens to seven days. It keeps the board honest.

Dawn block

05:45

Six coached sessions before eight. Doors unlock 05:30, coffee is on at the desk, and the platforms are yours until 08:00.

Lunch block

12:15

Thirty-five minutes, door to door. We keep towels stocked and the showers free of queues between 12:30 and 13:30.

Evening block

18:30

The busiest hour of the week is Tuesday at 19:00. If you want that slot, book it on Sunday night.
