01

Getting in the building

Harbor District · Zone 2

On foot or by bike

Six minutes from the kiln stop

Lines 22 and 28 stop at Kiln Street and Anchor Row. Bike racks sit under the loading-canopy awning, out of the rain, and there is a low-kerb approach the whole way from the towpath.

Driving

Twenty free yard spaces

Enter through the main gate and keep left of the chimney. Trainers and vans fit under the mezzanine beam at 2.4 m. No barrier, no ticket, no app.

Accessibility

Step-free to the platform deck

The loading-bay ramp is 1:12 with a level landing. Two accessible changing stalls, a level shower, and a bench press station set up with a low-profile rack for wheelchair transfer — tell the desk and a coach will rig it before you arrive.

Bring

Flat shoes and a bottle

Belts, wraps, chalk and towels are on the floor. If you only have running shoes for a barbell class, ask — we keep a spare rack of twelve pairs by size.

What we write for crews

Shift-proof conditioning

Fire, EMS, hospital and transit teams train around rotas, not around a class timetable. Andre builds a twelve-week programme with three fixed slots, a scaled line for every exercise, and a load that accounts for the fact that some of your people arrived off a night shift.

06

Good to know before your first visit

Six things we tell everyone

01 · Arrive ten early

Meet the coach at the desk

Say which class, tell them anything that hurts, and they will set your scaled line before the warm-up.

02 · Chalk is on the floor

Belts and wraps too

Liquid chalk at the racks, blocks of it on the mezzanine. Borrow a belt at the desk any time.

03 · Noise is normal

A rivet works makes sound

Bars rack loud and coaches call out. Ear defenders for the platform end are free if you want them.

04 · Film your sets

Three fixed angles

Tablets are mounted on platform two, four and seven. Ask the duty coach and they will send the clip.

05 · Kids are welcome

Family room, no fee

One family changing room with a bench, and a quiet corner off the mezzanine for a teenager with homework.

06 · Coffee at the desk

From 05:30, free for members

A two-group head, cups washed and returned. This is the real reason the dawn class keeps its spots.
