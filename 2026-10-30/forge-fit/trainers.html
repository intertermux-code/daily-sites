C-01 · Dana Reyes · Powerlifting

Dana Reyes

Dana built the programming system the whole gym runs on: twelve-week blocks, published targets and a test day every quarter. Before Forge she spent six years coaching a club that trained in a church basement, which is why she still insists on writing the session down before anyone touches a bar.

Her lifters hold seven of the ten club records across the three lifts. She coaches the Monday squat class, the Thursday heavy deadlift and every member who wants to stand on a platform for the first time — usually at 05:45, usually with coffee already going at the desk.

  • USAW Level 2 Sports Performance Coach, current.
  • IPF-certified technical official; judges our intra-club meets.
  • Competition total 612.5 kg at 83 kg bodyweight, 2024.
  • Runs the movement-screen standard every new member passes through.

What every coach here has to do

Non-negotiables
  1. Hold a current accreditation and complete twenty hours of continued education a year, on our time, not yours.
  2. Lift or train their own discipline weekly. A coach who has not felt a heavy single in six months teaches differently.
  3. Pass the safeguarding course and the spotting standard, re-tested every spring in front of the whole bench.
  4. Write the session before it is taught, and keep it on the board so a member can challenge the plan.
  5. Give feedback in the room, not after: one correction per lifter per set, in a sentence you can act on.
