01

How we train here

Teach · Load · Measure

Every member starts with a movement screen and a number: a back squat, a bench press, a deadlift, or the closest safe version of each that you can perform today. That number is the plan. From there you follow a block — four weeks of scheduled work with defined sets, reps and progressions, printed on the board and mirrored in your account.

Coaches teach in pairs on the floor, so one half of the room works while the other half is corrected. We keep class sizes at twelve for barbell sessions and sixteen for conditioning. If the movement breaks down, the load comes off. Nothing here is guessed, and nothing is left as a poster on the wall.

  • Movement screen inside your first two sessions — free, and re-run every twelve weeks.
  • Written blocks you can see on the board and in the app before you walk in.
  • Calibrated plates and competition bars, so your numbers mean something outside this room.
  • One coach per six lifters on every platform class, always.

Rule 01

Technique before load, every single time.

A lifter who can hold a neutral spine at 140 kg is a lifter who can train for twenty more years. We will stall your progress on purpose if your position is going. It is the only way a gym earns the word “coaching”.

— Dana Reyes, Head Coach

Three programs, one floor

Pick a lane or mix them

01

Powerlifting

Squat · Bench · Deadlift

Twelve-week blocks built around the competition totals. Wave loading, deliberate deloads, and a meet-day plan for anyone who wants to stand on a platform in front of judges. Beginners are coached from an empty bar; advanced lifters get individualised openers.

  • 60 min
  • Max 12 per class
  • 12 on the board
  • Beginner → competitor

Read the block

02

HIIT & Conditioning

Work capacity, measured in watts

Interval sessions on the sled track, row, bike and barbell complexes. Every class publishes its target average power, so you can see whether today was honest. Thirty-five minutes, hard, with a warm-up that does not waste your time.

  • 35 min
  • Max 16 per class
  • 12 on the board
  • All levels scaled

Read the engine work

03

Mobility & Rebuild

Range you can load

Forty-five minutes of controlled articular rotations, loaded stretching, breath work and single-leg stability. Built for lifters who are stiff, sore or returning after time off — and for anyone over forty who wants to keep training hard for decades.

  • 45 min
  • Max 14 per class
  • 7 on the board
  • Return-to-training
