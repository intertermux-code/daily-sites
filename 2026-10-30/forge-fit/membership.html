Your free training week

7 days · every class · no card

Seven days of everything on the board, a movement screen, and a coach who walks you through the block you would follow if you joined. Pick a start date and we will confirm by email the same day.

  • Every coached class is open to you for the week — powerlifting, conditioning and rebuild.
  • A forty-five minute movement screen with a named coach, scheduled inside your first two visits.
  • Your written block, so you can see exactly what twelve weeks would look like.
  • No card details, no automatic conversion, no follow-up call unless you ask for one.
  • Bring flat shoes, a water bottle and whatever you normally wear to move in.

06

The contract questions

Answered plainly
Is there a joining fee or a minimum term?

Neither. There is no joining fee and no minimum term beyond the thirty days of notice on every track. You can move between Foundation, Unlimited and Competitor at the start of any billing month.

What happens if I travel or get injured?

Foundation and Unlimited can pause twice a year for up to thirty days each; Competitor pauses up to ninety days. A signed note from a clinician pauses billing for as long as you genuinely cannot train.

Can I freeze my membership over winter?

Yes, within your two pause windows. We do not recommend it — the members who return strongest are the ones who dropped to Foundation instead of stopping entirely.

Do you take payroll deduction or employer billing?

We invoice three local employers directly, and crews of four or more from one workplace pay $124 per person. Ask for Andre at the desk or use the crew enquiry form on the visit page.

What if I do not like it after joining?

Full refund in your first thirty days as a paying member, no interview and no retention script. Fourteen people used that window last year; nine of them came back within six months.

Is there a better option for a student on a tight budget?

Foundation at the student rate of $69 gets you two coached classes a week and the whole open floor. It is the cheapest way to train here that still includes a written programme.
