01Read-only role · 4 minutes

Grant a read-only warehouse role

Pulseboard never asks for your raw credentials and never writes to your tables. Hand it a role scoped to the schemas you choose; our wizard tests the connection, then lists the columns it can see. Nothing leaves the warehouse except the result set your analyst asked for.

  • Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, Databricks, Postgres
  • VPC peering or private link, no public egress
  • SOC 2 Type II and GDPR data-processing addendum
02Three tables · one afternoon

Model events, users and accounts

Map an identifier column, a timestamp column and a payload, and the whole taxonomy appears: every event name, every property, the cardinality of each. The metrics layer lets you define “activated account” once so the funnel, the alert and the board all agree on what the number means.

  • Auto-detected property types, no tracking-plan rewrite
  • Versioned metric definitions with review history
  • dbt manifest import for existing models
03Day one onwards

Ask questions in plain language or SQL

Product managers build funnels by dragging steps; analysts drop into the same question as SQL and can read the generated query line by line. Boards refresh on warehouse wake-up, and an anomaly watch sits on the six metrics your team said actually matter.

  • Funnels, retention, paths, cohorts, distribution
  • Editable SQL with explain plans and row counts
  • Slack, PagerDuty and email alert routing

05 Why teams switch

Three objections we kept hearing, answered in the product

Every claim here is a link to a section on the features page, with the trade-off written down next to it.

01

Sampling was the whole problem

Engines that pre-aggregate hide the long tail: your enterprise churn, your worst funnel step, your one country that regressed. Pulseboard queries raw rows, so a segment of 214 users is 214 users.

Read on raw cardinality →
02

Nobody trusts two different numbers

Define the metric once in the metrics layer and every chart, alert and export resolves through it. When finance and product disagree, the review history shows which definition changed and when.

Read on governance →
03

Dashboards nobody opened

Anomaly watches run on your cadence and message the channel where the work happens, with the query attached. You find out from Slack, not from the weekly review.
