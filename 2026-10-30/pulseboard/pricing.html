Billing What we meter and what we do not

No charge per event, ever

Event-count pricing punishes instrumentation: teams stop tracking things they might want later to protect a bill. We meter the two resources that actually consume capacity — seats and scan — and publish both as visible numbers inside the product.

SeatsA seat is a person who opens Pulseboard in a calendar month. Viewers count; service accounts, alert routers and API tokens do not.
ScanCounted as event rows read by a query, with caching and refresh schedules shown separately so you can see what a board actually costs.
OverageNothing hard-fails mid-quarter. Past allowance, refreshes throttle first and a purchase order converts the throttle back to headroom.
WarehouseYour compute credits stay on your invoice. We estimate cost per query before it runs so nobody is surprised by a warehouse bill either.

FAQ Procurement will ask these

Billing questions

What counts as a seat?

A person who opens Pulseboard during the billing month. Someone who only receives Slack alerts, or whose dashboard is embedded in your own product, does not consume a seat. Service accounts and API tokens are free on every plan; we meter human logins, and the usage page lists them by name so you can see the count before invoicing closes.

What exactly is an event row scanned?

One row of your event table read by a query. Cached results do not re-count, and a dimension join is free. A funnel over 41 million rows counts 41 million; the same funnel opened again the next day, on the same table version, counts nothing. The workbench shows the estimate before the query runs.

Can we pay annually against a purchase order?

Yes. Annual invoicing is standard on Signal and Nucleus: one invoice, net 30, PO number on the order form, and multi-year terms available with a price cap. We invoice in EUR, USD or GBP and support bank transfer; card billing is available on Pulse.

Is there a free trial?

Not an expiring self-serve trial, because a warehouse connection needs a security review before it is meaningful. Instead: a 25-minute demo against a sample of your own data, then a 30-day guided onboarding included with every plan. If the first board is not in production use by day 30, cancel and pay nothing for that month.

What happens if we blow through the scan allowance?

Nothing breaks. Non-critical scheduled refreshes pause first, you get an email to the workspace owners with the top five queries by cost, and buying a capacity pack restores headroom immediately. We never hard-fail a query in the middle of a review.

Do you discount for startups and non-profits?

Forty percent off the first year for companies under three years that have raised less than €8 M, and the same rate for registered non-profits and public-health institutions, no year limit. Academic research groups get Nucleus at Signal pricing.

How do we leave if it does not work out?

Cancel from the billing page on month-to-month terms, or at renewal on annual terms. Your metric definitions and boards export as JSON, the SQL behind every saved question is yours to keep, and we delete result data within 14 days and confirm it in writing.
