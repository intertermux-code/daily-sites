05 Before you send

Three things people ask on the form

Do we have to connect the warehouse for the demo?

No. Most demos run on a CSV extract of a few thousand rows you send ahead of time, which is usually enough to model events and answer the question. Teams that want the real thing can connect a read-only role to a sandbox schema on the call; we ship the role script with the calendar invite.

Will this turn into a sequence of sales emails?

It will not. One form, one reply from whoever has capacity, and the thread stops when you say stop. Details are kept for 12 months so that a team picking the conversation back up in the spring does not start from zero, and deletion on request is confirmed in writing within 14 days.

Can you quote without a call?

Yes. Put your seats, engine, monthly event rows and desired refresh cadence in the form and choose “a fixed quote” under “also send me”. Median turnaround is six hours; the quote is itemised down to the capacity pack and shows the plan we recommend against your volumes, including when that is the cheaper one.
