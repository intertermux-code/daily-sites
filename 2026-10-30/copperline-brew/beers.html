How to Read Our Numbers

We print the readings we take, not the ones that sell. Here is what each dial on this page actually means.

ABV 0–12%

Wirecutter — 6.8% ABV

Alcohol by volume, measured on the distillation bench every batch. Our range runs 4.2% to 11.4%.

IBU 0–100

Wirecutter — 65 IBU

Chemical bitterness, not perceived. A 40 IBU hazy drinks softer than a 65 IBU West Coast because there is more sugar holding it.

SRM Colour 0–40

Rolling Mill — 17 SRM

How dark the beer is. 3 is a pilsner, 17 is our amber, 42 is the stout — the number we named the logo after sits at 14.

Gravity, start to finish OG → FG

Dryness Is a Math Problem

Original gravity is how much sugar is in the wort before fermentation; final gravity is what is left after the yeast stops. The gap between them is the beer’s body. Static Cling runs 1.044 to 1.010 — a small gap, so it drinks light. Ten Thousand Hours runs 1.112 to 1.034 — a huge gap with a lot still left behind, which is why it is thick and cellared.

Why the Wall Beats the Can

Every keg gets a date when it hits the wall and we write it on the handle. House average from tank to empty keg is nine days; the limit we hold ourselves to is fourteen. Cans leave the cooler at the three-day mark and stay good for about four months if you keep them cold and out of the sun — but hop aroma is the first thing to go, which is why the IPA is a tap beer and the amber is a can beer.

Pairings From the Truck Rotation

We do not run a kitchen. Four trucks hold court in the yard Thursday through Sunday, and we taste every beer against whatever is on the window that week.

Char & smoke P-01

Wirecutter IPA × Burnt Ends

The resin in a high-IBU beer cuts fat the way acid does. Order the burnt ends, then order a second Wirecutter before you decide you are not.

Brine & salt P-02

Patina Gose × Pickles and Oysters

Salt in the beer makes salt on the plate read as seasoning instead of brine. This is why the gose exists, and why it only lasts four months.

Roast & bitter P-03

Cold Draw Stout × Chocolate Torte

Match roast to roast. Anything sweeter than 70% cacao and the stout turns metallic; anything drier and you might as well drink coffee.

Fat & malt P-04

Rolling Mill Amber × Aged Gouda

Toffee malt and crystallised cheese are the same flavour at two different ages. This is the pairing we put in front of people who say they do not like beer.
