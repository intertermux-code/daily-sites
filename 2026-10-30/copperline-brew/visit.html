The Copper Door Is the One on the Corner

No cover, no reservations for the general room, and the bar does not stop pouring until the last person leaves. Twenty-minute walk from Jefferson Ave; the yard is free to park in until close.

By car R-01

I-75 to Jefferson Ave

Exit 49B, west on Jefferson for eleven blocks, left on Foundry. The gate at the north yard is open; spaces are unpaved and free until close. About 14 minutes from Midtown at off-peak.

By transit R-02

Eleven Minutes on Foot

The Jefferson Ave stop is a four-minute walk to the #43 and #84 buses; the Q-Line streetcar at Rosa Parks Center is a nine-minute walk. Both routes run past the last pour on Fri and Sat.

By bike / on foot R-03

Racks Inside the Gate

Ten spaces under the awning, visible from the bar. The Dequindre Cut greenway is a six-minute ride south; bring the dog, the patio has water bowls and a shade side.

Three rooms, one door

Where You Will End Up Sitting

Capacity numbers we actually enforce on busy nights, so you know what a Saturday at 8 PM looks like before you drive over.

Main Floor 30 seats

Long tables, north light

Six communal tables under the wired skylights, plus a 14-foot standing bar facing the brewhouse. Loud in the good way after 8 PM.

The Wall 10 + 4 lines

Ten house, four guest

Two lines are always in service for a cask or a special. Nitro blend on the end of the bar; the pilsner pours from a dedicated lager tower at 36°F.

The Yard 34 seats

Gravel, string lights, dogs

Food truck lane, two cornhole sets, one washer pit, and a shade side for summer. Heated from the glycol reclaim loop in winter.

House rules HR-05

What We Ask of You

Twenty-one and up after 9 PM. Under 21 is welcome until then, with an adult, on the patio and main floor. We card everyone who looks under thirty, including us.

Dogs yes, on the patio. Water bowls by the gate and along the wall. The brewhouse floor is wet and steel-toed, so dogs stay out of the tank room.

No outside alcohol, obviously. Cups, growlers, and crowlers only — and we purge your growler with CO₂ so it is worth carrying home.

Buy it here, drink it here. Michigan does not let you walk a six-pack to the car in an open case, so we will hand you a cooler bag at the bar and you can load up on Foundry.

Accessibility AC-01

Step-Free, Mostly

  • Ramped entrance at the north gate, 1:12 slope
  • Accessible restroom on the main floor
  • Bar height 40″ with a low counter section
  • Service animals welcome everywhere but the cellar
  • Loud music Thu–Sat; the quiet corner is table 6

Anything we have missed? Ask Ted before you come.

We cook nothing. That is the point.

Book the Brewhouse Tour

Walk the stack with Dana or Owen: mash-in to packaged, the barrel crawl, and four tastes pulled straight from the tank ports — including one that has not been approved for the wall yet. Twelve people per tour, and we genuinely need the headcount for grain math.
