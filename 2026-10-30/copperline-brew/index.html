The Wire · 12 BBL BH-01

One Stack, Ten Tanks, Two People on Flag Day

The Wire is a two-tank brewhouse we installed ourselves in the winter of 2014, welded into the footprint of the old drawing line so the floor still tells you where the copper ran. It does six batches a week, tops out at 12 barrels, and has never been replaced.

Mash tun 220 lb Lauter: manual rake Decoction: 3 rest Glycol: 4 tons

Read how the plant became a brewery

“We did not want a brewery that looked like a brewery. We wanted a room that already smelled like hot metal, and then we put tanks in it.”

Dana Whitlock · Co-founder & Brewmaster

Since 2014

Twelve Years, One Foundry Wall

Copperline started because Marcus Reyes found a lease nobody else wanted and Dana Whitlock had already quit the job that was supposed to pay the rent. Twelve years on, we still brew on the original stack, still crop our own yeast, and still hand out the anniversary batch instead of selling it.

  • Founded 2014 by two people and a loan
  • Independent, employee-owned since 2021
  • Spent grain to a farm 30 miles out, weekly
