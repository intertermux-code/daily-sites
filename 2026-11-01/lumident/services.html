Implantology & replacement

Replace one tooth without touching two

A missing tooth does not need a bridge. An implant is a titanium root placed in the bone you already have, with a ceramic crown screwed to it. Nothing healthy is cut away to make room.

We place roughly 240 implants a year across three clinicians, all of whom are on the same database you are — so your scans, your graft notes and your crown design stay in one record rather than three practices.

  1. Assessment and CBCT scanThree-dimensional imaging measures bone height, nerve position and sinus anatomy. We tell you at this appointment whether you have enough bone — no “we will see when we get there”.
  2. Written plan, two optionsImmediate load versus delayed load, with grafting costs shown separately if the ridge needs building. You take it home.
  3. Placement under local anaestheticForty minutes per root, guided by a printed surgical stent. Nitrous sedation available if you want it.
  4. Healing with a temporary toothThree to four months. You are never without a tooth in public — we make a hybrid or a bonded temporary on day one.
  5. Final crown and bite checkMilled in our lab room, colour-matched under three light sources, then reviewed at two weeks, three months and annually.

What £2,750 includes

CBCT and intraoral scans, the titanium fixture and abutment, surgery and anaesthetic, the ceramic crown, all review appointments for twelve months, and a written home-care plan.

What it does not include

Bone grafting (£380–£720) and sinus lift (£1,150) where the ridge has collapsed. Both are quoted after the scan, before consent, and neither is ever added to an invoice without a separate signed plan.

When we say no

If you smoke more than ten a day, have uncontrolled diabetes, or are under 18 with growth still underway, we will postpone rather than place. Failure rates in those groups are higher and we would rather wait.

Adults are the majority

Seventy-one per cent of our aligner patients are over 25. Second premolar extraction for lower crowding is a routine conversation, not a surprise halfway through.

Fixed retainers for a year, then a removable one

Every course ends with retention included in the price for twelve months. Relapse is the reason orthodontics gets a bad name; we do not charge you to fix it inside that year.

Orthodontics & alignment

Straight teeth, planned to the tenth of a millimetre

Clear aligners for most cases, fixed braces when the movement demands it. You see the finished position on a screen before you agree to start — including the compromises, not just the ideal.

  1. Scan and smile designDigital records take fifteen minutes. No impression putty, no gagging.
  2. Simulation you approveWe show the planned end result and the movements that get you there. If you dislike the outcome you do not start.
  3. Aligners in the postSix trays at a time, one per week, sixty to ninety minutes of wear to build back in each day.
  4. Monitoring every eight weeksScanned at home between appointments using an app we provide, so an in-person visit is only needed when something actually changes.
  5. RetentionBonded wire behind the front six teeth plus a vacuum-formed retainer, reviewed at one year and three years.

Which appliance suits your case

  • Clear aligners — crowding up to 6mm, mild rotations, spacing, and most relapse cases. £2,400–£3,900.
  • 6-week fast braces (front teeth only) — adults correcting a specific smile-zone problem. £1,650.
  • Fixed labial braces — deep bites, large rotations, extractions and bite corrections. £3,700–£4,600.
  • Interceptive treatment for children — assessed from age seven, expanders and removable appliances. £900–£1,800, NHS-eligible in many cases.

Cosmetic & restorative smile design

Start with the cheapest thing that works

Most people who ask for veneers need whitening and a small amount of bonding. We will tell you that in the first appointment, because the honest answer is cheaper for you and better for your teeth.

Whitening — £340

One in-chair session plus take-home trays and two syringes. Two to four shades lighter across six weeks, with desensitising gel included. Whitening does not work on crowns, veneers or fillings, so we sequence it before any replacement work.

Composite bonding — from £240 per tooth

Chipped edges, small gaps and worn corners reshaped in tooth-coloured composite, without cutting the tooth. Lasts five to eight years with care and can be topped up rather than remade.

Porcelain veneers — £690 per tooth

Prepared with a trial smile-in-composite first, so you approve shape and shade before anything is removed. Lab-made in Bristol, cemented with adhesive protocols we audit annually.

Crowns and onlays — £620

Pressed ceramic for front teeth, monolithic zirconia for molars. Same-day temporaries while the lab works, and a written occlusal check at fitting.

Shade, explained

Teeth are graded from darker to lighter on the Vita classical guide. Natural variation is normal: an A1 is not better than an A3, it is only lighter. We aim for the lightest shade that still matches your eye whites and skin tone.

Vita classical guide — A3 to B1

Marked: realistic target for a 38-year-old non-smoker after two weeks of trays

Smile design preview

A £60 preview appointment photographs your smile and builds a digital mock-up you can approve, adjust or reject. The fee comes off your treatment total if you go ahead.

Gum charting you keep

Six points per tooth, recorded every visit and printed for you. Six years of charts is the difference between “your gums look fine” and measurable evidence.

Children’s fluoride and sealants

Silver diamine fluoride stops an active cavity without a drill in young teeth; fissure sealants on new molars cost £28 each and cut decay risk substantially.

04

Preventive & hygiene

The appointment that prevents the other four

Hygiene here runs to fifty-five minutes with a registered hygienist, air-polishing for biofilm, hand instrumentation where pockets need it, and time to actually practise cleaning technique.

  1. AssessmentGum depths, bleeding points, recession and mobility recorded against your last chart rather than a memory.
  2. Deposits removedUltrasonic scaling plus glycin air-polish, then hand finishing on surfaces the machine cannot reach.
  3. Polished and fluoride-varnishedRoughness is where plaque returns first, so everything is polished smooth and varnished at 5,000ppm.
  4. Technique, not lectureFifteen minutes on the specific surfaces that bled, with the brushes and interdental sizes that fit your mouth.
  5. Recall set togetherThree, four or six months, agreed with you. Short recalls are for documented disease, not for the practice’s diary.

Periodontal therapy

Where pockets exceed 4mm with bleeding, a course of deep cleaning under local anaesthetic is more effective than repeated superficial scaling. Two visits, reviewed at eight weeks, then maintenance: £420–£850 for the full course.

Snacking, reflux and dry mouth

We spend real time on the causes: frequency of snacking, mouth-breathing at night, medication that dries the mouth, and whitening toothpastes that are too abrasive. Prescription high-fluoride toothpaste is £9 rather than £25.

Urgent & emergency

Seen within four hours, or we find you another chair

Two emergency slots every morning and two every afternoon, held open on purpose. Registered patients come first in the day; unregistered patients are still seen, at the same published fee.

  • Knocked-out adult tooth — pick it up by the crown, rinse in milk, put it back in the socket or keep it in milk, and get here within the hour. Success falls sharply after sixty minutes.
  • Knocked-out baby tooth — do not reimplant. Bring it in anyway and come straight in; we X-ray for root fragments.
  • Swelling with fever or difficulty swallowing — call the emergency line immediately. This is a same-day clinical priority, not a next-week appointment.
  • Lost filling or crown — keep the crown in a bag, avoid chewing on that side, and we re-cement or replace the same day in most cases.
  • Toothache that wakes you at night — usually means the nerve is dying. We can usually relieve it in a single 30-minute visit.

Outside our hours, ring the emergency line and the on-call dentist will triage you by phone. If you need accident-and-emergency care rather than dental care, they will tell you that in the first minute and call ahead for you.

Typical urgent fees

Examination with X-ray £85 · Pulpotomy or drainage £145 · Re-cementing a crown £110 · Emergency temporary filling £78. Full root treatment is planned as a separate, quoted course.

Insurance

We do not take commission on dental insurance. If your existing policy covers part of a fee, we will claim on your behalf and quote you only the balance.

Twenty-two local employers pay part of our membership plans. Ask reception to check whether yours is one of them — it takes a minute.

If a fee is genuinely out of reach, tell us. We will stage the treatment over longer periods or refer you to an NHS practice where the clinical result is equivalent.
