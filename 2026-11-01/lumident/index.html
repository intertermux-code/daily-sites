02

Your first visit, minute by minute

Fifty minutes. No drilling, no upsell, and nothing decided that you have not had time to think about.

  1. 00:00

    Forms already done

    We email your medical history form the day before. Fill it in on the sofa, or tell us and reception will do it with you.

  2. 00:08

    Look, listen, photograph

    Your dentist examines every tooth, gum pocket and joint, then shows you the intraoral photos on a wall screen — not a tiny mirror you cannot see.

  3. 00:26

    Two or three plans, ranked

    Always the cheapest that works, then the best long-term option, with the trade-offs written down. You take the paper home.

  4. 00:41

    You decide, later

    Nobody books you into treatment in the chair. Plans hold their price for ninety days, and a dentist calls you within two working days to answer questions.

  5. 00:50

    Hygiene before anything else

    If you want to start with a clean rather than a plan, that is a completely reasonable first appointment. Book it and we will see you.

The equipment, and what it changes for you

CBCT 3D scanner
Bone height, nerve position and sinus anatomy measured before a single implant is ordered. No guesswork, no second surgery to fix the first.
Intraoral scanner
Digital impressions in ninety seconds. No trays, no gag reflex, and a file we can send to any lab in the country.
Er:YAG laser
Gum recontouring and cold-sore treatment without a scalpel. Most patients resume work the same afternoon.
Nitrous oxide sedation
Conscious sedation for anxious patients and long restorative sessions. You drive yourself home afterwards.

Ceramic crowns and veneer try-ins manufactured in our lab room, so many cases finish in two visits rather than five.

07

Straight answers

The seven questions reception is asked most often, answered by the clinical team rather than the marketing department.

Do you take NHS patients?

Yes, within the Bristol NHS contract. Eligibility depends on age, pregnancy status and income band — reception can confirm in a two-minute call, and we will not push you toward private care if you qualify. Waiting list for NHS band 2 courses currently runs about nine weeks.

How much does an implant really cost, all in?

£2,750 covers the fixture, abutment, crown, scanning, surgery and twelve review months. Bone grafting, if your jaw needs it, adds £380–£720 and we confirm that at scan stage before you consent to anything.

I am genuinely frightened of dentists. What now?

Tell us on the booking form by ticking “I am anxious”. That books you a longer consultation, no drilling at the first visit, a raised hand signal to stop instantly, and a discussion of nitrous or oral sedation. Roughly a third of our patients tick that box.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes — 0% over 12 months on treatments above £600, subject to status, arranged in clinic on a tablet. Beyond that we offer 24 and 36-month plans at representative 9.4% APR. Membership plans from £22 a month cover exams and two hygien visits.

Will white fillings last as long as amalgam?

In small and medium cavities, yes — modern adhesive composites match amalgam at ten years in the studies we follow, and we use a rubber dam on every posterior filling, which is the detail that actually decides longevity. Large cavities are better served by a lab-made onlay.

What happens if I need to cancel?

Move or cancel free with 48 hours’ notice. Inside 24 hours we charge £30 for hygiene and £60 for surgical slots, because we could not fill them. Emergency patients are never charged for cancelling.

Are X-rays safe, and do I get the images?

Our digital sensors deliver roughly 80% less radiation than old film; a pair of bite-wings is about the dose of ten minutes of flying. Every image you take with us is yours — ask reception for a PDF or we will forward them to another practice directly.
