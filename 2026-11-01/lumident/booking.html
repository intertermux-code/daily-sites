      Before you come

      Five small things that make an appointment go better than you expect.

      Eat first, unless…

      Have a normal meal

      Except before a sedation appointment, where you need a light meal four hours beforehand and nothing at all two hours before.

      Bring

      Three items only

      Your medication list or boxes, any dental photographs from a previous practice, and your NHS number if you know it. Nothing else.

      Skip

      Whitening toothpaste that morning

      It temporarily alters how your enamel reads, which matters if we are matching a crown or veneer to your own shade.

      Allow

      Ten minutes either side

      Forms, a drink of water, and time to describe the problem properly. Running late by five minutes is fine — tell us and we will hold the chair.

      Bring with you

      Children, and their records

      Child-friendly waiting area with books, a lift with a buggy space, and we will let them sit in the chair and count teeth before anything else happens.

      Moving or cancelling

      48 hours is free

      Use the link in your reminder text, or ring reception. Inside 24 hours we charge £30 for hygiene and £60 for surgical slots — never for emergencies.
