03

Why these results differ so much

Two patients can pay the same £340 and get very different outcomes. Shade band, cause of staining and existing restorations decide more than the price does.

The shade ladder, honestly

Darker, warmer → lighter, cooler

Marked: where most take-home courses actually land after six weeks

Natural teeth are not white; they are translucent enamel over yellow dentine, and the dentine is what lightens. A two-shade gain is a good, honest result. Anything promising eight shades in an afternoon is selling you a filter.

What whitening will not do

  • CrownsBleach does not touch ceramic. Whitening first, then replacing restorations to match — never the other way round.
  • FillingsComposite edges stay exactly where they were. Small front fillings can be replaced afterwards, £110 each.
  • TetracyclineBanding from childhood antibiotics responds poorly to bleach. Veneers or bonding usually do the work instead.
  • FluorosisWhite spots can be improved by microabrasion in twenty minutes, £180, often before any whitening is needed.
  • AgeDentine thickens as we get older, so results plateau sooner. We say so at the consultation rather than after the second tray.
