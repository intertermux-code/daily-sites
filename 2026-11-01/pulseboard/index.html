Why teams switch

Stop re-deriving the same number every week.

Most analytics tools hand you a query builder and leave the judgment to you. Pulseboard keeps a memory: it knows which metric you own, which comparison you trust, and which explanation your executives accepted last time.

  • Definitions travel with the chart. Hover any figure for its source table, owner, and last change.
  • Comparisons are remembered. Save the segment logic once; reuse it in boards, alerts, and notebooks.
