How billing works

One invoice line you can explain to your CFO.

We count builder seats and governed metrics. Nothing else moves the number mid-term — not event volume, not dashboard views, not the size of the audience watching your boards.

  • Seats are monthly peak. If you hire eleven people in June, we bill the highest count in the term, then drop again.
  • Viewers never cost anything. Company-wide reading is the point of the product.
  • Pilot first, invoice after. Thirty days run without a purchase order; you only pay from the day you accept.
