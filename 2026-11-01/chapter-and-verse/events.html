The Standing Series

Recurring · p. 22

Four things that happen every week, whatever else the year does.

Every Thursday · 19.30

Thursday Poetry

Running since the second winter, when five of us sat in the back room and read to keep warm. Eight minutes each, no theme, no cover charge, and a rule that you may pass without explanation.

Front roomYear 14

Every Sunday · 10.30

Little Hands

Picture books read on the rug for pre-readers and the ones who are only nearly reading. Parents stay, most do not leave early, and there is a shelf of duplicates you may take home without asking.

Front room rugFree

First Saturday · 11.00

The Rare Books Table

Bring-and-value morning. We buy in cash up to £120 a transaction, and we will tell you when something is commoner than you hoped, which happens more often than the alternative.

Back roomCash on the day

Last weekday · 21.00

Closing Read

We turn the lights off one bay at a time and read ten minutes of whatever we are holding, out loud, to nobody. Regulars have started staying for it. We pretend to be annoyed.

Reserve by email Getting to the shop

We do not sell tickets. Names go on a card in a tin, and if you are not there fifteen minutes after the start we assume the rain won and give the seat to whoever is standing. Nobody has ever minded.

Recordings are made only when the reader has said yes, and they go on the shop’s page for six weeks. If you would like a poem read without your name attached, tell us before you stand up.

Children are at every event unless the listing says otherwise; Little Hands is built for them, and the Thursday poetry crowd has learned to talk around a small person asking for a drink of water.

“I have been to forty of these and I still do not know what a bone folder is until she explains it, twice, kindly.”
