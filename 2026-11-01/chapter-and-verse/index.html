§ 03

A Shop With Margins

Ground floor · p. 14

Two rooms, a basement, and a window seat that has caused three arguments this year.

We took the lease in the spring of 2011, when the unit next door was still a dry cleaner and the only rule was that we would not stock anything we had not finished. Fourteen years on, that rule has cost us money roughly eleven times and been worth it every single one of them.

The front room is new stock, faced outward so you can read the cards rather than the spines. The back room is secondhand, damp in winter, and holds the Gothic collection that put us on the map. The basement is the club room: fourteen chairs, one long table, a kettle that is louder than the radiator.

Come at ten on a weekday and you will have the place to yourself except for Tomás, who is shelving. Ask him anything. He will find the book, and then he will find the two books he thinks you actually wanted.
