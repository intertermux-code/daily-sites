What happens nextSteps

  1. Day 0 — you send this form.
  2. Day 1 — a real reply confirming or challenging your month.
  3. Day 3 — a sixty-minute call, no booking fee, cameras optional.
  4. Week 3 — your printed folio arrives: one route, two bases, pencilled transfers.
  5. Week 6 — we hold rooms and seats; £450 per person becomes the deposit.

Small print, honestlyTerms

  • Deposits are refundable in full for 21 days after you pay them.
  • We do not charge a planning fee for the folio, even if you do not travel.
  • Fares are quoted per person sharing; the single supplement is £340–£780 depending on the route.
  • If a season turns against us on the ground, we re-plan mid-trip at our cost, not yours.
  • Money is held in a bonded client account; you get the certificate with your confirmation.
