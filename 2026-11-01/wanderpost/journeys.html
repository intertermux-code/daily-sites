Included in every fareStd

  • Your named guide for the full trip, including arrival and departure days
  • All internal transfers — rail, ferry, 4×4, mule — booked and paid
  • Every dinner, and the market mornings that make them
  • Permits, temple night-entry fees, and hut reservations
  • A printed folio: hand-drawn route, packing list, phrase cards

Deliberately not includedExc

  • International flights — we book them, you hold the ticket and the mileage
  • Lunches on days you are on your own, which is half of them
  • Hotel upgrades you can arrange yourself once you know the town
  • Any “optional excursion” sold on the coach park of a temple
