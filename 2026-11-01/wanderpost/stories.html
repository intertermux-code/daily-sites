Lofoten · February

“I stopped counting the days around the fourth one. By the ninth morning I knew which neighbour was up before the light, and I said good morning in Norwegian, badly, and it was enough. We came for the aurora and left talking about the fish.”
Ingrid Holloway & Tom Bray Bristol · 14 nights, skrei season Guide: Lars Aarvik

Kyoto · November

“Haruko made us give up the list on day two. I resented it for a morning and then spent three hours in one garden with a raked gravel slope and a woman sweeping leaves into a shape that the wind kept ruining. That is the image I still have. Not one of the fourteen temples.”
Ada & Chidi Okafor Lagos / London · 19 nights, two bases Guide: Haruko Nasu

Patagonia · December

“Four days up to the spires instead of one bus morning. On the second night Iker put the torch out and the sky was so full I said something I would not repeat. I have been to the same mountain range twice since, on my own, and it does not give the same thing back without him.”
Marcus Deane Glasgow · 21 nights, estancia work Guide: Iker Barturen

High Atlas · April

“Fatima would not let us hold the spoon. Five mornings of watching, then on the sixth she handed me the wooden spatula and walked out. That tagine took four hours and we ate with three families from the village who had come because word travels. Nobody photographed it.”
Ruth Ellenwood Leeds · 16 nights, mule traverse Guide: Fatima Zerouali

Kyoto · March

“We took our eleven-year-old. The plan gave her two mornings a week with nothing arranged, which felt like a waste to me on day one. By day eight she had a friend at the paper mill, a Japanese vocabulary of eleven words, and a firm opinion about ferry timetables. She is fourteen now and still writes to Yoshie-san.”
Priya Raghunathan Dublin · 19 nights, parent and child Guide: Haruko Nasu

Lofoten · March

“Travelling alone at sixty-three, which my daughters described as brave and I described as bored. Fourteen nights and I never once had to make conversation. What I remember is the sound of the rorbu roof in wind and the fact that Lars asked after my wife, once, on the third day, and then not again.”
David Mulholland Belfast · 14 nights, solo Guide: Lars Aarvik
