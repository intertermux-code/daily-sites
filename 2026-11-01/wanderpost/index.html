The Method

Five months from first letter to first morning.

  1. A long conversation

    Sixty minutes, no booking fee. We ask about sleep, appetite, knees and books before we ask about countries.

  2. A season, chosen first

    The month is fixed before the map opens. Destination follows season, never the other way round.

  3. Hand-drawn draft

    One route, two bases, posted to you as a printed folio with the transfers pencilled in.

  4. We walk it again

    Every itinerary is re-walked within eighteen months of your departure. Things close; we check.

  5. Then we leave you alone

    One number, one person, answerable for the whole trip. Nothing to reconfirm at an airport desk.
