Getting Here

Park, Walk or Ride

The block is quiet after seven and the garage on 4th validates for three hours.

The Ankeny Garage entrance is on SW 4th, sixty metres from the door, and validates three hours at our host stand — bring the ticket up when you check in. Free surface parking on SW Clay and SW Main fills after six on weekends. No, we do not have a dedicated lot, and yes, the valet-style help at the curb is one of our hosts, not a paid third party.

Streetcar NS line to SW 5th & Jackson is four hundred metres: walk south on 5th, turn right on Ankeny. MAX Blue and Red lines to Pioneer Courthouse Square is a seven-minute walk through the bookblocks. The Aerial Tram does not help at night — it stops running long before our last ticket.

Step level from the street, a fifteen-centimetre threshold lip at the bar, and an accessible restroom on the ground floor. Four tables can take a wheelchair with the chairs pulled; mention it in the notes when you book and the host will keep one open rather than seat you and move furniture mid-service. Assistive dogs are welcome everywhere but the chef’s counter. The loft is lift-served.

The loft seats fourteen at one long table or stands thirty, with its own bar, service stair and sound. Monday buyouts are our cheapest nights and the only time the kitchen will cook a menu that is not on our board. Sommelier Nadia Reyes runs pairing dinners for eight to twenty on the first Thursday of alternating months — send a date range and a budget to the events address and she will reply with a plan.

Good to Know

Five Things Said at Almost Every Door

Dress

Nothing is enforced. Most guests arrive in a jacket without a tie; the counter gets smoke on whatever you wear, so choose accordingly.

Walk-Ins

Nine fire-bar stools are never reserved. Come before five on a weeknight, or after eight-thirty on Saturday, and you will very likely sit straight away.

Bill

Twenty percent service charge on tables of six or more, split any way you like. Card, cash, gift card, or a friend’s house account.

Take-Home

Bread, marrow butter and the dry-room trim are for sale at the host stand on Thursdays and Sundays until they run out.

Gift Cards

Sold at the stand and by phone, never expire, and settle any part of a bill. A wood-fired box of two can be couriered within the city.

Lost & Found

Coat check ticket goes in the drawer behind the bar for thirty days. Call the room with the date and the description and we will hold it.

Doors at Five

Put Your Name on Tonight’s Book

Two hours on the fire bar, four on the hearth, and a table that waits fifteen minutes for you.
