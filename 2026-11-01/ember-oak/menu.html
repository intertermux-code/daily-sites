Kitchen Notes

Before You Order

Four things we would tell every table if we had the time at the door.

The kitchen handles wheat, dairy, shellfish and tree nuts on one shared hearth, so we cannot promise a free-from plate. We can move a dish to the ash grill and rebuild a sauce, given a day’s notice. Tell us at booking and we will write your table’s version.

A 45-day primal loses close to eighteen percent of its weight to the crust and the bone, and we age only what sells within ten days. You are paying for the time in the cabinet and the herd that made it worth waiting for — not for an import label.

Half-portion pricing on everything from the fire, a small plate of bread, butter and potatoes for younger guests at no charge, and the kitchen keeps cooking until thirty minutes before close. Arrive late and the hearth will still be lit.

A flat twenty percent service charge is added to tables of six or more and distributed to the whole room, kitchen included. Tips on top go only to your server and are shared at their discretion. No one in this building works for tips alone.
