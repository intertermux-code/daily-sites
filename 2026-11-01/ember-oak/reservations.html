The Fine Print

Policies, in Plain Words

Four panels. Open one at a time.

We hold your table fifteen minutes past the booked time, then release it to the bar. Call and tell us you are running late and the table stays — that is the entire reason we ask for a phone number. A no-show on a party of six or more is charged the deposit, because that table was refused to someone else.

Nine to fourteen guests dine in the loft on a set three-course menu written with you a week ahead; fourteen to thirty standing can take the whole upstairs on a Monday buyout. A twenty percent service charge applies to every large party and goes to the whole room.

Families are welcome until eight in the dining room, with half portions on everything from the fire and a plate of bread and potatoes at no charge. Well-behaved dogs sit on the terrace year-round. The chef’s counter and the tasting menu are for guests over twelve, because the pass is hot and the service is long.

Gift cards are sold at the host stand and by phone, never expire, and can settle any part of a bill. Duplicate receipts are emailed on request with the date and the name on the table. Coat check runs on a paper ticket — the drawer behind the bar keeps anything unclaimed for thirty days.
