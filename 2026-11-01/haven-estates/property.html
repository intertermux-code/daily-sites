A Hundred Years of Stone, Three of Design

The Lantern House stood on Quarry Bend as a schoolteacher's house through the 1926 flood, then as a bakery office, then — quietly — as nothing at all for eleven years. When AtelierMarsh took the bones in 2021 they kept everything the valley made: the random-course stone, the oak newel post, the cellar where the bread proofed.

What they added is all glass and grace. A three-metre slot of a window in the kitchen that frames the orchard like a painting. A stair that turns once and lets the light fall through to the hall. Underfloor heating laid over the original flags, so the winter mornings stopped biting.

The result is not a museum and not a show house. It is a home where a family of five can lose each other happily — four bedrooms up, two of them sharing a dressing room, the fifth sleeping arrangement being the library daybed, which Roan insists on calling "the negotiation room."

“The best restoration I have walked in fifteen years, because it admits what it is old about.”

— Roan Achebe, senior agent

What Comes With the House

  • Walled Orchard

    Eleven apple and quince trees behind the 1920s brick wall; water laid on for the irrigation line.

  • Twilight Pool

    Sixteen-foot heated lap pool, west-lit by stone lamps on timers wired to the house system.

  • Chef's Kitchen

    La Cornue range, honed limestone benches, and a scullery with its own extractor — the mess stays hidden.

  • Double Library

    Two-storey oak shelving with the original rolling ladder, plus the daybed by the south window.

  • Cellar & Bake House

    Flagged cellar at a constant 12°C, with the 1926 proofing bench still in place and usable.

  • Annexe Studio

    Self-contained single room with WC and kitchenette above the garage — rented at $1,100 a month to date.

  • Whole-House Systems

    Motion-blinds on the west glazing, hard-wired security, 9.6 kW solar with battery, Tesla-ready charger.

  • Three-Car Barn

    Board-barn conversion with workshop bench run — currently sheltering a Land Rover and a boat trailer.

The Practical Questions

Single parties, forty-five minutes, hosted by an agent rather than left to a lockbox. We arrange them Tuesday through Saturday and will drive you around the neighbourhood first so you see the school run and the evening light before you see the house.

The guide is a range of opportunity, not a ceiling. We publish the dossier of nine sold comparables behind it; serious offers are reviewed with the owners on a fixed date, currently the 21st of December.

All fitted furniture, the La Cornue range and scullery equipment, the pool plant and covers, the library rolling ladder, and every light fixture except the owners' two brass chandeliers, which leave in January.

Quarry Bend sits inside the Old Harbor Primary catchment (rated outstanding, 900 m walk) and the Harbor Grammar boundary. The station park-and-ride is six minutes away; the city centre is thirty-four at 8 a.m., twenty-two at noon.
