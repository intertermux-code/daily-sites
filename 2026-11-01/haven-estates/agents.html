How the Studio Runs

  • Mondays and Sundays are for walking. No phones in, no offers out — the agents are simply in houses.
  • Fees are flat: 1.5% with no marketing surcharge, no "premium photography" line item, no clause for the in-house mortgage team because there isn't one.
  • Every listing gets a dossier — comparables, days-on-market maths, and the pricing argument in writing before a single word goes public.
  • We decline more than we accept. Roughly one house in five is turned down; if we think a seller will be let down by the market or by us, we say so at the kitchen table.

Pick the Agent, Not the Firm.

When you enquire, name the person you want — or tell us what matters and we will route you to whoever is best on it. Either way, a human answers within one working day.
