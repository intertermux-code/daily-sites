02. Capabilities

Instrumentation,
Not Decoration.

Every module in Pulseboard is engineered for signal fidelity. No vanity metrics, no decorative charts—only actionable telemetry presented with typographic precision.

01

Real-Time Cohort Streams

Track user behavior as it happens, not after batch processing. Our streaming engine segments cohorts on-the-fly with sub-second latency, allowing you to pivot campaigns while they’re still running.

  • Latency: <15ms p99
  • Retention: Infinite lookback
  • Segments: Unlimited nesting
02

Predictive Anomaly Detection

Stop reacting to outages. Pulseboard’s ML models learn your system’s baseline rhythm and alert you to deviations before they cascade into incidents. Configurable sensitivity thresholds prevent alert fatigue.

  • Models: Prophet + LSTM hybrid
  • Training: Continuous online learning
  • Alerts: Slack, PagerDuty, Webhook
03

SQL-Native Query Builder

For analysts who speak fluent SQL. Write queries directly against our columnar store with autocomplete, schema introspection, and saved views. Visualize results instantly without leaving the editor.

  • Dialect: PostgreSQL compatible
  • Performance: Vectorized execution
  • Collaboration: Shared workspaces
