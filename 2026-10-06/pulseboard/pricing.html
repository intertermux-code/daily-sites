Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans at any time?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing. Downgrades apply at the next billing cycle.

What happens if I exceed my event limit?

We never cut off your data. You’ll receive a notification at 80% and 100% usage. Overage is billed at a transparent per-event rate shown in your dashboard.

Do you offer discounts for startups?

Absolutely. Apply through our Startup Program page with proof of funding or incorporation for 50% off your first year.
