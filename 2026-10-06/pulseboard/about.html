04. Origin Story

Built by Engineers
Who Hated Dashboards.

We started Pulseboard in 2024 because we were tired of waiting 24 hours for "real-time" data. We believed analytics should be as responsive as the code that generates it.

The Signal Over Noise Manifesto

Most analytics tools are designed to impress executives in board meetings. Pulseboard is designed to help engineers debug production issues at 3 AM. We prioritize density over decoration, precision over prettiness, and speed over spectacle.

Our design philosophy borrows from Swiss International Style: grid systems, asymmetric layouts, and sans-serif typography aren't just aesthetic choices—they're functional decisions that reduce cognitive load when you're parsing millions of rows.
