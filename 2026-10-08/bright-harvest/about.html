Our Story

Bright Harvest began in 2010 when a group of community members recognized the growing need for food assistance in our area. What started as a small initiative distributing groceries from a church basement has grown into a comprehensive food security organization serving thousands of families.

Mission

To eliminate hunger in our community by providing immediate food assistance while addressing the root causes of food insecurity through education and advocacy.

Vision

A community where everyone has consistent access to nutritious, culturally appropriate food and the resources needed to thrive.

Values

  • Dignity: We treat every person with respect and compassion
  • Integrity: We operate with transparency and accountability
  • Community: We work collaboratively to strengthen our neighborhood
  • Excellence: We continuously improve our services and impact
