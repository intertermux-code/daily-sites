Join Our Volunteer Team

Become part of the solution to food insecurity in our community. Volunteers are the heart of Bright Harvest, helping us serve thousands of families each month through various programs and initiatives.

Volunteer Opportunities

Food Distribution

Help sort, pack, and distribute food to families in need at our warehouse or mobile pantry locations.

  • Weekday mornings and afternoons
  • Requires ability to lift up to 25 lbs
  • Training provided

Mobile Pantry Support

Assist with our mobile food distribution program that serves underserved neighborhoods.

  • Wednesday and Saturday shifts
  • Transportation provided
  • Customer service skills helpful

Kids Café Assistant

Help prepare and serve healthy meals to children while engaging in educational activities.

  • After-school and summer programs
  • Background check required
  • Experience with children preferred

Special Events

Support our fundraising events, food drives, and community outreach initiatives.

  • Flexible scheduling
  • Various roles available
  • Great for groups and families
