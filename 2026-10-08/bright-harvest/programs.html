Our Programs

Bright Harvest operates several programs designed to address food insecurity in our community through multiple channels and approaches. Each program is tailored to meet specific needs of different populations.

Mobile Pantry

Our Mobile Pantry brings fresh groceries directly to underserved neighborhoods, reaching families who face transportation barriers or live in food deserts. We visit 12 locations weekly, providing fresh produce, dairy, and pantry staples.

Impact

  • Serves 15+ locations weekly
  • Reaches 800+ families monthly
  • Provides fresh produce to food desert communities
Volunteer for Mobile Pantry

Kids Café

The Kids Café provides free, nutritious meals and snacks to children in low-income areas after school and during summer months. Our program combines nutrition with educational activities about healthy eating habits.

Impact

  • Serves 200+ children daily
  • Operates at 8 community centers
  • Provides 4,000+ meals monthly
Support Kids Café

Senior Support

Our Senior Support program delivers specially curated meals and groceries to older adults with dietary restrictions and mobility challenges. We prioritize foods that support healthy aging and manage chronic conditions.

Impact

  • Serves 400+ seniors monthly
  • Provides diabetic and heart-healthy options
  • Includes social connection component
Deliver to Seniors

Emergency Food Assistance

For families facing sudden crises like job loss, medical emergencies, or other hardships, our emergency program provides immediate food relief. We work with local social services to identify and assist those in urgent need.

Impact

  • Responds within 24-48 hours
  • Serves 300+ families annually
  • Provides culturally appropriate foods
