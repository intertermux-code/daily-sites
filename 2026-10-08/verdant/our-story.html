Our Story

Rooted in nature, crafted with purpose — the journey of Verdant begins with a simple belief that skincare should be both effective and gentle.

From Garden to Glass

Founded in 2018 by botanist Dr. Elena Greenfield, Verdant began as a personal quest to create skincare solutions that honor the skin's natural balance. After years of research into botanical extracts and their therapeutic properties, Dr. Greenfield established her laboratory in the Pacific Northwest, where she could source the finest organic ingredients.

Our commitment to purity means every ingredient is carefully selected for its proven benefits and gentle efficacy. We work exclusively with certified organic farms, ensuring that our botanicals are grown without synthetic pesticides or fertilizers.

Each formula is developed in-house using low-temperature extraction methods that preserve the integrity of active compounds. This meticulous approach ensures that every drop of our serums and creams delivers the full spectrum of botanical benefits.

Signature Ingredients

Wild Rosehip Oil

Rich in vitamins A and C, this golden oil helps regenerate skin cells and reduce the appearance of fine lines. Our rosehips are hand-picked from wild plants in the Chilean Andes.

Organic Jojoba Oil

Molecularly similar to our skin's natural sebum, jojoba oil provides perfect hydration without clogging pores. Sourced from desert farms practicing sustainable cultivation.

Plant-Derived Niacinamide

Our vegan niacinamide helps minimize pore appearance and regulate oil production. Extracted from locally sourced rice bran using eco-friendly processes.

Our Values

Sustainability

We package our products in recyclable glass and use biodegradable shipping materials. Our carbon footprint is offset through partnerships with reforestation projects.

Transparency

Full ingredient disclosure and sourcing information is available for every product. We believe in informed choices and honest labeling.

Efficacy

Every formulation undergoes rigorous testing to ensure visible results. We combine traditional botanical wisdom with modern science.
