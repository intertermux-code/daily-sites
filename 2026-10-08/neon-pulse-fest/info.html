EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW ABOUT NEON PULSE

VENUE DETAILS

Neon Pulse takes place at the iconic Metropolitan Complex, featuring multiple stages and immersive installations. The venue offers state-of-the-art sound systems, LED screens, and lighting effects that create an otherworldly atmosphere.

Address: 123 Neon Boulevard, Metropolis, NY 10001

Parking: Ample parking available on-site. Valet service available for VIP ticket holders.

Public Transit: Just 10 minutes from Central Station via Metro Line 2. Free shuttle service runs every 15 minutes from the station during festival hours.

EVENT HOURS

Day 1 (Dec 18): 4:00 PM - 2:00 AM

Day 2 (Dec 19): 2:00 PM - 2:00 AM

Day 3 (Dec 20): 12:00 PM - 1:00 AM

WHAT TO BRING

  • Valid ID and ticket (printed or on mobile device)
  • Comfortable shoes for dancing and walking
  • Light jacket or sweater (indoor venue can be cool)
  • Portable phone charger
  • Cash for food and merchandise (vendors accept both cash and cards)

WHAT NOT TO BRING

  • Professional cameras or recording equipment
  • Outside food or drinks
  • Large bags or backpacks (bag check available for smaller items)
  • Weapons, drugs, or illegal substances

FAQ

What happens if it rains? ▼

Neon Pulse is an indoor event taking place at the Metropolitan Complex, so weather won't affect the festival. Shows go on regardless of conditions outside!

Can I re-enter the venue after leaving? ▼

Yes, we offer re-entry throughout the festival. Make sure to keep your wristband on when leaving, as you'll need it to gain access again.

Are there age restrictions? ▼

The festival is 18+ for general admission. 16-17 year olds can attend with a parent or guardian present. Children under 16 are not permitted.

Is there food and drink available? ▼

Yes! We feature a variety of food trucks and vendors offering diverse cuisine options. Bars are available at each stage serving craft cocktails, beer, wine, and non-alcoholic beverages. Ticket holders receive food vouchers based on their ticket tier.

How accessible is the venue? ▼

The Metropolitan Complex is fully accessible with ramps, elevators, and designated viewing areas for guests with mobility needs. ADA restrooms are available throughout the venue. Contact us at accessibility@neonpulse.com for specific accommodation requests.

Can I upgrade my ticket at the venue? ▼

Yes, ticket upgrades are possible at the box office while supplies last. Payment can be made with credit card or cash.
