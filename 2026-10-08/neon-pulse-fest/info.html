INFO

Everything you need to know about Neon Pulse

Venue Information

Hartwell Arena

1234 Electronic Way
Detroit, Michigan 48226

Parking

Free parking available in adjacent lots. Arrive early as parking fills quickly. Valet parking available for VIP ticket holders.

Public Transit

Take the Q-Line streetcar to Grand Circus Park stop, then a short walk to the venue. Metro bus routes 1, 2, and 3 also serve the area.

Accessibility

Fully accessible venue with wheelchair ramps, elevators, and designated seating areas. ASL interpreters available upon request. Contact us 48 hours in advance.
