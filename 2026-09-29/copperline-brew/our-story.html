Our Brewing Heritage

From humble beginnings to becoming a cornerstone of the local craft beer scene, our story is one of passion, dedication, and unwavering commitment to quality.

The Beginning

Founded in 2010 by master brewer James Copperfield, Copperline Brewing Co. began in a small garage with a dream to create exceptional craft beer. What started as a passion project has grown into a beloved local institution known for our commitment to quality and community.

James had spent years perfecting his craft at various breweries across the country before returning to his hometown with a vision: to create beers that captured the essence of the region while pushing the boundaries of traditional brewing techniques.

Growth & Innovation

By 2013, demand for our unique brews had outgrown the garage. We moved to our current location, a converted warehouse that maintains industrial charm while providing space for larger batches and experimentation. Our original copper kettles, passed down through generations of James' family, remain the centerpiece of our brewing operation.

Our commitment to innovation led us to develop unique brewing processes, including our signature cold-conditioning method that enhances flavor complexity. We've also pioneered sustainable brewing practices, including water recycling systems and solar energy utilization.

Community Impact

Today, Copperline Brewing Co. is more than just a brewery – we're a community hub. We host weekly trivia nights, live music events, and collaborate with local restaurants and farmers. Our taproom has become a gathering place where locals and visitors alike come together over great beer and good company.

We continue to support local agriculture by sourcing our hops from Pacific Northwest farms and our malts from regional suppliers, ensuring freshness and supporting local economies. Each batch is carefully crafted and aged in our original copper kettles, maintaining the traditions that started it all.

Award Recognition

Over the years, our dedication to quality has earned recognition at numerous national and international competitions. Our Rustic Amber Ale received a Gold Medal at the Great American Beer Festival in 2018, while our Barrel-Aged Porter was awarded Best in Class at the World Beer Cup in 2020.

However, our proudest achievement remains the relationships we've built with our customers and the role we play in our community. Every beer we brew carries with it the story of our journey and our commitment to excellence.
