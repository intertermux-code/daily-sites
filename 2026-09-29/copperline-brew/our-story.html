OUR STORY

From blueprints to brews – the journey of Copperline Brewing

THE BEGINNING

In 2012, two engineering colleagues shared a passion for precision and craft beer. They envisioned a brewery that applied industrial methodology to brewing – measuring, calibrating, and perfecting each step of the process.

Starting in a converted warehouse, they installed custom copper brewing equipment designed to their exact specifications. The industrial aesthetic wasn’t just visual – it reflected their commitment to functionality and durability.

THE PROCESS

Every batch begins with detailed recipes and precise measurements. Our brewing system includes custom-designed copper vessels that ensure consistent temperature control and optimal flavor extraction.

We source the finest ingredients from trusted suppliers, maintaining relationships built on quality and reliability. Each ingredient is logged and tracked through our brewing process.

THE GROWTH

What started as a small operation has expanded while maintaining our core values. We’ve added production capacity, but never compromised on quality.

Our taproom opened in 2015, featuring the same industrial aesthetic as our brewing facility. Exposed copper pipes, steel fixtures, and reclaimed materials create an authentic atmosphere that celebrates our industrial heritage.

THE FUTURE

Today, we continue to innovate while honoring our engineering roots. Our team includes both experienced brewers and engineers who share our vision of precision craftsmanship.

We’re constantly refining our processes and experimenting with new recipes, always guided by our commitment to quality and consistency.
