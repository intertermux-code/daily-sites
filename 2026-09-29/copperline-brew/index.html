OUR STORY

Founded in 2012, Copperline Brewing began as a dream to bring industrial precision to craft brewing. Our founders, engineers by trade, applied meticulous attention to detail to create beers that are both innovative and approachable.

We've grown from a small taproom to a regional favorite, but our commitment to quality remains unchanged. Each batch is brewed with the same passion and precision that started it all.
