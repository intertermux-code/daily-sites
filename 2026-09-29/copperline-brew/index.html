Our Brewing Heritage

Founded in 2010 by master brewer James Copperfield, Copperline Brewing Co. began in a small garage with a dream to create exceptional craft beer. What started as a passion project has grown into a beloved local institution known for our commitment to quality and community.

We source our hops from Pacific Northwest farms and our malts from regional suppliers, ensuring freshness and supporting local agriculture. Each batch is carefully crafted and aged in our original copper kettles, passed down through generations.
