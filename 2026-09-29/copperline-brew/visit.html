Visit Our Taproom

Experience our craft beers in the heart of our brewery. Join us for fresh pints, brewery tours, and the warm atmosphere that makes Copperline special.

Location & Directions

Located in the historic district of Townsville, our taproom occupies a beautifully restored 1920s warehouse. The industrial architecture complements our brewing heritage, featuring exposed brick walls, steel beams, and our original copper brewing equipment on display.

Brewery Tours

Take a behind-the-scenes look at our brewing process with guided tours offered daily at 4pm, 6pm, and 8pm. Tours last approximately 45 minutes and include a tasting of three of our flagship beers. Advanced reservations are recommended, especially during weekends and holidays.

Food Options

While we don't have a kitchen, we welcome patrons to bring their own food or order from our rotating selection of food trucks that park outside our facility. We also partner with local restaurants for delivery to our taproom.

Special Accommodations

Our taproom is fully accessible with wheelchair ramps and accessible restrooms. We offer a quiet space for nursing mothers and have designated areas for those sensitive to noise. For private events, our event space accommodates up to 150 guests and can be reserved for corporate events, wedding receptions, and special celebrations.

Parking Information

Free parking is available in our lot located behind the building, with additional street parking available along Brewery Lane and surrounding streets. During peak hours, parking can be limited, so we recommend arriving early or using ride-sharing services.
