    The system

    One ring. Everything that matters today.

    Most planners are a list that scrolls forever. 3jS has a fixed circumference — twenty-four hours — so the shape of your day is something you can see at a glance.

    01
    ◷

    A clock, not a list

    Time is the x-axis. Sunrise sits on the right, noon at the back, midnight in front. An overfull evening is visible before you promise anything.

    02
    ✥

    Drag to reschedule

    Grab any light and slide it around the track. The time snaps to five-minute marks and the day rearranges in real time. No dialog, no re-entry.

    03
    ◉

    Priority as orbit lane

    High priority pulls inward toward the sun. Low priority drifts to the outer rim. You read the shape of your day before you read a single word.

    04
    ✷

    Supernova on done

    Completing a task detonates the node into particles and cools it to a spent orbit. Small, but it is the part your brain actually rewards.

    05
    ☀

    Light that follows the hour

    The sun runs its own cycle against your real clock — cold and blue before dawn, amber at the edges, white at noon. Open it at 6am and it feels like 6am.

    06
    ⌂

    Stays on your device

    Everything lives in this browser's local storage. No account, no sync service, no telemetry. Close the tab and tomorrow is still there.

    Three moves

    From blank sky to full orbit in a minute

    Name three things

    Type a task, give it a time, pick a lane. It appears on the track the moment you press enter. Most people never need more than five or six lights.

    Push them around

    Drag a node to the hour it actually belongs in. Switch to top-down view when the afternoon gets crowded, and spread things out by hand.

    Let them land

    Tick a task off and watch the ring fill. When the arc closes, the day is done — and the streak keeps itself warm for you.

    Day in review

    Your day leaves a shape behind.

    3jS keeps a record of every orbit you fly. Finished tasks, kept promises, and the days you quietly did not show up for.
