Everything in the ring

A to-do list with a circumference.

Twenty-four hours is a fixed circle. Put your tasks on it and the shape of your day stops being something you imagine. Here is everything 3jS does with that circle.

01 · The track

Time is an axis, not a field

A scrolling list has no end, so it never tells you that you have planned fourteen things after dinner. The 3jS track has a circumference, and when the lights bunch up you can see the collision before it happens.

  • 06:00 sits on the right, noon at the back, midnight in front — the ring maps directly onto a real clock face.
  • The lit arc sweeps from sunrise to the current moment, so the track is also a progress bar for the day itself.
  • Hour ticks every hour, heavier every six, so you can place a task by eye.

02 · Direct manipulation

Move a task by moving it

Re-typing a time is the friction that makes people stop maintaining a plan. In 3jS you grab the light itself and slide it around the track. It snaps to five-minute marks, the label re-renders live, and the list on the right re-sorts itself while you are still holding it.

  • Raycast picking — the node under the cursor lifts and glows before you commit to anything.
  • Occupancy relief — nodes that land on the same five-minute slot relax apart automatically so nothing hides behind anything.
  • Select to inspect — click without dragging and the task highlights in the panel, with its full title.

03 · Lanes

Priority has a radius

High priority pulls inward toward the sun. Medium rides the track. Low drifts out to the rim where it can be seen but not touched. A finished task collapses toward its own lane and cools to a spent grey, so completed work visibly recedes.

  • High — the innermost dashed lane, rose. Reserve it for the two or three things that actually matter.
  • Medium — the main track, amber.
  • Low — the outer lane, teal. Nice if it happens, fine if it doesn't.

04 · Feedback

The reward is the animation

Ticking a checkbox in a flat list is a two-pixel event. Ticking one in 3jS detonates the node into forty-four particles that fall away and fade. It takes 1.5 seconds and it is the single moment people mention.

  • Particle burst with drag, and gravity on the fragments.
  • Growth curve — new tasks scale up with an ease-out overshoot as they appear.
  • Selection lift — the focused node scales 1.45× and reveals its full title in 3D.
  • Full-orbit toast when the last task of the day lands.

05 · The sun

Light that knows what time it is

The sun is not decorative. Its colour and intensity are derived from your system clock: cold and blue before dawn, deep amber at the edges of the day, white and bright at noon. Open 3jS at 6am and it looks like 6am.

  • Noon — 0xfff3d6, point light at full intensity.
  • Dawn & dusk — blends toward 0xff8f45 at 22° above the horizon.
  • Night — 0x5a7bd6, intensity drops to roughly a fifth.

06 · Ownership

No account, no server, no trail

3jS is a static page. Your tasks are written to this browser's local storage under a single key and nowhere else. There is no sign-up, no sync, no analytics script and nothing to delete your day for you.

  • Works offline — the whole site, renderer included, is vendored locally.
  • Every day is kept, so you can page backwards through your own history.
  • Clear day is one tap away when you want a blank sky.
