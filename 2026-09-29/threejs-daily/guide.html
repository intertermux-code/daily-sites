Flight manual

Learn to fly the orbit.

Everything 3jS does is on the surface, but a few gestures are worth knowing. This is the whole manual — it takes about four minutes to read and about thirty seconds to learn.

Controls

Pointer, touch and keys

Dragging empty space moves the camera. Dragging a light moves the task. The app tells them apart for you.

Drag empty spaceOrbit the camera around the ring
Drag a lightReschedule that task, snapped to 5 minutes
Click a lightSelect it and reveal its full title in 3D
Scroll / pinchDolly between 8.5 and 34 units
Arrow keysRotate the camera when the scene has focus
◱ buttonToggle between orbit view and top-down view
⤢ buttonPull the camera out to see the whole system
? buttonOpen the controls sheet without leaving the day
NJump to the task field
/Same thing, for people who type like it
TSnap back to today from any past day
EscDeselect, and step out of the text field
EnterAdd the task and stay in the field
TabEvery control is reachable in order
SpaceActivate the focused button or link

First ten minutes

Setting up a day that holds

Clear the sample

The app opens with a sample orbit so you can see it work. Hit Clear day when you're ready to use it for real — nothing else needs setting up.

Three anchors, not thirty

Put in the two or three things that must happen. Give them real times. The rest can be added during the day, which is when you'll actually know what they are.

Switch to top-down

Use the ◱ button when the afternoon gets crowded. Looking straight down at the ring makes gaps obvious and spreading things out takes two seconds.
