SECTOR 7 INDUSTRIAL PARK

THE LOCATION

Located in the historic Sector 7 Industrial Park, the venue offers 50 acres of concrete, steel, and open space transformed into a neon playground.

  • Address: 404 Circuit Ave, Tech District
  • Shuttles: Running every 15 mins from Central Station
  • Parking: On-site parking available ($40/pass)

FREQUENTLY ASKED

What items are prohibited?

No glass containers, professional cameras, drones, or outside alcohol. See full list in Terms of Service.

Is there an age limit?

Neon Pulse is an 18+ event. Valid government ID is required for entry.

Can I bring my own food?

Sealed water bottles are allowed. Outside food is not permitted in the arena, but allowed in camping zones.

How does cashless work?

Your wristband is your wallet. Top up online before the fest or at top-up stations on site.
