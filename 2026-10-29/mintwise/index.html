§ 03

“Bank-grade” is our starting point, not the slogan

You are handing an app the shape of your entire life. It should be audited, encrypted, permissioned and honest about what it can see.

  • SOC 2 Type II

    Audited annually by an independent firm. Report available on request, NDA-free.

  • 256-bit AES

    Every record encrypted at rest; TLS 1.3 in transit; keys rotated every 90 days.

  • Read-only access

    Aggregators can balance and list, never pay. No transfers, no writes, no exceptions.

  • FDIC-insured partners

    Balances held at Lincoln Federal and Northbrook Trust, both FDIC members.

  • No data sales

    We charge you for the product. Advertisers get nothing — not even anonymised trends.

Mintwise is a budgeting and financial-literacy tool, not a bank or lender. Account coverage depends on your institution.
