§ 05 — Feature 04 / 09

The weekly number, and the four inputs behind it

Most budgeting apps ask you to plan thirty lines you will abandon by Thursday. Mintwise publishes one figure every Monday and shows its working.

Input one — committed outflows

Rent, mortgage, insurance premiums, council tax, loan payments and school fees that fall inside the window. Annual bills are smoothed into the week they actually land in, not spread evenly across a year you will never read.

Input two — your own rhythm

Mintwise studies your last twelve months of variable spending and keeps the shape of it: the Monday supermarket, the Friday take-away, the end-of-month social burst. The weekly number is built around your habits rather than a textbook average.

Input three — savings target

Set it once — a percentage of income or a fixed monthly amount. The number is what remains after the target and the committed bills have already been paid, which is the only version of budgeting that has ever worked.

Input four — reality so far

Wednesday spend is subtracted immediately, so Friday’s figure is honest. Overspend does not generate a red scold; it quietly reduces what is left, which is discipline enough.

Feature 05 / 09 — Forecast & runway

Ninety days ahead, not ninety days of archaeology

The projection line answers the question people actually ask: will this be okay? It walks forward from today’s balance through known bills, expected income and your median variable spend, then marks the first week a plan stops working.

Runway is the same maths with the income switched off — the number freelancers check before a slow quarter.

What Bill Radar does

90-day look-ahead

Bill Radar keeps a calendar of everything due, then checks it against the money that will actually be in the account by then. That combination is the difference between a reminder and a plan.

  • Detects bills from cadence, even when the merchant does not warn you
  • Marks a due date unpayable three weeks ahead, while there is still time to move money
  • Smooths quarterly and annual costs into weekly figures automatically
  • Syncs to any calendar app through an iCal feed on Plus and above
  • Tracks renewal windows for insurance and memberships with a two-week notice
