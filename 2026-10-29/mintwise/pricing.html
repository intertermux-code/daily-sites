§ 05 — Before you pay

The questions people ask us first

What happens when the free trial ends?

Nothing dramatic. Your accounts stay connected and your history stays put, but the app drops to the Starter plan until you choose a paid tier. You will get one notice four days before, never three emails in a week.

We do not ask for a card to start a trial, so there is nothing to charge by surprise.

Can I cancel mid-cycle?

Yes, in two taps from Settings → Plan. Monthly plans stop at the end of the current month with no cancellation fee. Annual plans are refunded pro rata for whole unused months — that is a policy, not a favour, and it is written into the terms.

Why is Household more than one Individual plan?

Because it is two people’s accounts, two sets of connections, and a shared statement engine. At $17 it is still cheaper than two Individual plans at $18, and it adds the contribution statements that make splitting costs unarguable.

Do you take a cut from banks or card issuers?

No. There is no referral revenue, no “recommended card” module, and no aggregated data sold to marketers — not even anonymised. Subscription revenue covers 100% of the business, which is exactly why we can show you an annual report and a no-NDA security pack.

Is the free plan crippled on purpose?

It is limited rather than sabotaged. Three accounts, live categorisation, the weekly number and full CSV export — enough to run your actual money if your money is simple. What you lose is history beyond 90 days, forecasting and Bill Radar.

Do you offer annual pricing for teams?

Advisor covers sole practitioners and small firms up to twenty-five clients. Beyond that, contact us through the support form and we will quote a multi-seat plan; typically around $0.90 per client per month with a single annual invoice.
