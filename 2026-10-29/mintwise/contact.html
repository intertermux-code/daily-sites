§ 03 — Common questions

The eight things support is asked most

Answered by the people who fix the problem, not by a template.

My bank stopped refreshing. What do I do?

Almost always the connection rather than the bank. Open Settings → Accounts, choose the account and tap Reconnect; that resolves 71% of cases in under a minute. If the balance is still stale after fifteen minutes, send us the form with the topic “A bank or card connection is not refreshing” and we will treat it as urgent.

Some institutions drop the session on weekends and holidays. The status page lists which are known to be flaky today.

Can Mintwise move money or pay anyone?

No. Every connection is read-only and scoped to balances plus transaction history. There is no transfer, payment or bill-pay capability anywhere in our API surface, so there is nothing for an attacker — or us — to misuse in that direction.

How do I delete my data completely?

Settings → Account → Delete everything. One confirmation screen, no phone call, no retention offer. Connections are dropped immediately, records leave the live database within an hour and backups age out within thirty days. You get a written confirmation when it is done.

Why is the weekly number different from my budget?

Because it accounts for what is already committed rather than what you planned. An annual insurance payment landing on the 22nd reduces that week whether or not you budgeted for it. Tap the figure and Mintwise shows the four inputs behind it line by line.

Do you support accounts outside the US and UK?

Yes — 12,400 institutions across the US, UK, EU, Canada, Australia and New Zealand, plus manual accounts anywhere. Currency is converted daily at the ECB reference rate and the original amount is kept on every record.

Is there a student or low-income price?

Individual is free for students, jobseekers and people registered with a debt charity. Mention it in the form; we do not ask for proof of income, and we do not ask you to explain your circumstances.

Can I share with a partner without seeing each other’s everything?

That is what the Household plan is for. Private categories are hidden from the other adult while still counting towards the shared savings rate, and each person’s individual accounts stay individually permissioned.

Do you have an app for Apple Watch or Android Wear?

Watch shows the weekly figure, today’s remaining amount and the balance of your largest account. It does not show merchant names or transaction detail, deliberately — a wrist is not where you want to read your spending.
