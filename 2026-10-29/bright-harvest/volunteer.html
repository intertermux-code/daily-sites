The fine print, plainly

What We Ask · What You Get

  • Sixteen or older for warehouse and market shifts; under-16 crews work packing tables with an adult.
  • Closed-toe shoes on the dock, always, no exceptions and no borrowing.
  • Route drivers complete a license check and a one-time background screening.
  • You get a fifteen-minute training, a named coordinator, service-hour signatures, and lunch on us.
  • Volunteers may shop the Saturday grocery line after their shift, same basket as any neighbor.

We sign school, scholarship and faith-based hours on the spot at the office window. Court-ordered community service is accepted in group sessions of four to eight on Saturday mornings — send the paperwork a week ahead so the supervisor has your docket number.

Free lot off Millrace Avenue with four accessible stalls, a level entrance at Room C, and an accessible restroom on the office side. Seated roles — packing, labeling, help line, data entry — are always available, so a wheelchair or a standing limitation never costs you a shift.

Groups of 4 to 30 take over the Thursday backpack line or the Saturday grocery shift. Email volunteer@brightharvest.org with a date, a head count and whether anyone on your side has food-handling training; we usually answer within two days.
