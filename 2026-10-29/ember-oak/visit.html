Getting Here

Two blocks from the Blue Line, three paid garages nearby.

  • TransitWestern station (Blue Line), seven minutes on foot north up Miller. The 20 bus stops at Miller & Grand, one block out.
  • ParkingGrand–Miller lot behind building 4 (validated, $8 after 5 PM). Two paid garages within four blocks; street parking on Miller is free after 7 PM.
  • BikesSix racks in the courtyard to the left of the door, under the awning. Divic station at the corner.
  • RideshareSet the drop-off to “412 Miller Street, east curb.” The west side is a loading lane after 4 PM.
  • AccessStep-free entrance through the courtyard gate on the north side, ramped at 1:12. Accessible restroom on the ground floor; the Grain Room table is reached by eight shallow steps with a handrail on both sides.
  • AssistanceCall ahead and a host will meet you at the gate, hold the door, and walk you in. We will also seat you away from the hearth draft if that helps.

X

Know Before You Come

Five honest answers about the room.

Open one at a time. We would rather tell you now than surprise you at the door.

Still have a question? Use the form below — it reaches the same three people who run the floor.

Is it hot in there?

The eight tables nearest the fire run six to eight degrees warmer than the window side, and the bar is genuinely hot by the second course. The room is ventilated but not air-conditioned to cold — a wood fire and a chiller do not co-operate.

Say “cool side” when you book and you will get a window or a back-corner table. Coat hooks are at both ends of the room, and a coat check runs until 11 PM.

Dress code

Nothing is enforced. Most guests land somewhere between a shirt and jacket and a good coat; the room is dark timber, candle and linen, so people dress to it naturally.

One real note: smoke clings. If you are carrying a cashmere coat or a suit you love, bring it to the check rather than wearing it in.

Timing

Plan on two and a half hours. Because there is only one seating, nobody is turned and nothing is rushed — dessert can arrive at nine-thirty and the table is still yours.

If you have a train to catch, tell the host at the door. The kitchen will run your food ahead of the fire and have it on the table in ninety minutes.

Children & dogs

Under-tens eat well at the 5 PM and 5:45 seatings on the half-portion hearth chicken. The courtyard patio is open April through October and is the friendliest table for a family and a dog together.

Water bowls live under the host stand. Ask and one appears before the bread.

Gift cards & receipts

Gift cards are cut in the room for $100, $250 and $500, or to any amount at the host stand, and never expire. Business dinners get an itemised receipt with the table name printed on it — ask when the check arrives.

We add twenty per cent automatically to parties of six and up, and it goes to the whole floor and kitchen, not a pool.
