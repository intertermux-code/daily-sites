How the hold works

Tables release every Monday at 9 AM for the week ahead. We hold your seating for fifteen minutes, then give the table to whoever is standing at the host stand.

Cancelling is not an offence — call or reply to the confirmation text and the seat goes back on the sheet the same night. Two unclaimed bookings in a year is the only thing that puts you at the back of the list.

Parties of nine or more are served the seven-course Fire Menu at the Grain Room table, $145 per guest, with forty-eight hours’ notice. Send the request and we will call you the same day to plan it.

Cancellation

Cancel any time up to two hours before your seating with no charge and no explanation required. After that, small parties are asked for $25 per guest; the Grain Room table is $60 per guest because the whole kitchen cooks for it in advance.

If illness or a delayed flight takes you out at the last minute, tell us anyway. We release the seat and re-book you first the following week.

Children

Under-tens are welcome at the early seatings — 5 PM and 5:45 PM — and we send the half-portion of the hearth chicken out before anything else arrives. No highchairs at the bar, two available in the dining room.

Older children eat the full menu and do it well; the wood-fired gnocchi is the house special for a nine-year-old.

Dietary needs

Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-conscious menus run in parallel every service. Put your needs in the notes field and the kitchen cooks to them before you sit down.

One honest limit: the dining room, the hutch and the cold cellar share a single small kitchen, so we cannot guarantee a plate free of trace wheat, dairy, nut or shellfish contact. Tell your server and we will be blunt with you about what we can and cannot do.

Large parties

The Grain Room table seats sixteen behind the kitchen, served the seven-course Fire Menu at $145 per guest with an optional pour flight at $68. Forty-eight hours’ notice, deposit of $50 per guest.

Full takeovers of the twenty-eight-seat room run Sunday nights only, when the fire is cold and can be lit to order for you. Email room@emberandoak.com for dates six weeks out.
