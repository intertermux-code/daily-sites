04 Questions the Desk Gets Weekly

Before You Subscribe

Does the briefing ever contain sponsored links?

Never. The mail server has no slot for them and the cooperative’s articles forbid advertising income. €0 of last year’s €612,000 came from ads, and the funding table on the masthead page is audited annually.

How long is a typical issue?

Median 1,940 words, which is about eight minutes with coffee. The lead dispatch on the site runs longer — fourteen minutes for this week’s — but the briefing itself is written to be finished in one sitting.

What happens to my address after I confirm?

It sits in one Postgres table on a machine in Berlin with your cadence preference and nothing else. No open tracking pixels, no third-party analytics, no profile building. Delete requests are executed within 24 hours and you get written confirmation.

Can I ask the desk to measure something?

Yes — the last field on the form exists for exactly that. Eleven readers’ requests went into the queue this month and two became dispatches. If we can’t run your measurement, we tell you why in the desk notes.

Do you send a physical edition?

Once a year: a 64-page newsprint almanac with the year’s datasets printed as tables, sent to members. It is the only thing Signal sells, and it is priced to cover the paper.
