Registry 02 / Facility

A hall, not a club.

An old freight warehouse on Gravier Way, taken over in 2016 and rebuilt around one idea: enough iron that nobody ever queues for a bar.

There is no tanning, no juice bar and no contract phone on the wall. Chalk is free, plates are calibrated to the gram, and every platform has a fixed camera so your coach can replay a set before you load the next one.

Method 01

Test, then prescribe.

Week one is a measurement week: vertical jump, 200 m row, overhead depth, top single. Nothing gets prescribed until those four numbers exist.

Method 02

One coach, six rows.

Our coached floor ratio is one coach to six lifters per block — written into the staffing plan, not a marketing claim.

Method 03

Progress is a decision.

You advance when the coach signs the wave off, not when you finish a workout. Two missed sessions and the block repeats.

Registry 04 / This week

Registry 05 / Field notes

From the platform.

// verified members, 12+ months on file

  1. Book the free session

    Pick a window on the membership page. A coach calls you the night before to confirm, not a bot.

  2. Get screened on the floor

    Fifteen minutes: overhead depth, hinge pattern, ankle range, your history. Then four baseline numbers.

  3. Lift inside the session

    You train the real coached class on day one, at your own load, with a coach beside the platform.

  4. Read your first block

    You leave with a written twelve-week plan and a start date. Decide afterwards — nothing is pre-authorised.
