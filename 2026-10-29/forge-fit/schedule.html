How booking works

Seats open seven
days ahead at 06:00.

The most contested windows are Tuesday 18:30 and Thursday 17:30. If you work a day job, the 06:00 and 12:15 blocks exist precisely so you do not have to fight for them.

  1. Pick a window

    Choose the session above, note the coach and the hall. Every coached class is drop-in friendly for your first week.

  2. Hold it in the system

    Booking opens 7 days ahead. Trial lifters are booked by the desk, so a phone call or the trial form is enough.

  3. Arrive fifteen minutes early

    Chalk, belt and a short brief from the coach before the clock starts. Late arrivals train the following block.
