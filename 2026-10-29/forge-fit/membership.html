Widget / load estimate

Body mass index,
and what we do with it.

Enter your height and bodyweight to place yourself on the reference scale. Then read the second half: at Forge the index is only a screening gate — the number that decides your programming is the load you can move.

Why we still show it. A very low index with a stalled lift usually means you are under-fuelled, not under-trained. A high index with a strong deadlift is not a problem to fix first.

Where it stops being useful. Index does not distinguish muscle from fat, so a 95 kg lifter at 180 cm reads “obese” while squatting 220 kg. Your coach ignores the label and works from your screen plus your top single.

What we measure instead. Five-point range screen, three-lift top single, 200 m row split, and a waist-to-height ratio recorded at the same session.

Reference bands follow the World Health Organization adult classification. This tool is an estimate for training placement and is not medical advice.
