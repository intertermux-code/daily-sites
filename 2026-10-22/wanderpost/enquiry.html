    What happens next

    1. Within two working days — a reply from your planner with three or four questions.
    2. Week two — a fifteen-minute call. Voice, email, or written back-and-forth, whichever you think in.
    3. Week three — a pencil draft with the empty days marked, and a candid note on what we would change.
    4. Only then — a deposit, and never before a house is confirmed in your name.

    Before you write

    Four questions we are asked in nearly every reply

    Do you accommodate dietary and medical needs?

    Yes, and we ask early rather than apologising late. Kitchen staff at every house we book are told in advance, and we carry a written card in the local language for each need you have.

    What if I need to change my dates?

    Move them once, free, up to ninety days out — the houses we use would rather do that than lose you. After that we will find you the same beds on different dates more often than not.

    Do you take children?

    On Marrakech and Kyoto, gladly, from about eight years old and with the empty days intact. On Patagonia we say no below sixteen, and we mean it for their safety rather than our convenience.

    Is there anything you refuse to arrange?

    Animal encounters that involve holding, feeding, or riding for photographs; orphanage visits; and any itinerary that adds a second country to make the price look generous. We have turned down money four times for these reasons.
