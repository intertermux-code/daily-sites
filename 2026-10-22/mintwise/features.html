03 · Floor budgeting

One number, protected before you spend a cent

Forget fourteen category envelopes you abandon by February. Tell Mintwise the balance you refuse to fall under this week; it schedules the fixed bills inside that number and shows you only what’s genuinely free.

  • The floor recalculates all day — a direct debit landing at 3pm moves it by Thursday, not never.
  • Money above the floor is yours to spend with no guilt meter and no red text.
  • Weekly or monthly cadence, per account, with a partner view that never nags.

Security

Access that ends where reading ends

Mintwise never holds your money, never has payment initiation rights, and never needs your bank password beyond the first handshake with your institution’s own authorisation page.

01

Read-only by design

Our tokens are scoped to account information and transaction permissions. Initiation is technically impossible, even with a compromised key — and your bank’s consent dashboard shows exactly what we hold, revocable in one click.

02

Encrypted, split, and minimal

Bank identifiers live in a separate vault from transaction data, both at AES-256 with keys rotated every thirty days. We never store balances in analytics, and no engineer can read a single account without two approvals and a logged reason.

03

Audited in public

SOC 2 Type II annually, penetration testing quarterly by Halcyon Labs, and a published incident log — including the small stuff. Our business model is your subscription, so there is no ad broker to sell you to.
