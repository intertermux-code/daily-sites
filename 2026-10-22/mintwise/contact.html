Mintwise never moves money between your bank accounts. Vault funding happens inside the Mintwise ledger — a shadow record of what you’ve committed to save — and the transfer to your savings provider is one you make, or one you set up once at your own bank.

Households on Household and above can enable a standing instruction at their bank that pays the vault account monthly. We give you the reference string; the bank holds the mandate.
