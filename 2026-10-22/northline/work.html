Case 001 — Halo Freight

Fourteen weeks,
one branch.

Client
Halo Freight, Copenhagen
Scope
Brand system, commerce rebuild, motion kit
Team
2 designers, 3 engineers, 1 strategist
Year
2025
Ask for the full deck
Challenge

Forty years of freight habits, one stubborn checkout.

Halo moved 11% of Nordic sea freight and looked like it. Six acquired brands meant six logotypes, four typefaces and a checkout that abandoned 71% of carts. Leadership wanted one identity and a faster purchase path without pausing operations for a quarter.

Approach

We rewrote the vocabulary before the pixels.

Two weeks with dispatchers and buyers produced a naming system and a verbal territory. Then a proprietary grotesque, a chrome-and-ember palette, and a token library exported straight into the checkout codebase — so the brand guide and the front end shared one source of truth from week four.

  • Token pipeline
  • Pairing sessions
  • Weekly demo
Outcome

The numbers moved and stayed moved.

Checkout completion rose 38% in the first sixty days. Design-to-merge time dropped from nine days to three. Halo retired four legacy logotypes, and the same team now runs a monthly experiment slate on retainer.

  • +38% completion
  • −67% merge time
  • 1 identity
