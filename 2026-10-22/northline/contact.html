After you press send

Four steps,
no black box.

We are explicit about the sales process because you have sat through enough opaque ones.

Day 1

A partner reads it

No triage bot, no business developer. Freja or Tobias reads the brief, decides whether we are the right studio, and writes back with a first read on scope and budget band.

Day 2–4

Forty-five minutes on a call

With the maker who would lead it, not a pitch team. You talk, we ask the annoying questions, and we both decide if this is a yes. Nothing is prepared in advance for your benefit.

Week 2

Written proposal, one PDF

Named deliverables, the phase plan, who is on the pod, fixed price with inclusions and exclusions, and the two risks we can already see. Valid for thirty days.

Week 3

Plot phase begins

Kick-off workshop, access to your data and customers, and the first demo date in the calendar. From that point you see a working link every Friday.
