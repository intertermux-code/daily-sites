How it runs

A normal
fortnight.

Same rhythm whether you buy a brand or a build. You always know what is happening and who is on it.

Meet the team
Monday, 09:30

Scope call, not a status call

The partner leading the work takes the call with you. Decisions get made there rather than parked for a deck. Written note lands by 11:00 with the week's single most important question.

Wednesday, all day

Build block with your engineers

Shared channel, shared branch. Designers review in the browser, engineers pair on component boundaries, and nothing gets signed off in a static file that breaks the moment it meets a real content length.

Friday, 15:00

Demo the actual thing

Live URL, real data, mobile first. We show the working product, list what regressed, and set the next fortnight's bets. Recordings stay in the archive so an absent stakeholder never needs a summary.

Every six weeks

Money and risk, in one page

Budget spent, budget remaining, forecast to completion, and the two risks that could move the date. One page, plain numbers, no agency poetry.
