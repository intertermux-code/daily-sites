05 / Small print

The five rules we actually enforce

Refunds

Full refund up to 14 days before the Friday, minus the booking fee. After that we transfer your pass to anyone you name, free, right up to the gate.

Resale cap

Sell above face value and the pass is cancelled at the door with no refund. Our official transfer page charges nothing.

Under-18s

None, including with an adult. We check passport or driving licence at every gate, all night.

Lost wristbands

Replace at the box office window for £10 with photo ID. The old band dies the second the new one prints.

Cancellation

If the festival cannot run we refund everything, fee included, within thirty days. Headline changes are not a cancellation.

Deadline

Tier 2 closes at 8,000 or 09 November — whichever comes first

Tier 3 prices are set at £219 for the same weekend pass. Two payments of £63 today is a lot cheaper than two payments of £110 in December.
