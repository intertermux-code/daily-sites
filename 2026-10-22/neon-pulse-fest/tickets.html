What a Pass Gets You In

One Gate, Four Rooms, Zero Upsells

There is no paid fast lane, no paid viewing area and no “VIP only” artist anywhere on the bill. The only thing money buys past the gate is a shorter queue and a better place to stand — the music is identical in every room at every price.

Entry opens at 17:30 for pre-registered wristbands and 18:00 for everyone. Arrive before 20:00 on Friday and Saturday and you will be inside in under ten minutes; after 22:30 the average wait is twenty-two, in the heated marquee.

  • Wristband posted free, with a $6 refundable deposit released at close
  • Photo ID checked once, at the gate, not at the bar
  • All four rooms on every tier — the Bunker is first come, first counted

Before You Buy

Groups, Age, Transfers, Refunds

Groups of Ten or More

Ten weekend passes cost nine, one shared order with a single transferable name list. Crews, labels and collectives get a locker block and a reserved entry slot. Email groups with the roster and we hold the allocation for five days.

Discount applies automatically at ten — no code needed.

Age Policy

Eighteen and over, no exceptions, because the licence covers served alcohol until 06:00. Photo ID is checked at the gate, not at the bar, so bring it in from the cold once. Under-18s are welcome at the December Sunday matinee in the Solar room, 14:00–17:00, free with a ticketed adult.

Transfer & Resale

Send a pass to someone else in the app up to 24 hours before the night starts, free of charge. Face-value resale through our own exchange is the only route that keeps your buyer’s entry valid; anything bought elsewhere is voided when we scan the wristband.

Refunds

Full refund until 30 November, no reason required. After that we refund 60 %, and from 10 December passes are non-refundable but always transferable. If a headliner cancels we refund the affected night on single-day passes, and we refund 100 % if we cancel the whole weekend.

Weather, travel delays and illness are covered by the transfer window, not the refund window.

Prices Change on 07 November at 12:00

Phase 3 lifts Weekend General to $339 and closes single-day sales entirely. If you are unsure about dates, buy the tier you would want and use the free transfer window.
