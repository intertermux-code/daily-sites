03 / Access & conduct

Every stage is step-free. That was condition one of the licence

We rebuilt the ramp network in 2024 with the Manchester Access Panel. If a route is not step-free we mark it closed rather than call it a shortcut.

  • Viewing platform — Main Hall, level 2, capacity 60, book with your pass
  • Companion tickets — free, one per ticket-holder, no proof required for repeat visits
  • Quiet room — former fitting shop, signposted from the Yard bar, staffed 24 hours
  • Hearing loops — all four bars, plus ear defenders on loan at every gate
  • Accessible WCs — 14 units, nearest 30 metres from each stage door
  • Assistance dogs — welcome everywhere except the Rooftop Yard bar, water bowls provided

04 / FAQ

Twelve questions the crew answers most

Ordered by what lands in the inbox first. Anything not covered here goes to the contact form at the bottom of the page.

Gates close at 04:00 for new entries on Friday and Saturday — after that you cannot get back in if you leave, though you can stay inside until 06:00. The last record drops at 05:55 and the house lights come up at 06:00. Exit routes stay staffed and open until 07:30 so nobody is pushed out into the cold at once.

Expect 2 to 6°C, high humidity and a real chance of rain on the walk from the shuttle. Three of the four rooms are indoors and get hot enough to dance in, so dress in layers you can shed. The Rooftop Yard is covered on three sides and heated by four industrial lamps, but it is still a roof in December — a waterproof shell beats a jacket, and wellies beat trainers. Umbrellas are a prohibited item because they are a weapon in a crowd.

Re-entry runs until 04:00 on day passes and is unlimited on weekenders. Use the same gate you came in on — the wristband is scanned each time, so a bent or soaked band is the usual reason re-entry fails. If yours breaks, the box office window at Gate C prints a replacement for £10 with photo ID.

No camping — this is an urban indoor site with neighbours, and the licence does not allow overnight sleep outside. We hold room blocks at four hotels within a twenty-minute walk; the code PULSESLEEP gets 15% off when you book direct before 1 December. Day-use showers at the Ferrous Street leisure centre, 400 metres away, are £3 and open from 06:30.

Your pass doubles as a payment band. Top up online before you travel, at eleven load stations on site, or at any bar. Cards and cash both work at the stations, and no top-up fee applies on site. Unspent credit is refunded automatically to the card you used, within ten working days — you do not need to claim it. Spent bands are recycled; keep yours as a bookmark.

Tier 1 sold out in 41 minutes and is closed permanently. Around 60 passes return each week from failed payments and name changes, and they go straight to the return queue in the order people joined. Queue holders get a twenty-minute hold by email and SMS. If you do not want to wait, Tier 2 carries exactly the same rights and is £50 more until 9 November.

A headline change is not grounds for a refund — that is standard across the industry and is in the terms on your purchase email. We will always name the replacement within 48 hours and post it on the lineup page. If the festival cannot take place at all, you get everything back including the booking fee, within thirty days, with nothing to claim.

Controlled substances are illegal to possess here and finding any gets your pass cancelled at the gate. What we do run is an over-prioritised welfare presence: five trained first-aid points, a staffed quiet room open around the clock, and a text line — 07700 900418 — where you can describe a situation and get a response without a steward arriving. You can text it about yourself or about a stranger. Nobody is searched or reported for asking for help.

Phones and compact cameras are fine. Detachable-lens cameras, audio recorders and tripods need a press pass, which we issue free to music photographers and community media — apply from the contact form below by 1 December. Two hard rules: no flash after 23:00 in the Acid Cathedral, and no photographing anyone who has not clearly consented. Stewards wear a yellow band if they can approve a shot on the spot.

Eleven kitchens around the yard, open the whole weekend. The Yard kitchen runs a proper hot menu from 19:00 to 03:00 with vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free main dishes at £6.50. Halal and vegan street food stays open until 05:30, and there is a 24-hour bakery van at Gate A serving filled baps from the small hours. Free filtered water is at every bar — bring an empty plastic bottle.

Lost property is logged continuously at the Gate C welfare tent and photographed into an online register within two hours. Check the register from the morning after; anything unclaimed travels to the box office in Manchester town centre for fourteen days. Phones, keys and passports are held in a signed safe and never leave the site until you collect them with ID.

Yes, and it is free. Use the official transfer link in your purchase email up to 24 hours before the gates; the new holder gets their own wristband and the photo-ID check follows them. Reselling above the price you paid is against the terms and gets the pass cancelled at the door — we publish every transfer price on the resale page so buyers can see what the band actually cost.
