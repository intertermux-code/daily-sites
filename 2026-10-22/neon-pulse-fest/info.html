Site operations · read before Friday

Meridian Yards, Pier 9

Nine hectares of decommissioned dockland sheds on the east bank, three nights of continuous music, and a crew that has run this site since 2019. Here is the practical file: how you get in, where you go, what you can bring, and who to ask.

Address: Pier 9, Meridian Yards, East Bank ME1 4QD Nearest rail: Meridian Central, 1.9 km Site phone: +44 20 7946 0312 Open 60 hours

02 — getting there

Shuttles Run Every Twelve Minutes

There is no general parking at Meridian Yards — the site is on a working peninsula with 340 spaces, all of them allocated. Plan on the shuttle, the train, or the bike valet.

Arrive · Friday

Beat the 19:00 crush

Half of Friday's entry happens between 18:00 and 19:30. Arriving after 20:00 typically means a six-minute queue instead of twenty-two. Gate 4 stays quiet all weekend if you are in a group with mobility needs.

  • Peak18:20 → 19:40
  • QuietestAfter 21:00

Leave · late night

Shuttles track the last set

Shuttle buses keep running until fifteen minutes after Warehouse 9 closes, so you can watch the sunrise set and still get to the station without a taxi. Saturday's queue peaks at 05:45.

  • Last bus07:15 Fri/Sat
  • StandSlipway Road loop

Cold · December

Dress for 3 °C and wind

The concourse between Mainframe and the Dome is 400 metres of open quayside and the river wind cuts through it. Layers, closed shoes, and a hoody you do not mind losing. No umbrellas past the gate.

  • Forecast avg3 °C, 24 km/h
  • ShelterMarket Row, all rooms

03 — access & inclusion

The Site Is Built
Step-Free After Gate 1

Every stage, bar, water point and toilet block is reachable on level ground or by ramp. If you need something we have not listed, email the access desk before 4 December and a named coordinator will answer you personally.

Raised viewing platforms

Step-free ramp access with space for four wheelchairs and two companions per room, plus fold-up seating for up to twelve people with fatigue or pain conditions. Platforms are staffed from doors to close.

Free companion ticket

Every registered disabled pass carries one companion ticket at no charge, issued automatically at checkout. Add a second companion if your support needs require it — the access desk will confirm by email within two working days.

Quiet room and hearing protection

A lit, carpeted, low-sound space beside the Vapor Dome with water, beanbags and two welfare staff, open all night. Free foam ear plugs are at every stage door and at all eleven bar points; the Dome is a permanently light-safe room with no strobe.

Accessible toilets and changing places

Each toilet block has at least two accessible cubicles with grab rails and level thresholds. A full changing-places unit with hoist is beside Gate 1, and all blocks are heated and staffed overnight.

Changing placesGate 1, one unit Cubicles2–4 per block CleaningEvery 40 minutes

Blue badge parking and drop-off

Thirty-six adapted bays within 40 metres of the accessible gate, plus a staffed drop-off loop that operates all night. Book a bay through the access desk with your pass number; unbooked blue badge holders are accommodated from a reserve of eight bays.

04 — staying over

Camp Nine Opens at Midday Thursday

Eleven hundred people sleep on site across a pitched field, a pod row, and a small heated bunkhouse. It is a short walk from Warehouse 9, which is either a feature or a problem depending on how you sleep.

December averages on the quayside sit near 3 °C with a 24 km/h river wind, and the 05:00 finish feels colder because you are wet. Wear layers you can shed indoors, closed shoes, and a hoody you can lose.

  • Every room is heated except the Slipway rig
  • Blankets are handed out at the Dome from 23:00
  • Hot drinks on Market Row from 17:00, $3.50

Lost property is logged at the welfare desk inside Gate 1 and photographed into an online register each evening after close. Phones and wallets are kept in a safe and never left unattended. Claim by email with a description and we post it back for the cost of tracking.

Rooms are limited to 104 dB(A) at front of house and monitored continuously with a public readout at each stage door. Free foam plugs are at every bar and stage entrance. The Bass Reactor and Warehouse 9 run closest to the limit; the Vapor Dome is the quietest room by a wide margin.

Small personal quantities are handled welfare-first: hand something in at the amnesty bin and you keep your pass. Anyone who feels unwell should go to the medical point beside Gate 2 — staff will not call the police on you for seeking help. We do not operate a pill-testing service on this site.

Sealed snack food is fine, and an empty reusable bottle is encouraged — free filtered water is available at three points marked WA on the plan. No glass, no alcohol, no coolers. Dietary requirements are listed against every trader stall on the site board and in the app.

We run three temporary cells on the peninsula, so signal is usable most of the night but slow at 05:00 when everyone posts at once. Free event Wi-Fi covers all indoor rooms — connect to NEON-GUEST. Set a meeting point anyway, and charge at the $5 phone lockers in Camp Nine and Market Row.
