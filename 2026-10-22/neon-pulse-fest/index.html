01 / Transmission_01

Built inside an ironworks, wired for low frequencies

Kilnworks Yard spent ninety years casting railway iron. Since 2019 it has cast something else: a weekend of techno, house, electro and bass music under a 14-metre clerestory roof.

We kept the crane rails, the brick furnace chimneys and the iron floor plates. We added Funktion-One arrays, 220 metres of neon trunking and a fog system that reads the crowd density. The result holds 12,000 people per night and never quite feels the same twice.

  • Four stages — Main Pulse Hall, Acid Cathedral, Neon Bunker, Rooftop Yard
  • Cashless wristbands — top up online before you travel, no queues at the bar
  • Safe-space policy — trained stewards on every stage, zero tolerance for harassment
  • Late-night transport — free shuttle to Piccadilly every 15 minutes from 02:00

06 / Field Notes

What everybody asks us in the first week

Getting there

Trams run every 10 minutes from Piccadilly to Droylsden and the site shuttle takes the last 900 metres. Free shuttle out from 02:00 to 07:00, every 15 minutes.

Weather plan

Mid-December in Manchester means 3°C, 88% humidity and rain on the rooftop. The Rooftop Yard is heated and covered on three sides; the other rooms are indoors all night.

Wellies, not umbrellas. Umbrellas are a prohibited item.

Accessibility

Step-free routes to every stage, a viewing platform at Main Pulse Hall, ear defenders on loan at all four gates and an assistance-dog rest area by the Yard bar.

Companion tickets are free — one per ticket-holder, booked alongside your pass.
