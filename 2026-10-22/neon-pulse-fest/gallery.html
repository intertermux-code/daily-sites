01 / Frame of the year

05:41, and 900 people facing east

Rhiannon Vasey shot the frame that now prints the poster. Halcyon Brake had just closed a two-hour dub set, the lamps had gone off, and the sky over the gantry turned the colour of the mist machine.

Nobody organised it. The crowd moved to the east rail on its own, roughly at the same moment, and stayed there for eleven minutes while the last two records played. We have never been able to schedule that and we no longer try.
