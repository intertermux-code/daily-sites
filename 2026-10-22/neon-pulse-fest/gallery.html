The 2025 Aftermovie

Eleven minutes, four rooms, no narration and no sponsor cards. It opens on the rig check at 16:20 on Friday and ends on the deck at 05:58 on Sunday, in real order. We cut it the same way every year because the point is the length of the night, not the highlight reel.

The film is published on the second Friday after the festival. This page links it the day it goes up — the crew posts the stills set to the gallery first.

Your Roll Too

Tag #neonpulse26 and we pull the best ninety frames of the weekend into the site gallery by name, with a printed zine for whoever gets picked. Phone shots are welcome — the only banned thing is filming during the AV show.

Nine photographers cannot cover five thousand people, and the frames that end up on the wall are usually someone’s blurry shot from the back of the Bunker.
