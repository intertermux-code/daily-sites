Reading the Bill

How the Rooms Overlap

Every slot is booked against the room next door on purpose. When the Mainroom headliner starts at 23:00 on Saturday, Afterglow runs a vinyl-only warm set and the Bunker goes dark and loud — so the crowd that does not want the big show still has a night.

Nothing plays at the same tempo in two rooms at once, and no artist gets scheduled against themselves: if you want to hear Odessa Blight and Marisol Vane on the same night, the set times below genuinely allow it, with four minutes to cross the building.

Six Names to Build Around

The Heads

Marisol Vane · Saturday, Pulse Mainroom

Marisol Vane — Saturday 23:00

Ninety minutes, one continuous mix, and the warehouse AV show we have been trying to stage since 2022. Truss, strobe and a live camera rig feeding the two side screens. No phones allowed in the pit — our own crew shoots it and the film goes up in January.

Tetsuo Aramaki — Sunday 03:00 to 06:00

Three hours, no support, closing the solstice weekend into actual daylight. He has played a sunrise set in every city he has visited since 2017 and asks for the same thing here: doors to the deck unlocked from 05:00.

Halcyon Drift — Friday 01:00

The Bristol pair bring their dub-techno live rig back to New York for the first time since 2024, with the tape-delay system rebuilt around our bunker sub array.

Amara Fontaine — Saturday 01:00, Afterglow

Vinyl only, two hours, filter at full tilt. The mirrored wall makes it the most photogenic room on site and the least quiet place to be at 02:00.

Odessa Blight — Saturday 02:00, Bass Bunker

Back for a third year in the 480-cap concrete box with the lights off. Two hours, no support act, and a door count our security team enforces to the single digit.

Georgia Vaux — Sunday 01:00, Afterglow

Italo re-edits, a Juno stacked over the CDJs, and the one set on the weekend where the mirrored wall gets used properly. She closes Afterglow at 03:00 both nights she is booked.

Plan the Last Hour

Room Curfews, Written Down

Sound ends when the room closes — not “approximately”. Neighbouring streets on West 23rd have a 06:00 noise permit, so Sunday is the only night that runs to sunrise.
