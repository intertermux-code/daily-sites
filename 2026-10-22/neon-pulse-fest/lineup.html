02 / Crossovers

The three clashes worth planning around

Every headliner wants the same window. Here is how our bookers would spend those ninety minutes if they held a pass.

Fri 00:00 — Cathedral vs Main

Mox Tyne opens his three-hour acid journey at the same minute Kiln Club finishes on the Main Hall. Walk the gantry bridge: eight minutes door to door.

Our call: hear the last twenty minutes of Kiln Club, cross at 00:40, catch Mox Tyne from the delay tower.

Sat 02:00 — Rooftop vs Bunker

Gold Span's last record drops as Halcyon Brake starts, and Unit 9 is peaking two floors below. The lift to the roof is out of service after 01:30.

Our call: take the stair early, bring a jacket, stay for the 04:00 terrace sunrise.

Sun 04:00 — the closing two hours

Sister Amplitude in the Cathedral and Ferro-Light's finale in the Bunker overlap completely. The Cathedral is the quiet room; the Bunker is not.

Our call: Bunker until 04:30, then the nave for the last ninety minutes with the organ.
