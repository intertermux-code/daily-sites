Reading the index

Three Rules That Save
Your Saturday

The index is deliberately dense, so plan against it the way the crew does.

  1. 01 · Pick one anchor set per night, then fill around it. Warehouse 9 and the Bass Reactor peak at the same hour — you cannot see both.
  2. 02 · Allow twenty minutes between rooms. Mainframe to Vapor Dome is a 400-metre walk through the outdoor concourse, and 19:00–22:00 crossings are slow.
  3. 03 · Long sets are worth more than short ones. Four 90-minute sets beat twelve 30-minute ones, and the schedule is built for that deliberately.
