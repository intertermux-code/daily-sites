02 Good to know

What happens after you submit

  • Front desk checks the slot against the live calendar.
  • You get a text confirmation the same afternoon.
  • New patients receive a 4-minute intake video by email.

Bring with you

  • Photo ID and insurance card (if you have one).
  • Medication list — even the “just vitamins” kind.
  • Any recent X-rays from another clinic, on a phone is fine.

Cancellations, plainly

Reschedule free up to 4 hours before your visit. Same-day no-shows without a message are charged the missed-appointment fee printed on your quote — the chair was reserved for you.

First-visit pricing

The new-patient exam and 3D scan is $189, credited toward any treatment you start within six months. Hygiene visits for returning patients run $150–$340 depending on what your gums actually need.

Prefer a human?

(425) 555-0117, Mon–Sat during clinic hours. Or email hello@lumident.care and a coordinator will call you back.

03 Your first 90 minutes

Know the room before you’re in it.

Scroll sideways

Minute 00

Check-in, standing

The desk hands you a tablet, not a clipboard. ID, insurance photo, one signature. Average time: four minutes.

Minute 15

The conversation

Your dentist — not an assistant — asks what bothers you and what you’re afraid of. Write it down beforehand if it helps.

Minute 35

Records & scan

Photographs, a wand scan, and only the X-rays your history calls for. You see everything on the chairside screen as it’s taken.

Minute 70

The plan on paper

You leave with one page: options, fees, timing, and a bold “nothing starts until you choose.” Take it to dinner before deciding.
