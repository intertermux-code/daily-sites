Case 03 Implants

One molar, placed through a printed guide.

Grace lost a molar to a fracture and let the gap sit for two years — until neighbours began tipping into it. Mirela printed a surgical guide from her CBCT scan, placed the implant in 47 minutes, and crowned it five months later. The tipped neighbours were righted with bonding, not braces.
