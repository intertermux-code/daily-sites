02 The visit

An appointment, chapter by chapter. Walk the line →

Scroll sideways

Stage 01

The Listen

Twenty minutes before any instrument moves. You describe the problem; we photograph, chart, and confirm what we heard before we touch anything.

20 minutes · No chair yet
Stage 02

The Scan

Intraoral 3D scan and low-dose CBCT imaging replace the goop trays and film retakes. You watch your own anatomy render on the screen beside you.

15 minutes · Digital only
Stage 03

The Quote

A one-page written plan: every option, every fee, every timeline. Insurance estimates calculated while you wait — nothing leaves this room a surprise.

Same day · In writing
Stage 04

The Work

Noise-cancelling headphones, ceiling screens, and numbing paced to your comfort. Weighted blankets in every room if you want one.

Guided placement · Nitrous available
Stage 05

The Aftercare

A check-in call at 48 hours, a photo review at two weeks, and hygiene visits scheduled before you leave. Guarantees only mean something with follow-up.

48h call · 2-week review
