02 Getting here

Park once, walk ninety seconds.

01

By car

Free patient parking in the garage behind the building, elevator to Suite 240. From I-405 take Exit 12; the drive from downtown Bellevue is seven minutes off-peak.

Garage, Level P1
02

By light rail

Sound or Line L to Bellevue Downtown, then a 90-second flat walk east on 116th — Skylight corridor signage guides you. We time hygiene slots to the train so you’re never late.

Skyline · 90s walk
03

By bus

King County routes 545 and 560 stop at 116th & Main. Route 545 connects direct from Seattle via the SR 520 bridge — park-and-ride free after 9:30 AM.

Routes 545 / 560
04

Accessibility

Step-free entrance, automatic doors, wheelchair-height treatment chair, accessible restroom on our floor, and assistive listening at the consult desk. Tell us when booking and we’ll prep the room.

ADA-compliant
