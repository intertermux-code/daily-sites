01 Implant dentistry

Replace the tooth. Keep the bone.

An implant is a titanium root placed into the jaw, then crowned to match your neighbouring teeth. We plan every case on a 3D scan before the day of surgery, print a surgical guide, and place through that guide — so the implant lands where the crown needs it, not where the drill was convenient.

Single teeth, bridges on implants, and full-arch “teeth in a day” cases are handled in-house; complex bone grafting is co-managed with our periodontist on staff, never outsourced to an unfamiliar surgeon.

  1. Guided placement from CBCT scan
  2. Grade-5 titanium, US-traceable lots
  3. Temporary crown same day in most cases
  4. 10-year implant + 5-year crown guarantee
  5. Sedation and nitrous available
  6. 48-hour and 2-week check-ins

02 Orthodontics & aligners

Straight teeth, measured monthly.

Clear aligner therapy handles the crowding, gaps, and mild bites most adults and teens actually need fixed. Every four to six weeks you come in for a two-minute scan; we compare it against the simulation we promised you on day one and adjust the next trays from real movement, not an estimate made months ago.

For cases aligners can’t finish — severe rotations, big bites, surgical spacing — our low-force bracket system moves teeth slower and more comfortably than the rubber-band era you remember.

  1. 3D treatment simulation before you commit
  2. Monthly scan-based progress verification
  3. Teen and adult protocols in one fee
  4. Retainers included through 12 months
  5. Emergency tray swaps within 24 hours
  6. 18-month interest-free payment plans

03 Cosmetic & preventive

Design the smile before you change a tooth.

Veneers and bonding are proportion work. We start with a photograph, map your smile line against your facial midline and eye line, and build a trial smile in composite that you live with for three days before anything permanent is cut. You approve the shape; the lab matches the shade under three lighting conditions.

Preventive care — hygiene, fluoride varnish, sealants, night guards — is what protects whatever you build here, so it lives under the same roof and the same records.

  1. Digitally planned smile design
  2. 3-day trial smile before approval
  3. Hand-finished porcelain by two labs
  4. Take-home whitening from $280
  5. Hygiene visits from $150, no upsell script
  6. Night guards milled, not boiled

05 Payment & questions

How people pay, and what they ask.

CareCredit · 0% for 18 months

Plans of $1,000+ split across 18 interest-free months. We pre-fill the application with your quote; approval usually takes minutes at the desk.

In-house, no credit check

For aligners and hygiene memberships we carry our own payment calendar — deposit today, remainder monthly, with no third party involved.

PPO & HSA/FSA

We file PPO claims directly and estimate your out-of-pocket before treatment. HSA and FSA cards work at checkout for everything clinical.

Do you take Medicaid or dental-only discount plans?

We participate in Apple Health (Medicaid dental) for exams, hygiene, and basic restorative care for children. We are not in-network for discount plans like Careington, though we will happily bill them as a secondary benefit if your plan allows reimbursement.

How long does an implant really take?

Placement is one visit of 60–90 minutes. The jaw then bonds to the titanium for three to six months — that wait is biology, not scheduling. In stable bone we sometimes fit a temporary crown the same day; your scan decides whether that’s you.

Are aligners as good as braces?

For crowding, spacing, and mild bite issues — yes, and faster in many adult cases. For severe rotations, big skeletal bites, or cases needing tooth movement in bone that hasn’t grown, brackets hold the line better. We say which one you’re in at the first visit instead of selling you either.

What happens if I hate the shape of my veneers?

You’ll have already worn the trial smile for three days and signed off on it, so surprises are engineered out. If the final result still feels wrong, we adjust or remake within the guarantee period — one remake is on us, always.

Is the $189 exam fee charged twice if I need a specialist too?

No. One exam fee covers your records — scan, X-rays, photos, chart — and those travel to any specialist on our staff the same day. The fee is also credited toward treatment you start within six months.
