Queries Membership Questions

Is The Trial Really Free?

Yes — five sessions, no card details, no auto-enrolment at the end. The only thing we ask is that you show up and tell the coach if something is not working. If you decide not to join, you leave with your card and your numbers.

What Happens If I Travel?

Freeze up to eight weeks in a calendar year, in blocks of two weeks or more. Your booking priority and your logged numbers stay where they are, and your billing pauses with the freeze.

Can I Change Tiers Mid-Month?

Upgrade immediately with the difference pro-rated. Downgrade at the next billing date so the desk can re-seat your block bookings. Both take one conversation at the front desk, no forms.

Do You Have Showers And Lockers?

Eight hot showers, forty day-lockers and twenty-four rented lockers at $18 a month. Towels are not provided; bring your own or buy a bagged set at the desk for four dollars.

Is There Parking?

Nine spaces behind the roller door, free for coached members, first come. The G train at Nassau Avenue is a six-minute walk and there is a bike rack for twenty along the foundry wall.
