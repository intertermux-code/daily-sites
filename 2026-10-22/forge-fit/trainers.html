Method How We Coach

Correction Between Sets, Not Shouting Across The Hall

Two coaches run every coached block: one on the platform working setups, one on the clock keeping the clock honest. That is the whole trick, and it is why we cap blocks at twelve rather than selling a room of forty.

  • Every athlete is watched at least twice per session — once warm, once fatigued
  • Load changes are made out loud, with the reason, and written to your card
  • Nothing is corrected while you are mid-rep under a heavy bar unless it is a safety call
  • Coaches train alongside the members they coach; the board is open to staff too

Standing Orders Staff Standards

Named, Never Rotating

A coach who takes your opener owns your closer. If staff cover is unavoidable, the covering coach reads your card before touching the bar.

Continuing Education

Four coaching days a year, paid by the gym, away from the building. Two of this year's went to an IPF coaching clinic and one to a return-to-sport course.

Safety Calls Are Free

Any coach can pull a lifter out of a set for a failing rep, and it is not argued afterwards. The board records the reason and the athlete keeps the number from the last good rep.
