Standing Orders Check-In & Cancellation

Tap The Card Reader

One reader by the roller door, one by Studio B. Your tap is what puts your name on the board — turning up and standing in the corner does not count as attending a block.

Ninety Minutes Notice

Miss a coached block without cancelling ninety minutes ahead and it is logged as a miss. Three misses in a fortnight moves your booking priority down the queue for that week.

Waitlist Works

Full blocks have a live waitlist. The board releases the freed slot to the next name automatically, and the coach texts you. Nobody gets dropped without being told first.

Index Session Glossary

What the names on the board mean
Term Meaning On The Floor Typical Duration
Iron Hour A single lift, worked properly, start to finish. Bar, belt, no circuits. 55 min
EMOM Every minute on the minute — you earn back the rest you did not waste. 20 min of the block
Chipper One long list of reps, done in order, as fast as you can carry it. 18–25 min
RPE 8 Two clean reps left in the tank at the end of the set. The coach checks your bar speed. Per set
Opener Your first logged max at the start of a block. Every number after it is measured against this. Week 1 only
Back-off Working sets dropped to a lighter percentage after a heavy single, for volume without damage. 3–4 sets
