01 The House Standard

Log Every Rep

You get a numbered card the day you join. Squat, bench, deadlift, row, carry — written down, dated, reviewed on the whiteboard every Sunday night. Motivation is not a training plan; a log is.

Load Before Height

We teach the barbell first. Hip hinge, brace, rack position, descent. No box jumps, no slam cycles and no kipping until your movement holds up under a working set you can measure.

Small Rooms, Loud Bars

Blocks cap at twelve athletes with one coach on the platform and one on the clock. Nobody hides at the back. Everybody gets their setup corrected between sets.

03 The Floor

Built Like A Workshop, Not A Studio

Concrete, steel and calibrated plates. The hall was a foundry annexe before it was a gym, and we kept the crane rail, the roller door and the noise. What you will not find: mirrors wall-to-wall, a juice bar, or a playlist that decides your rest period for you.
