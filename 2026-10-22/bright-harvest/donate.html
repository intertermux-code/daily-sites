January reality

Last January the pantry served 2,610 households — our busiest week in sixteen years — while one-off giving had fallen 44 percent from December. The cold room held 1.9 tonnes.

Questions people actually ask

Is my monthly gift easy to stop?

Reply “pause” to any of our emails and it stops the same day. We do not phone you, and we do not keep charging a cancelled card.

Do you take food instead of money?

Yes — sealed, in-date items from the accepted list, dropped at 14 Mill Lane between 08:00 and 16:00. Money is still better for us, because we buy produce at grower prices and buy the specific things shelves run short on.

What does “94 cents in food” leave for everything else?

Six cents covers van fuel and insurance, cold-room power, the part-time coordinator, and food-safety certification. Our two board stewards are unpaid and the depot rent is waived by the Riverdale Credit Union.

Will my name appear anywhere?

Only if you ask. Gifts under $500 are never published, and you can request a receipt under a initials-only name if you use the pantry yourself.
