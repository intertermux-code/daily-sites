Contact

The door on Mill Lane is open. So is this form.

Ask for help, offer help, or tell us we've got something wrong. A person reads every message — usually within a working day, always within three.

Four ways to reach us

  • Depot 14 Mill Lane, Ashcombe RD4 2JB. Step-free entrance at the yard gate; blue-badge parking directly outside.
  • Phone (01632) 448 900 — Mon to Sat, 08:30 to 17:00. Nadia or whoever is on the sort answers; there is no queue system.
  • Email hello@brightharvest.org for anything, ask@brightharvest.org if you would rather not say your name.
  • In person Any open shelf hour. You do not need an appointment to talk to a steward.

If it's tonight and there's no food

Ask for an emergency shelf bag

Ring the depot and say “tonight”. A volunteer packs a two-day bag and leaves it at the hatch, or brings it to you if you cannot travel. This happens about nine times a month and we never log who took one.

Call (01632) 448 900

Sent — a person will read this

Thank you for writing. Nadia Rahman or the relevant steward replies within one working day, and within three at the outside.

Who reads it

Five people, two of them volunteers

No call centre, no outsourced inbox. Every message goes to a named person who can actually change something.

Coordinator

Nadia Rahman

Runs the roster, the satellite shelves and the emergency bags. If you emailed about help or a shift, this is the hand that answers. Twelve years in valley welfare work, and she will tell you when the answer is a different agency.

Head cook

Priya Raghunathan

Open Table Fridays and the Cook Well curriculum. Took over the pantry kitchen in 2019 after cooking 300 covers a week in the city, and said the valley version was better work.

Route lead (volunteer)

Peter Lindqvist

Schedules the two rescue vans and holds the grower relationships. Retired haulier, still argues with Harte & Sons about crate depth.

Treasurer (volunteer)

Grace Oyelaran

Keeps the ledger you can ask for, signs the audit letter with Holloway & Pike, and answers anything about receipts, employer matching or legacy gifts.

Finding the place

Depot and the three satellite shelves

Ashcombe

14 Mill Lane

Main pantry and cold room. Bus 41 stops at the grain store. Yard gate is the accessible entrance — the front door has a step.

Fenwick Row

Old post office porch

Thursdays 10:00 to 12:00. Bread, produce and tinned goods on a trestle table under the awning.

Gretna Hall

Community kitchen

Cook Well workshops on the second and fourth Saturday, 10:00 to 12:00. Creche on request.

Upper Wick

St Bride's Hall

Open Table dinners, Fridays from 17:30. Level entry from Church Lane, hearing loop at the west door.

Interpreter cover for Spanish, Tagalog and Polish is on call within the hour — say the word on the phone or in the form and we'll have someone on the line when you arrive.
