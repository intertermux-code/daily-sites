Before you come

What To Expect When You Walk In

Four things we tell every first-time visitor, in the order they usually ask about them.

01 · The bar

Order At The Rails

Four brass rails, no table service, no app. Tell us the number or the name; if it is on its last keg we will say so before you commit to a pint. Halves on every line, thirds on anything over 8%.

02 · The food

A Short Menu, Cooked Hard

Pork pies and sausage from Ashcombe, soft-shell crab in August, fries, cheese boards and a pudding that changes weekly. Vegetarian and vegan plates are on the board, not an afterthought — ask and Danneke will pair them.

03 · Kids & dogs

Patio Yes, Hall After 19:00 No

Dogs are welcome everywhere on the patio and at the first two tables inside; water bowls live under the bar. Under-21s can sit on the patio with an adult until 19:00. We keep a crisps-and-juice box at the gate for £2.50.

04 · Payment

Card, Cash, Or A Tab

Contactless everywhere, cash accepted at the rails, and keg-deposit accounts for regulars. Cans and bottles in the shop take the same; we do not take reservations deposits by text.

Step-free · quiet hours

This Is A 1911 Building. We Made It Work.

The hall is step-free from the yard gate, and the yard gate is level with the cobbles. We removed four thresholds in 2020 rather than put a ramp in front of a door we liked.

  • Level entry from Foundry Row and from the yard; both doors are 1,100 mm
  • Accessible WC next to the shop, 150 mm hoist rail, grab bars both sides
  • Hearing loop at all four rails; menu and beer list also printed at 18 pt
  • Quiet hour every Wednesday 11:00–12:00 — no music, extractor on low, lights at 60%
  • Assistance dogs everywhere, including the barrel room on tours
  • Tour route has one 90 mm threshold at the cask room; we can reroute it if you tell us at booking

If something is missing from that list, call us and we will fix the list or the building, whichever is cheaper.

Saturdays · 10:00 · twelve places

Book A Cellar Tour

Ninety minutes through the working building: the mill room, the 10 hL brewhouse, the cask rail hung from the 1911 crane, and the barrel room with sets B-06 to B-11 open and unpumped.

  • Four halves poured by hand, plus a paddle of pies from Ashcombe
  • £18 per person, refunded on the night against any food order
  • Groups of 4–12; individuals welcome — we pair you up at the rail
  • 21 and over only, ID required at the gate, no exceptions for partners
  • Wheelchair-friendly apart from the cask-room threshold — tell us and we reroute

We run tours at 10:00 and 14:00 on the first and third Saturday of each month, weather permitting on the patio section.

Private hire · the hall, the yard, the rails

Take The Whole Room

Foundry No. 4 seats 96 seated, 140 standing, and the yard takes another 40 under the awning. We have held birthdays, wakes, work Christmases, one wedding and a bicycle-club AGM that ran three hours over.

  • Exclusive hire Wed–Tue, from 17:00 — the brewery keeps brewing next door
  • Three drinks packages, or a keg-and-tab account with your own cap on it
  • Kitchen, fryer, two ovens and a pie warmer; external caterers allowed, no charge

Questions We Answer Daily

Can we bring our own cake?

Yes, and we will put it in the cold room. We ask for a plate and a knife, and we will not charge you for the candles.

Is there parking for a minibus?

One minibus space in the yard, free, no booking. Tell us the plate at booking and we leave the gate unlocked.

Do you do non-alcoholic?

Two house options on the rails: a hoppy cold-brewed tea that uses our Simcoe, and a fermented elderflower. Both £3.60, both from the same kitchen.

Can we buy the beer in the glass?

Glasses and taps are ours. Growlers and half-litre bottles are in the shop, refills £11.50 any beer on tap, and Sundays they are half price.

Do you ship?

Nationwide in insulated fours, packed Mondays and Tuesdays so it lands before the weekend. Cask is collection only — it will not survive a courier van.

Are tours really free?

The tour is £18 and we refund it against food on the same visit, which is our way of making sure you eat with us rather than standing in the yard.

Is the beer vegan?

Fifteen of the seventeen on the list are. Only Foundry Saison and Barrel No. 6 Tripel use isinglass-free but honey-conditioned oak; both are marked on the wall card.

What happens if it rains?

The awning covers 40 people and the hall takes the rest. We have never cancelled a Saturday tour for weather — we move it indoors and open a barrel earlier than planned.
