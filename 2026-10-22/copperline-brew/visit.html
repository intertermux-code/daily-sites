Opening Hours

Open Six Days, Closed Mondays for Brewing

Mondays we clean the drains, plate the lab samples, and brew nothing you can buy. The kitchen stops forty-five minutes before close and the last pour is called fifteen minutes out.

Getting Here

Walk, Ride, or Park in the Truck Court

By Car

Twenty free spaces in the gravel court east of the building, plus street parking along Foundry Row after 6 PM. From I-240 take the Haywood Road exit, three lights, then a right onto Foundry.

On Foot or Bike

The greenway crosses the block two hundred yards north; there is a covered bike rack under the tin awning and a pump on the wall that still works.

Transit & Rideshare

Bus 5 stops at Foundry & Iron. Rideshare pickup and drop-off is at the east gate so cars never block the loading dock.

Accessibility

Step-free from the sidewalk through the blue door, 34-inch aisles between the benches, an accessible restroom on the main floor, and six companion seats at the bar.

Dogs are welcome on the patio and water bowls are at both ends. Large groups of eight or more should book, because the long bench fills first on Fridays.

Guided Tours

Ninety Minutes Behind the Tap Wall

A brewer walks you through the mill: grain loft, mash filter, cold room cut into the shipping bay, and the wood room with the eleven bourbon barrels. Four half-pours at the end, one of them from cask. Tours cap at fourteen people so everyone can hear over the filler.

Reserve a Tour Spot
  • Saturdays, 1 PMStandard tour — $20. Includes four pours. Under-21 guests may attend and receive juice samples.
  • Second Thursday, 6 PMBeer & Bread — $34. Rye & Route bakes alongside the kettle; six pairings and a loaf to take home.
  • Third Sunday, 2 PMLab Night — free. Nina Osei shows how a batch gets cleared: plating, gravity, and dissolved oxygen. Kids welcome.
  • Any weekday, 4 PMPrivate group walkthrough — $18/head. Minimum six, maximum twenty. Book two weeks ahead.

Reservations

Hold a Table Before the Room Fills

We keep tables until twenty minutes past the booking time; after that they go to whoever is standing at the rail. For groups over fourteen, or for the whole room, email hello@copperlinebrew.example and Dev will answer.

Seating for 14 or more requires two weeks notice.

Before You Come

Questions the Bar Gets Every Night

Can I bring my own growler?

Yes, any clean glass or stainless vessel up to 64 oz. We rinse it, weigh it, fill it, and stamp the date. Ceramic jugs with narrow necks cannot be filled safely, so bring those to the counter and we will decant into a provided jug.

Is there food?

Rye & Route parks in the truck court Thursday through Sunday, 12–9 PM: pretzels, beer cheese, fried chicken sandwiches, and a rotating stew. Outside food is allowed on the patio, and the grocery two blocks south stays open late.

Are kids welcome?

Until 8 PM, yes, with an adult. We keep apple and ginger juice behind the bar, and the third-Sunday Lab Night is explicitly designed for curious ten-year-olds.

Do you sell cans to go?

The Payoff Window is open whenever the taproom is, with house four-packs, single 16 oz cans, crowlers, and the December Ironwork release by bottle when it lands.

Can we rent the space?

Monday evenings after brew day, and Sunday after 9 PM. Capacity is 120 standing, 84 seated. Email us and we will send the buy-out sheet with the sound curfew spelled out.

Is the beer vegan?

All twelve current pours are unfined and unfiltered with no animal-derived adjuncts. Night Shift uses oat, not dairy. Every label states it plainly.
