How the Board Fills

Fourteen Days from Grain to Glass

Mill No. 4 runs a two-week cycle for ale and a five-week cycle for lager. We mash at 6:30 AM, the yard birds hop the same morning, and cans move across the alley to the cold store by Thursday.

  • Day 1Mash & lauter. Floor malt from the Weyerman line, decocted for the lagers.
  • Day 2–4Boil & whirlpool. Ninety minutes for colour stability, hop-back at flameout.
  • Day 5–9Fermentation. Open tops for the English strains, conical for everything else.
  • Day 10–13Dry hop & cold crash. Two charges, then down to 1 °C without gelatin.
  • Day 14Package or pour. Cans stamped with the mill-shift number and the day they left the filler.
