Reading the card

ABV, IBU, SRM — Plain Words

Three numbers on every card. Here is what they actually mean before you commit to a pint.

Strength

ABV — Alcohol By Volume

What the yeast ate and turned into alcohol. Under 4.5% drinks light for a full evening; 5–7% is our working range; above 8% we pour thirds and give you water with it.

Bitterness

IBU — International Bitterness Units

Measured in the lab, not guessed. Below 25 reads soft, 25–45 balances most ales, above 55 is assertive. Malt hides bitterness: our 32 IBU amber tastes rounder than a 32 IBU pilsner.

Colour

SRM — Colour Scale

One is pale straw, ten is copper, thirty is black with a tan head. It comes from the kiln, not the roast — which is why our black lager drinks clean and our amber drinks malty.

Brewhouse calendar

What Lands, And When

We brew on a Tuesday and Thursday, can on Friday, and change the wall every fortnight. Nothing stays on the list because it is familiar — only because it sold.

  • Jan–Feb — Wire Draw Doppelbock finishes; the lager wall comes back to pilsner
  • Mar–Apr — Spring best bitters, fresh-hopped Golden Promise trial brews
  • Jun–Aug — Slack Water Gose and Garden Gate Kölsch; patio pours until 23:00
  • Sep — wet-hop week; we pick at 06:00 and mash at 14:00 the same day
  • Oct — Torchlight Märzen under the marquee, plus the cask club dinner
  • Nov–Dec — barrel releases: Foundry Saison, Barrel No. 6 Tripel, winter reserve
