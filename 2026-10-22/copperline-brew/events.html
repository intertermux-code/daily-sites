The Running Order

Five things repeat whether or not the board is full. All free unless stated; all at the rails.

Wed 11:00 – 12:00

Quiet Hour

No music, extractor on low, lights at sixty percent. One staff member on the rail, hand-poured only. Best time of the week to read the wall.

Thu 19:30

Cask Club

Free for members with a pint bought; £4 for a guest. Fourteen nights a year with a cask that will not be repeated, and a hand-written card you can keep.

Fri 20:00

Session Night

Local three-pieces, no cover, sound checked at seven. We keep the back room quiet for anyone who would rather not, and the patio heaters go on early in autumn.

Sat 10:00

Cellar Tour

Ninety minutes, twelve people, £18 refunded against food. Brewhouse, cask rail, barrel room, and four halves poured by Peter at the long bench.

Sun 15:00

Board & Refill

Half-price growler refills all day, games on the west tables, and Danneke's pudding at four. Under-21s welcome in the hall on Sundays, no ID theatre.
