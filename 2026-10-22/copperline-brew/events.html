The Weekly Board

Five Nights That Run Themselves

These repeat every week of the year except the last week of December. Show up, sit anywhere, and the room will already be doing something.

Tuesday · Mill Trivia

7 PM · Free · Six rounds

Brewer-written questions with one round always on the building. Winning table gets a pin of Night Shift; last place gets a consolation pour of Anvil Light at $5.

Wednesday · Long Table Supper

6:30 PM · $26 · Book ahead

One bench, one pot, eight seats to strangers. Rye & Route cooks, we pour four beers against the menu, and the conversation does the rest.

Thursday · Cask & Condition

From 4 PM · Free · Nine casks a month

Real ale pulled by handpump through a beer engine built from mill parts. Cellar temperature, soft carbonation, and usually gone by ten.

Friday · Brass on the Dock

8 PM · Free · Live until midnight

Horn players, upright bass, whatever walks in. The loading ramp becomes the stage and the patio doors stay open all night.

Saturday · Brewhouse Tour

1 PM · $20 · Four pours included

Ninety minutes through the grain loft, cold room, and wood room. Capped at fourteen people so you can hear over the filler.

Sunday · Dogs & Pints

12–5 PM · Free · Patio

The quietest hour of the week. Bowls out, biscuits from the cart, and a slow pour of Copper Penny before the kitchen closes.
