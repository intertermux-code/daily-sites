Chapter one · 2009–2012

We Bought A Room Full Of Dead Machines

Two engineers and a cartographer found a bankrupt brass foundry with the tools still on the hooks. They kept the cranes, the windows and the floor. They brought in six fermenters and a 10-hectolitre brewhouse wrapped in copper the colour of the old nameplate.

Nine years of permits later, Rivet No. 1 poured. The first batch was an amber ale for the crew that helped lift the brewhouse in — 12 gallons, gone in a Saturday. Firebox Amber is still No. 07 on the list, still brewed to the same dog-eared card.
