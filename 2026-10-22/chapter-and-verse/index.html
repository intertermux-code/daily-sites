Cobb Street · Open till eight

The kettle is on. The shelves are hand-set.

We buy stock the way we would furnish a room: one title at a time, from a person who has read it. Seventeen years of that has filled two floors with 12,480 books, a rebinding bench, and a back room where thirty-eight reading circles argue about endings on most evenings of the week.

Ask for Nora. She is on the poetry ladder, as usual.

How the shop works

Three Things We Do Instead of an Algorithm

I.

Everything Is Shelved in the Open

No boxed bestsellers face-out, no end-cap of whatever the distributor is pushing. If we could not explain the spine to you, it does not get a place on the rail.

II.

Recommendations Are Written Down

Every pick leaves the desk on a card with a name and a reason at the bottom. Three thousand of them have left this shop; some have come back annotated.

III.

We Repair What We Sell

The bench on the mezzanine re-sews, re-backs, and re-cases anything older than your handwriting. Trade-in credit on any book we would happily put back on the shelf ourselves.
