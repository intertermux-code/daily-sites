    § 04

    Questions Before You Sign

    Do I have to have read the book?

    No. Say which page you reached and the chair will bring the room there. The one thing we ask is that you do not spoil later chapters for people who have not got there — which is also the rule for neighbours and car parks.

    What if I want to join two clubs?

    Ten per cent of our members do, and the form’s last field is where you say so. Pick one as your first choice and mention the second in the notes; we will hold a standing seat in whichever has room.

    Is it a shop meeting, or a class?

    Neither. Nobody marks your reading. The chair’s job is to keep eight people from talking over four and to make sure the newcomer gets a turn — that is the whole of the authority on the night.

    Can I bring a guest?

    Once, and tell us in the notes so we set a chair. If your guest wants to come back, they sign the book like everyone else — no membership, no obligation, no list of names we can sell.

    What happens to my details?

    Your email goes on one club list and nowhere else. We do not sell or share it, there is no newsletter attached to signing up, and replying “leave me off” to any message takes you off within the day.

    I am shy. Will I be made to speak?

    You will be asked your name and nothing else. Several members have come to six meetings running without saying a word and then talked for twenty minutes at the seventh. Both are allowed.
