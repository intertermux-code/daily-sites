§ 04

What to Expect at an Event Here

  1. Chairs before books. Arrive ten minutes early if you want a seat at the front; the room fills from the good lamps outward.
  2. Nobody quizzes you. Club nights assume you read what you could. If you got to page sixty, say so and talk about page sixty.
  3. The five dollars is for coffee and biscuits, and it goes to the bakery on the corner that supplies both.
  4. Books are on trestle, not pushed. Every event has a table of the titles mentioned, at counter price, with our shelf card on top.
  5. Silence is fine. At readings you are allowed to listen without contributing, and nobody will ask you to.
  6. We keep a waitlist by hand. If a night says full, write your name in the till book — roughly one in four cancellations reaches the list the same day.
