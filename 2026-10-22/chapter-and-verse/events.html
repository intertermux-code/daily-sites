House Notes

Arrive ten minutes early

The room is small and the door is heavy. Latecomers wait for a gap between paragraphs, which is usually only a minute or two.

Tea is not a service charge

Hojicha on Saturdays, builder’s tea the rest of the week, both free, both in the pot that has a chip in it on purpose.

No recording, no filming

People read things badly on purpose here. Please keep phones in pockets and notes in paper — we sell a cheap one by the till.

Bring the dog, or don’t

Muriel, our lurcher, sleeps in the back room and prefers children. The front room stays scent-free for anyone who needs it.

The Back Room, If You Want It

Twenty-four chairs, one long table, a wall socket, and a kettle. Free for literature groups, neighbourhood associations and anything that would otherwise meet in a pub: $40 an hour for everyone else, half price before noon.

Write to hello@chapterandversebooks.com with a date and a rough headcount, and we’ll tell you honestly whether the room is the wrong shape for what you’re doing.
