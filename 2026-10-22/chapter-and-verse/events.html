The Shape of a Year in This Shop

Sixty-four events is not a programme so much as a season table: heavier in autumn, thin in August, and one enormous argument in March that has run for eleven years.

Jan

January → March

Long Books & the March Argument

Six-session circles start on the heaviest titles we stock, because nobody reads Middlemarch in July. March closes with the annual debate — two books, one evening, sixty people, a show of hands that has never once settled anything.

14 eventsCircles: 6 sessions each
Apr

April → June

Bench Season & Poetry Month

The weather improves and the mezzanine fills: re-sewing, case-making, and two Saturday clinics for anyone who has broken a good book. May belongs entirely to poetry, including a wall we paper with other people's verses.

18 events12 bench places per class
Jul

July → August

The Porch & the Shortest Programme of the Year

Nine events, most of them outside, most of them in the evening. We move the trolley onto the pavement, the till goes on the wall, and the shop keeps the hours the heat wave allows.
