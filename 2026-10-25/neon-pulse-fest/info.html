Pier 94, Read Before You Arrive

A working warehouse on the Hudson with a pier deck over the water, two hundred metres from the nearest subway stair. Here is everything the gate, the wristband and the access desk will ask you — written out so nobody has to queue to find it.

Site: Pier 94 at The Edge Doors: Fri 18:00 · Sat 16:00 · Sun 14:00 Close: 04:00 · 08:00 · 02:00 Payment: Cashless

01 · Venue

The Room Is the Pier

Pier 94 gives us 3,800 square metres under cover plus an open deck facing the river and the Manhattan lights. Winter air carries low end cleanly, which is why Main Pulse sits outside and the two loudest rooms sit inside the old loading bays.

03 · Arrival & Leaving

Get In, Get Home Standing

There is no car park at the pier and the neighbourhood doesn't have one either. Plan on the train, the shuttle or the bike.

Subway
A/C to Fulton Street, then the M32 north to Kent & Feiner (8 min). G to Nassau Avenue, then a 12-minute walk south.
Festival shuttle
Free, Atlantic Terminal ↔ Gate A, every 20 minutes from 23:00 to close, and from 06:30 on Saturday for the sunrise crowd.
Bike valet
Attended rack inside Gate B, 90 spaces, free with a pass. Staffed from gates to clear.
Rideshare
Designated pickup at Kent Avenue & Feiner Street — never at the gate. Follow the amber marshals after close.
Cabs & SAR
Safety abstract: our night crew walks anyone to the pickup point in groups. Ask any marshal for "the walkout".
After close
Saturday clear is 09:30. The first reliable subway is the 5:30am 2/3 at Atlantic Av–Barclays; the shuttle covers before that.

04 · Access

Nothing Here Needs a Letterhead

Our access desk answers questions before you buy, and we don't ask you to prove anything to get a companion pass. Email access@neonpulse.fm and a human replies within one working day.

  • Step-free routes from both gates to all four stages and every bar.
  • Accessible viewing platforms at Main Pulse, Magenta Dome, Cyan Bunker and Afterglow — unbooked, always held.
  • Companion passes free with any paid accessible pass; one per holder, no proof required.
  • Quiet room at Z-07: low light, no music, sofas, staffed continuously.
  • BSL-interpreted headline sets on Saturday; ask when you book the pass.
  • Ear defenders (foam and graded) free at every bar and the access desk.
  • Assistance dogs welcome; relief area behind Z-09, water bowls provided.
  • Charging lockers for powered wheelchairs and medical devices at Gate B (12 bays).
  • Accessible toilets: 14 cubicles across three blocks, all with hoists except Afterglow.

05 · Conduct & Care

Three Nights, Zero Harassment

The floor belongs to whoever is on it. If someone's behaviour is making it unusable for you, tell a marshal in a hi-vis amber vest — they are briefed to act, not to take a report.

Consent
Touching anyone without a clear yes is a removal offence. No warnings, no second pass.
Hate & discrimination
Racist, sexist, homophobic, transphobic or ableist behaviour gets you escorted out and banned from future editions.
Photography
No flash, no tripod shoots outside the photo pit, and never into someone's face without asking. The Bunker is camera-free entirely.
Drugs
We don't police your body, but dealing, selling or pressuring others means immediate removal. Testing kits are free at Z-07.
Reporting
Say it to any amber vest, call the care line on your wristband print, or text the number on every bar mirror. Anonymous reports are actioned.
Prohibited
Weapons, drones, glass, aerosols, hover devices, outside alcohol, professional cameras, animals other than assistance dogs.

06 · Frequently Asked

Twelve Questions the Gate Always Gets

Expanded on 24 October. If your question isn't here, the box office answers mail in the order it arrives — usually within a day.

Ask the box office Ticket policy

Questions and answers

One bag per person, no larger than A4 (21 × 30 cm), clear or opaque. Empty your pockets of glass, aerosols and anything sharp. Sealed water bottles are allowed in, and free refill fountains are at all four bars — so the bottle you carry is optional.

Fully cashless. Contactless card, phone wallet, or the top-up tab on your wristband. Top-up terminals are at Gate A, the Food Deck and outside the Bunker, and they take notes if you want to load cash in once — the balance never expires and refunds automatically in January.

Unlimited re-entry on all pass types until each room's last set. Scan out at the gate side reader, scan back in the same way. Afterglow overnight passes re-enter through Gate B only after 03:00 because the Kent Avenue lane switches to egress.

You must be 18 on the night and the name on your pass must match the photo ID we scan at the gate. Acceptable ID: passport, driver's licence, state ID card or the photocard your venue accepts — no student card without a photo date of birth. Refused entry is not refundable, so check the name spelling when you transfer.

The festival continues. Three of the four rooms are roofed and heated, and Main Pulse is a covered deck with wind-breaks on the river side. We will delay a headline set rather than play into a lightning hold, and any hold over 40 minutes gets a text to pass holders plus a bar credit.

Play windows are fixed and our crews stop the music on the clock. The only thing that moves is a handover running a few minutes long between two artists in the same room. The schedule drops 20 November and any change after that goes to the site screens and the dispatch email, never by word of mouth.

Yes. Friday and Sunday day passes are live, Saturday sold out in three minutes and sits on the returns list. Single-night passes cost the same at the gate as online, and one day-pass holder can move to a 3-day pass by paying the difference at the box office before midnight on 1 December.

Free phone charging lockers at Z-09 — 240 bays, token-operated with your wristband, overnight safe. Powered wheelchair and medical-device charging has twelve bays under the Gate B canopy with step-free cabling. Both are open from gates to clear and neither needs pre-booking.

Nine stalls on the Food Deck, open the entire night, half the menu under $12. Every stall offers at least one vegan and one gluten-free dish, and allergen boards sit at each hatch — not on one central notice nobody reads. Filters: no reservations, cashless, and a seated area under the Dome gantry.

Refunds are only due if the event is cancelled outright or if we cannot offer you the access you booked. Everything else runs through transfer: free named transfer to a friend until 1 December, then our official resale desk relists at face value into the returns queue. No third-party resale is honoured at the gate — the wristband name check is real.

We are not a camping festival — the site is on water. The nearest hotel blocks are along Atlantic Avenue and in Williamsburg, roughly 15 minutes out of the gate at 3am. Our accommodation partner holds a room block for pass holders until 1 November; the code prints on the confirmation email.

Pick a zone number, not "by the bar" — there are eleven of those. The agreed meeting points printed on the site map are Z-07 doorway, the Food Deck stair, and the pier rail under the Dome gantry. Phone masts get overloaded at 01:00; the site runs a free message desk at both gates if a call won't connect.
