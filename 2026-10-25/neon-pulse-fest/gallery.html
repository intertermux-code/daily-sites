Nine years on the pier. Shoots the main stages, mostly 35mm fixed, never a flash on the deck.

Marisol Dune

Corridors, queues, the small hours. Available-light only, pushed to ISO 6400 and happy about it.

Tavi Beck

Crowds from above — gantry, scaffold, the Dome rig. Long exposures at the handovers.

June Achebe

Booth portraits and the welfare room. The only photographer allowed past the Bunker tape, and she doesn't shoot in there.
