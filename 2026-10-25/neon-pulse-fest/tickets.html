What the Wristband Opens

One Scan, Every Room

Pass types differ in comfort, never in music. With a $119 Sunday pass or a $749 VIP weekend, you can stand in front of the same speaker in the Bunker for four hours.

Wristband
RFID, printed and posted in early November. Activate at Gate A or B; no queue needed if you scan in at the rail.
Room metering
Cyan Bunker holds 2,200. On Saturday after 01:00 the wait runs 12 minutes — VIP and accessible holders use the second lane.
Re-entry
Unlimited, all tiers, until each room's last set.

Fine Print, Plainly

Four things people ask the box office most. The rest of the policy set is on the info page.

policy — a.txt

Age & ID

18+ only, every night. Photo ID scanned at the gate against the pass name — even for VIP.

Weather & refunds

The festival runs rain, sleet or shine; the pier is covered and the dome is heated. Full refund only if the event is cancelled outright.

policy — b.txt

Transfers & resale

Free named transfer until 1 December. After that, our official resale platform matches you to the returns queue at face value.

Lost wristband

Wristbands are RFID and replaceable at the box office for $10 with photo ID, twice per pass maximum.
