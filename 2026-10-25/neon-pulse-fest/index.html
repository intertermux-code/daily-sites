01 · The Signal

Not a Lineup. a Circuit

Neon Pulse began in 2019 as a single borrowed sound system in a Red Hook garage. Seven years later it is three nights on the waterfront, built around one rule: the music never stops while the site is open.

Every stage runs a continuous hand-to-hand set chain, so the artist who closes Magenta Dome hands the decks to the artist who opens Cyan Bunker. You follow the energy instead of checking your phone. Sound is Funktion-Air, tuned nightly by our crew against a measured target curve — 31 Hz to 16 kHz, ±2 dB across every room.

02 · Headline Transmission

The Three Names That Own the Weekend

Full day-by-day grid, including every support bill and b2b handover, lives on the lineup page.

Friday Headline

Voltage Saint

Main Pulse · 00:30–02:00 · Live A/V

The Berlin-based producer closes out night one with a ninety-minute live set — no playback, analogue drums through the pier's own steel resonance. Her 2026 Hardlight record gets its first full east-coast run here.

Saturday Headline

Nullbyte

Magenta Dome · 01:00–03:00 · Two-hour special

Two hours, one tempo ramp from 128 to 174 BPM with no verbal breaks. Nullbyte debuts the Cyan Bunker drum-and-bass edit suite that half the site will be hunting for the rest of the winter.

Sunday Close

Axiom b2b Vanta

Cyan Bunker · 22:00–02:00 · Back to back

The closing back-to-back returns to the warehouse room where the festival started. Four hours, two decks, no headliner staging — and the lights come up slowly, over twenty minutes, on purpose.
