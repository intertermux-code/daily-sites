The spine of our work

From Field to Table in Five Hops

Every crate that enters Bright Harvest follows the same short, traceable line. Here is what happens between a carrot leaving a row and landing on a family’s dinner plate.

  1. 01

    Harvest & Hand-Off

    Partner farms flag surplus rows and grocers flag short-code produce by 4pm. Our refrigerated route van picks up along nine loops the same evening.

    4:00–9:30pm · 9 collection loops

  2. 02

    Inspect at the Dock

    Trained volunteers check temperature, firmness, and labelling. Anything that fails the taste-and-safety panel is weighed and sent to our compost partners instead.

    Reject rate: 3.1% · Every crate logged

  3. 03

    Cold Sort & Store

    Produce is washed, trimmed, and crated into three grade lines in our 40-foot cooler. Grain, tinned goods, and shelf-stable items keep their own dry room.

    2,400 sq ft · 4°C holding

  4. 04

    Pack the Way Families Choose

    Instead of mystery hampers, neighbours shop a pantry shelf layout and pick what their household actually eats — halal, kosher, baby, diabetic-friendly, and pet food all included.

    shopper-chosen · 7 dietary lines

  5. 05

    Deliver & Stay Awhile

    Sites open six days a week. Van routes reach four rural towns, and every stop keeps a benefit-enrolment desk and a warm pot of soup on the table.

    34 sites · Avg. visit 22 min

Four standing programs

Pick the Door That Fits Your Street

Browse all programs and schedules→

  • Market-Style Pantries

    Shop a grocery-layout pantry in Riverton, Fairview, and Old Mill. Take what you want, leave what you don’t.

    Open six days · 2,100 households/week

  • Mobile Harvest Vans

    Two refrigerated vans loop four rural towns and six apartment campuses where the nearest grocer is 14 miles away.

    18 stops · Thursdays & Sundays

  • Kitchen Cookshops

    Ninety-minute hands-on classes that build five weeknight meals out of whatever is in season and on the shelf.

    Free · Childcare at the door

  • Grow-Your-Own Plots

    Eighty raised beds on donated land, seeded and mentored so households harvest their own summer vegetables.

    Waitlist 2 weeks · Tools provided
