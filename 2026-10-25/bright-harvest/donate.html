Accountability

The Nine-Day Life of a Fifty-Dollar Gift

Follow one real transfer from your bank to a dinner table, with the paperwork we keep at every hop.

  1. D1

    Gift Clears

    Processor settles the transfer. Finance books it against whichever fund you designated, and the receipt number is issued the same afternoon.

    Settlement: 1 business day

  2. D2

    Buy Order Written

    Our produce coordinator reads the week’s shortage board — squash in Old Mill, calcium in Riverton — and writes one buy order against local partners.

    Wholesale target: $0.42/meal

  3. D3

    Loaded at the Farm

    Cut in the morning, crated by noon. Green Valley Co-op holds our pallets in its cooler so produce never waits on a hot dock.

    Avg. field-to-cooler: 6 hours

  4. D5

    Sorted by Volunteers

    Two dock shifts grade and crate the delivery. Every pound is weighed onto the scanner log so your gift ends up in a public number, not a brochure one.

    99,240 lb logged this year

  5. D6

    Shopped, Not Handed Out

    Saturday at Riverton: the household chooses its own vegetables. A staff note records the site and date; no names, no questions, no forms at the gate.

    34 sites · open six days

  6. D9

    Counted in the Letter

    The meal total rolls into the monthly Harvest Letter, alongside what failed inspection and got composted. You see the misses too, not only the wins.

    Reported monthly since 2014

Beyond a single gift

Other Ways to Put Food on the Table

Talk to us about any of these→

  • Harvest Circle (monthly)

    Twenty-two neighbours now give $10–$40 a month. The steady number is what lets us sign a season contract with Riverbend Farms instead of buying spot.

    Cancel by one email · upgrades welcome

  • Workplace Food Drive

    Run a collection for the two most useful items on our shortage board — pasta sauce and cooking oil — or collect for our cash match pool instead. We bring the bin, the poster, and the count sheet.

    Minimum 3 weeks · pickup included

  • Employer Matching

    Fairbridge Credit Union, Northline Schools, and Sunbelt Logistics all match staff gifts dollar-for-dollar up to $1,500. Send us your portal link and we’ll file the claim.

    Last year’s match: $41,800

  • Donate a Harvest

    Growers with an unharvested field row or a short-code pallet: we collect, insure the pickup, and issue a charitable receipt for the appraised value.

    9 collection loops · same-week pickup

  • Legacy & Stock

    Name Bright Harvest in a will, or transfer appreciated stock through our broker so neither side pays the capital gain. Our director handles the paperwork with your advisor.

    No minimum · receipts as normal

  • Birthday & Funeral Funds

    Families raise in a neighbour’s name all the time. We set up a page, send the shareable card, and publish the total so everyone sees what the collection bought.

    Set up in one email reply

Before you give

Questions Donors Actually Ask

Does my gift buy imported food?

Almost never. 78% of purchases last season came from farms inside the three counties, and we pay a fair wholesale price rather than dumping surplus onto them. When local supply gaps in February, we buy regionally before we buy nationally.

What if I give monthly and need to pause?

Email hello@brightharvest.org with your receipt number and we pause or reduce it that day — no phone call, no retention pitch. Around thirty people have done this and come back.

Can I direct my gift to a specific pantry site?

Yes, though we ask you to name a program rather than a single address so food can move where the week demands. If a site you care about runs short, our coordinator will tell you exactly what it needs.

Why not donate canned goods instead?

We accept them gladly at four drop points, and 14% of meals still start there. But cash buys fresh, buys the specific shortage, and skips the sorting, storage, and freight that make a donated can cost us more than it feeds.

Are my details shared with anyone?

No. We do not sell, rent, or trade donor lists, and we do not take part in co-op mailings. The only public list is an opt-in donor wall in the Riverton lobby.
