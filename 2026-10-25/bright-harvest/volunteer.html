Volunteer

Two Hours Here Moves a Ton of Food

Fourteen volunteers work the Riverton dock on a Thursday. Between them they grade, wash, and crate about 4,800 pounds of produce — the entire Thursday supply for three counties. That is the shift you are signing up for.

  • Training on the job, first shift shadowed
  • 16+ unaccompanied, under 16 with a guardian
  • Closed-toe shoes, we lend the vest

Your spot is held — welcome to the crew

Bring closed-toe shoes and arrive ten minutes early for a vest, a locker, and a short safety walk. We start every new volunteer shadowed on the sort line, so nobody is dropped onto a pallet lift cold.

  • Enter through the west dock door, 118 Dockside Lane — the orange one.
  • Coffee and a biscuity breakfast on the table from 7am.
  • Running late or changed your mind? Text the shift line, no apology needed.

What to expect

Your First Four Weeks, Honestly Described

No mystery, no cold start. This is exactly how a new volunteer runs at Bright Harvest.

  1. WK 1

    Shadowed Sort Line

    You pair with a veteran for the whole shift. You learn the three grade lines, the scanner log, and which crates go to which site. Nobody asks you to drive, lift alone, or meet shoppers.

    Arrive 10 min early · Breakfast provided

  2. WK 2

    Pick Your Lane

    Your coordinator checks in for five minutes and moves you toward the job you liked: pantry floor, van loading, kitchen, or plots. Most people discover they wanted something different from what they signed up for.

    Switch any time

  3. WK 3

    Short Training Block

    Ninety minutes on food safety, allergen separation, and shopper privacy. Paid, catered, and held on the last Sunday of the month — plus the licence check if you want the van.

    Food-safety certificate · Optional

  4. WK 4

    You Get Named a Route

    A standing weekly slot, your own locker, and a lanyard colour so staff know you can be trusted with keys. Roughly seven in ten volunteers reach this point; the ones who do stay an average of 2.6 years.

    2.6-year average tenure

Groups & teams

Bring Twelve People and a Thursday Disappears

Book a team day→

  • Team dock day

    Two to three hours of sorting with a briefing and a wrap. Sunbelt Logistics brings its whole dispatch team every quarter; we supply vests, gloves, and a photographer if you want proof for the intranet.

    6–40 people · free · boots required

  • School service block

    Classes of fifteen or more run a guided pantry-floor rotation with reflection worksheets tied to provincial health and citizenship outcomes. Two teachers now bring the same group three years running.

    Grade 7+ · consent forms at the door

  • Faith & neighbourhood crews

    St. Anne’s parish takes one Saturday a month, start to finish: set up, shop floor, pack-down, and wash-up. You can do the same with your block, running club, or reading group.

    Recurring or one-off · childcare room free

Before you sign up

Fair Questions About the Work

Do I need experience or a background check?

No experience for any role. A police check is required only for van runners and anyone working the kids’ session at cookshops — we cover the fee and it takes about ten days.

Can I volunteer if I use a food pantry myself?

Yes, and around a dozen people do exactly that. Nothing about receiving food disqualifies you, we don’t record why you come, and the code of conduct protects every shopper equally, including you.

What if I can’t lift much?

Tell us on the form. Pantry hosting, cash room, evening admin, school-snack packing, and the soup table are all seated or light-lift roles, and roughly a fifth of our roster works them.

Is there a minimum commitment?

One shift. Truly. If it suits, we’ll offer a standing slot; if it doesn’t, we’d rather you came twice than signed up for a year and never showed.

Can teenagers earn service hours here?

Yes from age 14 with a guardian’s signature, on the sort line and packing tables. We sign official hours sheets at the desk the same evening, and we’ve signed 3,400 hours for local students since 2018.

Not the right fit?

Money Works Too, and It’s Faster

If the dock isn’t your thing, $50 buys the same forty pounds you’d sort in an hour — without the commute. Both keep the table set.
