Getting here

Bus, Bike, Car, or On Foot

The Riverton hub sits two blocks from the Millgate interchange, with covered bike racks at the north door and free parking on the dock apron after 4pm.

  • Bus — routes 4, 11, and 12 stop at Dockside & Mill; shelter seating at both.
  • Step-free — automatic doors, 90cm aisles, accessible WC, and a van ramp to the curb.
  • Interpreters — Spanish and Portuguese on-site Tuesdays; ASL booked with 24 hours’ notice.
  • Delivery help — staff carry to your car or van; ask a host at the desk.

Growers, grocers, and hosts

Three Ways Organisations Join In

Talk to Marcus Oyelaran, partnerships lead — partners@brightharvest.org or (555) 740-2299. He answers in one working day, usually the same afternoon.

  1. A

    Donate Produce

    Unharvested rows, short-code pallets, pack-out rejects. We collect on nine evening loops, insure the pickup, handle the food-safety paperwork, and receipt at appraised value.

    412 partners today · No cost to you

  2. B

    Host a Stop

    A parking lot, church yard, or forecourt for ninety minutes a week. We bring tables, crates, signage, insurance, and two staff; you bring the space and, if you like, a kettle.

    18 van stops hosted · 3 sites vacant

  3. C

    Fund a Route or a Shelf

    Named sponsorships start at $2,500 a season: one van refrigeration week, one calcium shelf, or one cookshop term. We publish what it bought and never put your logo on a shopper.

    Six shelves open · Receipted gift

Practical questions

Before You Ring the Bell

I need food today. What’s the fastest route?

Walk into whichever site is open: Riverton Mon/Tue/Thu/Sat mornings, Fairview Wed/Fri 10am–2pm, Old Mill Tue/Sat 11am–3pm. Nothing to bring, nothing to sign. If all doors have closed for the day, call us and we’ll arrange a van drop.

Can someone collect for me if I can’t get out?

Yes. Ask for the delivery list at any desk or call the office; a volunteer driver takes your choices from a photo list of what’s on the shelves that morning. Around 70 households use this each week.

Do you accept gift cards, airline miles, or old stock?

Gift cards, yes — they convert to produce at the Meridian Market till. Airline miles, no. Pallet returns and unsellable stock we assess case by case; photograph the load and email partnerships before anyone unloads it.

Is it safe to come without papers?

Completely. We do not record immigration status, we do not share information with any government agency, and our sites do not host enforcement appointments. Nobody is turned away for lacking identification.

Where can I see your numbers before donating?

The monthly Harvest Letter carries what came in, what went out, and what failed inspection. Ask the front desk for the last twelve issues or tick the letter box on the donation page.
