02 — How we make it

Ninety minutes from bed to beaker

We harvest at dawn when the essential oils sit highest in the leaf, take the trays ninety minutes down the road to the lab, and cool them before extraction. That single constraint shapes everything: smaller batches, honest dates on the base, and formulas that behave the same way in July and January.

Field before formula

We choose the plant first, then write the product around what it genuinely does — not the other way round.

Ten is the whole line

Adding an eleventh formula means retiring one. That rule is why nothing here has been reformulated into obscurity.

Third-party every batch

Purity, heavy metals and preservative efficacy are tested externally. Certificates are searchable by batch code.
