03 — Asked most

The five that reach the desk weekly

If your question is answered here, you can skip the form and go straight to the shop — everything below is what we tell people in reply anyway.

How do I return glass for a refill?

Rinse four empty vessels, let them dry upside down, and pack them in the box your last order arrived in. Request a prepaid label from hello@verdant.skin and post it within ten days. Four clean vessels earns a free pour of any formula; pouch orders and returned glass do not affect your sixty-day return window.

Which formula should I introduce first?

The cleanser, then the essence, then one treatment. Barrier health decides whether an active is tolerable at all, so we would rather you spend two weeks on cleanse-and-hydrate before the vitamin C or the clay mask enters the routine.

Why is my bottle a different colour this time?

Botanical extracts carry pigment and it moves with the season. Calendula water from an April cut is paler than a September one, and we do not add colour to even it out. The batch certificate will show the same active concentration within 0.2% either way.

Can I use Verdant alongside a prescription?

Usually, with the cleanser and the cushion cream being the safest partners around tretinoin or a barrier-damaging treatment. Send your prescriber’s list to the bench and we will flag any of our formulas that overlaps in actives rather than guessing.

Do you ship outside the United States?

We ship to Canada, the UK, Japan and Australia from the farm, duties calculated at checkout. Everywhere else is served through our two Tokyo stockists and our Melbourne stockist, who carry the same monthly batch as the online shop.

Is the SPF fine on deep skin tones?

Bloom Mineral SPF 30 uses non-nano zinc dispersed in a fennel-and-glycerin base, formulated and panel-tested on tones from Fitzpatrick I to VI. If it ever casts on your skin, photograph it, and we will refund the bottle the same day.
