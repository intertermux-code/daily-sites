Before you add

Shipping, plainly

When does my order leave the farm?

Orders placed before 11am Pacific leave the same afternoon, Monday through Thursday. Fridays we pack, harvest, and occasionally both.

What does shipping cost?

$5 flat under $60 and complimentary above it. Ground in the US arrives in two to four days; we do not fly formulas cross-country.

Can I return an opened bottle?

Yes — sixty days, opened, and we refund the full price. Send a photo of how the skin reacted and the batch number, and the money goes back the same day.

Do you blend for sensitive skin?

Eight of the ten formulas are unscented. The two that carry botanical scent — the night balm and the body lotion — list the exact essential oil percentage so you can decide.
