    02 — The method

    Thin first, thick last

    Every Verdant routine stacks the same way: cleanse, water layer, treatment, seal. The quiz only chooses which bottle fills each slot — which is why a four-step routine from us takes three minutes, not twelve.

    Cleanse — 40 seconds

    Clover Milk Cleanser on dry skin first to lift sunscreen, then water to turn it to milk. Rinse lukewarm; hot water is a barrier problem pretending to be a preference.

    Prepare — 10 seconds

    Morning Dew Essence pressed into skin that is still damp from the rinse. Water is the vehicle; applying it dry wastes half the bottle.

    Treat — 20 seconds

    The goal decides this slot: vitamin C drops for tone, the clay mask weekly for texture, the compress for hydration, the cushion cream for redness.

    Seal — 20 seconds

    Mineral SPF if the routine is a minute long, squalane if three, the night balm if you have time to warm it between the palms first.
