How we photograph

Same ruler every time

A retractor, a grey background card, a 100 mm macro lens at f/16, and a colour reference in frame. Nothing is retouched, and no image is shown that was taken in a worse light than its comparison.

What we decline

Cases we sent away

Four patients last year left without cosmetic treatment because the complaint was not visible in the mouth. We would rather lose the fee than reshape healthy enamel to fix a photograph.

Consent

Published by permission
