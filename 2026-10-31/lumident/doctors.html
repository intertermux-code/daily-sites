Standards

Audited, not assumed

Sterilisation logs are reviewed monthly by an external practice-development officer, and our radiology equipment is dose-audited each February. Ask at the desk to see either file.

Training

Twenty days a year

Every clinician completes twenty hours of continuing education annually, with at least four hours on managing dental anxiety. Two of us teach the same course at OHSU.

Access

Getting in the door

Step-free entrance on Halden Row, a 90 cm treatment aisle, hoist transfer by arrangement, and a quiet waiting corner away from the street.
