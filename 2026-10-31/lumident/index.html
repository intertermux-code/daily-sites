Section 02 — Protocol

How a visit runs, hour by hour

Four entries, in order. Nothing is improvised, and nothing is billed that was not on the written plan.

  1. 01

    Arrival & records

    You check in at the desk, then sit in the lounge rather than a corridor. We confirm medical history, insurance details, and the reason for the visit in writing before the chair is prepared.

    0:00 – 0:10
  2. 02

    Examination

    Your clinician works through the chart tooth by tooth, narrating findings as they appear on the overhead screen so you can see exactly what we see.

    0:10 – 0:40
  3. 03

    The written plan

    We print a three-column plan — now, soon, later — with a fixed price beside every line, then walk you through it. You take a copy home; you are never asked to decide in the chair.

    0:40 – 0:55
  4. 04

    Treatment or referral

    If work was pre-authorised it begins that same visit. Otherwise the coordinator books the slots, sets the payment plan, and emails your confirmation within the hour.

    0:55 – 1:00
