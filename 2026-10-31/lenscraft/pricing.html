Questions on cost

Before you ask.

Anything not answered here gets a reply within twenty-four hours.

Ask me directly →

Do you charge for travel?+

Included travel is 40 miles from Leith for elopements and 120 miles for The Daylight. Beyond that I quote fuel, ferry or train at cost with a £40 handling line — never a percentage, and always before you sign.

What happens if you are ill on the day?+

I keep a standing arrangement with two documentary photographers in Edinburgh and one in Inverness. If I cannot travel, one of them shoots your day at your booked package rate and I grade the files myself. In fourteen years that has happened once.

Can we pay in instalments?+

Yes. A 25% deposit secures the date, then three optional instalments, with the balance due twenty-one days before the wedding. Bank transfer or card, no surcharge.

Is our date held while we decide?+

Enquiries hold the date for seven days at no cost, and I tell any other couple asking about the same weekend that it is on provisional hold. After that the date releases unless the deposit is paid.

Do we have to buy an album?+

No. The Archive includes one and The Daylight includes one because most couples want a physical copy, but you can take a £480 credit off either package instead and order prints later at the same trade rates.

Do you release the raw files?+

You get every delivered frame as a full-resolution TIFF and JPEG with a print release, plus the graded catalogue file. Unselected raws are not delivered — that is the edit, and the edit is the product.
