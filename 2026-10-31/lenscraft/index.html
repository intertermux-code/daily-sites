Operating method Four stages

How a Lenscraft day runs.

Nothing about the schedule is improvised on the morning. We plan the light, you plan the party, and the photographs take care of themselves.

Read the full approach →

Stage 01 · Six months out

The timeline audit

We rebuild your running order around the sun. Two hours of golden light at your venue gets a named slot, and the group shot takes four minutes, not twenty.

Stage 02 · Four weeks out

Shot list, cut small

You get a thirty-item must-have list, not a hundred. Everything else is my job to find — the hands, the hallway, the uncle asleep in the snug.

Stage 03 · The day

One camera, no shadow

I work silently in available light, in the clothes you invited me in. You will rarely hear the shutter and never hear me ask you to do that again.

Stage 04 · 28 days later

Edit, then album

Every frame hand-graded, a first look at 80 selects within ten days, then a layflat proof you mark up before the final file drop.
