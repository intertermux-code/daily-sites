How the room books

Two seatings, five to nine and nine to close. The counter of eight is the only seating we take for the seven-course Ember Table, Wednesday through Saturday.

Parties of nine or ten are seated at the long oak table and ordered together — call us for those, because the menu differs from the printed one.

Walk-ins & the bar

Eight stools at the bar are never reserved. They go at 5 p.m. sharp, and the full menu is cooked there, including the turbot when it lands.

Ring (503) 555-0148 from 4 p.m. to hear what the fire is doing tonight.

Cancellations

Let us know four hours ahead and there is no charge. Beyond that we bill 25 dollars a seat, because the coal bed was dressed for you and the farms were paid on Friday.
