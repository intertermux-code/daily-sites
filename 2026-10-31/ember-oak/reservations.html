Before you book

How the Book Works

Sixty days out

Reservations release every morning at 9:00 a.m. for the same date sixty days ahead. Monday the ovens are cold and the book is closed.

Walk-ins at the counter

Sixteen seats each service are never released online. Come before 18:00 or after 20:30 and you will very likely eat that night.

Large parties

Seven to twelve go on the Long Oak Table with a set fire menu. Send the request with a note and the host calls you back the same day.

Cancellations

Cancel free up to four hours ahead. Late cancellations and no-shows carry twenty dollars a head, because the coals were lit for you.
