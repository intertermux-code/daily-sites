01 — The House

A Brick Hearth Built by Hand in an Old Iron Foundry

In 2014 we gutted a foundry on Foundry Street, kept the chimney, and laid a hearth in it with reclaimed firebrick. Nine years later nothing on our pass has touched a gas burner. Oak carries the long cook; apple sweetens the last ten minutes.

Chef Rhiannon Pike buys whole animals, splits the wood herself on Tuesdays, and rewrites two sections of the menu every fortnight depending on what the coals do well.

  • Whole-animal butchery, nothing trimmed away
  • Wood cut, split, and rested eighteen months
  • Producers inside a ninety-mile radius first
