Before You Come

The five things we are asked most.

If your question is not here, the bar answers the phone from four o’clock and knows exactly what the coals are doing.

Do you take walk-ins?

Eight bar stools are held back every night and go at 5 p.m. sharp. You get the full menu, the same fire, and usually a better conversation. We do not seat walk-ins at dining tables during the 7 p.m. rush.

Can you cook around allergies?

Yes, with two days’ notice for anything serious. The kitchen is one open fire, so we cannot promise zero cross-contact for shellfish or wheat — we will tell you honestly which plates are safe and rewrite the rest.

Is there a vegetarian menu?

Six starters and two mains stand on their own without the grill’s meat juices, plus a full ash-baked vegetable at the counter. Sundays the fire lunch can be converted entirely if you tell us when booking.

What should we wear?

Nothing you mind smelling like smoke afterwards. The room runs warm in winter and the alley door opens in summer. No dress code, no jackets required, and the candles do the rest.

How late can we order?

Last food orders are 9:15 p.m. Tuesday to Thursday, 10:15 p.m. Friday and Saturday, and 2:15 p.m. on Sunday. The bar keeps pouring for an hour after the coals are drawn down.
