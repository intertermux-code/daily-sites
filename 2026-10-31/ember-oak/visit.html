Sunday Hearth

One long lunch service from 11:00, cooked off the embers raked the night before. The whole hog is on from noon until it is gone, usually by three. No reservations after 14:00 — the room is first come.

Private Dining & Buyouts Private dining

The Long Oak Table seats twelve beside the fire, and the whole room holds sixty-four standing. Buyouts run Mondays when the hearth is lit for one party only. Write to events@emberandoak.com with a date and a head count.

Before You Come

Is the menu the same in summer?

Two sections change. Cold ash-grown vegetables, day-boat shellfish, and whole fish take the heavier winter cooks from June to September. The hearth never goes out — it simply runs cooler and faster.

Can you cook around an allergy?

Usually, if we hear it before you order. Everything shares one fire and one grill, so we cannot promise a separate surface for coeliac or severe shellfish allergies. Nuts, dairy, and gluten are workable on most courses.

Do you take children?

Welcome until 20:00, Tuesday through Sunday. No fire-menu courses under eight, and the counter is not safe for little ones who like to stand. The back hall has four booths that fit a family of five.

What about the wait list?

Sixteen seats stay unbooked every service. Put your name at the host desk and wait on Foundry Street — we text when a table clears, and we hold it for eight minutes.

Is there a dress code?

None, though coats come off and shirts stay on. Note that smoke is a real ingredient here: fabric carries it home, and we keep a cedar-scented spray by the cloakroom for exactly that reason.

Getting Here

  • On foot. Eight minutes from the Kennedy Street end of Olneyville; the last block is cobble, so heels sink.
  • By bus. Routes 92 and 51 stop at Wickenden and Foundry, two minutes from the copper lantern.
  • Parking. Free lot behind the building, eleven spaces, entered from Kiln Alley. Street parking on Foundry is two-hour after 18:00.
  • Bikes. A rack of twelve rings sits under the delivery arch; we do not lock it up for you.
  • Access. Step-free entry from the alley side, one accessible restroom on the ground floor, and four tables with 36-inch turn radius. Call ahead and the host will hold one.
