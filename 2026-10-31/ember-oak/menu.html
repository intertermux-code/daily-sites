How to Read This Menu

  • Wood tags name the fuel and the minutes over coals — oak for long heat, apple for the sweet finish.
  • Two woods, no gas. Nothing on this list is sped up on a burner, so a slow course can arrive eight minutes late.
  • Allergens. Tell your server before you order; the kitchen cooks in one shared fire and cannot guarantee a separate surface.
  • Service. Kitchen takes orders until 21:15 Tuesday to Thursday, 22:30 Friday and Saturday, 19:15 on Sunday.
