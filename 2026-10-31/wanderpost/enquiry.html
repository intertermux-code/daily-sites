What Happens Next, in Order.

  1. 01

    Two working days: a guide's reply

    Not a holding email. The person who will lead your route reads it and answers, including whether your month is the one we would change.

  2. 02

    A thirty-minute call

    Who is coming, what bored you last time, whether you want looking after or left alone on day four. Most trips are settled here.

  3. 03

    A written plan and a price

    Day-by-day, itemised, with the flight options at cost. Deposit is £750 per person and the balance is due seventy days out.

  4. 04

    The brown envelope

    Fourteen weeks before departure: route notes, paper map, a letter from your guide, and a stamp from the place you are going.

Is my data kept safe?

We store enquiry details for eighteen months on servers in the UK, share them only with the guide running your route and our flight provider, and delete them on request. No third-party advertising trackers run on this site.

Do you take private or company groups?

Yes — up to sixteen on Marrakech and Kyoto as a private departure, with the same guides and a different price. Ring the office, because the calendar matters more than the headcount.

What is the cancellation policy?

Full refund of the deposit up to ninety days out, then 40% of the fare, then nothing inside thirty days. Every booking is ATOL-protected (10152) and financially bonded through ABTOT, so if we fell over tomorrow your money and your flight home are covered.

Can I change my month after booking?

Once, free, up to six months out, subject to the departure not being full. Nine people a year do it and usually move to February or late May — the months in the month picker with the green dots.
