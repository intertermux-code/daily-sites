Kyoto: The Long Temple Season

£6,480Per person · 21 days

  • WhenLate Feb – Nov
  • PaceSlow · 6–8 km daily

Three weeks in one neighbourhood: a restored timber house in Kamigyo, a standing table at a five-seat breakfast counter, and enough mornings at the same temple gate that the keeper starts a sentence rather than finishing it. Marrakech: The Medina Hours

£4,620Per person · 14 days

  • WhenOct – Apr
  • PaceSlow · walking city
  • PartyMax 8
  • BasisTwo riads + mountain camp

Ten days inside the walls, learning the clock of a city that changes character at five in the afternoon. Two questions that settle it

Do you want to be warm and still, or moving and cold? Kyoto and Marrakech are the standing-still trips: one base, one market, one walk you repeat until you stop noticing it. Patagonia and Lofoten move every second or third day and involve weather judgement calls made out loud, on a ridge, with a map.

The second question is about appetite. If a fourteen-day trip feels like a lot, Marrakech is the shortest route we run and the easiest to extend — most travellers add three days in the Atlas mountains at the end and none of them regret it.

“We booked Patagonia expecting mountains and got a lesson in patience. Day nineteen, the wind dropped for two hours, and nobody on the trip has stopped talking about it since.”

Tom & Priya Sandhu · Patagonia, January 2026
