Getting Here Without Trouble

Parking, Transit,
and the Long Way In

Ferndale is dense and the lot fills on Friday. Here is what actually works, in the order we would try it.

The Lot Behind

Twenty spaces off Loomis, free, no attendant. Full by 7 pm on a Thursday with music, always full on a Friday barrel release.

Nine Mile Station

Four blocks east on the SMART bus line, every twelve minutes until 10 pm. Walk down Ridge Road and you will smell the kettle before you see the doors.

Bikes and Dogs

Two racks under the hose tower, always usable. Dogs welcome on the patio and the long bar, not on the canning floor. We keep a bowl by the door and nobody has ever complained about the water.

Asked at the Bar Most Weeks

Before You Come

Do you take table reservations?

No. Twenty stools, six tables, first come. On a Friday with music the bar fills around 6:30 pm and turns over slowly; the patio and the loft stay easier. If you are a party of six or more, call ahead and we will hold the long table until 7 pm.

Can I bring children?

Under-21 guests are welcome in the taproom and patio until 8 pm with a parent, and the kitchen will feed them. Nobody under 21 goes onto the cellar floor or the canning room, tour or no tour.

Is there non-alcoholic beer?

Two on the board at all times — a hop-water we brew for the crew and a de-alcoholised pilsner pulled off the same batch as Steam Whistle. Both are $5 and poured in the same glassware, because a paper cup is a bad way to judge anyone’s choice.

Do you sell cans to go?

Yes, at the bar and at the door, Monday through Saturday. Four-packs of Hook & Ladder, Chrome Horizon, Brass Ring, and Copperline Sour; single tallboys of whatever we canned that morning. Barrel bottles are limited to two per person on release day.

Are you wheelchair accessible?

The taproom, bar, restrooms, and patio are all step-free, and the bar height works at 34 inches. The cellar floor and the loft are reached by stairs only — tell us at the bar and we will bring the tour to you instead.

Can I fill a growler from any tap?

Any line except the two barrel pours and the nitro. Barrel beer goes out in 16 oz crowlers so it keeps its carbonation; nitro has to settle in the glass and does not survive a bottle.
