Take It Home

Cans, Bottles,
Growlers, Crowlers

Everything we sell goes out of the building it was brewed in. No co-packer, no long cold chain, no beer older than a month on our shelf.

Growlers

32 oz, filled to the brim, capped tight. Free for the first fill, $3 after. Drink within three days and keep them cold — we do not add CO2 to the bottle.

Canned Freshness

Every can carries two dates: the pack date stamped on the seam and a best-by of 90 days on the lid. Light and time are the only two things that hurt this beer, and both are avoidable.

“If it isn’t good on the second pint, we don’t bottle it.”

House Rule No. 2
90
