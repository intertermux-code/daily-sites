Chapter One · The Firehouse on Ridge Road

Built From Hose,
Wire, and Copper

Three founders, one condemned engine house, and a 1,400-square-foot brewhouse that fits between the original apparatus floor and the drying loft. Here’s the wall of it.

“We kept the hose tower. Everything else we melted down and started over.”

Marge Kestrel · Founding Brewer
1912

Year the firehouse was built

Seven barrels

Our largest batch size on purpose. If a recipe can’t be tasted in one afternoon, it doesn’t get a line.

The Name

Copperline

The copper pipe that ran from the street hydrant to the engine house doors. Locals called it the copper line. We borrowed it and never asked.

“Amber beer, chrome light, one rule: pour it like you’d drink it.”

Dov Amaya · Co-Owner
