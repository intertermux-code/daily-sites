02

Operating doctrine

Four rules

“We do not chase sweat. We chase the number going up next month.”

— Mara Voss, head coach
  • R.01

    Technique before load, every single time

    Your first three sessions are bar-only. A coach films your squat, bench, and deadlift, and you get the footage with frame notes before you put weight on the bar.

  • R.02

    Written programming, not vibes

    Every block is published seven days ahead. Sets, reps, rest, and target RPE are printed on the board and in your phone, so you never negotiate with yourself mid-set.

  • R.03

    Rest is programmed, not forgiven

    Two down days minimum. If you log fewer than six hours of sleep three nights running, your coach drops the top set — that is the rule, not a favour.

  • R.04

    Measure what you claim

    Body composition, jump height, and lift totals get re-tested at week six of every block. The chart goes in your logbook and you own it.
