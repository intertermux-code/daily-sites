04

Before you ask

Six answers
Do I need to be strong before I join?

No. Roughly four in ten members had never touched a barbell when they walked in. The first three sessions are bar-only, and the Wednesday technique lab exists specifically so nobody has to learn in front of a loaded platform.

What happens if I miss a fortnight?

Nothing punitive. Tell your coach and the block restarts at the previous week’s loads rather than your last working set. Forge Pass includes 60 pause days a year, FORGE+ includes 120.

Is there a contract or joining fee?

No joining fee on any tier. Monthly memberships roll and cancel from the member area in about forty seconds. Annual is twelve months paid up front, two months free.

Can I train on my own instead of in classes?

Yes. The gate is open 05:00–22:00 on Forge Pass and around the clock on FORGE+. Eleven platforms stay unbooked; a floor coach is on the platform Tuesday and Friday evenings if you want a set checked.

I have a shoulder that bothers me. Should I stay away?

Come in and say so on the form. Cass Nolan runs a Wednesday evening clinic that clears variations for you, and Ines Duarte covers the load-management side. We will scale the plan rather than send you home.

Do you have showers, and what do I actually need to bring?

Six showers, two changing bays, and a lockable store. Bring flat-soled shoes, a water bottle, and a small towel if you are taking a class; we rent belts and sleeves at the desk for two dollars a session.
