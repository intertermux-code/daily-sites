How we coach

Standard across all six
  • 01

    Every coach films

    iPad on a tripod at every platform. You can ask for your clip at any point in a session and it lands in your member area within two hours, with spoken notes.

  • 02

    Every coach reads your log first

    Your last session’s RPE, sleep, and soreness notes are on the screen before you walk up. The plan changes if your body says so — that is the job, not a perk.

  • 03

    Every coach says stop

    Form breaks and the set ends. No one at Forge will talk you into a rep that hurts your back, and you can tell any of them to leave it out.

  • 04

    Every coach keeps training

    All six hold a platform day of their own each week and compete or test at least twice a year. You are being coached by people who still do the thing.

“Ask me anything about the set you just did. If I do not know, I will find out before tomorrow’s board goes up.”
