Non-negotiables

The coaching standard at Forge

  1. Nobody trains unsupervised in class

    Coaches stay on the platform side of the room for the whole session. Phone use is for logging and timers, not scrolling.

  2. Every plan is written down

    Verbal programming gets misremembered. Your block exists as a document you can read, print and question.

  3. Pain stops the set

    Sharp pain ends the lift, full stop. Coaches regress the movement, log it, and refer out when it needs someone with a licence to diagnose.

  4. Coaches keep training

    Each of us maintains our own programme and tests in the same blocks as members. It is easier to explain a bad week when you had one last month.

Getting the right coach

How we pair you

Primary coach

On your trial week you meet two coaches. By the end of your first month we assign a primary — usually whoever leads your main track — who owns your plan, reads your log and signs off test day.

Swap any time

Not a fit? Say so at the desk and we move you that week, no awkwardness, no explanation required. Coaches expect it. It is far better than two years of training with someone whose style grates on you.

Review at six months

A thirty-minute sit-down with your primary coach: numbers, bodyweight, injuries, goals, and whether the track still suits your life. Most of these conversations change something real.
