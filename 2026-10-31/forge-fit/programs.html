PL-01

Powerlifting

12-week block
Squat · Bench · Deadlift

The iron protocol

Three competition lifts, taught in the order that keeps you injury-free: position, then load, then effort. Novices run a linear progression with three sub-maximal exposures a week. Once you hold a 1.5× bodyweight deadlift you graduate to wave loading at RPE 7–9, with a deload built into weeks four and eight, and a test at week twelve.

Mon · Wed · Fri 06:00 / 12:15 / 18:30 No lifting experience required

Session anatomy · 68 minRepeatable

  1. A

    Tissue and joint prep — 10 min

    Hips, ankles, thoracic spine. Fixed sequence so you can run it on the road without a coach.

  2. B

    Position work — 12 min

    Empty bar, tempo holds, and a paused rep at top of the main lift. Coach checks depth and brace.

  3. C

    Main lift — 25 min

    Top set at prescribed RPE, then back-off volume for reps. Rest periods are on the board: three minutes on heavy days.

  4. D

    Accessory pair — 15 min

    One horizontal pull, one single-leg or hinge variation. Alternated superset to keep the hour honest.

  5. E

    Log and leave — 6 min

    Totals into the sheet, one line on how it felt. Your coach reads it before the next session.

EN-02

Conditioning / HIIT

6-week cycle
Engine · Sled · Ergometers

Build the engine

Interval work designed for people who also lift heavy. Nothing that wrecks your squat next day: sled pushes, rowing, ski erg, and bike intervals with chest-strap heart-rate targets projected on the wall. You finish in under twelve minutes of true high intensity, and the coach scales the rest to your recovery score.

Tue · Thu · Sat 06:30 / 07:45 / 12:15 / 19:15 Straps provided

Session anatomy · 44 minHR-guided

  1. A

    Build-up — 8 min

    Nose-breathing row or ski to bring you to zone 2. Strap on, target zones on the board.

  2. B

    Primary intervals — 14 min

    Alternate weeks: 6 × 60 s at zone 5 with 2 min float, or 10 × 30 s sled efforts.

  3. C

    Strength carry-over — 12 min

    Kettlebell, sled, or core under load. Deliberately low-skill so fatigue does not make you dangerous.

  4. D

    Down-regulation — 10 min

    Walking, nasal breathing, and hip flexor release until heart rate drops below 110. Non-negotiable.

CH-03

Mobility & Chassis

Always open
Range · Tissue · Airtime

Keep the chassis sound

The twenty minutes almost everyone skips, taught properly. Loaded end-range work for ankles, hips, and thoracic rotation — the three things that decide whether your squat is deep or your lower back pays for it. Sunday adds a full recovery hour with sauna, plunge, and twelve-minute soft-tissue circuits.

Daily 06:00 · 25 min Sun 10:00 · 60 min Rehab-safe

Session anatomy · 25 minDaily

  1. A

    Breath and rib set — 4 min

    90/90 breathing with an overhead reach. Diaphragm first, everything else second.

  2. B

    Ankle and hip cascade — 9 min

    Knee-to-wall progressions, squat holds with a wedge, and crock sit rotations. Left and right are counted separately.

  3. C

    Loaded end range — 8 min

    Split squats, Jefferson curls at light load, and landmine rotations. Light on purpose; the range is the prize.

  4. D

    Transfer to lift — 4 min

    Two empty-bar squats to feel the range you just bought. Then you go to work.
