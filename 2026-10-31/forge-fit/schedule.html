How the week loads

Recovery-first
Mon / Wed / FriPLHeavy compound
Tue / Thu / SatENEngine intervals
Every morningCH25 min chassis
SundayRReset only

Lifting days and engine days never overlap on purpose. If you want both in one week — and most people should — you run PL on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, and EN on Tuesday and Thursday. Saturday is the optional third engine day; Sunday is not a training day, whatever the board offers.

03

Booking rules

Four lines
  • 01

    Hold a slot seven days out

    The board opens at 05:00 each day for the week ahead. Coached classes cap at ten on the platform and sixteen on the engine floor.

  • 02

    Release by two hours before

    No-show three sessions in a month and your booking privilege moves to sixty-minute notice. We are not precious about it — it stops someone else lifting.

  • 03

    Drop-ins cost twenty-five dollars

    Pay at the desk, bring a friend for the second session free. Drop-ins get the technique lab, not the peaking squad.

  • 04

    Open platform needs no booking

    Members with a Gate Pass train whenever the doors are unlocked. Coaching is on the floor Tuesday and Friday evenings.
