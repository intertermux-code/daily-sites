House rules

How holding a space works

  1. Book seven days out

    The grid unlocks each Thursday at 06:00 for the following week. Full Forge members get first pick for 24 hours before trial members can book.

  2. Cancel ninety minutes ahead

    Cancel inside the window and it counts as a miss. Three misses in a block and your booking window shrinks to three days — it is our way of protecting the class, not punishing you.

  3. Waitlist moves you up

    Every full class has a waitlist. When a space frees, the next name is texted automatically and has twenty minutes to claim it before it passes on.

  4. Arrive chalked and five early

    Platforms start on the minute. Late arrivals get cut into the warm-up, not the working sets, and the coach will fill you in while you load.
