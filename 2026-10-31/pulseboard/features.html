Panel 01 // live boards

A board that repaints while you watch it.

Tile any metric onto a shared console and it stays current without a refresh. The tail window, the breakdown, and the filter state live in the URL, so the link you paste is the view your teammate sees.

Streaming tail, not a nightly rebuild

Events land on the board the moment they clear validation. You can watch a launch unfold minute by minute instead of waiting for a batch job to confess what happened overnight.

  • Tail windows — 1 min to 90 days, switched inline with ⌘ ⇧ T
  • Pin & freeze — hold one tile while the rest of the board keeps streaming
400 ms p95 paint
96 tiles per board
∞ viewers, no seat cost on read-only

Layouts that read like a cockpit

Grid snapping, grouped rows, and a density switch keep a 90-tile board legible on a wall display and on a laptop. Named views let support, growth, and platform each open the same board at their own zoom level.

  • Density modes — comfortable, compact, and display for TV walls
  • Annotation lane — drop deploy marks and campaign windows straight on the axis

Share a link, not a permission meeting

Every board carries its own access policy. Internal viewers get full drill-down; external stakeholders get a scoped snapshot that expires on the date you set and never leaks row-level identifiers.

  • Scoped snapshots — expiring links with column-level redaction
  • Watermark audit — who opened which view, logged for 400 days

Panel 02 // modeling

Your metrics, versioned like source code.

The metric tree is the single place where “activated”, “churned”, and “revenue” get their meaning. Boards, funnels, and alarms all read from it, so a number has one owner and one history.

Compose instead of re-deriving

Build a metric from other metrics. Ratios, deltas, moving windows, cohort-relative comparisons, and currency normalisation are primitives — not a SQL snippet someone pasted into a spreadsheet in 2023.

  • Dependency graph — see every parent, child, and consumer before you edit
  • Guarded publish — dry-run a change against 90 days of history first
86 metrics median per workspace
90 d definition history
1 owner per metric

Changes leave a trail you can defend

Every publish records who changed what, why, and which boards moved as a result. When the number in the board deck disagrees with last quarter, the diff answers before anyone sends an email.

  • Impact preview — “this edit repaints 14 boards and 3 alarms” before you commit
  • One-click rollback — return a tree to any stored version

Warehouse-native when you want it

Run Pulseboard definitions as models in your own Snowflake, BigQuery, or Postgres and keep the compute where it already lives. The tree stays the interface; the numbers never leave your warehouse.

  • Push-down mode — compiled queries against your tables, no copying
  • Imported history — bring 18 months of legacy events forward on day one

Panel 03 // detection

Alarms that hand you the evidence.

A red number at 3 a.m. is not an insight. Pulseboard alarms learn the shape of each metric, break out the segments that caused the deviation, and name the change that landed in the same window.

Baselines that respect your calendar

Monday morning and Monday-night are different animals. Rules learn per-weekday and per-hour behaviour, seasonality around campaigns, and the noise floor of low-volume segments, so quiet metrics stop crying wolf.

  • Static bands — hard ceilings and floors for compliance and error budgets
  • Learned bands — recomputed nightly, explainer included on every breach
41 s detect → notify
62% less noise
73% closed same day

Correlation without a war room

Deploy marks, experiment transitions, campaign starts, and warehouse job states sit on the same axis as the metric. When a rule fires, Pulseboard lists the overlapping events and ranks them by segment fit.

  • Segment drill — the alert body already contains the worst three breakdowns
  • Ack & assign — claim an incident from Slack without opening the console

Escalation paths people can predict

Rotations, quiet hours, and severity ladders are configured once and enforced everywhere. Nothing pages a VP at midnight for a metric nobody owns, and nothing sits unacknowledged for six hours either.

  • Rotations — PagerDuty and native schedules with override windows
  • Post-incident export — timeline, metric states, and acknowledgements as Markdown

Panel 04 // governance

Security review passes the first time.

Analytics tools fail audits for the same reasons: vague access, silent exports, and data that quietly crossed a border. Pulseboard treats access as an instrument you read, not a settings page you forget.

Access you can enumerate out loud

Roles map to teams and groups rather than individual emails. Every board, metric, and export carries an explicit policy, and one screen answers “who can see revenue?” for your auditor and your legal team.

  • SSO & SCIM — Okta, Entra ID, Google Workspace, with enforced JIT
  • Column redaction — PII stripped at read time, not at ingest
SOC 2 Type II, annually
EU / US region pinning
400 d audit log retention

Privacy by default, provable on request

Identifiers are hashed at the edge, IP addresses never persist to storage, and deletion requests fan out to every replica inside the SLA. Subject-access exports come out of the console rather than a support ticket.

  • Retention dials — per-stream windows from 30 days to 5 years
  • Erasure receipts — signed confirmation once all replicas report back

A record that survives the team

Definitions, dashboards, alarm histories, and metric diffs stay readable after the person who built them leaves. New analysts get orientation from the change log instead of an oral history.

  • Workspace export — full tree, boards, and rules as portable JSON
  • Handover pack — generated on offboarding, with owners flagged for orphaned metrics

Auxiliary instruments

Six more controls on the same panel.

They share the metric tree, the keyboard map, and the access model — no separate tool to learn, no second login to lose.

Experiment Readout

Attach a running experiment to the board and watch its effect on your north-star metric with confidence bands, without exporting to a stats notebook.

⌘ E — attach variant

Retention Ledger

Cohorts anchored to arrival day, tracked forward with payback, feature adoption, and expansion all held on one grid.

⌘ L — open ledger

Segment Workbench

Build a reusable segment once — plan tier, region, behaviour window — and apply it everywhere, including inside alarms.

⌘ S — new segment

Query Notebook

For the day a tile is not enough: a compose-mode notebook that reads the same definitions and stays governed by the same policy.

⌘ J — open notebook

Pipeline Doctor

Schema drift, replay lag, and duplicate keys surface here before they corrupt a board. Includes one-click replay of a quarantined batch.

⌘ D — run doctor

Board Broadcast

Publish a board to a wall display or a daily digest. Tiles repaint on the display; digests land in email and Slack at 08:00 local.

⌘ ⇧ B — broadcast
