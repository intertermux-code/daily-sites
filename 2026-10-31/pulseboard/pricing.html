Clarifications // billing

Questions procurement always asks.

01 Do you charge per event or per seat?

Neither as a meter. Each plan states an event ceiling and a seat allowance up front. Exceeding the ceiling does not trigger an automatic invoice — our team calls you, we look at the trajectory together, and you either raise the plan or buy a reserve.

02 What counts as an event?

One accepted payload reaching storage, after validation and deduplication. Retried or quarantined rows are not billed. Identify calls, group updates, and property writes count as events too, because they consume the same pipeline.

03 Can read-only viewers really be unlimited?

Yes, on all three plans. Support desks, leadership, and partner teams get scoped, expiring links at no cost. Read-only accounts cannot export raw rows or edit the metric tree.

04 How does the 17% annual saving work?

Annual terms bill twelve months at the equivalent of roughly ten monthly payments, and the rate is locked for the full term even if list prices move mid-year. Add-ons follow the same cycle as the plan they attach to.

05 What happens if we downgrade or cancel?

Downgrades take effect at the next renewal, and boards survive with the tiles that exceed the new limit marked read-only rather than deleted. On cancellation you get a full workspace export — metric tree, boards, alarm rules, and 90 days of raw events as Parquet — within five business days.

06 Do you offer startup, academic, or nonprofit rates?

Operator at 50% for two years for teams under 25 people and less than $3 M raised, and free Mission workspaces for registered nonprofits and accredited research groups. Send the context on the contact page and we apply it before your first invoice.

07 Which payment methods and tax handling do you support?

Card and SEPA for monthly, bank transfer or purchase order for annual. We charge VAT, GST, or sales tax where the rules require it, reverse-charge EU businesses with a valid VAT ID, and issue invoices in USD, EUR, GBP, or CAD.
