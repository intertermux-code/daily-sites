Flight plan // what the call looks like

Thirty minutes, three parts, no deck.

0–8 min: your mess, on screen

You describe the number your team argues about. The engineer opens a sandbox workspace and starts building it, live, from a sample of events shaped like yours. If it cannot be modelled in eight minutes, we say so on the call.

8–22 min: alarm, history, and the diff

We attach a learned alarm to the metric, change its definition, and show you the impact preview and the version diff. This is the part most tools skip, and it is the reason teams move to Pulseboard.

22–30 min: terms, limits, and next step

You get the event ceiling that fits your volume, the residency answer for your region, and a written quote or sandbox URL before you leave the call. If Pulseboard is wrong for you, we say that too — three prospects a month hear it.

Clarifications // before you write

Short answers to the frequent questions.

01 Do I need engineering on the demo call?

No. Product and growth leads attend solo all the time. If you want a deeper look at the query engine or the warehouse connector, bring one engineer to the second half and we will split the session.

02 Can you sign our security questionnaire first?

Yes. Tick the briefing checkbox on the form and you will get our completed CAIQ-style answers, the SOC 2 report under NDA, and the sub-processor list the same day we reply.

03 What if our data cannot leave our region?

Pilot and Operator run in EU or US regions. Mission pins each workspace to a region and offers warehouse push-down so the numbers never leave your own tables. Australia, Japan, and India are available with the residency module.

04 Do you offer a sandbox without a call?

Yes. A 14-day Pilot workspace with the SDK keys and sample data is available on request through this form — mention “sandbox only” in the message and we will provision it without scheduling anything.

05 I already have a contract problem or outage.

Existing customers should mail crew@pulseboard.io with the workspace name, or use the escalation path in your onboarding pack. Severity 1 gets an incident bridge, not a form response.
