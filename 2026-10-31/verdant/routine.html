§ 03

How to Add a Step

Four rules that prevent almost every “this broke me out” email we receive.

Patch test, properlyBehind the ear, two nights, normal amount. If it stays quiet, the face is usually fine. Most reactions show by night two, not night ten.

Rule 01

One New Bottle a Fortnight

Two at once means you never learn which one caused it, or which one worked.

Rule 02

Water Before Oil

Serum on damp skin, cream on top, oil or balm last. Reversing it blocks the water step entirely.

Rule 03

Actives at Night

Bakuchiol and willow bark go in the evening. Zinc goes in the morning, every morning, including cloudy ones.

Rule 04

Judge at Thirty Nights

Not the next morning. Barrier change is measurable around night thirty; tone and texture around week eight.
