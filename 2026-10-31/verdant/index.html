§ 03  /  Ingredient No. 01

Centella, fermented slowly

Centella asiatica is not new. It has been used in South Asian medicine for four thousand years, and modern analysis finally caught up: the triterpenes madecassoside and asiaticoside measurably shorten the time a disrupted barrier takes to close.

We buy whole leaf from a third-generation co-op in Kerala and ferment it in Oregon for twenty-one days. Fermentation breaks the leaf sugars that make a standard extract sticky on skin, so the actives sit at four percent instead of the usual one.

From the lab book, 14 March“Batches 214 through 219 all passed madecassoside assay at 0.38%. Smell: green tea and hay. Refuse nothing.”
