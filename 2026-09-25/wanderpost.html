Why Wanderpost

Slower by Design, Not by Accident

Anyone can stack six cities into nine days. We do the opposite: one region, one season, and enough mornings to actually notice where you are.

  • One Place, Many Layers

    Weeks built around a single region — its food, crafts, weather and walking routes — so the place unfolds instead of flashing past.

  • Local Hands on Every Day

    Guides, cooks, skippers and weavers are paid properly and named in your itinerary. Your money stays in the valley you're walking through.

  • Lighter Footprints

    Trains and ferries wherever the map allows, small family stays over chains, and 1% of every journey funds trail and medina conservation.

  • A Human on Call

    One planner from your first call to your flight home, plus a 24-hour line in every destination. No call centres, no ticket numbers.
