Why Wanderpost

We plan less, so you experience more

Most itineraries are checklists with a logo. Ours are conversations — with a Kyoto tea grower, a Patagonian baqueano, a Marrakech zellige master, a Lofoten skipper — written into days that breathe.

  • Small by design

    Six travelers, one guide, one long table. Anything larger and a place stops talking to you.

  • Local hands

    Every stay, meal and guide is owned by residents. Roughly 78% of your fee stays in the region.

  • Lighter footprints

    Rail and ferry first where the map allows, and a published carbon ledger for every single journey.

  • Room to wander

    Two unscheduled afternoons in every week, on purpose. The best stories never appear on a schedule.
