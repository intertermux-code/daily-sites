About

The Woman Behind the Lens

Hello — I’m Camille. For twelve years I’ve photographed weddings the way I’d want my own remembered: honestly, warmly, and without a single forced smile.

I trained as a documentary photographer before I ever touched a wedding, and it shows. I work mostly in available light, mostly from the edge of the room, and mostly without you noticing. The portraits we do make together take twenty minutes, never more — then I hand you back to your people and your champagne.

My studio sits in a former print workshop in Florence, where every gallery is edited by hand and every album is bound by a bookbinder two streets away… because the day deserves better than a hard drive.
