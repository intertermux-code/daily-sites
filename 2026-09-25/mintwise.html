Good Questions

Everything You Want to Know

Still curious? Our support team replies within one business day — from real humans.

Is Mintwise really free to start?

Yes. The Sprout plan is free forever — no credit card, no trial countdown. You can track spending, run one goal vault, and use the savings calculator at zero cost. Upgrade only when you want automation like round-ups and adaptive budgets.

How does Mintwise connect to my bank?

We use read-only connections through trusted aggregation partners. Mintwise can see transactions to power your budgets, but it can never move money out of your accounts or view your login credentials. You can revoke access at any time from Settings.

What exactly is a savings rate?

It’s the share of your take-home pay you keep instead of spend. If you earn $4,000 and spend $3,000, your savings rate is 25%. Financial planners often suggest 20% as a healthy benchmark — the calculator above shows yours instantly.

Is my money FDIC-insured?

Funds in Mintwise goal vaults are held at our partner bank, Member FDIC, and insured up to $250,000 per depositor. Mintwise itself is a financial technology company, not a bank; checking accounts at your own bank remain covered by that institution.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Cancel in two taps inside the app — no phone calls, no retention mazes. Your plan stays active until the end of the billing period, and you can export all your data as CSV before you go. We keep it painless, because that’s how trust works.

Does Mintwise work outside the United States?

Mintwise currently supports US and Canadian bank connections, with the UK and EU launching later this year. Join the waitlist in the footer and we’ll email you the moment your region goes live.
