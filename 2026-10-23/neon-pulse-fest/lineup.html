Stage index

Five rooms, five contracts

Each stage publishes its own rules before the weekend. Read them once and the grid makes sense.

Pulse Main
Cap 4,200 · full hang, four delay tiers, headliners at 23:30 Fri, 23:00 Sat, 00:00 Sun.
CRT Warehouse
Cap 1,800 · 40-tube television wall, industrial programming, no requests policy on long sets.
Magenta Dome
Cap 1,100 · inside-projection 360°, vinyl-only bookings, seated edge for the first hour.
Cyan Basement
Cap 600 · phones discouraged, unannounced substitutions, sets of 3–4 hours.
Rooftop Sunrise
Cap 900 · open air, heated from 05:00, runs until 09:00 Sunday, blankets lent free.

Timetable

Night by night, hour by hour

Open one night at a time. Times are Pacific and locked; substitutions are posted here first.

Friday is deliberately slow. The hall fills between 18:00 and 21:00 while Ambient Dock runs the freight room, and the first peak arrives at 23:30 with Phosphor Choir.

  • 18:00 Doors · freight hall + rooftop open · Ambient Dock, Harbour Static
  • 19:30 Pulse Main · Lasermonk
  • 21:00 Pulse Main · Vocoder Ghosts
  • 21:30 Magenta Dome · Mirror Tax
  • 23:30 Pulse Main headline · Phosphor Choir
  • 00:00 Magenta Dome · Italo Sector
  • 01:00 Cyan Basement · Sub Limiter (until 04:00)
  • 04:00 Last call, Friday · shuttles every 20 min

The busiest night of the weekend. All five stages overlap from 21:00 to 00:00 — capacity is enforced at each door, so pick a room early rather than queueing at ten to eleven.

  • 16:00 Doors · Cloakroom opens 15:30
  • 18:30 Magenta Dome · Amber Relay
  • 19:00 CRT Warehouse · Gasket
  • 20:00 Pulse Main · Static Saint
  • 21:30 Magenta Dome · Sister Amber (vinyl only)
  • 22:00 CRT Warehouse · Null Pointer
  • 23:00 Pulse Main headline · Kilowatt Kid
  • 01:00 CRT Warehouse · Hex/Dec (3 hrs) · Cyan Basement B2B · Tape Hiss Council
  • 02:00 Rooftop · CRT Queen dub pass (until 05:00)

Sunday has two halves. The morning belongs to the rooftop and finishes at 09:00; the evening restarts at 18:00 and runs to 03:30, with the site cleared by 17:00 and the cloakroom open until 19:00.

  • 04:00 Rooftop · Laptop Sunrise
  • 06:30 Rooftop · Bass Cathedral (organ + sub, until 09:00)
  • 09:00 Rooftop closes · hall reset for the evening
  • 18:00 Evening doors · all stages reopen
  • 20:00 Pulse Main · Dub Oracle
  • 21:00 CRT Warehouse · Axiom Grave
  • 22:00 Magenta Dome headline · Neon Widow
  • 00:00 Pulse Main closing · Voltage Saint
  • 01:30 Cyan Basement · Fault Line (until 03:30)
  • 17:00 Sound sweep · site clear by 19:00

Two announcement waves remain, both at 07:00 Pacific, both published here before anything else.

  • 4 November — 22 names, mostly Basement and Dome residents.
  • 2 December — 16 names plus the final two Warehouse slots.
  • Substitutions — if an act withdraws, the replacement is posted within 12 hours and pass holders get a refund option on that night only.

Booking is handled by three people and a shared spreadsheet. If you want to play a slot, use the booking form on the info page rather than the announcement list.

Pick your night

Saturday sells first. Every year.

Single-night passes exist, but the three-day tier is the one that lets you stay for the 04:00 set and sleep on the rooftop at 07:00.
