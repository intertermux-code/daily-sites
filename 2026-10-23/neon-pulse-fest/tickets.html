At the door

Your pass is a wristband, not a screenshot

Scan once at the gate, and the band is registered to your order for the whole weekend. Photographs of a barcode will not get you in, and neither will a screenshot of somebody else’s band — the scanner checks the name against the ID you show.

Wristband collection opens 30 minutes before every gate time, and replacement takes about four minutes at the info desk with photo ID. Read the entry, search and re-entry rules on the venue page before you plan your Friday.

Paid add-ons are limited to two things: the balcony deck and a locker. Everything else on site is included or pay-at-the-bar.

  • Locker, Dock Street — $18 per weekend, 40 × 30 × 60 cm, bookable until 10 December.
  • Balcony upgrade — $260 on top of any 3-day pass, 19 remaining, first come.
  • Cloakroom — free, unlimited items, queue-jump on 3-day tiers.
  • Water — free at twelve fountains; bring a bottle or take a $2 reusable one.
  • Earplugs — free at every stage door, the good foam kind, not the orange ones.
  • Shuttle — free with any pass, loops Downtown / Little Tokyo / Highland Park.

Passes are transferable for free until 12 December, refundable until 1 December with no reason given, and refundable right up to the gates if the weekend goes wrong in a way that is our fault.

  • Until 1 Dec — full refund including order fees, one click in the portal.
  • 1–17 Dec — free name transfer; no refunds, because the site is already built.
  • Weather or system failure — if a stage loses power for more than 45 minutes, that night is refunded pro rata automatically.
  • Illness or bereavement — email the desk and we will refund after 1 December too. No documents required.

Buying

The queue, in four lines

step 01/queue/join

Doors to the shop

The store opens at 07:00 PT on release days. There is no pre-queue the night before — the counter starts at zero and refresh tokens are issued fairly.

step 02/cart/limit

Four per person

Card, address and device must match across the order. Bots and scalpers get the same limit as everyone else, and the limit is the reason resale is cheap.

step 03/pay/plan

Split it in three

Choose the plan at checkout: 3 × $99 on the 3-day tier, charged monthly, cancel any time before the last installment with the pass held until then.

step 04/ticket/lock

Name-locked barcode

Your pass carries your legal name. Transfer inside the portal and the barcode re-issues to the new name — scanning at the door checks ID against it.
