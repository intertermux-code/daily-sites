Dock Street · Terminal Arts District

Venue Facts & Answers

The freight terminal at Dock Street holds 9,600 people across five rooms. Here is how to reach it, how to move around inside it, and what happens if something goes wrong.

The building

One terminal, five rooms, no grass

Everything is indoors except the rooftop deck, which is covered and heated from 05:00. Concrete floor, steel grid, and a ceiling high enough that the main room never feels like a club.

Getting there

Come by rail. Seriously.

There is no parking at the terminal and the three nearest lots are residential. The shuttle loop and the metro line carry the whole site if you use them.

Rail + walkrecommended

L · Little Tokyo / P · 6th & Grand

Six-minute covered walk from the P line to the Dock Street gate. Trains run every 10 minutes until 01:00, then every 20 until 02:40 — after that, take the shuttle.

TAP works at all gates

Free shuttle loop3 stops

Downtown · Little Tokyo · Highland Park

Two buses on a 22-minute loop, every 20 minutes from 15:00 to 07:15, every 10 minutes from 23:00 to 03:00. Show any pass, no reservation, accessible ramp on both vehicles.

Queues are marked on the pavement

Car + rideshareavoid if you can

Drop-off at Alameda, not Dock

Rideshare pickups use the Alameda Street corner only — Dock Street is a bus-only kerb from 18:00. If you must drive, the signed park-and-ride at Rio Hondo is $14 and connects to the loop.

No parking on Pier 9 · towing enforced

Access

Everything reachable without stairs

The terminal was a freight building first, so level routes exist — we kept them. Access requirements are handled by email within two working days, not at the gate.

$ mail access@neonpulse.fm

  • 01Companion tickets are freeEvery access holder gets one carer pass at no cost on any tier. Mention it in the email and it is added to the order.
  • 02Step-free routes to all five stagesRamps at both entrances, two elevators to the rooftop deck, and no temporary cabling across walkways — everything is flown or trunked to the sides.
  • 03Viewing platformsHeight-adjustable platforms at Pulse Main and CRT Warehouse, booked per set rather than per night, with space for a wheelchair and two standing companions.
  • 04Quiet roomOpen all night beside the Warehouse, lit low, no sound above 45 dB, staffed by two people trained in de-escalation rather than by security sent off-shift.
  • 05Hearing protection and interpretationFree foam and filtered plugs at every door. ASL interpretation for the Friday headline and the Sunday closing set on request, booked by 8 December.
  • 06Accessible toilets and changing placesFour banks plus one changing-places facility with hoist and bench, nearest to the Dock Street entrance, queue marked with a time estimate.
  • 07Assistance dogs welcomeWater bowls at both info desks and a designated relief area by the north alley. Hearing dogs are treated identically.
  • 08Sunflower lanyard schemePick one at any gate — it marks invisible needs to the whole crew, including which staff have been briefed on it that night.

Safety

Harm reduction, not moral panic

Two medical stations, a drug-checking service run by an independent health charity, and a policy that nobody gets ejected for asking for help.

Medic & Welfare

24/72 stations

Naloxone on every barrier, two paramedics permanently on site, and a triage route that does not cross the dance floor.

Drug Checking

Fri–SunIndependent

Run by a health charity, not the police. Results are verbal and anonymous, and nothing you bring gets confiscated.

Consent & Reporting

3 desksBriefed crews

Harassment reports go to a welfare lead, not a bouncer with a radio. Ejection is available for patrons who make the floor unsafe.

Sound & Hearing

100 dB capLeqs metered

Every stage logs its own average exposure. Posters show the measured number the morning after, including the ones we would rather not publish.

Bring this, not that

  • Small bag up to 25 × 35 cm (fanny pack is ideal)

  • Camera with an interchangeable lens under 6″ — press lanes excepted

Gates throw at 18:00 and the search lanes are empty until 19:15. Arrive in that window, get your wristband on, and you can leave and re-enter without queueing during the 23:30 headline crush.

Two free cloakrooms: Dock Street north and the Basement stair. Unlimited items, no size limit short of luggage, open until 30 minutes after the last stage closes. Three-day passes skip the queue.

Sealed and empty is fine; filled is not, because it is a liquid at the search. Twelve fountains give cold filtered water on every level, including the rooftop, and bottle refill points sit within 40 metres of every stage door.

We push the change to the site screens within three minutes and, for 3-day and Balcony tiers, twenty minutes ahead of the slot as an alert. Substitutions are never announced in advance of the room being told first.

Fully, including fees, until 1 December, no reason required. After that, transfers stay free and refunds apply only for illness, bereavement or our own failure — email the desk and a human answers.

No. Neon Pulse is 18+ across the whole site, including the rooftop, because the licence covers a licensed warehouse and a curfew that runs past school nights. Bring ID even if you look forty.

Pulse Main averages 98 dB(A) at the booth with a 104 dB peak ceiling; the Basement runs hotter because it is smaller. Free filtered plugs sit at every door, and the exposure log is posted on the day-one bulletin board each morning.

Personal photos and short clips are welcome everywhere except the Cyan Basement, which is a phones-off room, and the four acts who asked for no strobe-lit filming — they are marked on the timetable with a small camera slash.

Four kitchens plus two late-night slots, all with clearly separated vegan and gluten-free prep surfaces. The Dome yard runs halal and a nut-free fryer. Prices are capped at $11 for a main, and water is never for sale.

Wristbands are registered to your order, so a replacement takes about four minutes at the info desk against photo ID. If you lose one inside the site, use the internal lane at any stage door — you do not need to leave and queue again.
