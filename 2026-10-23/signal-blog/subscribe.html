Before You Sign Up

Straight answers, including the inconvenient ones.

Is the dispatch really free?

Yes, and it is not a teaser for a paid product. There is no paywall at Signal. Reader subscriptions and one-off contributions cover 85% of our operating budget, which is why the free tier is not a marketing funnel — it is the thing itself.

What happens to my email address?

It lives in one mailing system operated by a European provider, used to send you the dispatch and nothing else. We do not run tracking pixels and we do not share lists with anyone, including conferences. Write to privacy@signal.press and the address is removed the same day, with a line in the deletion log.

Why ask which desks I want?

Because a dispatch that tries to summarise six desks becomes a list of headlines. We would rather send two paragraphs about the Standard by Committee reconstruction than eight bullet points nobody reads. You can change the mix in one click in any issue.

Do you accept press trips, review units or event invitations?

No to all three. We buy the hardware we test at retail and publish the receipts. If a vendor funds a reporting trip we decline it in writing and note the decline in the story. Two standing-order exceptions exist — a conference talk we were paid to give, disclosed inline — and both are listed in our conflict register.

What if you get something wrong?

We print the fix, keep the wrong sentence visible, and date the note. Eighty-eight corrections and four retractions since 2017, all in the archive. The corrections ledger is a separate monthly email; it is the most-read thing we send after the long read.

Can I reply to it?

Yes. Every dispatch is answered by the desk editor whose name is at the top of it, and tips sent that way are treated under the same source-protection rules as the secure email line.
