    The Maintainers Holding Up the Modern Internet

    Open Source Dana Whitfield 18 min

    We traced eleven of the most depended-upon packages in production software back to the individuals who review their pull requests. Nine of those people fund the work themselves. Two of them are retired. One answers to a foundation board that meets quarterly and has never once looked at a dependency graph.

    Why it matters · The upstream failure risk in your stack is almost never the code. It is the calendar of one unpaid person.

    Access & Policy Rebecca Mendes 11 min

    Why it matters · Universal broadband was built by people who could knock on the door of the person not paying.
