Questions That Arrive Most Often

Can I use Dewpoint Serum with a prescription retinoid?

Yes — niacinamide and alpha-arbutin both play nicely with tretinoin and adapalene. Apply the serum to dry skin, wait a minute, then your retinoid, then Calica Cream. If you are in the first fortnight of a new prescription, hold the serum back until your skin has settled.

Why is there no scent at all? A little fragrance would feel nicer.

Because the 26 EU-declared fragrance allergens are the single largest cause of contact allergy in the products people bring to dermatologists, and none of them do anything for your skin. Fermented green tea has a faint savoury note for the first few seconds after application. It disappears.

How long does one bottle last if I use it properly?

Cleanser roughly three months, Dewpoint and Nightbloom about eight weeks at two pumps per use, Calica Cream eight to ten weeks, Fernwater Mist three months with daily spraying. The supply length is printed on every carton so you can reorder on schedule rather than guessing.

I am pregnant. What should I avoid?

Everything in the range is formulated without retinoids, salicylic acid at leave-on levels, or essential oils. Bakuchiol has less pregnancy data than niacinamide, so if you would rather not take the chance, use Cleanser, Serum and Cream and pause Nightbloom until after. Ask your obstetrician, not us.

Does the Calica Cream work under makeup?

It sets matte in about ninety seconds, which is why the 4pm crowd uses it. If you prefer a dewier base, apply Fernwater Mist first and let the cream sit for two minutes before foundation.

Where are the products made and tested?

Formulated and filled in Porto by our own nine-person team, batch-tested by an ISO 17025 accredited laboratory in Coimbra, and stability-tested in-house at 40°C for three months. No contract factory, no white-label shortcuts.
