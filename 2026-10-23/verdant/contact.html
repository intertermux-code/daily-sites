Refills

Pouches And Bottle-Back

Refill pouches cost 15% less than the bottle and use 40% less plastic. Nine empties returned to our Utrecht depot earns a new bottle of your choice, posted free, twice a year.

Returns

Sixty Days, Opened

If a formula does not suit you, send it back within sixty days even if it is half used. We refund the full amount and cover the label — no reason required, though we always ask what went wrong.

Patch Test

Four Nights Before You Commit

Apply a rice-grain amount behind the ear on four consecutive nights. No sting, no spreading redness by night four means the face is fair game. Actives — the serum, essence and balm — deserve this every time.
