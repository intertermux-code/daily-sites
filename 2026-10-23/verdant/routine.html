    Why these, and how to use them

      This quiz reflects our own formulation logic and panel data. It is not a diagnosis. If you are on a prescription for acne, rosacea, or eczema, send the answers to our formulators and we will tell you what to keep and what to run past your doctor.

      How the Matching Works

      Nothing here is an algorithm guessing from your selfie. It is four rules we wrote by hand.

      Rule one

      Barrier Before Actives

      If your skin reads as tight or reactive, we send Calica Cream first and hold the treatment serum back. Actives on a broken barrier feel like failure and get abandoned.

      Rule two

      One Job Per Step

      You will never be matched two products that do the same thing. No toner under a mist, no second moisturiser, nothing that just makes the shelf look busy.

      Rule three

      Cap by Your Tuesday

      Two minutes caps at two products, four minutes at three, eight minutes at four. A routine you skip is worth nothing, however correct it looks on paper.

      Rule four

      Never Nightbloom and Acids

      We do not recommend bakuchiol on the same night as an exfoliating acid, and we will say so even though we sell neither acid. Layering both is the fastest route to a red week.
