Record  /  Field notes, 2019 to now

Everything We Know, Written Down

Verdant began as a notebook kept on a windowsill in the Dordogne — cuttings, steeping times, and which skin reacted to which. That notebook became the only way we know how to run a skincare line: one entry per ingredient, one dose per entry, one panel per season.

6 core ingredients 12 finished formulas 40-person panel 7 growers named below

Section 02  /  Origin

A Windowsill, Then A Laboratory

In 2019 our founder Ilse Rovin was formulating for other brands and keeping a private record of what she was not allowed to put on a label: the calms, the repairs, the ones that took four months to show anything. She left with a backpack of dried samples and a rented bench in Bordeaux.

The first twelve months produced three usable formulas and eleven failures. Each failure kept its page. That archive is why we still ship only twelve products — a formula earns its place by beating the page before it, not by joining a seasonal campaign.

Today the bench is in London and the field work is in the Dordogne, with a chemist, two technicians, and the same notebook rule: if we cannot write the dose, we do not add the ingredient.

Section 06  /  Timeline

Seven Years Of Entries

2019

Rented bench, first notebook

Three usable formulas from fourteen attempts. Sage Renewal Serum is attempt nine.

2020

First forty-person panel

We commit to 12-week readings and publish the failures in the Ledger Letter.

2021

Sea fennel moves to culture

Wild collection stops entirely. Cost rises 31%; every lot becomes identical.

2022

Doses printed on the bottle

Regulators asked why; customers asked us not to stop. Retailers now copy it.

2023

Refill pouch launched

40% less plastic per ml. One third of cream orders now arrive as refills.

2024

Bakuchiol balm, after 3 years

Fourteen base revisions to stop the tacky finish that made earlier versions unusable.

2026

Twelve formulas, still

Two candidates sit in testing. Neither has beaten the page it would replace.
