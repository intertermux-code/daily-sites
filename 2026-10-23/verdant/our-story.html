We Grow the Actives
Before We Bottle Them

Verdant started on a pharmacy bench in Porto with one stubborn question: why does a 4% niacinamide serum sting some faces and do nothing for others? Fermentation turned out to be the answer, and it reshaped the whole range.

01

A Bench, Two Pharmacists, One Refusal

Inês Ferreira and Rui Salgado spent nine years dispensing prescriptions beside a shelf of ninety-nine skincare products. Almost every return had the same shape: someone bought a strong actives serum, burned their barrier, and came back blaming their own face.

They refused the usual explanation that sensitive people simply cannot use actives. The published studies show benefit for reactive skin too — the failure was in delivery, not dose. So they quit the counter, rented bench space in a friend's analytical lab, and started fermenting.

02

Fermentation Does the Work a Harsher Dose Would

Botanicals ferment with the help of benign lactic cultures. Those cultures cleave large polyphenols into smaller molecules your skin can actually walk through, and they release extra sugars and amino acids as a by-product. The result is a green-tea concentrate that hydrates while it delivers actives.

We ferment our tea, oat and centella bases for between seventy-two and ninety-six hours at 34°C, then test every batch for pH, molecular weight distribution and microbial load before it goes anywhere near a filling line.

Because more of each active reaches living tissue, we can print a lower number on the box and still get the reported outcome. Dewpoint Serum carries 4% niacinamide rather than the 10% that became fashionable, and the stinging complaints fell to zero across our last two panels.

“A higher percentage is not a stronger product. It is a louder label with more ways to go wrong.”
Inês Ferreira, co-founder and head formulator

03

Four Growers, Two Regions, Named on the Carton

We buy from four suppliers and we could name them on every pack because there is nothing to hide. Centella grows on two humid terraces north of Porto that the Costa family has farmed the same way since 1978. Colloidal oat comes from a biodynamic co-operative in the Alentejo, milled within nine days of harvest so the lipids stay intact.

Green tea leaf arrives from a single estate in Uji, Japan, shaded for twenty days before picking — the shading is what raises theanine, and theanine is what makes our ferment feel calm rather than tight.

Anything synthetic is bought from two EU chemical suppliers under audited REACH documentation, and every incoming drum is tested by an outside lab for heavy metals before use. Nothing goes into a formula on a certificate alone.

04

Twelve Weeks, 120 Faces, and a Formula We Deleted

Nothing ships until it survives a twelve-week consumer panel on the skin type it was designed for — including people with rosacea, people on tretinoin prescriptions, and people who lied on every previous questionnaire about how often they actually wash their face.

We measure transepidermal water loss at baseline, week four and week eight, photograph under cross-polarised light, and log every complaint the same day. A formula that raises stinging reports above 8% goes back to the bench. In 2025 that killed a vitamin C serum we had already spent fourteen months developing, because L-ascorbic acid at a low pH kept hurting the exact people we wanted to reach.

What replaced it is Dewpoint Serum: niacinamide and alpha-arbutin, no acid load, no oxidation problem, no orange stain on the bathroom towel.

  • Patch tested on 120 volunteers with self-identified reactive skin
  • Independent challenge testing to ISO 11930 for preservative efficacy
  • Stability at 40°C for three months, so a summer parcel is not a lottery
  • Every complaint published in our quarterly formulation note

Six Ingredients We Will Not Trade Away

Each earns its place with a study, not a story. The rest of the list is short on purpose.

Fermented base

Green Tea Ferment

Shade-grown Uji leaf, fermented 72 hours. Delivers catechins at a smaller molecular weight and adds measurable hydration in the same step.

Calming

Centella Asiatica

Standardised for madecassoside. Six-week data shows visibly reduced redness around the nose and cheeks without thinning the stratum corneum.

Soothing

Colloidal Oat

Milled within nine days of harvest. The only botanical with an FDA-monograph history for itch and dryness — we use it in the cleanser so it survives rinsing.

Barrier lipids

Ceramide 1, 3, 6:7

Blended at the 3:1:1 molar ratio found in healthy skin. Missing one of the three is why so many "ceramide creams" underperform.

Tone

Niacinamide 4%

Enough to hit the sebum and pigment endpoints reported in trials, low enough that nobody in our last panel reported flushing.

Renewal

Bakuchiol 1%

A plant-derived alternative with twelve-week trial data overlapping 0.5% retinol — and without the peeling fortnight that costs people their confidence.
