Why we cut the shelf down to six

Most Routines Fail From Volume, Not Potency

The average bathroom drawer holds eleven products and roughly 340 unpronounceable ingredients. Every one is another chance for irritation — and irritation is what turns redness, peeling and breakouts into permanent features.

We formulated backwards from barrier biology: ferment the actives so they absorb at a lower dose, buffer each one with centella and colloidal oat, then ship nothing that has not earned its place over twelve weeks of consumer testing.

  • Fragrance-free, essential-oil-free, dye-free
  • Every dose printed on the carton, not hidden in marketing
  • Patch-tested on 120 volunteers with rosacea-prone skin
  • Refill pouches use 74% less plastic than a second bottle
