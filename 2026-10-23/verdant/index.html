Section 03  /  Method

Fewer bottles, longer records

We are not adding a thirteenth formula this year, or the next. A line that stays at twelve is a line we can actually measure — each batch goes back to the same panel of forty volunteers and the same barrier reading at 72 hours.

What we take out is a long list: essential oils, drying alcohol, denatured fragrance, walnut shell, and every ingredient whose purpose is to make the product feel more expensive than it is.

  • Doses printed on the bottle, not buried in a patent
  • Batch-level ingredient sourcing, traceable by lot number
  • Refill pouches at 40% less plastic, posted to your door
  • Panel testing on 40 people, not 400 surveys
