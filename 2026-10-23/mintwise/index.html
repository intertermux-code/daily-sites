The four moves

Four quiet moves,
then it runs itself.

Set up takes one evening. After that, Mintwise works in the background and only speaks up when something needs your attention.

  1. 01Connect
  2. 02Categorise
  3. 03Automate
  4. 04Grow
01

Connect in two taps

Link 12,400 banks, brokers, card issuers and payroll providers through Plaid and Stripe Treasury. Mintwise reads balances and transactions with scoped, revocable tokens — your bank password never reaches our servers, and disconnecting takes one press.

  • Accounts sync every 15 minutes, day and night
  • CSV and OFX import for the holdouts
  • Shared household accounts stay visible to both partners
02

Categorise itself around your life

Our model has studied 1.9 billion transactions, so “Trader Joe’s” is groceries before you open the app and “Blue Bottle” is coffee. Correct one misfire and the rule is written into your ledger permanently — the app gets quieter the longer you use it.

  • 46 categories, 12 custom slots, unlimited rules
  • Subscriptions detected automatically, renewal dates included
  • Splits one transaction across several budget lines
03

Automate before temptation

Tell Mintwise the number you want to keep and it divides every paycheck across savings, debt and investing on the morning it clears — while the balance is still zero and nothing is yet spent. Miss a target and the shortfall is quietly made up next cycle, never in one painful gulp.

  • Payday, weekly or custom cadence, with a spending buffer you choose
  • Round-ups sweep the cents into the goal you pick
  • Bills are ring-fenced first, so no automation overdrafts you
04

Grow where the rate is best

Mintwise watches 94 high-yield accounts and moves idle cash to the strongest safe rate it finds, then tells you why. Goal portfolios chart the glide path to a deposit, a wedding or a retirement date — and warn you six months early when the plan slips.

  • Cash ladder suggestions in 1, 3 and 6-month buckets
  • Rate alerts never more than one per week
  • Projection errors flagged the month they appear

Why it sticks

Built for the budget you actually have.

Most finance apps assume a steady salary and a spouse who never surprises you. Mintwise assumes the opposite: variable hours, an annual insurance bill nobody mentioned, a friend’s wedding in Lisbon. It plans around the irregular costs instead of pretending they are not there.

  • Sinking funds that fill themselves a little each week
  • Irregular bill calendar smoothing 31 annual charges into monthly slices
  • Partner view with private lines and shared goals, no awkward arithmetic
  • Downside mode that rewrites the plan the week your income drops
