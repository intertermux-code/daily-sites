Help centre

The ten answers we give most

Can Mintwise move money out of my bank account?

No. Connection tokens are read-only: they permit balances and transactions to be fetched, nothing else. Transfers only ever run to a destination you verified yourself with a micro-deposit, and only under an automation rule you created. Revoking access from your bank ends it immediately.

My bank will not sync. What should I try first?

Reconnect the account rather than refreshing it — nine in ten stuck syncs clear there. If it still fails, your bank is likely in a maintenance window (we publish a live institution status in Settings → Connections). Worst case, export CSV from your bank and drop it into the app; the ledger fills in while we chase the provider.

How often does data refresh, and does it drain my battery?

Connections sync every 15 minutes on the server, not on your phone. The app fetches what changed when you open it, so background battery use is effectively zero. Canopy members can set a shorter interval or force a sync manually.

An automation overspent my buffer. How is that handled?

Autoflow checks projected balance before it moves anything and stops rather than drawing below your buffer. In the rare event a bank posts transactions out of order and a fee appears, we cover the overdraft charge — send the statement line to support and it is refunded, no argument.

Is my transaction history sold or used to train anything?

We never sell data and we have no advertising business. Categorisation models train on de-identified merchant patterns aggregated across millions of ledgers, not on your named transactions, and you can opt out of contributing in Settings → Data.

How do I delete everything for good?

Settings → Account → Delete, one press, no phone call and no retention maze. Ledgers go within 30 days and you receive a signed deletion certificate. Backups roll out of existence over the following 35 days, which is the industry-standard window we audit against.

Does it work for couples who keep some money separate?

Yes, that is the usual arrangement. A Grove plan covers two people: shared accounts, shared goals and joint budget lines are visible to both, while any account can be marked private — the other person sees that it exists in the total, never what is in it.

What happens to my automation if I pause the plan?

Flows pause and every rule is kept exactly as you set it, for two years. Resuming restarts the schedule on the next payday — nothing is silently back-filled while you were away.

Is there an API, and can I pipe data into my own spreadsheet?

Canopy includes a read API with token-scoped access for categories, balances and dated transactions. Google Sheets and Excel add-ins are published and a webhook fires after each completed autoflow.

Do you support accessibility tools properly?

VoiceOver and TalkBack labels are maintained on every screen, dynamic type up to 34 points is tested, colour never carries meaning alone, and there is a full reduce-motion mode. Found a gap? Tell us — accessibility reports are triaged weekly.
