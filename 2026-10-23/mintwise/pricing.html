Before you choose

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Read access continues until the end of the paid period, then the account locks. You can export the full ledger at any time, including after cancellation. Delete everything with one press and we confirm in writing within 30 days, including copies held by sub-processors.

Is the 90-day refund offer real?

Yes. If your savings rate has not improved within 90 days of your first autoflow, email support and we return the full subscription price, even on an annual plan. Nine hundred and four members used it last year; the average approved refund took 11 minutes to process.

Do you take commissions from the banks you recommend?

No. Mintwise earns nothing from partner institutions for placing your cash. We publish the full rate table we draw from, refreshed nightly, and the recommendation is simply the highest yield that meets your liquidity requirement.

Can I switch plans mid-cycle?

Upgrades apply instantly and we charge the unused portion pro rata. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal so nothing you automated stops working halfway through a month.

What does the free Sprout plan leave out?

Everything is there except scale: four accounts, one automation, a 30-day forecast and no sinking funds. Many members stay on Sprout on purpose — it is a genuine product, not a demo.
