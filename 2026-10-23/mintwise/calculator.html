    Once you know the number

    Three things to do with the rate you just found.

    1. Under 10% — raise the ring-fenced bill fund first. Leaks usually come from annual charges being paid from a monthly balance, not from coffee.
    2. 10% to 20% — set one payday autoflow rule at the number this calculator shows you keep, and stop watching it. That single change accounts for most of the 38% median lift.
    3. Above 20% — the work shifts from saving to placement: cash ladder for anything you need inside two years, goal portfolios for the rest, and a rate check each quarter.
