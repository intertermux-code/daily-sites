Join Our Team

Volunteers are the heart of our organization. Discover meaningful ways to contribute to our mission of fighting hunger in our community.

Volunteer Opportunities

Food Distribution Assistant

Help sort, pack, and distribute food items to families in need. Tasks include organizing donations, preparing food boxes, and assisting clients during distribution hours. Great opportunity for individuals or groups.

Time commitment: Flexible shifts available during weekday afternoons and Saturday mornings.

Mobile Food Pantry Driver

Transport food donations to various locations throughout our service area. Drivers must have a valid license and clean driving record. Training provided.

Time commitment: Typically 2-3 routes per week on scheduled days.

Administrative Support

Assist with office tasks including data entry, answering phones, filing, and helping with correspondence. Perfect for those who want to support our mission behind the scenes.

Time commitment: Flexible hours during business days.

Community Outreach Ambassador

Represent Bright Harvest at community events, farmers markets, and local gatherings. Share information about our programs and recruit new volunteers and donors.

Time commitment: Event-based, typically 2-4 hours per event.

Garden Maintenance Volunteer

Help maintain our community gardens by planting, weeding, watering, and harvesting produce. No experience necessary—we'll teach you everything you need to know!

Time commitment: Seasonal work, flexible scheduling.

Why Volunteer With Us?

  • Meaningful Impact: Directly help families in your community access nutritious food
  • Skill Development: Gain valuable experience in nonprofit operations, teamwork, and leadership
  • Community Connection: Meet like-minded people who care about social issues
  • Flexible Scheduling: Choose volunteer times that work with your schedule
  • Training Provided: Comprehensive orientation and ongoing support
  • Recognition: We celebrate our volunteers' contributions regularly
