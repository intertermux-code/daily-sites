Your Impact

Did you know? A donation of just $25 provides 50 meals to families in need. Your generosity helps us:

  • Distribute nutritious meals to families facing food insecurity
  • Support our educational nutrition programs
  • Maintain our emergency food assistance services
  • Build sustainable community gardens

Other Ways to Give

In-Kind Donations

We accept donations of non-perishable food items, hygiene products, and household essentials. Visit our main location during operating hours to drop off donations.

Corporate Sponsorship

Partner with us to make a larger impact in our community. Corporate sponsors help fund our major programs and special initiatives.

Planned Giving

Consider including Bright Harvest in your estate planning. Legacy gifts help ensure our mission continues for future generations.
