Our Story

Learn how Bright Harvest began its mission to fight hunger and strengthen our community.

From Humble Beginnings

In 2010, a group of community members noticed a growing need for food assistance in our area. What started as a small initiative to share surplus produce from local gardens has grown into a comprehensive food security organization serving thousands of families.

Bright Harvest was founded on the belief that access to nutritious food is a basic human right. Today, we operate multiple distribution centers, run educational programs, and coordinate with over 40 partner organizations to ensure no one in our community goes hungry.

Our Core Values
