Our Story

Rooted in nature, guided by science.

From Garden to Glass

In 2018, founder Maya Chen began experimenting with botanical formulations in her kitchen, driven by a personal quest for clean, effective skincare solutions. Frustrated by the abundance of synthetic ingredients in mainstream products, she sought to create something different—skincare that honored both the power of nature and the precision of science.

What started as a personal project quickly evolved into Verdant, a brand committed to minimalist formulations that work in harmony with your skin’s natural processes. Our philosophy centers on the belief that less is more: fewer ingredients, cleaner formulations, and better results.

Today, we source our ingredients from sustainable farms around the world, partnering with growers who share our commitment to environmental stewardship. Each ingredient is carefully selected for its proven efficacy and gentle nature.

Our Key Ingredients

Rosehip Oil

Vitamin C-rich oil that promotes cell regeneration and helps diminish the appearance of fine lines and hyperpigmentation.

Hyaluronic Acid

Plant-derived hyaluronic acid that attracts and retains moisture, keeping skin plump and hydrated throughout the day.

Niacinamide

Vitamin B3 derivative that helps regulate sebum production, minimize pores, and strengthen the skin barrier.

Squalane

Plant-based squalane that mimics the skin's natural oils, providing deep hydration without clogging pores.

Our Values

Clean Formulations

We avoid parabens, sulfates, synthetic fragrances, and other potentially harmful ingredients. Every product is formulated with transparency and safety in mind.

Sustainable Packaging

Our containers are made from recycled materials and designed for refillability. We're committed to reducing our environmental footprint at every step.

Science-Backed Results

While we celebrate natural ingredients, we ensure their effectiveness through rigorous testing and evidence-based formulations.
