Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within the US. International shipping varies by destination.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship to most countries worldwide. Shipping costs and delivery times vary by location.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. Products can be returned in original condition for a full refund.

Are your products cruelty-free?

Absolutely. We never test on animals and are certified cruelty-free by Leaping Bunny.

How do I track my order?

You'll receive a tracking number via email once your order ships. You can also check the status in your account.

Do you have physical stores?

We currently operate online only, but we're planning to open select retail locations in 2026.
