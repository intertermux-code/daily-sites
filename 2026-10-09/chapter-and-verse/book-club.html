About Our Book Club

Founded in 1992, our book club has brought together passionate readers from across the community. We meet every third Thursday of the month at 7:00 PM in our Community Room, where we discuss our monthly selection and enjoy light refreshments.

Our selections span various genres—from contemporary fiction to classic literature, memoirs to science fiction. We encourage diverse perspectives and thoughtful discussion while maintaining a welcoming atmosphere for readers of all backgrounds.

This Month's Selection

The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo

Taylor Jenkins Reid

Reclusive Hollywood icon Evelyn Hugo finally decides to tell her life story—but only to one reporter, Monique Grant. As Evelyn recounts her rise to stardom and her seven marriages, a deeper connection between her and Monique emerges.
