Our Story

Three decades of literary passion and community

From Passion to Purpose

In 1987, Margaret and Thomas Chen opened Chapter & Verse with a simple vision: to create a sanctuary for book lovers in the heart of our community. What began as a modest 400-square-foot space with a few hundred carefully selected titles has grown into the town's beloved literary hub.

Our commitment to curating exceptional books and fostering literary community has remained unchanged. Each title on our shelves represents hours of thoughtful consideration by our experienced staff, who share a deep appreciation for the written word in all its forms.

Our Mission

We believe that books have the power to transform lives, connect communities, and expand horizons. At Chapter & Verse, we're dedicated to putting the right book in the hands of the right reader, whether that's a beloved classic rediscovered or a debut novel that speaks to the soul.
