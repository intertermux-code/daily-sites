Our Story

Three decades of fostering a love for literature in our community

A Legacy of Literature

In 1987, Margaret and Robert Chen opened Chapter & Verse with a simple mission: to create a sanctuary where book lovers could discover new worlds and connect with fellow readers. What started as a modest 800-square-foot space has grown into a beloved community hub spanning over 3,000 square feet.

Our shop began with just 2,000 carefully selected volumes, handpicked by Margaret during months of research in publishers' catalogs and visits to literary events. Today, we house over 25,000 titles ranging from rare first editions to contemporary bestsellers.

The store's distinctive name reflects our belief that every book holds both chapters of narrative and verses of wisdom. We've maintained our commitment to curating selections that challenge, inspire, and transport readers while fostering genuine connections between authors and audiences.

Our Mission

At Chapter & Verse, we believe that independent bookstores serve as cultural anchors for their communities. We're dedicated to:

  • Promoting literacy and a lifelong love of reading
  • Supporting local and emerging authors
  • Providing a welcoming space for diverse voices and perspectives
  • Hosting meaningful literary events that bring people together
  • Maintaining personal relationships with our customers
