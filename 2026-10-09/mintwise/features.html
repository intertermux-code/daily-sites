Powerful Financial Tools

Discover how Mintwise transforms the way you manage money with our comprehensive suite of features

Budget Management

Take control of your spending with our intuitive budgeting tools. Set monthly limits for different categories, track your progress in real-time, and receive alerts when you're approaching your limits. Our smart categorization automatically sorts your transactions, making it easier to see where your money goes.

  • Automatic transaction categorization
  • Custom spending limits per category
  • Real-time progress tracking
  • Spending alerts and notifications

Investment Tracking

Monitor your investment portfolio performance with detailed analytics and insights. Track stocks, bonds, mutual funds, and retirement accounts in one centralized location. Get personalized recommendations based on your risk tolerance and financial goals.

  • Multi-account portfolio aggregation
  • Performance analytics and reporting
  • Asset allocation analysis
  • Dividend and interest tracking

Credit Monitoring

Stay informed about your credit health with daily monitoring and alerts. Access your credit score and report, track changes over time, and receive personalized tips to improve your credit standing. Get notified of any suspicious activity that could indicate identity theft.

  • Daily credit score updates
  • Full credit report access
  • Fraud alert monitoring
  • Identity theft protection

Bill Management

Never miss a payment again with automated bill tracking and reminders. Schedule payments in advance, track due dates, and analyze your recurring expenses. Our system learns your billing patterns and helps you optimize your cash flow.

  • Automatic bill detection
  • Scheduled payment setup
  • Late fee prevention
  • Recurring expense analysis
