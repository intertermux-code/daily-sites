Frequently Asked Questions

Your financial data is protected with bank-level security measures including 256-bit SSL encryption, multi-factor authentication, and continuous monitoring. We never store your banking credentials and comply with PCI DSS standards. All data is stored on encrypted servers in secure data centers with restricted access.

We use secure API connections provided by your financial institutions to access your account information. You'll need to log in directly with your bank's credentials through a secure connection. We only access read-only data necessary for tracking and analysis. Your login credentials are never stored in our systems.

If you forget your password, you can reset it by clicking the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. We'll send a secure password reset link to your registered email address. For additional security, you may also be asked to verify your identity through other methods if you have two-factor authentication enabled.

Yes, you can export your financial data at any time. Go to Settings > Data Export to download your transaction history, budget data, and reports in CSV or PDF format. This ensures you always maintain ownership of your financial information and can take it with you if needed.

Your account information is typically updated in real-time or within 15 minutes of any changes at your financial institution. Some banks may have longer update cycles (up to 24 hours) depending on their systems. You can manually refresh your accounts anytime by clicking the refresh button in the app.
