VENUE INFORMATION

LOCATION & ADDRESS

Neon Pulse Festival takes place at the Cyberpunk Convention Center, located at 123 Digital Avenue, Neo City. The venue features multiple indoor and outdoor stages with cutting-edge audiovisual technology.

PARKING & TRANSPORTATION

Ample parking is available at the venue with designated areas for general attendees, VIP guests, and staff. Public transportation options include the Metro Line 7 (Cyber Station) and several bus routes. Ride-sharing drop-off zones are marked at the main entrance.

FESTIVAL HOURS

Friday: 6:00 PM - 2:00 AM
Saturday: 4:00 PM - 3:00 AM
Sunday: 4:00 PM - 1:00 AM

FAQ

What items are prohibited at the festival? ▼

Prohibited items include weapons, illegal substances, glass containers, professional cameras with detachable lenses, large backpacks (over 12" x 12" x 6"), outside food and beverages, drones, and anything that may obstruct other guests' views. Small personal cameras and phones are permitted.

Can I re-enter the festival after leaving? ▼

Yes, re-entry is allowed during operating hours. Make sure to keep your wristband intact when exiting, as you'll need it to gain access again. Re-entry is only possible through the main entrance.

Are there accommodations for guests with disabilities? ▼

We are committed to providing accessible experiences. The venue offers wheelchair-accessible entrances, viewing areas, restrooms, and parking. ASL interpreters can be arranged with advance notice. Contact accessibility@neonpulse.com for special accommodation requests.

What happens if it rains? ▼

Neon Pulse is a rain or shine event. Some stages are covered, while others are outdoors. We recommend checking the weather forecast and bringing appropriate gear. In case of severe weather conditions, updates will be communicated via our official social media channels and email.

Is there an age restriction for the festival? ▼

The festival is strictly 18+ unless accompanied by a legal guardian. All attendees under 18 must present valid ID along with a completed minor waiver form signed by their legal guardian. VIP passes require attendees to be 21+ due to alcohol service.

How do I contact customer support? ▼

For ticketing inquiries, email tickets@neonpulse.com. For general questions, reach out to info@neonpulse.com. During the festival weekend, visit our information booth at the main entrance or approach any staff member wearing official badges.

HEALTH & SAFETY

COVID-19 PROTOCOLS

We follow local health guidelines and recommendations. Current requirements may include proof of vaccination or negative test results. Hand sanitizing stations are available throughout the venue. Face masks are recommended in crowded areas.

ON-SITE MEDICAL SERVICES

Medical personnel and first aid stations are available throughout the venue. The main medical tent is located behind the information booth. If you or someone around you needs assistance, locate a security guard or staff member immediately.
