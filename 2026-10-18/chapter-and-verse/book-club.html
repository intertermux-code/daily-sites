The Circles

Eighteen Chairs, One Kettle, Every Week

Twenty-eight reading circles run out of this shop. There is no membership fee, no set book to buy, and no member who has ever been told they read too slowly.

How the Matching Works

  1. Night before genre. A circle you can actually get to beats a circle of your dreams.
  2. Pace, honestly given. We do not place fast readers where they will feel hurried, or slow readers where they will feel behind.
  3. One book you loved, one you are avoiding. That pair tells us more than any survey.
  4. You may always move. Two nights in and it is wrong, say so. We have moved people mid-sentence.
