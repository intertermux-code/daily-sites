The Mosaic Wall

Everything We Love, Pinned at Once

Down the left stair of the shop there is a wall nobody is allowed to tidy. Portraits, quotations clipped from dust jackets, spine fragments, and the notes our booksellers write to one another. This is that wall, translated for the web.

“

A room without books is like a body without a soul.

Cicero, quoted on the stair since 1996
Bookseller’s Rule No. 4

Sell the Book, Not the Jacket

If a customer leaves with something they had not planned to buy and cannot explain why, we have done our work. Our staff picks are written by hand and re-shelved with the title.

“

I read to know I am not alone.

Attrib. C. S. Lewis — note card, Room Two

Four Books We Will Push on You

Pick 01

Middlemarch

George Eliot

In the Shop This Week

Six Nights, Every Week

Thursday is the read-aloud; Sunday is the long one, where we bring a chair from the front room because the room always fills. Entry is free and the tea is on us, but the second pot is by donation to the repair fund.

  • Thu 7:30

    Lamplight: Dickens’s Bad-Weather Heroes

    Forty minutes of reading from Our Mutual Friend, then open argument.

  • Fri 6:00

    Translation Night: Russian, Out Loud

    Pevear and Volokhonsky against the Garnett versions. Take sides.

  • Sun 10:30

    Open Shelving Morning

    Bring a book you have finished; leave with one you have not.

The Chapter & Verse Circles

Twenty-Eight Clubs, One Doorbell

Every circle meets in our back room with eighteen chairs and a kettle. Tell us how much you read and how much you like arguing, and we will put you somewhere you will not want to leave.

  • No membership fee — buy the book from us, or do not.
  • Reading pace agreed by the circle, never by us.
  • Loan copies held for members who cannot buy.
