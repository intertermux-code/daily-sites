The Second-Hand Desk

We Buy Back the Books You Finished

Bring them to the counter with a note of why you stopped. Half our classics case arrived that way, and a third of them went straight back out again.

01

What We Take

Classics, criticism, poetry and travel. Cloth bindings welcome, water damage not. We refuse modern thrillers, anything with a laminated jacket, and books published this century unless they have earned it.

02

What We Pay

One third of what we will sell it for, in cash or shop credit at half again the rate. Condition decides; provenance decides more. A signed copy is a conversation, not a price.

03

What We Repair

Re-sewing on the bench each Monday, £4 for a paperback, £18 for cloth. Anything older than 1900 we photograph before touching, and the photograph goes on the Mosaic Wall.
