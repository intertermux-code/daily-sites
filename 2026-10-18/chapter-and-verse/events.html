House Rules

How an Evening at Chapter & Verse Works

i

No Microphone

If you can hear the back row, the room is the right size. That is why our readings cap at forty and our author events move to Room Three.

ii

Books Stay Open

Read from whatever edition you own. We will not make you buy ours, though we will happily hold it for you while you argue.

iii

Tea, Then Silence

The kettle goes on at the top of the hour and stops when the reading starts. Arrive after that and take a chair in the doorway rather than a seat.

iv

Accessibility First

Step-free entry from Cobbett Lane, hearing loop in Room Three, and large-print copies of every read text at the counter. Tell us on the form what you need and we will build the night around it.

Room Hire

Take the Shop After Six

Monday evenings, when the shop is closed, we let Room Three to other people’s reading groups, writing classes and small presses. Eleven thousand books stay on the shelves; you get the keys, the kettle and a trolley.

  • £45 an hour, or £130 for the evening, shelves and all.
  • Projector, hearing loop and step-free access included.
  • Charities and schools: ask, and we will say yes.
