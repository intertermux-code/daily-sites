02

House rules

The Verdant Standard

2.1

Print the Whole Deck on the Front

No QR codes, no “full list on our site.” If it does not fit on the label, the formula is too long and we go back to the bench.

2.2

State the Percentage That Does the Work

We publish the active concentration that our clinical data supports, not the maximum the region will tolerate. Stronger is rarely better; consistent is.

2.3

Refill Before You Rebuy

Every glass bottle is designed to be kept. Return two empties and we post three refilled pouches at a third less than buying new.

2.4

Test on People Who Volunteer

Our panel is 240 volunteers across four skin tones and three climate zones. Nobody is paid in product to say something is nice, and we publish the drop-outs as well as the wins.

03

Sourcing

Three Plants We Actually Buy Ourselves

We travel twice a year to the farms behind our extracts and pay above market for the whole harvest, which is the only way to insist on a slow drying schedule. Ask for the grower’s name on any bottle and we will tell you.

Fern & Bracken Extract

Dryopteris filix-mas

Hand-cut in August from a shaded valley north of Kyoto, then air-dried on cedar racks for eleven days. It carries the calming polysaccharides in our Elixir.

Garden Mint

Mentha spicata

Grown on Fünen by a two-person organic market garden that also supplies our Copenhagen café. Cold-pressed within six hours of cutting, so the water stays green rather than brown.

Peppermint Water

Mentha × piperita

The hydrosol left after steam distillation, which most brands throw away. We use it as the first ingredient in our Cleansing Gel instead of plain water.
