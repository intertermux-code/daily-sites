Formula policy

What Goes In, What Stays Out

Each of these earns its place with a measured reason, printed on the carton beside the percentage.

  • Niacinamide at five per cent, where the data supports it
  • Three molecular weights of hyaluronic acid
  • Fern and mint extracts dried on our own racks
  • Bisabolol from evening primrose, not synthetic parfum
  • Squalane pressed from olives, never shark
  • pH buffers that keep the barrier at 5.0–5.5

What Stays Out

  • Added fragrance of any kind, including essential oils
  • Drying alcohols in the first three ingredients
  • Plastic microbeads and glitter
  • Oxybenzone and octinoxate
  • “Proprietary blends” that hide the concentration
  • Any animal-tested ingredient, anywhere in the world
03

Order of use

Layer Thinnest to Thickest

AM

Cleanse, Treat, Hydrate, Protect

Leaf & Stem on lukewarm skin, then two drops of Clarifying Niacinamide, then Morning Dew, then two fingers of Sun Veil fifteen minutes before you leave.

PM

Cleanse Twice, Then Seal

The first cleanse removes the day, the second cleanses the skin. Follow with your treatment and finish with two drops of Night Bloom or Calm Repair while the serum is still tacky.

2×

Enzyme Polish, Never More

Use the Reset Polish on two non-consecutive evenings. Skip your treatment serum that night and go straight to moisturiser.

04

Service

Delivery, Returns, Refills

We pack in Copenhagen on weekdays and post carbon-neutral. Orders over $60 ship free; everything else is a flat $6. Europe usually arrives in two working days, the rest of the world in five to seven.

Open the bottle, use it for thirty days, and if your skin disagrees with us, write to the studio and we will refund it. We would rather lose the sale than keep a customer using the wrong thing.

Return two empty glass bottles in the prepaid sack and we will send three refilled pouches for a third less than buying new. Every refill keeps the pump and the cap you already own.
