03

Common questions

Before You Write

You usually do not need to choose. We publish the one concentration our panel data supports, and it is the same for everyone. If a stronger version would genuinely help you, we would have made it — the reason our niacinamide is five per cent rather than ten is that eleven per cent of our panel flushed at six and there is no evidence the extra works harder.

Stop for three days, then reintroduce twice a week over damp skin. If it still stings after a fortnight, it is the wrong formula for you and we will refund it under the thirty-day skin promise — write to the studio with the batch code printed on the base of the bottle.

Yes to the cleanser, moisturiser and sunscreen — most people on retinoids live on those three. Hold your treatment serum until your prescriber says your skin has settled, and skip the Enzyme Reset Polish entirely while you are on a prescription strength.

Rinse two empty glass bottles, put them in the prepaid sack from your last order, and post them. Three refilled pouches arrive for a third less than three new bottles. Keep your pump and cap — they are rated for at least three refills. There is no subscription; you order a refill when you run out.

We ship to forty-one countries. Sun Veil travels to most of them, but filters are regulated as drugs in a handful of markets and we will not register in a country that requires animal testing to do it. If your address rejects SPF at checkout, the reason is regulatory, not technical — and we are happy to tell you which.

Opened, used, thirty days in — return it and we refund the full amount and compost the contents. We ask only that you tell us what went wrong, because that note is what changes the next batch. Return postage inside the EU and UK is on us.

Trade & press

Stocking Verdant

We supply twenty-six independent pharmacies, clinics and concept stores across Europe. Minimum opening order is twelve units across at least three formulas, and we ask that you keep the front-label deck visible rather than stacked face-down.

We do not wholesale to marketplaces, and we do not sell in any country whose regulations would require us to test on animals.

Press Kit

Founder portraits, workshop photography, full INCI lists with percentages, and our published panel results including drop-out rates. Samples ship to named writers, not to inboxes that ask for a “collab.”
