The Last Rack in the Building

Nineteen years of in-house hardware ends on a Tuesday in Newark. What replaces it is not the cloud the industry promised — it is somebody else’s cloud, on a contract nobody in the room was allowed to read.

By Devan Rao ◆ Photographs by Anke Fuldner ◆ 14 min read ◆ Friday, 16 October

The cooling loop was the first thing to go quiet. For nineteen years it ran at a pitch you could hear from the loading dock, and when the technicians drained it on the last Thursday of September, the building sounded, for the first time in anyone’s memory, like an ordinary warehouse. Haluk Demir has worked the floor since 2007. He stood at the end of aisle four and listened to nothing, and then he went to get coffee, because there was nothing else to do yet.
