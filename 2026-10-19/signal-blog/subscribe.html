    How an Edition Gets Made

    Page E2  ·  Monday to Thursday
    1. M

      Monday, 10 a.m. The desk meeting

      Everything on the table, including what is not ready

      Every reporter states what they have, what they do not have, and what they are still waiting on a lawyer to answer. Roughly a third of what is discussed never gets filed, and that is the intended rate.

    2. T

      Tuesday – Wednesday Reporting and check

      The fact-checking desk takes the draft apart

      Every figure, name, date, and characterisation goes to Wren Halloran with its source attached. Nothing publishes until the list is closed. Two pieces have been held for more than a month by this step.

    3. W

      Thursday, 9 a.m. Right of reply

      Subjects see the specific claims, in writing

      Five working days, always. A refusal to respond is reported as a refusal to respond, in the same paragraph as the claim it concerns. This is the step most publications have quietly stopped doing.

    4. T

      Thursday, 6 p.m. Send

      The edition leaves on our own mail server

      No third-party sender, no open-tracking pixel, no re-engagement campaign you did not ask for. Delivery problems are answered by a person, usually the same day.

    Why This Costs Nothing and Charges Nothing

    Page E5

    A newsroom that sells placements eventually sells the sentence next to them. That is not a moral claim, it is an observation about how nine years of invoices behave: the thing you were paid for gets written with more care than the thing you were not, and the difference becomes visible to readers long before it becomes visible to the editor.

    So the cooperative wrote the prohibition into its operating agreement in 2023, where changing it now takes a two-thirds vote of the staff. Advertising is 0.0% of income and has never been anything else. The rest is members, archive licences, ring-fenced grants with named grantors, and a print annual that loses a small amount of money every year in the correct spirit.

    If you read technology news for a living and you have never paid for any of it, the free edition is a genuine offer. If you find yourself opening ours before the others, membership is how that keeps existing.
