§ 03 Where the shop keeps its quiet p. 58

Two rooms, a cobbler’s lease, and fifteen years of shelves

We took the unit at 27 Winsley Street in the spring of 2011 because it had a sprung floor, a north window, and a history of mending things. The cobbler who ran it before us left a bench at the back; we put books on it and never moved it.

Fourteen thousand titles later the plan is unchanged: sell the book, tell the truth about it, and leave the lamp on. The Reading Room at the back holds thirty chairs, a wood stove we over-stoke every February, and a rule that you may sit in it for as long as you like without buying a thing.
