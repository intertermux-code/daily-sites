§ 02 House rules for the classics wall p. 22

Why the shelf is small and the shelf marks are real

Shelf note

If a title appears on this page it is in the building. We take it off the list the same day it goes out the door.

  1. One edition per title

    We stock the translation or text we would recommend to a friend, not all nine. If you want the other one, we will order it by Wednesday and it costs you nothing extra.

  2. Shelved by author, marked by language

    The marks on these pages are the ones on our spines — Library of Congress, because the shop began life next to a branch library and we never could see the sense in reinventing it.

  3. Condition graded out loud

    Used copies say so: good means read with creased spines, very good means handled carefully, fine means we are quietly proud of it. No euphemisms.

  4. Holds, not queues

    Reserve any title on this page and we will put it behind the counter for five days with your name in pencil on a slip. Bring it back if nothing changes; we would rather it lived with you.
