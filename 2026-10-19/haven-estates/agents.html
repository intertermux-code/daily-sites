How a Haven File Runs

Three Steps, and the Same Face Through All of Them

01 · Conversation

You Talk, We Listen

One hour, no forms. Your advisor maps what you actually need — light, water, schools, silence — and tells you honestly whether we hold it.

02 · The Wall

Curated, Not Paged

You are shown three homes, not thirty — listed, off-market, and Thursday previews included, with a second unaccompanied look at each.

Negotiation, inspection, escrow, and the movers — all carried by the same advisor, in writing, on one page you can read at breakfast.
