The Residence

Built by the Architect Who Lives Two Doors Down

Marlowe House steps down its ridge in three quiet bands: arrival and kitchen at the top, the great room in the middle, and a lower gallery that opens — entirely — to the water. Oak floors, lime plaster, blackened steel. Nothing shouts.

The current owners commissioned it in 2019 and are downsailing to the bay at Merrow. It has been on the market for eleven days and shown twenty-two times, always by appointment, always with the file held by one advisor.
