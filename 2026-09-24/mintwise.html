Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers.

Is my bank information secure?

Absolutely. We use 256-bit encryption and read-only access to ensure your data is safe. We never store your banking credentials on our servers.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. Your access will continue until the end of your billing cycle.

Does Mintwise support international banks?

We currently support over 12,000 financial institutions across North America and Europe. We are adding new connections every week.

Do you offer a mobile app?

Yes! Mintwise is available on both iOS and Android. Download it from the App Store or Google Play Store to manage your money on the go.
