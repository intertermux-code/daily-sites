§ 04.3 Guardrails

The parts of a price list that usually get hidden

Written as we would want them written if we were shopping for a finance app on a $1,900 month.

Never a paywall on your own data

Downgrade to Seed and the whole ledger stays readable and exportable. We have never charged for an exit.

Half price, no proof theatre

Students, SNAP households, and full-time carers pay 50% off. One sentence in the support form is the whole process.

Price frozen inside a term

If we raise Plus to $12, your paid annual run continues at the rate you agreed. We have raised once since 2021, for grandfathered-only exceptions.

Refunds without an interview

Thirty days, any plan, one click in Settings → Billing. We do not ask why and we do not retain the data behind a cancelled account.

One revenue line

Subscriptions only. No offers, no cashback placements, no “suggested products” in the transaction feed, no partner data feeds.

Employer & credit-union billing

Invoices, purchase orders, and bulk seating for workplaces that run financial wellbeing programmes — email us on the support page.

§ 04.4 Billing notes

Six questions about the invoice itself

Product and security questions live on the support page.

It drops to the Seed tier: read-only, whole history, CSV export always available. New transactions stop syncing because your connection is disabled — re-enable it any time by resubscribing, and the gap fills retroactively for up to 400 days of history.

Yes. Going monthly → annual credits the unused portion of the current month against the annual invoice. Annual → monthly takes effect at your renewal date and we email the day before it does.

Half price for students, households receiving SNAP or equivalent support, and people caring full time for a family member. Send one line through the contact form; we apply it manually within a day and do not require documents unless the account is shared.

No. We pay Plaid and Tink for connection volume and receive nothing from your bank. There is no interchange share, no referral fee for opening an account, and no sponsored row in any list you see.

Sales tax, GST, or VAT is calculated at checkout by billing address and shown before you confirm. Every charge produces a PDF receipt; EU customers can supply a VAT number for a reverse-charge invoice, and employers can request net terms on the Household plan.

The 14-day Plus trial needs no card, so there is nothing to refund and nothing to cancel silently. On day fifteen the app drops to Seed and shows you exactly what you would gain by staying — no dark pattern, no countdown popups.
