§ 05.3 Colophon

Three things we are still slow at

Published because a support page that admits nothing is a marketing page.

01 · Amortised costs

Annual bills smoothed over twelve months

Works in the ledger, but the forecast chart still shows the raw charge the week it lands. Fix scheduled for the December release; the workaround is a dedicated envelope.

02 · Small banks

Credit unions on legacy connectors

Eleven institutions still sync at file upload rather than continuously, so a charge can appear a day late. CSV import solves it in forty seconds.

03 · Weekends

Sunday replies

We close Sundays. Tickets raised after Saturday noon wait until Monday 07:00 ET — 6% of the queue, and the one complaint we have not designed around yet.

Churn runs at 3.1% a month. Anyone who wants the number we are least proud of, ask — we publish it on purpose.
