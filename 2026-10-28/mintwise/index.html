§ 01 Safeguards

Built like a vault, laid out like a notebook

Four guarantees, each one independently audited, none of them buried in a policy PDF.

SOC 2 Type II

Audited annually by Coalfire. Report available to any member on request.

256-bit encryption

AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, keys rotated every 90 days.

FDIC-insured partners

Balance data is read-only. Mintwise never holds your money or moves it.

No data resale

We don’t sell, swap, or “anonymise” your transactions for advertisers.

Connections use OAuth through Plaid and Tink. Your bank credentials never touch a Mintwise server, and revoking access takes one click — we’ll walk you through it.

§ 02 Method

Eleven minutes a week, filed the same way twice

Most budgeting apps ask you to become an accountant. Mintwise asks for a Sunday coffee and four small decisions.

  1. Step 01 · 2 min

    Connect, then forget

    Link checking, credit, savings, and one brokerage. Mintwise pulls last night’s activity each morning at 4 a.m. and reconciles duplicates it finds across accounts.

  2. Step 02 · 3 min

    Approve the rules it drafts

    The classifier writes a sorting rule for each merchant the first time it appears. You tap “keep” or “change” — after about fifty transactions, the ledger runs itself.

  3. Step 03 · 4 min

    Fund the envelopes

    Move next month’s income into named envelopes: Groceries, Car, Gifts, Future Self. Overspending in one borrows from another on purpose, not by accident.

  4. Step 04 · 2 min

    Read the one-page summary

    Sunday evening you get a single screen: what landed, what left, your savings rate, and the one line item that moved most since last month.

§ 05 Marginalia

Notes from three households

Long-term members who let us quote their actual figures.

Bring one other person in without merging everything

The Household plan gives two adults one ledger with private envelopes, joint envelopes, and a split rule per merchant. Nobody has to read the other’s coffee habit, and both see the rent.

  • Per-merchant splits — 50/50 on rent, 70/30 by income on groceries.
  • Private by default — each adult’s personal envelopes stay hidden.
  • One shared goal jar — deposits tracked against both contributions.
