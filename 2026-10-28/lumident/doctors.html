01 / Clinicians

In order of who you will most likely meet

Who does what, and on which days.

Dr Amara Osei

BDS, MFDS RCS, DipImplantology · Lead clinician & owner

Amara spent eleven years in oral and maxillofacial surgery at Southmead Hospital before opening LumiDent in 2014, mostly because she was tired of watching patients discharged with a plan nobody funded. She places every implant fixture in this building and personally signs each written treatment plan. Her clinic style is unhurried and quietly funny; she refers IV sedation cases to a colleague rather than pushing through.

Dr Felix Bramwell

BDS, MSc Orthodontics · Alignment & growth guidance

Felix trained in Manchester and does one thing: moving teeth. He reviews aligner cases twice a month, which is the boring discipline that makes the finish match the estimate. Half his patients are over thirty and he is unusually good at explaining why a small relapse at forty is not the same problem as a crowded arch at fourteen.

Dr Ada Okonjo

03 / Standards

What you can hold us to

Registration, training and the complaints drawer.

All five dentists and two hygienists are registered with the General Dental Council and display their numbers in the reception. The practice is CQC-regulated under provider number 1-2049387, last inspected without recommendations.

Every clinician completes a minimum of 40 verified CPD hours a year — far above the GDC's 35-hour cycle requirement — plus quarterly in-house sessions on sedation, safeguarding and resuscitation. Our defibrillator is checked on the first Monday of each month and the log is on the wall by the door.

Complaints, in practice

Say it at the desk and Niamh responds within two working days in writing. If you would rather not tell us at all, the Dental Complaints Service and PALS both handle it free, and their details are printed on the same card as our own.

Data & photographs

Clinical photographs are stored on a UK-hosted system with your named consent. Nothing from this practice — including gallery images — appears anywhere without a signed release, and you can withdraw it at any time by asking.

Access

Step-free entrance from Wapping Wharf, a hearing loop at reception, wheelchair-accessible surgery 1 with a ceiling hoist, and large-print or audio treatment plans on request.
