03 / Travel & access

Plan the last two hundred metres

Arriving without a fight.

By bus

Routes 8, 42 and 73 stop at Queen Street North — 90 seconds on level paving. Route 24 drops you at Narrow Quay, four minutes along the towpath.

By train

Bristol Temple Meads is a 12-minute walk. Follow the Floating Harbour towpath: it is flat, lit until late and step-free the whole way.

By car

Two validated patient bays on Queen Street — show your appointment text at the desk and we clear the ticket. Castle Park multi-storey is three minutes away and free after 18:00.

By bike

Six bike spaces under the canopy beside the lift entrance, plus a locker you can borrow for the length of an appointment.

Wheelchair & pram access

Level entrance from the quay, lift to first floor, accessible surgery 1 with a ceiling hoist, and a step-free toilet on the same floor as reception.

Hearing, sight & language

Hearing loop at the desk and in surgery 1. Large-print and audio treatment plans. Interpreters booked free of charge — ask for Bosnian, Polish, Somali, Welsh or BSL when you book.

Coming with children

Two buggies park inside the door, a bottle-warmer lives under the reception shelf, and the paediatric wall has a mirror at child height so a first visit starts with a look rather than a chair.

Changing your appointment

Reply “cancel” to our confirmation text and it is gone. Up to 24 hours before, there is no fee and no lecture. Inside 24 hours we may charge the material cost if a laboratory order is already cut.

04 / Before you come

Bring these four things

A ten-minute list that saves an appointment.

  1. Your medication list

    Photo of the box labels is perfect. Blood thinners, bisphosphonates and inhalers change what we do on the day.

  2. Recent X-rays, if you have them

    Anything from the last two years on your phone or on a disc. We will read them rather than repeat your dose.

  3. Your dental practice details

    Name and address so we can request the record, with your written permission at the desk.

  4. The question you are embarrassed about

    Say it early. Roughly one consultation in five is mainly about a smell, a bleed or a filling from 2011 that nobody will admit to.

If you are frightened

Write “anxious” in the notes box on the booking form. Your first visit becomes a conversation with no instruments, no chair, and no treatment plan you have to respond to.

Nothing decided yet?

Twenty minutes, free, no referral needed.

Come and look at the room before you commit to anything. We will show you the price list on the wall and let you read it in peace.
