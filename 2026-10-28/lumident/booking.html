02 / Arriving

What happens in the first ten minutes

You will not sit in a waiting room long.

Medical history is taken on a tablet in the chair while you drink the tea we press on you. Bring your medication list and any recent X-rays on a phone — that is genuinely all.

  1. Desk — 3 minutes

    Give your reference from the confirmation text. Two validated parking spaces on Queen Street are free for patients, plus step-free access from Wapping Wharf.

  2. Chair — history, not paperwork

    Your clinician reads the form with you and asks the questions a GP would miss: bleeding gums, clenching, snoring, refills you forgot to collect.

  3. Look & photograph

    Full-mouth digital X-rays at a fraction of old film dose, intraoral photographs, and a gum chart if it has been more than a year.

  4. Plan on paper

    Costed, numbered, handed to you. Book the treatment then or take it away — the free consultation stays free either way.
