01 / The room

Why we rebuilt a dental practice

Most people dread the chair. We redesigned the chair.

Dentophobia is not irrational — it is a response to being lied to about cost, held in a queue, and worked on under a cold fluorescent tube. LumiDent removes those three things and calls it basic hospitality.

Ceilings are 3.4 metres. Each surgery faces a window, so you watch the river rather than a lamp arm. Every treatment plan is printed before anyone picks up an instrument, and the number at the bottom is the number on the invoice.

03 / How a visit runs

Four appointments, start to finish

The sequence, in plain English

  1. Consult — 20 minutes, £0

    Sit down. You tell us what bothers you; we look, photograph, and say whether we can fix it. No instrument leaves the tray until you agree a number.

  2. Record — 45 minutes

    Intraoral scan, digital X-rays and a shade chart. You get the files the same day, because they are your teeth.

  3. Plan — printed & signed

    Options in order of invasiveness, each with cost, visit count and risk. Take it home; nothing is booked until you reply.

  4. Treat & review

    Work happens in numbered stages. A six-week review and a twelve-month re-check are included in the price, not sold separately.

Comfort, unlisted as standard

Nitrous oxide, noise-cancelling headphones, a blanket warmed on request and a raised hand that stops work immediately — every one of these is free and needs no mention in advance.

06 / Money

No deposit surprises

The number we quote is the number we charge.

Fixed fees cover the anaesthetic, the imaging, the review appointments and the laboratory work. If a tooth turns out to be worse than the X-ray showed, we tell you before we treat and the original plan still stands for everything already agreed.

Payment is 30% at the start and the balance at the end, or interest-free over ten months on anything above £1,000. We do not sell membership plans and we do not require one to be seen.
