Case 01 / Veneers, upper arch

“I stopped laughing with my hand over my mouth.”

A 44-year-old teacher with enamel worn down by night grinding and banding from teenage antibiotic staining. She wanted the front six teeth fixed but refused orthodontics. Bonding was rejected as too fragile for a heavy bite.

  1. Wax-up & trial — 2 visits

    The laboratory built the proposed shape in wax; Elin copied it into a temporary mock-up the patient wore at home for seven days before any tooth surface was prepared.

  2. Preparation — 1 visit, 2 h 10

    0.5 mm minimally-prepped laminates, rubber dam, shade taken in north light rather than under the operatory lamp.

  3. Cementation — 1 visit

    Six units bonded, bite adjusted, night guard made the same week because veneers without a guard are a warranty claim waiting to happen.

  4. Review — 6 weeks, 12 months

    Both included in the fee. Margins checked against a probe, photographs repeated in the identical setup.

03 / Honest pictures

Our photography rule

Same lamp, same retractors, no filter.

Clinical photographs are taken with the cheek retractors the patient chose, on the same retractors-in-the-door setup, at the same colour temperature, before any anaesthetic and after full healing. We do not whiten the background, we do not crop the lip line to flatter a result, and we do not show a case we did not treat.

The diagrams on this page are CSS-drawn recreations rather than photographs, so that patients can be displayed publicly with the identifying detail removed. Every one of the four is signed off in writing by the patient it belongs to, and any of them can be withdrawn the day they ask.

  • Consent is separate

    Treatment consent and publication consent are two forms. Declining the second has never affected anybody's care here.

  • Real photographs on request

    Ask at reception and we will open the untitled image library with you in the consultation room.

  • Complications published too

    Two of the 212 documented cases needed a replacement veneer within the warranty. We show those when you ask, because otherwise the gallery is advertising.
