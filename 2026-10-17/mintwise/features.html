Panel 01 · Budgets
Autopilot budgets

Budgets that re-tune themselves each week.

Static budgets fail the first surprising week. Mintwise reads your real spending rhythm and nudges each envelope so the month still lands on target.

  • Rollover logic. Unspent grocery money drifts forward instead of vanishing at midnight.
  • Fairness alerts. If an envelope is about to run dry two weeks early, you get a heads-up while there is still time.
  • One-tap override. Lock any envelope to a hard number when you want manual control. Nothing moves without your say.

Round-up vaults

Spare change that quietly becomes a fund.

Coffee for $3.70 rounds to $4 and the 30 cents heads to a vault. Multiply that across a month and the emergency fund fills without a single transfer you had to remember.

  • Choose the multiplier. Round to the nearest dollar, or double it for members who want faster momentum.
  • Route anywhere. Send the vault to savings, an investment account, or debt payoff. You pick the destination.
  • Ceiling guard. Set a daily cap so an unusual spending day never drains your checking balance.

Panel 03 · Radar
Bill radar

Every charge, tracked a week before it lands.

The radar watches recurring and one-off bills, then shows a countdown you can actually plan around. No more opening your banking app in a cold sweat on the 28th.

  • Upcoming ledger. A running total of what must clear in the next 7, 14, and 30 days.
  • Low-balance warnings. If a due bill could push an account negative, you hear about it days ahead.
  • Price-creep detection. When a usual bill jumps, we flag the change so you can dispute or switch.

Subscription audit

Find the recurring money you forgot about.

Mintwise lists every merchant that charges you on a cycle, groups them by category, and estimates the annual cost of the ones you stopped using.

  • Zombie detector. Highlights services you have not opened in months yet still pay for.
  • Duplicate finder. Catches when two plans overlap, like two cloud-storage subscriptions.
  • Annual view. Sees a $12/mo tool as the $144/yr commitment it really is.

Panel 05 · Goals
Goal lanes

Give every goal a date you can trust.

Tell Mintwise what you are saving for and when you want it. We forecast the landing day from your real behaviour, then keep you honest with weekly deltas.

  • Backed-in plan. “Deposit $172 every Friday” beats a vague intention. We pick the number, you approve it.
  • Drift alerts. If a goal slips from on-track to at-risk, the lane turns amber before the deadline crunch.
  • Shared lanes. Rent, a trip, or a joint fund, each with visible progress both people can see.

Privacy vault

Architecture that makes misuse hard.

Read-only connections, encryption at rest and in transit, biometric unlock, and a full export and purge you can trigger yourself. Your data is a tool you lend, not a product we sell.

  • Never moves money. Credentials are read-only. Mintwise cannot initiate a transfer even if asked.
  • Zero ad auctions. We do not sell, rent, or broker your transaction history. Period.
  • One-tap deletion. Delete your account and we purge your history within 24 hours, then send you the log.
