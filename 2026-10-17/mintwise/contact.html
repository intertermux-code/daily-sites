Yes. We connect to your accounts through read-only links, which means Mintwise can read activity but can never move money. Credentials and data are encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit, and we are independently audited to SOC 2 Type II.

Never. Mintwise is paid for by subscriptions, not advertising or data brokerage. We do not sell, rent, or broker your transaction history to any third party. Aggregate, de-identified trend figures power our benchmarks and are impossible to trace back to you.

Active accounts refresh roughly every 60 seconds while the app is open, and at least four times a day in the background. Some institutions batch overnight, so a pending charge may show its posted date a day late.

Start with a manual refresh in the accounts list. If that fails, re-link the account once; passwords and bank-side sessions are the usual cause. When it persists, choose the syncing topic above and we will trace the connection with you.

Mintwise reads from more than 12,000 institutions across the US, UK, and EU, including the major high-street banks, credit unions, brokerages, and retirement providers. Search for yours at sign-up; if it is missing, tell us and we usually add it in the next release.

Any plan exports a full CSV of transactions, categories, and balances. Paid plans add a formatted PDF history, and Constellation adds an API. Deleting your account removes everything within 24 hours and we send you the deletion log.
