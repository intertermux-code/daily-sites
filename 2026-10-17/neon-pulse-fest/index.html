The Festival

Built for the Hours After Midnight

Neon Pulse started in 2019 as a single warehouse room with two turntables and a borrowed laser rig. Seven years later it is the West Coast’s loudest argument that the night is the main event.

This December you get three nights on the harbor — a working pier district transformed into a walk-through sound system. The Neon Mainstage holds 18,000 under a canopy of moving heads. The Pulse Dome wraps you in 360° spatial audio. The Laser Garden is exactly what it says: a forest of beams you can dance inside.

No day-gates, no filler. Every act on the poster has a reason to be there, and every stage runs until the ferries start moving again at dawn.

  • Gates 7:00 PM → close 4:00 AM, all three nights
  • 21+ festival, cashless grounds, lockers from $12
  • Free re-entry until 1:00 AM each night

Beyond the Stages

The Pier Comes Alive Between Sets

Neon Pulse is a walk-through city after dark. Between sets, the district keeps moving.

  • Bass Alley — a covered block of sound-system sculptures you can stand inside, tuned by local crews.
  • The Rain Room — a controlled downpour synced to a generative ambient score. Bring something you don’t mind soaking.
  • Neon Market — 40 vendors, record fair, and the only food court that stays open until 4 AM.
  • Charging & Chill Lounge — quiet zone, free water refill, and 900 lockers on site.

Everything sits within a six-minute walk of the Mainstage. No shuttles between stages, no missed transitions.

The Arc

Three Nights, Three Characters

  1. Friday, Dec 18 · 7 PM – 4 AM

    Opening Night: Pure Techno

    The festival starts cold and fast. Warehouse headliners, minimal lighting, no talk. If you only understand one night, make it this one.

  2. Saturday, Dec 19 · 6 PM – 4 AM

    Peak Night: All Stages Hot

    The biggest card of the weekend runs simultaneously — house on the Mainstage, bass music in the Dome, and a Laser Garden takeover until sunrise.

  3. Sunday, Dec 20 · 6 PM – 3 AM

    Closing Night: Trance & Sunrise

    Euphoric sets and a 6:40 AM harbor sunrise finale with Midnight Mira. Early-bird Sunday-only passes release in November.
