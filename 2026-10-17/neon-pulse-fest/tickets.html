After You Buy

How the Wristband Works

You never touch paper at Neon Pulse. Here’s the whole lifecycle of your pass, from checkout to the last song.

  1. Checkout · 90 Seconds

    Buy and Name-Lock

    Pay in full or split into three. Your pass locks to your name at purchase — that’s what kills scalpers, not fine print.

  2. By Dec 10 · Mail

    Wristband Ships Free

    A contactless RFID band arrives in a festival sleeve. Lost it? Freeze and reissue it from your account in two taps.

  3. Night Of · Gate Scan

    Tap In, Load Pre-Commit

    Scan at the gate, pre-load a cashless budget from your phone, and dance. Refunds on unused balance post within 72 hours.

  4. Can’t Make It? · Fan Exchange

    Transfer Once, Officially

    Move your pass to a friend at face value through the exchange. You keep the wristband; the name changes.
