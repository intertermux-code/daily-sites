Know Before You Go

Info & Venue

The answers to everything the group chat keeps asking — gates, transit, accessibility, cashless rules, and who to wave at when you’re lost.

The Grounds

Pier 44 Harbor District, Los Angeles

Twelve acres of working waterfront — cargo yards, a covered hangar, and an open pier apron facing the channel. For three nights in December, the district becomes one connected festival site.

All three stages sit inside a six-minute walk of each other, so you can chase a transition instead of planning one. Gates open at 7:00 PM Friday and 6:00 PM on Saturday and Sunday. The last set ends at 4:00 AM — and the Laser Garden keeps a low ambient light on until the sun actually rises.

  • Address for GPS: 4400 Harbor Avenue, Los Angeles, CA 90045
  • Gates: Gate A (Mainstage), Gate B (Dome), Gate C (VIP & Ultra)
  • Will-call & box office: Gate A, open 4:00 PM to 1:00 AM nightly
  • Rideshare drop-off: Harbor Ave & Pier Lane, signed from the freeway exits

Essentials

Getting There & Getting Around

Transit First

The Metro C Line drops you 900 meters from Gate A — a 12-minute walk with marshals and light art along the way. Trains run every 10 minutes until 1:30 AM and shuttle-bus from 1:30 to 5:30 AM.

Parking & Rideshare

On-site parking is limited and pre-purchase only ($35/night, Lot H). Rideshare has a dedicated two-lane pickup on Pier Lane — never at the freeway exit, always inside the district.

Gates & Hours

Friday gates 7:00 PM–2:00 AM entry. Saturday and Sunday 6:00 PM–3:00 AM entry. Music runs to 4:00 AM all three nights, and re-entry closes at 1:00 AM each night.

Accessibility

All stages have ADA viewing platforms with companion seating. Golf-cart transport runs between gates on request. Service animals welcome; ASL interpreters at every Mainstage headliner.

Cashless Grounds

Your wristband is your wallet. Pre-load it in the app, tap to buy, and any unused balance auto-refunds within 72 hours. Cash is accepted only at will-call and the locker desk.

Safety & Wellness

Two free wellness tents with water, earplugs, and trained peer support — no questions, no judgment. Lost-and-found and a buddy-reunification point live at the Neon Market arch.
