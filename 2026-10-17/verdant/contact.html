05 / Policy

Returns, Reactions
and the Fine Print

Plain terms, no forms to print.

Returning an Order

Email the studio within thirty days of delivery. We refund the full price as soon as the tracking shows your parcel moving — we do not wait for it to reach the bench. Opened products are fine; we ask you not to mail back anything you reacted to, so keep it or bin it.

Refill pouches are not returnable once the seal is broken, for obvious reasons. Unopened pouches return like anything else.

If Something Stings

Stop using it, rinse with cool water, and write to advice@verdant.skin with the lot number printed on the base. A short, sharp sting that fades in a minute is usually a barrier telling you it is over-loaded; a warm, itchy flush that lasts is a sensitivity, and you should not use the product again.

Either way we will replace the bottle and take the item off your next recommendation.
