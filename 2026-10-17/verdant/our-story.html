01 / Origin

The Winter My Hands
Cracked Open

Ilse spent nine years formulating for other people’s brands in a rented lab off Broughton Street. In the winter of 2018 she developed contact dermatitis from a fragrance blend she had signed off herself, and could not find anything on the shelf that would not sting her forearms.

She milled Skagit oats in a coffee grinder, suspended them in a squalane base, and wrote the numbers in a ledger because she did not trust her memory. Forty jars later, friends were asking for the same cream. The ledger became the registry you can read on this page.

We still keep it the same way: every formula has a number, every number has a date, and nothing ships unless the batch notes say it behaved.

“We do not have a hero ingredient. We have a hero percentage.” Ilse Vandermeer · Founder & Formulator

The first run sold out at the Friday market in under two hours, mostly to people with eczema who had been told to stop using everything. Ilse wrote the percentages on the lid in chinagraph because she had not designed a label yet.

That original formula is unchanged except for one thing: we dropped the preservative that we now know caused a reaction in a small number of customers, and moved to a broader-spectrum system at half the dose.

What we will not
formulate with

Four exclusions, held since the first batch, and reviewed each year with our testing panel.

  • 01 — Synthetic fragrance or musk. If a product needs to smell like something, we use a hydrosol and say so.
  • 02 — Denatured alcohol in anything left on the skin.
  • 03 — Physical exfoliants with an uncontrolled particle size.
  • 04 — Essential oils above 0.3% on the face, and never on the lips or lids.

04 / Practice

Four Rules We
Hold to the Letter

Written on the wall above the filling bench, unchanged since 2019.

Rule 01

Publish the Number

Every active is listed with its percentage. If we cannot print a number for it, it does not go in.

Rule 02

Retire, Do Not Pad

Two products have been discontinued. The line has never grown past eight, and it will not grow to nine.

Rule 03

Test the Dirt

Clays, botanicals and waters arrive with a certificate of analysis, and we re-run metals on every clay lot ourselves.

Rule 04

Answer the Email

The studio inbox is read by a formulator, not a triage queue. One working day, usually an hour.
