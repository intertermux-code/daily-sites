§ 01 Powerlifting

Squat, Bench, Deadlift — in Twelve-Week Blocks

This is the loudest program in the building and the most structured. You are given a percentage, a rep target and a rest period; you log what actually happened. Every fourth session is the one you look forward to — it is where the block shows its work.

Who It Suits

Lifters with at least six months of consistent barbell work who can already hold a parallel squat under a warm-up bar. If that is not you yet, run the six-week On-Ramp track (it is included with Foundry and Ironclad) and join the next block with a clean baseline.

How the Block Progresses

  • Wk 1–4Accumulation. Higher reps, moderate load, technique cues every set. Nothing above RPE 7.
  • Wk 5–8Transmutation. Load climbs, reps drop, singles introduced at RPE 8 on the last work set of the week.
  • Wk 9–11Realisation. Openers rehearsed, attempts trialled, accessories trimmed back to what protects your lifts.
  • Wk 12Deload or meet weekend. Whichever is on the calendar; both are planned, neither is a surprise.

Marginal note. If you miss two sessions in a week, take the following session at 90 % of the prescribed load instead of trying to cram the volume back in.

§ 02 HIIT & Engine

Intervals Prescribed by Zone, Not by Noise

Forty-five minutes, three to five times a week, on equipment that rewards honest pacing. We write the work in heart-rate bands and rate of perceived exertion, so the athlete who can hold 185 bpm and the one returning from a shoulder operation both get a session that belongs to them.

Anatomy of a Session

  • 0–8 minRamp. Bike or row at zone 1, then hip, ankle and thoracic prep done barefoot on the mat line.
  • 8–12 minPrimer. Two short efforts at target zone so your heart rate arrives before the first real interval.
  • 12–38 minThe block. Work-to-rest ratios rotate weekly: 1:1, 1:2, 1:3, then 2:1. Modalities never stack — a sled day is a sled day.
  • 38–45 minDown. Zone 1 breathing, recorded effort score, one mobility takeaway you keep for the week.

Scaling Without Cheating

Every interval has a documented substitute: the row becomes the ski erg if your lower back is talking, the sled becomes the stepmill if your knee will not flex, and a 24 kg kettlebell becomes a 16 kg one without anyone commenting on it. Effort is measured against your own zone, not the athlete on the next machine.

Marginal note. Engine work sits after strength in the week, never before a heavy lower session. Coaches will move you if you get it backwards.

§ 03 Mobility & Repair

Loaded Range Work for People Who Sit and Lift

Thirty-five minutes, barefoot, twelve mats, one physiotherapist-led coach per class. This is not stretching to music — it is strength trained through a full range, with a self-test each Friday so you can prove the range is yours and not just borrowed for ten minutes.

What a Class Covers

  • AnklesDorsiflexion loading against a wall and a kettlebell; the fix for heels lifting in a deep squat.
  • Hips90/90 work, deep adductor lunges with a bar overhead, controlled pigeon. Loaded, not passive.
  • ThoracicOpeners on the foam roller, then rotation under a light press so the new range gets a job.
  • ShouldersFlexion and extension screens, then weighted hanging progressions from 20 seconds up.
  • BreathFive minutes of ribcage expansion drills. It is the part everyone skips and the part that changes the other four.

Who Books It Most

Lifters with a stuck squat depth, desk workers with a locked hip hinge, and returning athletes rebuilding after a physio referral. Dr. Tomás Reyes runs the Tuesday and Friday evening classes and reviews every screen result logged in the app.

Marginal note. Bring nothing but a water bottle. Mats, blocks, bands and the roller stock are in the rack by Studio B.

§ 04 The Method

One Rule Set Across All Three Programs

Whichever lane you train in, the same four principles decide how your week is written and when it changes.

forge@foundry:~/method — read only
01 Measure before you prescribe. Every athlete arrives with a baseline session: squat, hinge, press, pull, plus a 500 m row and a range screen. 02 Load is a percentage, effort is a number. Percentages come from your tested top, not an estimated one, and RPE is taught in week one. 03 Progressive overload, conservatively applied. We add 2.5 kg to a lift when two sessions land at or under the target RPE — never because it is Tuesday. 04 The logbook is the program. Coaches read it the evening before your session; if it is empty, you get asked why. $cat deload.md

When Blocks Change

New blocks open on the first Monday of each month. Deload weeks are scheduled, not earned: week four of every block drops volume by a third whether you feel fresh or not, because the point of a deload is the next block, not this one.

§ 06 Common Questions

Before You Pick a Lane

Can I train two programs at once?

Yes, and about half the floor does. The usual pairing is powerlifting twice weekly with two engine sessions. Mobility slots in as the third or fourth day rather than as an extra, so the week stays at four sessions.

I have never touched a barbell. Where do I start?

On-Ramp: a six-week technique track run Monday and Wednesday at 18:00, included with Foundry and Ironclad memberships. You will leave it with a tested baseline, a logbook account and a place in the Novice powerlifting class.

Is HIIT at Forge safe if I am returning from injury?

Provided your clinician has cleared you for impact-free conditioning, yes. Sleds, rows and bikes give us a full engine menu with no landing forces. Tell the coach what you were cleared for and the prescription changes around it.

Do I need my own equipment?

No. Belts, straps, sleeves, chalk, kettlebells up to 48 kg and calibrated plates are on the floor. Most athletes buy flat shoes in their first month because squishy running shoes cost you stability under load — we can recommend three pairs at any budget.

What happens if I fall behind on a block?

You carry the sessions across rather than repeating the week. Coach Vane reviews any logbook that slips more than three sessions behind and either trims the volume or moves you into the next block’s first two weeks.
