§ 02Guest Pass

Book a Session Before You Commit

One coached class, no payment, no card details. Pick a session that fits your week, tell us who you are, and a coach will meet you at the door with a loaner belt and a tour of the floor.

Used only to text you if the class fills or moves rooms.

Optional. One line is plenty.

Read Membership Instead
Guest pass reserved. Athlete, a coach will confirm your session by email within two working hours and text the room the night before.
  • ArriveTen minutes early. First-timers get a floor tour and a loaner belt.
  • BringFlat-soled shoes, a water bottle, a towel. Nothing else.
  • CancelText the floor line and the space goes to the waitlist — no hard feelings, no charge.

§ 03Open Floor

Train Unscripted, With a Coach in the Building

Solo programming? The floor is yours on your own plan. One coach is on duty during every open block — free to ask a question about a setup, never hovering.

Evening Platforms

20:00 to 21:30, all three platform bays plus the dumbbell rack and bands. Busiest block of the week; booking ahead is unnecessary, but arrive before 20:30 if you want the rack with the cameras.

Peak-Hour Etiquette, Written Down

One bench, one rack, twenty minutes between heavy sets during 17:30–19:00. Clip your plates to the rack, chalk the bar only on the platforms, and put every weight back before you leave the floor.
