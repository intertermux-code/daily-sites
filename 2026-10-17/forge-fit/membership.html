03.1Muscle counts

Why the Number Lies

BMI divides mass by height and knows nothing about what that mass is made of. Lifters, rowers and anyone rebuilding after a layoff will read high.

03.2What we track

Lifts, Waist, Recovery

Ironclad members get a waist measurement, a shoulder-to-waist ratio and a monthly lift test. That trio says far more than a BMI ever will.

§ 05Terms in Plain English

The Small Print, Without the Small Print

Is There a Minimum Term?

No. Every membership is monthly and cancels by email before the 25th. Annual plans are billed ten months for twelve, and unused months are refunded at the same rate if you leave early.

What Happens to My Logbook If I Cancel?

It stays yours. Export it as a spreadsheet or a PDF on the way out, or leave it dormant for up to two years and pick the same history up when you return — plenty of members cycle through twice.

Can I Freeze My Membership?

Yes, for illness, travel or a meet camp: four weeks a year on Solo, six on Foundry, eight on Ironclad. Billing pauses on the day you ask, not on the day we get round to it.

Do You Run Sessions for Under-18s?

The Saturday 11:00 youth skills class is open from fourteen, with a parent or guardian on the viewing bench for the first four weeks. Under-14s train one-to-one with a coach on Sunday mornings.

What If I Injure Myself on the Floor?

Coaches log every class, so an incident can be reviewed against the plan that was written. We hold public liability insurance, and the first aid kit and two trauma-trained coaches are by the squat racks.

Still Not Sure Which Tier?

Take the free trial week with Foundry-class access, train three sessions, then choose. Most athletes who start on Solo move up after their second coached class.
