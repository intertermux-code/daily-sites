§ 03Coaching Standard

What You Should Expect From Any Forge Coach

03.1Before class

Read Logbooks

Your coach knows last week’s loads before you walk in. If a session changes, they will tell you the reason, not just the new number.

03.2During class

Cue One Thing

One correction per athlete per set, spoken while the bar is down. We do not stack three cues on a lifter mid-attempt.

03.3After class

Write It Up

Session notes land in your logbook within twenty minutes, including what worked, what was dropped and what the next session adjusts.

Train With a Named Coach

Ironclad membership pairs you with one of the six lead coaches, gives you a written twelve-week block and books a fortnightly one-to-one session.
