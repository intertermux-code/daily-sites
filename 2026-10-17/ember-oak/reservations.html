Late & no-show policy

We hold your table fifteen minutes. After that the fire keeps cooking and the seat goes to whoever is at the bar. Call us and we will almost always find you something — we just cannot promise the same table.

Large parties

Seven and eight go to the long table on Wednesdays and Sundays, one eight-course family service, $78 a head. Email tables@emberandoak.com with a date and we will send the whole thing back the same day.

Gift of a dinner

Gift cards are issued in $50, $100 and $200 amounts, never expire, and can be collected at the bar. Ask for one on any visit or email the front of house.

02 Choosing where to sit

Three rooms, one fire, very different evenings.

The fire counter

Twelve stools an arm’s length from the coals. You will be cooked for directly, and you will get the pan juices on a piece of bread. Loud in the best way.
