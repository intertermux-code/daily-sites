02 Menu · Written at 2:30 pm, served from 5:00

Everything here has been over coals.

The card below is the autumn one. If a line has moved or disappeared, the kitchen decided that that morning, not the printer. Prices are in US dollars; service charge is added for parties of seven or more.

Asterisk * = cooked directly in the embers.
A dagger † = available every day we are open.
Tell us about allergies when you book, not when you arrive.

One card, three sections, written after the kitchen ate.

Nothing here is cooked in a gas oven. Where a dish needs low, even heat it gets the coals banked out and left in the falling flame of the hearth, which is why the dessert list is short.

Small things for the middle of the table, most of them designed to be eaten with your hands and the flatbread that arrives twenty seconds later.

05 Before you order

Dietary needs

Vegetarian and vegan menus exist and are not an afterthought — say the word when you book and the kitchen will cook a full parallel service. We have a gluten-free bread and every fire station has a clean grate section.

Children

Welcome until 8:00 pm, and we will happily put a flatbread and a carrot in front of anyone under twelve at no charge. There is no kids’ menu because there is no need for one.

Service charge

A 20 per cent service charge is added to tables of seven or more and distributed evenly across the whole team, kitchen included. It is not a tip and we do not treat it like one.

Changes

Prices hold through the season. If a farm cannot deliver, the dish comes off rather than being filled in with something bought from a catalogue.
