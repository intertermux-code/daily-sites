Manage Your Email

    Change cadence or beats

    Every footer carries a preferences link. It opens a page with no login, and the change takes effect on the next send.

  • 02

    Pause instead of leaving

    A 30-, 60- or 90-day pause exists for people who are about to have a hard month. It resumes itself and sends nothing while it runs.

  • 03

    Switch an address

    Move your membership to a new address from the preferences page. Both sides must confirm; we do not transfer paid terms on request alone.

  • 04

    Delete everything

    One click removes your address and every stored preference within 24 hours. Our mailer keeps a tombstone record of the deletion date and nothing else.

Asked Often

Is there a paywall?

On none of the reporting. Membership buys the editions, the print quarterly and a vote on what we chase.

Do you A/B test subject lines?

Once a quarter, on the Daily Wire, and the winning variant is disclosed in the next issue's editor note.

Can I reply?

Yes. Every edition replies to a real desk address, and a person on it answers about 40 a week.
