How the index works

A Beat Has to Earn Its Slot

Every topic here is owned by a named person, and every one of them was argued for in an open meeting. If we cannot tell you who checks the docket on a Tuesday, we do not create the page.

Counts are live against the archive and refresh with each issue. A topic with fewer than ten stories is listed, but marked as developing — and if you think it should have more, the request board is open to members.

Two kinds of beat exist here. The first is a standing brief: somebody reads the docket, the filing, or the teardown every week whether anything is happening or not. The second is a running thread — the interconnection queue, the firmware ledger — where the dataset itself is the story and the article is only this month's diff.

  • Beats are renamed in an open meeting, minuted and published.
  • Retired beats stay in the index with their final count and the last story filed.
  • No beat appears here unless at least one person has filed three stories on it.
