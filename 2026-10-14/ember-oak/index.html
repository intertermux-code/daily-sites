01 / THE HEARTH PHILOSOPHY

The Flame carries no deception.

At Ember & Oak, our hearth is anchored by 800°C binchotan embers and Japanese white oak logs sourced from sustainable hardwood groves. We reject high-tech gadgets in favor of pure cast iron, hand-forged steel grates, and precise temperature zones dictated solely by radiant heat and distance.

The Open Kitchen — Hand-forged steel grill hearth engineered for dual radiant searing.

Every ingredient — from 45-day dry-aged Hokkaido A5 Wagyu to heirloom maitake mushrooms — is treated with wabi-sabi reverence: celebrating natural imperfections, seasonal maturity, and the unmistakable nuance of woodsmoke.
